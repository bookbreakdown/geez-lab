NOTES:
- Line 3 ተጠመቀነ: the fifth glyph reads as plain ቀ (no ቅ side-stroke visible); standard would be ቅ. Transcribed as seen.
- Line 3 end / line 4 start: read as በእ / ሚን. The line-4 initial glyph has the bottom-right hook this hand uses for the 3rd order (compare ሢ line 15, ሪ line 10), so ሚ not ሙ. The line-3 final glyph has the flat top bar this hand uses for እ (compare አ with curled top in ሰብአ line 15, እምአ line 12). Word as written is unclear to me.
- Line 4 አብ: initial glyph read as አ but the top is ambiguous between the curled አ and the barred እ forms.
- Line 4 and 10, 18: the double separator is written as two ፡ close together; transcribed as ።.
- Lines 5 and 6: a red cross-of-dots mark (transcribed ፨) followed by a black ፡.
- Line 7 (red rubric) ቀሌምንጠስ: the fifth glyph shows no ring on top, so ጠ rather than ጦ; red ink may be faded.
- Line 11 ሃይማኖቶ and line 24 ፍኖቶሙ: ኖ and ቶ are written with a fused loop on top; read as ኖ/ቶ from the ነ-foot and ተ-cross, not ዮ.
- Line 13 initial glyph: has the bottom-left leg identical to the ኃ of ብዙኃን (lines 11, 13) and unlike the ኀ of line 1, so transcribed ኃው (standard would be ኀው).
- Line 16: the first ወ of ወወበሃይማ has a horizontal stroke above it (scribal cancellation of a dittograph). Both ወ kept in the transcription.
- Line 20 እምቀድስት: ቀ vs ቅ unclear; a small foot may be present. Transcribed ቀ.
- Line 23 initial glyph: a ጸ/ደ-family shape with a small ring at lower right; read as ጼ (ወይጼወኑ), but ዴ is possible from shape alone.
- Line 24 ወጠቀደ: read glyph by glyph; word not recognised.
