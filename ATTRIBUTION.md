## Other scholarship and reference texts

The historical experiments used Sylvain Grébaut’s French translation as an
indirect semantic witness for earlier Qalementos books. That work is based on
**d’Abbadie 78**, not Ma IX 1, and cannot establish exact transcription accuracy
for this manuscript.

Alessandro Bausi’s 1992 Italian study and translation is relevant scholarship.
Its prose is not a source for this repository’s English and is not included.
Modern English reference translations and answer packs from the private research
workspace are also excluded. Short third-party quotations embedded in research
records retain their source rights and are not offered under our licence.
