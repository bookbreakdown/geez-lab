# Current research status

Prepared October 15, 2026, for [EthiopianCanon.com](https://ethiopiancanon.com).
**Unreleased; expert review pending.**

## What exists

The main corpus contains **68 consecutive sides, 156r–189v**, representing
34 manuscript leaves. It was drafted during the September 17 run. Earlier trials
and comparison files overlap and must not be added to 68 as if they were distinct
completed pages. See [CORPUS.md](CORPUS.md) for file counts.

The private acquisition index held 250 book-allocation rows, all marked as
available. Those describe **249 unique sides**, 152r–276r, because 241r occurs
in two book allocations. Images are excluded from this public repository. A
fresh clone has no downloaded-image progress until the downloader checks it.

The run reported 18 scriptural citation checks, six confirmed numeral sets on
six sides, and 62 checked joins with one unresolved. These are historical run
reports, not an independent audit or an automatic accuracy measurement. With
68 consecutive sides, 67 neighbouring joins are available for inspection;
available joins and historically checked joins are different counts.

## What remains uncertain

- Character error rate on this manuscript has not been measured against an
  exact, expert transcription. An English witness checks selected meaning.
- Recurring letterforms and spellings vary between AI readers. Review should
  flag editorial choices without silently standardising the scribe’s spellings.
- The working key has no secure positive shape for the 30 and 50 signs.
- Several numerical differences may be genuine witness variants. Othniel and
  Abimelech are candidates for expert adjudication, not settled corrections.
- Text identification across the range is incomplete. The run identified the
  *Rest of the Words of Baruch* on 165v–166v; other identifications remain disputed.
  The old catalogue-derived book ranges are provisional working labels.
- The neighbouring sides 187r/187v have an unresolved continuity issue.

## Where the run stopped

Bulk drafting stopped before 190r when the historical reader’s allowance ran
out on September 17. Later local additions include a calibration reading of
43r and related notes. File modification times do not establish when the
underlying experiment occurred. No completed expert review is included here.

## Publication boundary

This package shares research and tools. It does not add Books IV–VII to a
published Bible, establish a critical edition, or justify a “first English
translation” claim. Those require scholarly and bibliographic work beyond this
repository. See the [roadmap](ROADMAP.md).
