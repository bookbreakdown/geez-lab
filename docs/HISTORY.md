# Research history and corrections

A public account of the Ge’ez Lab investigation at
[EthiopianCanon.com](https://ethiopiancanon.com). This is a curated history,
not the complete internal run log. Events are grouped by recorded research date;
some original log timestamps were corrected during the run.

## September 13, 2026: feasibility

Tests altered Ge’ez Enoch passages to distinguish reading from simply recalling
a familiar English translation. Several readers followed the altered content.
The result applied to the tested typed passages, not to unknown manuscript prose.
A separate clean-type image trial found substantially different transcription
performance between runs. It did not establish handwriting accuracy.

## September 14–15: a source, a sample, and comparisons

The investigation moved from difficult EMML microfilm to Tübingen Ma IX 1.
A three-side English sample was prepared from 153r, 153v, and 154r. Although the
transcription marked no characters illegible, translation required 35 interventions
across 72 lines. That mismatch exposed the inadequacy of a question-mark count.

Two-pass comparison tooling and independently produced drafts followed.
Large disagreements undermined the plan to automate reconciliation. Those rates
measured similarity between outputs, not accuracy against an exact transcription.
Earlier briefs proposing automatic confidence from agreement are superseded.

## September 17: a calibrated run

A small resolution comparison did not materially improve agreement with an
independent reader. Attention shifted to a scribal key, fresh contexts, page
continuity, and quotations as limited checks. The main run saved 68 consecutive
side drafts, 156r–189v, before the reader’s allowance was exhausted.

The run reported 18 citation checks, six numeral sets confirmed on six sides,
and 62 checked joins with one unresolved. These are the original run’s reports;
this public preparation did not independently repeat the scholarly checks.

The *Rest of the Words of Baruch* was identified in the presumed Book IV range.
That finding prompted doubt about applying book boundaries from another witness
to this manuscript. Other work-identification and source-boundary hypotheses
failed or remained disputed.

## Corrections that must travel with the results

- A proposed systematic low-tens reading bias partly resulted from comparing
  different manuscript witnesses. It was retracted. Upward corrections based
  on that prior introduced additional errors in calibration readings.
- Greek-derived numeral-shape analogies did not reliably predict this hand’s
  tens. The key retains the failure and the still-unanchored 30/50 shapes.
- Rank-list length did not reliably distinguish source layers. A continuous
  passage contained different list lengths.
- A proposed general reverse-order quotation habit did not hold across the corpus.
- Nonstandard spelling should be preserved and editorial choices flagged.
  Cross-reader spelling drift remains unresolved.
- Labels such as `groundtruth` and `calibrated` describe experiment roles;
  they do not certify that their contents are correct.

## October 15: public preparation

A separate repository was prepared with source provenance, portable tools,
research status notices, source credits, and contribution guidance. Original
readings were preserved; private operations and reference answer packs were
excluded. No release, publication claim, or scholarly endorsement follows from
packaging the research.
