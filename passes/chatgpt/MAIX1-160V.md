## UNCERTAIN
L2–3 እግ / እሞ: unresolved verb; exact middle consonants uncertain.
L3 ነሳሒያን: could refer to penitents or receivers of confession; role is not securely established.
L4–5 እመከመ፡ነስሐ / ዝሙ፡ይንግል: provisional reading; possessor and subject of revealing are uncertain.
L7 ወፈዲቁ: unresolved word; not supplied with a conjectural meaning.
L8–11 መኰንንት … ወይቀውሙ: the rank noun is provisional, and the intervening phrase about eating, the Christian and speech is not securely read or parsed.
L12–13 ይበው, ይትከነዩ, ይትረሰው: three provisional verbs; none has a secure translation in this pass.
L14–16 ዘየሐውር፡ቀብሮ፡እስከ፡እቱ … እቱ: provisional letter sequence with unresolved burial-related syntax.
L17–19 ይግምእ, ለእግለ, እምዕድ: provisional readings prevent a secure interpretation of the death-judgment clause.
L19 ዘይትዐገሥ: provisionally 'who endures'; final character uncertain.
L20–22 ኅዳገ፡ነፍስ፡ወመጽብሐን … እንግፍዎሙ፡እምዕድ: this entire sequence is highly provisional and not securely translated; recognizable individual words do not establish its sentence meaning.

Opening: 45%. Middle: 30%. End: 25%. Only the repeated impurity formula and some isolated clauses are reasonably secure. The ruler/eating passage, burial clause, death-judgment clause and L20–22 are not fit for print; substantial portions of both translation sections remain unresolved.
