## UNCERTAIN
L3 ይስተሐይድ: unresolved verb concerning a virgin; consonants and inflection provisional.
L4 ይወግር፡ዐይነ: first verb uncertain; no idiomatic or anatomical act inferred from the apparent 'eye'.
L5–6 አርከሳ፡ድንግልናሁ: object suffix and possessor appear inconsistent; retained rather than regularized.
L7–8 እስከ፡ወይነስሕ: apparent conjunction after 'until' retained; verbal form needs review.
L13–14 በሥርዓተ፡ተባዕት: provisionally 'in the manner of a male'; this interpretation is less secure than the broad letter sequence.
L15 ወእመቱ: unresolved term/subject; no female subject or parallel has been silently supplied.
L16 ይዝሙ: provisional verb interpreted as fornication; exact final vowel uncertain.
L17–18 እለ፡አልባስነ፡መተ፡ዘማት፡ወመዋስበት: highly provisional sequence; no secure translation of the qualifying phrase is possible in this pass.
L20–22 ወእምከመ፡ፈጸመ … ይግበር: temporal and verbal relationship uncertain; no object is restored as manuscript text.

## CONFIDENCE
Opening: 45%. Middle: 42%. End: 62%. L3–4 and L13–18 are not fit for print; the penitential framework is more secure. Both translations retain unresolved spans rather than claiming a complete interpretation.
