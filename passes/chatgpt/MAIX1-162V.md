## UNCERTAIN

L2,11,13,19–20,23 እዘዝዎ: command verb morphology uncertain throughout; spelling retained as read.
L3–4 እለ፡ይእቱ፡ህግ: unresolved relative construction; the role of the leaders is not inferred.
L7–9 ወነገረ፡ዕወስት፡ወይትአመን፡በት፡ይግበር፡እመ፡ዘይስት: provisional sequence; much of the first prohibition is not securely legible to this reader or translatable.
L13 ሊቃናተ፡ቤት: 'leaders of the house' retained without supplying 'church' absent on this line.
L15 ዘየሐልቅ: unresolved verb concerning the self; no specific injury has been inferred.
L16 በእቀብ: unresolved instrument or modifier.
L16 ፈውሰ: provisionally 'remedy'; the noun may be misread, and the resulting statement remains uncertain.
L21–22 ሃነ፡ኀለፈ፡ሕይወተ፡ዘእሁ: first word unresolved, possessive phrase provisional; literal 'passed beyond his own life' is not a secure idiomatic interpretation.
