## PROSE
"And this is what I say: by my own will I came, so that I would taste death. And after I had died and risen [unresolved word] the teaching of the apostles as far as the ends of the world."

[A phrase, "and those who received", is struck through here.] He commanded us too, that we should teach as far as the ends of the world. Those who receive our teaching enter into life. Those who do not receive our teaching he [judges?] severely, upon their own heads, and rebukes them greatly because of the word he told them; and they will not escape judgment [unresolved word], because our word is the word of the Son, which he took from his Father. He taught us, and our word was not empty. Even if it becomes a witness against them for judgment, it is as our Saviour said to the Jews: "If I had not come and told them, they would have been saved [sentence continues on the next folio]

## UNCERTAIN
Lines 1–2 — ብሂልየ፡በፈቃድየ፡ is written twice, at the end of line 1 and again at the start of line 2. Transcribed both times as written; looks like dittography, not corrected by the scribe.
Line 5 — ፈጻፍድ (continuing ተ from line 4, so ተፈጻፍድ). Every glyph is legible (ፈ ጻ ፍ ድ) but the word is unknown to me; I could not resolve it to any form and did not guess. Translated as a gap.
Line 5 — ትምሕርተ with ሕ (and ትምሕርትነ lines 10, 12): standard spelling is ትምህርት with ህ. Kept the manuscript's ሕ.
Line 7 — ወአለ፡ተወክፉ፡ has a line ruled through it (scribal cancellation). Transcribed inside square brackets; left out of the prose.
Lines 7, 11 — ወአለ / ወአለሰ with አ, against ወእለ with እ in line 9. Both kept as written.
Line 10 — ይበውዑ: read the last glyph as ዑ; ኡ (ይበውኡ) is possible.
Line 12 — ጽኑዔ: read the last glyph as ዔ; ዓ (ጽኑዓ, "severely") would be the expected form.
Line 13 — ይዬኖሙ: the second glyph is the least certain reading on the page. I read it as ዬ; the sense wants a verb of judging (ይኴንኖሙ or ይዴይኖሙ) but neither matches the glyph count or shape well enough to write it. Translated as "[judges?]".
Line 13 — ለዕለ: read as ለ; ላዕለ (ላ) is the expected form.
Line 13 — ርእስሙ: read the third glyph as ስ; ሶ (ርእሶሙ) is the expected form.
Line 16 — ይደኃ: glyphs read as ይ ደ ኃ (the second may be the same glyph as line 13 position 2). I could not resolve the word; left it untranslated.
Line 19 — ሐሪነ (continuing ወመ from line 18): read the second glyph as ሪ; ሬ or ረ (ወመሐረነ, "and he taught us") are possible.
Line 19 — ወኤከነ: read as ኤ ከ; ኢኮነ (ወኢኮነ, "and it was not") is a plausible alternative and is how I translated it, flagged with (?) in the sense.
Line 20 — ቀለነ: read the first glyph as ቀ; ቃለነ ("our word") is the expected form.
Line 20 — ዲእቲ: read the first glyph as ዲ; ይእቲ ("it is") is the expected form.
Line 24 — ነገርኩሙ: read as ኩ ሙ; ነገርክዎሙ or ነገርክሙ would be standard. Kept as written.
Line 24 — እምድኅኑ: last word of the page, sentence incomplete; read as ድ ኅ ኑ. Translation "they would have been saved" is from the glyphs only; the clause does not close on this folio.
Punctuation — the red-dotted marks at lines 9, 11, 22 are transcribed as ። (section marks); the black double points elsewhere as ፡.

## CONFIDENCE
Opening (lines 1–8): 80%. The dittography in lines 1–2 is certain; the word ተፈጻፍድ in line 5 is the one thing I would not put in print.
Middle (lines 9–16): 70%. Lines 9–12 and 14–15 are solid. ይዬኖሙ (line 13) and ይደኃ (line 16) I would not put in print; ለዕለ፡ርእስሙ needs a second reader.
End (lines 17–24): 75%. Lines 17–18 and 21–23 are solid. ወኤከነ፡ዕራቃነ፡ቀለነ (lines 19–20) and the incomplete final word እምድኅኑ are readings, not print-ready text.
