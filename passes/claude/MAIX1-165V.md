## UNCERTAIN
ወኤያሪ|ስያሙ (lines 1–2): read as ወኤያሪስያሙ; not a form I can resolve. Second letter could be ኤ or ኢ; first letter of line 2 could be ስ or ሰ. Left unresolved in translation.
ዒዋዌሆሙ (line 3): read letter by letter as ዒ ዋ ዌ ሆ ሙ; the first glyph has a small attached mark at lower right (ዒ rather than ዐ). Word not resolved; possibly a scribal deformation of another word. Left unresolved.
ምዝብ (line 4): the third glyph ብ has a horizontal stroke through it, which may be a cancellation. If cancelled the word is ወእምዝ ("and then"). Transcribed as written with the letter retained.
ስበ (line 4): read as ስ (sixth order), standard orthography ሶበ ("when"). Manuscript form kept.
ተመይጠሙ (line 4): read as ጠ (first order) + ሙ; standard would be ተመይጦሙ / መየጦሙ. Manuscript form kept.
ወአባ (line 7): read as ወአባ ("and Abba"); second glyph could be አ or ኣ. Meaning as a title before ባሮክ is inferred from the letters only.
ለኤርምያሱ (line 7): final glyph read as ሱ rather than ስ; standard ለኤርምያስ. Could be ስ with the word divider run into it.
ተሣለተ (line 9): read as ተ ሣ ለ ተ; not a verb I can resolve. Third and fourth glyphs could be ሀ ለ (giving ተሣሀለ, "had mercy") but the strokes as seen read ለ ተ. Manuscript form kept, unresolved.
ግሥሃ (line 9): read as ግ ሥ ሃ; second glyph ሥ or ሠ, third ሃ or ሀ. Word not resolved.
ለዕሊነ (line 10): third glyph read as ሊ; standard ለዕሌነ ("upon us"). Manuscript form kept.
ወይሰየም (line 10): third glyph read as ሰ, fourth as የ; expected ወይሰይም ("he will appoint"). Manuscript form kept.
ከህናተ (line 11): first glyph read as ከ not ካ, second as ህ; standard ካህናተ ("priests"). Manuscript form kept.
መዛግ|ርተ (lines 11–12): first glyph of line 12 read as ር; the expected word would be መዛግብተ ("treasurers"). Could be a scribal ር for ብ or an unread letter. Manuscript form kept.
ት|ከት (lines 12–13): first glyph of line 13 read as ከ; expected ትካት ("former times"). Manuscript form kept.
ከሀይያን (line 14): third glyph read as ይ; it could be ድ (giving ከሀድያን, "deniers, unbelievers"). Manuscript form kept.
ወኢት|ሃብ (lines 14–15): read as ሃ (fourth order); standard ወኢትሀብ ("do not give"). Manuscript form kept.
ውሉደ ፡ ዝኩ (line 15): read as written, "the sons of that one"; the construction is odd and the second word could be something else. Translation follows the letters only.
ወኢ ፡ ትንሣእ (line 15): there is a word divider between ወኢ and ትንሣእ where one word (ወኢትንሣእ) is expected. Transcribed with the divider.
ወለቱ (line 16): read as ቱ; accusative ወለቶ expected. Manuscript form kept.
ኢያተመዓዕ (line 18): read as ኢ ያ ተ መ ዓ ዕ; standard ኢይትመዓዕ ("not be angry"). Second and third glyphs uncertain between ያተ and ይት. Manuscript form kept.
ወኢያፍቅፍዕ (line 19): read letter by letter as ወ ኢ ያ ፍ ቅ ፍ ዕ; verb not resolved. The ቅ and second ፍ are both doubtful. Left unresolved in translation.
ኤርምያሰ (line 21): final glyph read as ሰ; standard ኤርምያስ. Manuscript form kept.
ሀለው (line 21): final glyph read as ው; standard ሀለዉ. Manuscript form kept.
ከ|ኸለደውያን (lines 21–22): line 21 ends with ከ and the first glyph of line 22 is ከ with an additional mark above it, transcribed as ኸ. Context would expect ኀበ ፡ ከለዳውያን ("among the Chaldeans"); as written it reads ከ ኸለደውያን. Third glyph of line 22 read as ደ, standard ዳ. All kept as seen.
ለዠቂቀ (line 23): second glyph is ደ with an additional stroke above it, transcribed as ዠ; expected ደቂቀ ("children of"). Kept as seen.
ዓቁ (line 23): read as ዓ ቁ; expected ዑቁ ("beware"). First glyph uncertain between ዓ and ዑ. Manuscript form kept.
?ጉ (line 23): a dark stain sits between the divider after ዓቁ and the final glyph ጉ; the spacing allows one glyph under the stain. Written as ? followed by ጉ. Not resolved.
አዓስቲያክሙ (line 24): second glyph read as ዓ; expected አንስቲያክሙ ("your wives"). Could be ን. Manuscript form kept.
ዝንሣእክሙ (line 24): first glyph read as ዝ; expected ዘንሣእክሙ ("whom you took"). Manuscript form kept.

## CONFIDENCE
Opening third (lines 1–8): 65%. The names and the sentence about returning to their country are secure; ወኤያሪስያሙ and ዒዋዌሆሙ are unresolved and I would not print either.
Middle third (lines 9–16): 70%. The list of priests, [treasurers], rulers and the prohibition on intermarriage are clear in outline; I would not print ተሣለተ ፡ ወይቤ ፡ ግሥሃ, መዛግርተ, or ከሀይያን without a second witness.
End third (lines 17–24): 60%. The exhortation to Israel and the address to the children of Israel are clear; I would not print ወኢያፍቅፍዕ, the ከ|ኸለደውያን join, ዓቁ ፡ ?ጉ, or አዓስቲያክሙ without reconciliation.
