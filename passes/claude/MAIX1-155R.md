## LITERAL
[…]yu. And he makes-rise the sun from the west, and the moon also toward Ilam(?). And at that time will arise Zebulun and Naphtali, ḫəśāda(?) tānawʿā(?) and Capernaum; you boast, Zebulun and Naphtali; they will be sought(?), Chorazin and Bethsaida, because he/they liken(?) this man [to] Christ. And when they (f.) saw his works, they were not ashamed, because he was not upright, the one who sees, but all his work [was] with partiality. And at that time God will withhold the rain from heaven, and it will become the house of winter(?), and the earth will remain alone without dew and storm(?); and the rain of the earth will become qabāri(?) and storm; and the springs of water also — he will not give a spring of water; and the sea will become dry land; and those days will be shortened(?), the evil ones, as he said in the gospel; and when those da[ys] are not shortened […]

## PROSE
[…]. He will make the sun rise from the west, and the moon as well toward Ilam(?). At that time Zebulun and Naphtali will rise up, ḫəśāda(?) tānawʿā(?) and Capernaum. You boast, Zebulun and Naphtali. Chorazin and Bethsaida will be sought out(?), because this man is likened(?) to Christ. When they saw his works they were not ashamed, because the one who sees was not upright; all his work was done with partiality. At that time God will withhold the rain from heaven; it will become the house of winter(?), and the earth will be left alone, without dew or storm(?). The rain of the earth will become qabāri(?) and storm, and the springs of water will give no spring of water. The sea will become dry land. Those evil days will be shortened(?), as he said in the gospel, and if those days are not shortened […]

## UNCERTAIN
- L1 first glyph ዩ: Y-shaped top with a ring at the foot; read as ዩ (tail of a word from the previous folio), but ዶ is possible. Not translated.
- L2 ወርኅኒ: the third glyph read as ኅ (ḫə); the diacritic is small and ኀ is possible.
- L2–3 መንግለ፡ኢላም: the word ኢላም is read letter by letter and is not recognised; treated as a proper noun "Ilam(?)". Could be a corrupted or unfamiliar form.
- L3 ይተነሣ / L4 እ (ይተነሣእ): the second glyph read as ተ; in this hand ተ and ት are hard to separate (standard would be ይትነሣእ). Kept as written.
- L4–5 ኅሣ / ደ (ኅሣደ): all three glyphs legible but the word is not recognised; ሣ could be ሤ, the L5 initial ደ could be ዴ (a small stroke at the top right). Left untranslated.
- L5 ታነውዓ: legible but not recognised; the first glyph is ታ rather than ተ. Left untranslated.
- L5 ፤ after ወቀፍርናሆም: lower dot has a tail; read as ፤, plain ፡ possible.
- L6 ትትሚካህ: third glyph read as ሚ (standard orthography would be ትትሜካህ); both initial glyphs carry the ት foot-stroke.
- L7 ይተሐሠሡ: fifth glyph read as ሡ (ሠ with a ring); ዉ is possible. Second glyph ተ/ት ambiguous as above. Meaning "they will be sought" is a guess from the root; flagged.
- L7 ኮራዚ: written without a final ን; kept as written. Followed by ፤ (tailed lower dot).
- L8 ያመስለሁ: glyphs ያ-መ-ስ-ለ-ሁ are each legible but the form is not a recognised verb shape (perhaps ያመስሉ or ያመስልዎ intended). Kept as written; translation "liken(?)" is uncertain.
- L10 ርእያ: third glyph read as ያ (bar, not ring); ርእዩ is possible. Translated as 3 f. pl. "they (f.) saw".
- L10–11 ኢይተሐፈ / ሩ: L11 initial read as ሩ (ring at the foot); ራ possible. ተ/ት ambiguous.
- L11 ኢኮነ: second glyph read as ኮ; ኮ/ኰ diacritic small.
- L11 ርቱዓ: final glyph read as ዓ; ዕ (ርቱዕ) possible.
- L12 ፤ after ግብሩ: tailed lower dot; ፡ possible.
- L12–13 በአድ / ልዖ: L13 second glyph read as ዖ (standard በአድልዎ would have ዎ); kept as written.
- L13 ይከልዕ: final glyph read as ዕ (bar on top); standard ይከልእ would have እ. Kept as written.
- L15 ቤተ፡ክረምት: read as written; "house of winter" is a literal rendering only.
- L17 እግበለ: second glyph read as ግ (flat top, straight stem, foot at left); ን (standard እንበለ "without") is possible and the sense favours it. Translated as "without" but the letter is kept as ግ.
- L17 ወአውል: legible; unusual form (the same word appears as ወአውሎ on L19). Translated "storm(?)" on the assumption of ዐውሎ; flagged.
- L18–19 ቀባ / ሪ (ቀባሪ): L19 initial read as ሪ; ሬ possible. Word not confidently identified; left as qabāri(?).
- L19 ፤ after ወአንቀዕተ: tailed lower dot; ፡ possible.
- L19 ወአውሎ: spelled with አ rather than ዐ; kept as written.
- L20 ኢይሁብ: third glyph read as ሁ (ring); ሀ possible.
- L22 ሐጅራ: second glyph has a flat top bar with a stroke, read as ጅ (a letter of the Amharic extension); ጽ or ፅ (cf. L24 ኢሐፀራ, same root) may be intended. Kept as written; translation "shortened(?)" from the L24 parallel.
- L3, L13 ። : written as two stacked colons (four dots); transcribed as the standard full stop.
- Left margin shows offset ink from the facing page on several lines (L22–24 especially); no marginal annotations transcribed.

## CONFIDENCE
- Opening (L1–8): 80%. Individual glyphs are clear; the words ኢላም, ኅሣደ, ታነውዓ, ይተሐሠሡ and ያመስለሁ are transcribed as seen but not confidently read, and I would not put "Ilam", "ḫəśāda tānawʿā", "will be sought" or "liken" in print.
- Middle (L9–16): 90%. Clean hand, standard vocabulary; only ርእያ, ርቱዓ, በአድልዖ and ይከልዕ carry orthographic doubt. "House of winter" for ቤተ፡ክረምት should not go to print without checking.
- End (L17–24): 85%. እግበለ (ግ/ን), ወአውል, ቀባሪ and ሐጅራ are the weak points; "qabāri" and the whole clause "the rain of the earth will become qabāri and storm" should not go to print as translated.
