## LITERAL
…to you they [ይኄስሑ — verb unresolved; if read ይኔስሑ: "repent"]. And if — repentance — as the elders of the church say to him, let him do (it). And when he has completed his repentance, let him enter and let him be gathered in and let him draw near. [ርኩብ] and [ሰዑብ — both unresolved] is he who does the work of the pagans and joins in the making of feasts with them: let him stand (apart) from the church, and until he completes all his repentance; and let him do, let him do (what) they commanded him, the elders of the church. And after he has completed his repentance, let him enter and let him be gathered in and let him draw near — all this, abandoning his former deed, and not returning to it. Until repentance [ዐሊይ — unresolved]: wisdom, the fear of God, and good counsel, and all that they do; and his praise also [ይትበር — unresolved] forever. Keep this canon and give it to the appointed ones of the church so that they keep it.

## PROSE
…to you they [repent — reading uncertain]. As for penance, let him do it as the elders of the church direct him. When he has finished his penance, let him come in, rejoin the congregation, and receive communion. [ርኩብ and ሰዑብ — unresolved] is the man who practises pagan rites and joins them in celebrating their feasts: let him be kept out of the church until he has completed all his penance, and let him do what the elders of the church have commanded him. After he has finished his penance, let him come in, rejoin the congregation, and receive communion — all this on condition that he abandons his former conduct and does not go back to it. Until repentance [ዐሊይ — unresolved]: wisdom, the fear of God, sound counsel, and everything they do; and his praise too [ይትበር — unresolved] forever. Keep this canon, and hand it to the appointed officers of the church so that they may keep it.

## UNCERTAIN
- L1 `ይኄስሑ`: second glyph read as ኄ; could equally be ኔ (ይኔስሑ, imperfect of ነስሐ "repent"), which would fit the passage. Ring on the right is clear, body shape between ኀ and ነ not decisive. Verb left untranslated.
- L1 final glyph `ን`: written narrower and with a hook at the top unlike the plain ን ending L4; considered ኅ. Read as ን giving ን|ስሓ across the line break; flag as a form difference.
- L6 `ይቀረብ` (and L15 `ይቀረብ`): fourth-order ረ visible in both, where ይቀርብ would be expected; kept as written.
- L6 `፡፡`: two black word-dividers with a red ornament (four red dots) between them after ይቀረብ — treated as a section mark, not text.
- L6 `ርኩብ ፡ ወሰዑብ`: both words legible glyph by glyph but neither resolves to a word I can identify; ኩ could be ኵ; ዑ read with confidence. Left untranslated rather than guessed.
- L6 final glyph `ው` (giving ውእቱ with L7): the bottom tick that distinguishes ው from ወ is present but small.
- L8-9 `በዓ|ለት`: manuscript reads በዓለት; standard would be በዓላት. Kept as written.
- L11-12 `ወይ|ግበር ፡ ይግበር`: the verb is written twice in succession; apparent dittography. Both kept.
- L12-13 `ሊቃ|ቃናት`: ቃ repeated across the line break (ሊቃ at end of L12, ቃናት at start of L13); apparent dittography. Both kept.
- L12 `አዘዝዎ`: no relative ዘ- before it; read as written.
- L14 `እምድኅረ`: fourth glyph read as ኅ (stroke on the right); ኀ possible.
- L16 `ኃዲግ`: first glyph read as ኃ (ḫā); ኀ possible.
- L16 `ዘቀደሚ`: last glyph has a ring low on the right, read as ሚ; ሜ possible; third glyph ደ could be ዳ (ዘቀዳሚ "of before, former" would be standard). Translated by sense as "former"; form flagged.
- L17 `ወኢይገቢዕ`: second glyph read as ኢ; the verb form with ቢ and ዕ is non-standard (ወኢይገብእ expected); kept as written, translated as "not return".
- L18 `ዐሊይ`: read glyph by glyph as ዐ-ሊ-ይ; the second glyph has the foot of ሊ rather than the box of ቢ, but ዐቢይ "great" would make sense of the phrase. Left untranslated.
- L20 `ሠናይት`: a ት follows ሠናይ with no divider; feminine form kept as written.
- L21 `ዘይገብሩ`: second glyph has the small ring of ሩ; ረ (ዘይገብረ) possible.
- L21 `ወስብሐቲሁኒ`: seventh glyph read as ኒ; ኔ possible.
- L21-22 `ይትበር|ለዓለም`: no ክ at the end of L21 or start of L22, so ይትበረክ "be blessed" is not what is written; ይትበር as it stands is not a form I can resolve. Left untranslated.
- L22 `፡፡`: two dividers after ለዓለም, taken as a sentence end.
- L23 `ወሀቦሙ`: third glyph read as ቦ; ሆ considered.

## CONFIDENCE
- Opening (L1-8): 80%. The verb ይኄስሑ in L1 and the pair ርኩብ ፡ ወሰዑብ in L6 are legible but unresolved; I would not print any rendering of those three words.
- Middle (L9-16): 85%. Text is clear; the two dittographies (ይግበር ፡ ይግበር, ሊቃ|ቃናት) are certain as readings but need an editorial decision. ዘቀደሚ needs a check.
- End (L17-24): 75%. ዐሊይ (L18) and ይትበር (L21) are not resolved and the sentence "until repentance … forever" does not parse cleanly as I have it; I would not put L18-22 in print without the second pass. L22-24 (the closing canon formula) is secure.
