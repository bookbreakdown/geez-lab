- Line 1, word 2 `አፅ`: the second glyph is a ring with a top-left tail and a bottom-right foot. Read as ፅ; ው (giving `አው`, "or") and ዖ are both possible. Left untranslated rather than guessed.
- Line 1 `ኢነገርክኑ`: the ኢ in this hand has a bottom-left foot that looks like ኤ; read as ኢ (same shape recurs at lines 5, 8, 14, 18). ክ is sixth-order, so the subject (you/I) is not recoverable from the form.

- End (lines 17–24): 65%. Lines 17–18, 20b–22a and 24 are clear. `የሐውፍ ፡ ተቢሁ`, `ወይእታ ፡ እማሬ` and `ይስጸሕ` are not.
