## PROSE
When the branches of the fig tree grow tender, know that the time of harvest is near. The fig tree, too, has sprouted, and they are called [ቲራን]. These will be witnesses at his hand, and his angels will receive them into joy, and not a hair of their heads will be lost. After him Enoch and Elijah will come down. They will preach, and they will put him to shame, the [መምስለይ], the enemy of righteousness and the [ጼደ] of falsehood. Then their heads will be cut off, and Michael and Gabriel will raise them up and carry them into the garden of joy, and the [drops] of their blood will not fall on the earth. And the last enemy, death, he will abolish, and he will come from [ours] out of heaven to establish the covenant of his kingdom with his chosen ones. ## UNCERTAIN
- Line 1 እዕፁቂሁ: first glyph read as እ (the scribe's እ carries a small foot); standard orthography would be አዕፁቂሁ. Kept as written.
- Line 2 እእምራከመ: two glyphs read እእ, then ራ (not ሩ); no word divider between ራ and ከመ on the page. Standard would be አእምሩ፡ ከመ. The first glyph could also be ኤ. Kept as written.
- Line 3 ማዕረር: second glyph read as ዕ (looped top), where ማእረር would be standard. Kept as written.
- Line 3 and line 4 and line 12: the marks I have written ፨ are red four-dot clusters mixed with black dots; they are section separators, not letters. In line 4 and line 12 a black ፡ precedes the red cluster.
- Lines 4–5 ቲ|ራን: the last glyph of line 4 is a ተ-form with a lower-right foot (ቲ); line 5 opens ራን. The resulting word ቲራን is not one I can identify. The glyph could conceivably be ጊ or ዪ; I have not resolved it.
- Line 4 ይስመዩ: the first glyph is the ይ/ደ form (see the general note below); read as ይ from context. Second glyph read as ስ (right leg extended), where ይሰመዩ would be standard.
- Line 5 እላ: first glyph could be ኤ (ኤላ); the scribe's እ has a foot that makes እ/ኤ hard to separate. Read as እ.
- Line 6 ወይትቀበልዎ: third glyph read as ት (cross with a lower-right foot), but it could be ተ (ወይተቀበልዎ). Second glyph is the ይ/ደ form, read as ይ from context.
- Line 8 ኃጐል: sixth glyph read as ጐ; could be ጕ. Standard ይትኃጐል.
- Line 9 ርእሰሙ: third glyph read ሰ (no right ring), where ርእሶሙ would be standard. Kept as written.
- Line 9 ወእምድኅረሁ: final glyph read as ሁ (ህ with a lower-right ring); ዝ is a less likely alternative.
- Line 10: after ወኤልያስ፡ there is a small extra mark that may be a second ፡ (i.e. ፡፡). Written as single ፡.
- Line 12 መምስለይ: glyphs read መ ም ስ ለ ይ; the final glyph is the ይ/ደ form. The word is not one I can identify; possibly a corruption. Kept as written.
- Line 13 ወጼደ: second glyph read as ጼ (ጸ with a foot), third glyph is the ይ/ደ form, read as ደ. The word is not one I can identify. Kept as written.
- Line 13 ወስቤሃ: second glyph read ስ, where ወሶቤሃ would be standard. Kept as written.
- Line 17 ወነጠጠጣ: second glyph is a bare vertical stroke with a top flag, which is how this scribe writes ነ (compare ገነት at the start of the same line). The fourth glyph has two arches like ጠ plus a short leftward tick at mid-height; I have read it ጠ, but the sense wanted (ነጠብጣብ, "drops") would require ብ. Fifth glyph ጣ (ጠ with a right leg). Word continues with በ at the start of line 18. Unresolved.
- Line 18 ደሙኒ: first glyph is the ይ/ደ form; read as ደ from context ("his blood"). ይሙኒ is the alternative and has no sense I can find.
- Line 18 ዲቡ: first glyph read as ዲ (foot extended right), second as ቡ (ring on right); ዲበ ("upon") is what the syntax wants. Kept as written.
- Line 19 ወለደኃሪኒ: six glyphs ወ ለ ደ ኃ ሪ ኒ; standard would be ወደኃሪኒ without ለ. Kept as written.
- Line 20 ይሠዕር: third glyph read as ዕ, where ይሠዐር would be standard. Kept as written.

- Line 21 እምሰማይ: third glyph read as ሰ, but it could be ለ (እምለማይ); the prong count is not clear at this resolution.

- General: this scribe writes ይ and ደ with what looks to me like the same letterform (a closed loop on the upper left, stem to the lower right with a leftward foot). Every ይ and ደ in this transcription is resolved by context, not by shape: lines 4, 6 (twice), 10, 11, 12, 13, 16, 18, 19, 20, 22, 23. Where context does not decide (lines 12, 13, 18) I have said so above.
- Red ink: ሚካኤል (line 15), ገብ|ርኤል (lines 15–16), ሚካኤል (line 24), and the numeral ፫ (line 24, with red bars above and below) are in red. Written in the same script as the black text.
- Right margin: letters visible along the right edge are bleed-through / the facing leaf, not part of this folio, and are not transcribed.

## CONFIDENCE
- Opening (lines 1–8): 80%. The fig-tree sentence and "not a hair of their head will be lost" are secure. ቲራን (lines 4–5) I would not put in print.
- Middle (lines 9–16): 75%. Enoch and Elijah, the beheading, Michael and Gabriel carrying them to the garden are secure. መምስለይ and ወጼደ (lines 12–13) I would not put in print; the sentence "the enemy of righteousness and the [ጼደ] of falsehood" needs a scholar.
- End (lines 17–24): 65%. ወነጠጠጣበ ደሙኒ (lines 17–18) and እምዚአነ (lines 20–21) I would not put in print; "the drops of his blood will not fall on the earth" is my reading of the sense, not of the letters. The trumpet sentence and ኅሩያኒሆሙ are otherwise sound.
