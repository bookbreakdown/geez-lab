## UNCERTAIN
- Line 1 final glyph: read as ዘ; the same word in lines 4, 11-12 and 16 ends ዓዛሁ, so ዛ is possible. The glyph is angular (not the scribe's round ዕ), so I have not read ዕ. Transcribed as seen.
- Line 2 first glyph: read as ሁ (looped). Could be ሉ. Transcribed as seen.
- Line 2 ተምዕዙ: the ዕ is round and clear; but the vowel of ም (ም vs መ) is not certain in this hand.
- Line 3 መን?ሱ: third glyph unread; shape is in the ጀ / ደ / ጸ family with a cap. Marked ?.
- Line 3 ቀዲስን: read exactly as ቀ ዲ ስ ን. Standard orthography would expect ቅዱሳን ("holy ones"); the vowel orders are not what I see, so the manuscript form is kept. Doubtful.
- Line 3 ወኒራን: word unknown to me; ኒ could be ኔ. Transcribed as seen.
- Lines 3-4 እ/ለመ: read as seen; no word division visible; meaning unresolved.
- Lines 4, 12, 16: ዓ in መዓዛሁ read as ዓ (ring on the left leg), ዛ as ዛ. The word reads consistently across the folio and ያምዕዝ / ምዕዝ in lines 23-24 support the root "fragrance", but the ዓ/ዋ distinction is a hand-shape judgement.
- Line 5 ይመልዕ: first glyph ይ vs ደ — this scribe's ይ is rounded with a left tail and I read ይ here. The final ዕ is round. Whether ል is ል or ሉ is not certain.

## CONFIDENCE
- Opening (lines 1-8): 55%. The proper names and ውስተ፡ምድር / ለክርስቶስ / ለእግዚእነ፡ኢየሱስ፡ክርስቶስ / ለመሃይምናን are secure; line 3 is the weakest line on the folio (one unread glyph, three unresolved words), and line 1's final glyph and line 2's first glyph are hand-shape guesses.
- Middle (lines 9-16): 60%. ውስተ፡ከርሰ፡ለድንግል፡ማርያም፡እሙ፡ለብርሃን is secure and the red rubric is clear. እግዘ (twice), እሎሁ, እምዕማያት, ለማያውያን and the vowel order of ሙደይ are all doubtful.
- End (lines 17-24): 60%. ዕፍረት, ዘተክዕወ, ያምዕዝ, ውእቱ, ወእመኒ are secure. ለምክ, ውለተ, ተባውቀኪ and the identity of the verb ተሠውጠ are not.
- Would not put in print: "ቀዲስን፡ወኒራን፡እለመ" (line 3-4); "እግዘ" as either ግ or ን without a second witness; "እሎሁ፡እምዕማያት"; "ውለተ፡ሙደይ"; "ለምክ"; "ተባውቀኪ"; any English rendering of ተሠውጠ — every "transferred" above is a placeholder, not a translation.
