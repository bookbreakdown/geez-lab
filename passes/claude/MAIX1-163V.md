## LITERAL
[ṭawta] and [sätäk] [mäsleza] wild-beasts, and [mäbsiʿa] [kǝlǝs], and [zäti] blood: let him stand [away] from the house of Christians until he completes his penance. And after he has completed his penance, according as the elders of the house of Christians say to him, let him [yǝbäs — enter?] and let him be admitted-to-the-congregation and let him draw-near. Unclean and [sǝʿub] is he who [yǝṭäfǝr] by [yaf] and by [ǝsǝtä] and by [kǝkäb — star?], and for all [sǝrigälat] and [ṭaryan]: let him stand [away] from the house of Christians until he completes his penance. And when he has repented and has done according as the elders of the house of Christians commanded him, let him enter and let him be admitted-to-the-congregation and let him draw-near. Unclean and [sǝʿub] is he who makes [šǝrbatatä] and [qunozʿatä]; and the woman also, who has in her [sǝbsǝb] pure, which God created for the sake of seed, for-those-who [dä] she bore by the law of the canon: and let her stand [away] from the house of Christians. [1]

## UNCERTAIN
- l.1 ጣውታ ፡ ወስተክ ፡ መስሌዛ — read letter by letter; none is a word I can identify. The ጣ could be ጠ; the ሌ after መስ could be ሊ or ል; the ዛ could be ዘ with a stray stroke. Possibly animal names continuing from the previous folio (l.2 opens ርዊ, giving አርዊ "beasts").
- l.2 ወመብሲዓ — fourth glyph read as ሲ; could be ል followed by ዓ (ወመብልዓ). ክልስ unidentified.
- l.2-3 ወዘ|ቲ — read as ቲ; could be ተ. ወዘቲ ደመ is not a form I can parse.
- l.3, l.12, l.24 እምቢተ/እምቤተ — the scribe's ቤ and ቢ are near-identical; transcribed as ቤተ (standard ቤተ ክርስቲያን) but the glyph could equally be read ቢ. Same glyph at l.7 ቤተ and l.16 ቤተ.
- l.4, l.6, l.13 ፈ — the fä glyph carries a ring on the right leg and could be read ፌ; transcribed as ፈ (ይፈጽም, ፈጸመ) on the grounds that this hand writes ፈ that way, not by checking any other folio.
- l.6-7 ይቤ|ልዎ — ቤ/ቢ ambiguity as above; read as ይቤልዎ "they say to him".
- l.7, l.15 ሊቀናተ — first glyph could be ሌ; transcribed ሊ. Standard form would be ሊቃናት/ሊቃውንተ; manuscript has ቀ (not ቃ) and final ተ.
- l.8 ይበስ — read as ይ በ ስ; the parallel at l.16 reads ይባእ "let him enter", so this may be a scribal variant or my misreading of ባእ as በስ. Transcribed as seen.
- l.8, l.17 ወይተከንስ — read with ተ (bottom bar visible); standard would be ወይትከነስ. Translated "be admitted to the congregation" on the assumption it is derived from ከነሰ/ቤተ ክርስቲያን; the meaning is not certain.
- l.9, l.18 ስዑብ — legible but unidentified; left untranslated.
- l.10 ዘይጠፍር ፡ በያፍ ፡ ወበእስተ — all legible, none identified. ጠ could be ጣ. Context (list of things one does "by") suggests divination practices.
- l.11 ወበክከብ — read as ክከብ; probably ኮከብ "star" with the first ኮ written as ክ. Transcribed as seen.
- l.11 ስሪገላት — could be ሰሪገላት or ስረገላት; unidentified.
- l.12 ወጣርያን — unidentified; ጣ could be ጠ.
- l.19 ሥርባታተ — first glyph read as ሥ; could be ም or ሠ. ወቁኖዝዓተ — ቁ could be ቆ; unidentified.
- l.20 ወእሲተኒ — read as written; standard "and the woman also" would be ወብእሲተኒ. Either the ብ is omitted by the scribe or the second glyph is a compressed ብእ.
- l.20-21 ስብ|ስብ — line-break word ስብስብ unidentified; could be a dittography of ስብ.
- l.21 ንጼሕ — glyph read as ጼ; standard ንጹሕ "pure" would have ጹ.
- l.22-23 ለእለ|ደ ፡ ወለደ — the ደ at the start of l.23 does not parse with ለእለ; possibly a false start for ወለደ. Transcribed as seen.
- l.24 final ፩ — the last glyph looks like the numeral one, but could be a ፡ with a stroke or a section mark.

## CONFIDENCE
- Opening third (ll.1-8): 55%. The list in ll.1-3 is transcribed glyph by glyph but not understood; ይበስ (l.8) would not go to print.
- Middle third (ll.9-16): 70%. The formula (unclean ... let him stand from the church until he completes his penance ... let him enter, be admitted, and communicate) is secure; the list ዘይጠፍር ፡ በያፍ ፡ ወበእስተ ፡ ወበክከብ ፡ ወለኵሉ ፡ ስሪገላት ፡ ወጣርያን is legible but untranslated and would not go to print.
- End third (ll.17-24): 60%. ሥርባታተ ፡ ወቁኖዝዓተ, ስብስብ, ንጼሕ and ለእለደ ፡ ወለደ would not go to print; the closing canon-law formula is secure.
