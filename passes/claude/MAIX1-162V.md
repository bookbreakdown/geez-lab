## UNCERTAIN
L1 በዛመዋ — second glyph read ዛ (stem with top-right bar; alt ዘ), fourth glyph ዋ (ወ with bottom-right tick). No word recognised; may continue a word from the recto or relate to the root ዘመወ. Left untranslated.
L1, L3, L10, L11, L13, L18, L20 ቢተ — the manuscript has ብ with a short leg-stroke (ቢ) every time, where standard orthography is ቤተ. The scribe's ቤ in L6 ሶቤሁ has a clear ring, so the distinction is real in this hand. Kept as ቢተ, not normalised.
L4 ነሥ — final glyph smudged; read ሥ (wide body, hooked top-left). Alt ስ; the same word in L19 is written ነስሐ.
L5 ይትከነሰ — final glyph is plain ሰ with no sixth-order mark; L14 has ይትከነስ. Kept as written.
L7 ወነገሩ — final glyph read ሩ (ረ with bottom-right mark); alt ሪ. Could be "they told" or "its word/matter"; LITERAL gives the verb, the alternative is noted here.
L7 final ፈ — an isolated glyph after the ፨, followed on L8 by ውስተ. Read ፈ (alt ፍ). It forms no word I can read; not translated, not bridged.
L8 ወይተአመን — second glyph is ተ (foot, no top stroke), not ት; standard would be ይትአመን. Kept as written. ተአመነ may mean "is trusted / is believed" or "confesses"; both are given in LITERAL.
አ / እ throughout — the hand draws both with an open top-left hook and I cannot separate them reliably at this resolution. Instances in L2, L8, L9, L11, L12–13, L19, L22, L23 are read by the standard form of the word. L9 አው ("or") could be እው.
L13 ቢተ followed by L14 ይትከነስ — ክርስቲያን is not on the page; nothing has been supplied.
L14 ። — written by the scribe as two ፡ side by side (four dots); transcribed as ።.
L14 ስዒብ — first glyph read ስ (top-right hook, alt ሰ); second glyph ዐ with a bottom-right stroke, read ዒ (alt ዑ, though the scribe's u-rings on ቱ and ሁ are blobs, not strokes). No word recognised; not translated.
L15 ዘየሐዙ — fourth glyph is H-shaped with a bottom-right ring, i.e. ዙ; the ሐ is a clear ሐ, not ኀ. Word unresolved. The surrounding sense (enemy of his own life, killer of himself, "in his flesh") suggests a self-mutilation verb, but the manuscript form does not match any I know, so it is not emended or glossed in PROSE.
L16 ፈጠፈ — both ፈ carry the scribe's usual top-right hook (compare በፈቃዱ in the same line and ወፈጺሞ in L24), so read as ፈ not ፍ. Final glyph has a smudge at lower right; alt ፍ or ፌ. No word recognised; not translated.
L17 ሥጋሁ — read ሥ (top-right hook); alt ሠ.
L21 ጸላዒ — final glyph ዐ with a bottom-right stroke, read ዒ rather than ዔ.
