## PROSE
[...let him] come in, be received into the congregation, and be admitted to the offering.

Unclean and [səbub] is the man who keeps watch on a virgin and [?gər] her [baʿəno] like an unclean man. If his [salihu] is unclean, his virginity, he is to stand outside the church until he repents. He is to do whatever the elders of the church tell him. When he has completed his penance, he may come in, be received into the congregation, and be admitted to the offering.

[Səʿub] and unclean is the man who makes himself [bäśärru] a male so that they may lead another man's wife astray, or who otherwise makes himself the means by which they fornicate with women who have no husband, whose husband has died, or with a widow. He is to stand outside the church until he repents. When he has completed what the elders of the church told him, he is to do it, and he may come in, be received into the congregation, and be admitted to the offering.

[Səkub] and unclean is everyone who eats [...]

## UNCERTAIN
- L1, L12, L23 ወይቀረብ / ይቀረብ: read as ረ (ra) in all three places; standard form of "let him draw near / be brought near" would be ይቀርብ (ር). The hand consistently shows the ረ hook without the ር top stroke; kept as written.
- L1, L11, L23 ወይትከነስ: fourth letter read as ነ; could be ኑ. Taken as ተከነሰ "be gathered into the congregation".
- L2 ወስቡብ: read ስ-ቡ-ብ. Final letter is ብ (dash on left), not ሕ as it first appears at page scale. Not a word I can identify. Note that L12 ስዑብ and L23 ስኩብ stand in the same formulaic slot ("X and unclean is he who ..."); the three are probably one word written three different ways, but I cannot say which. Not translated; transliterated in brackets.
- L3 ያስተሐይጽ: final letter read ጽ; could be ፅ. Taken as causative-reflexive of ሐየጸ "look at, watch".
- L4 first letter: written `?`. The glyph is a heavily inked oval with a stem and top flag; it is either ቁ or ዑ, and I cannot decide between them. Neither ቁግር nor ዑግር is a word I recognise. The word is transliterated as [?gər].
- L4 ባዕኖ: third letter read as ኖ (ነ with ring); could be ዶ. Not a word I can identify; left in brackets.
- L4 interlinear: a small superscript character (looks like ፑ or ዩ) is written above the line between ከመ፡ and ርኩስ, in a smaller hand. Ignored per rule 6; flagged here.
- L5 ሰሊሁ: read ሰ-ሊ-ሁ clearly, but I cannot identify the word. Transliterated in brackets.
- L7 ወይኒስሕ (L8 carries the ሕ): standard would be ይኔስሕ (ኔ) and without the initial ወ after እስከ. Kept as written; the same verb at L19 is ይኒስሕ without ወ.
- L8 ሕ፡፡ and L12 ይቀረብ፡፡: two word-dividers in sequence, apparently serving as a section stop (፡፡ rather than ።). Kept as written.
- L8 እዘዝዎ: initial እ where standard is አ (አዘዝዎ). Kept as written.
- L9 ኀቤተ: read as one word ኀ-ቤ-ተ; looks like a haplography for ኀበ ቤተ ("to the house of"). Kept as written; the LITERAL line reflects the unclear syntax.
- L10 ወእምድኅረ: fifth letter read ኅ; glyph is cramped, could be ኀ.
- L13 በሠርሩ: fourth letter read ሩ (hook top-right, dash bottom-right). Alternatives ቱ or ቍ. The word is unidentified in any reading; transliterated in brackets. Second letter is clearly ሠ.
- L13 ዘይትረሰይ and L15-16 ዘይተረሰይ: the two occurrences differ (ት vs ተ at the syllable break). Both kept as written. Fifth letter read ሰ; could be ስ.
- L17 አልቦን: third letter read ቦ on the sense ("they have no husband"); the glyph is close to ሮ. Flagged as ቦ/ሮ ambiguous.
- L17 ዘሟተ: second letter read ሟ (loop plus tail); standard ዘሞተ "who has died" would be ሞ. Kept as written.
- L18 ወመዓስበተ: second letter read ዓ; taken as መዓስብ "widow" in accusative. Form is unusual.
- L18 አምቤተ: initial አ where L6 has እምቤተ. Kept as written. An ink blot follows ተ at the line end; nothing appears to be written under it.
- L24 ዘይበልዕ: read five letters ዘ-ይ-በ-ል-ዕ ("who eats"); at page scale it looks like six with a ደ, but zoomed there is no extra letter.

## CONFIDENCE
- Opening (L1-8): 70%. Letters are legible but three words (ስቡብ, ?ግር ባዕኖ, ሰሊሁ) are unidentified, and the L4 initial is unresolved.
- Middle (L9-16): 75%. ኀቤተ and በሠርሩ are the weak points; the rest is a clear formulaic passage.
- End (L17-24): 80%. Structure is clear and repeats the formula; ዘሟተ, አልቦን and ስኩብ need a second reader.
- Not for print without a second witness: "ወይ?ግር፡ ባዕኖ" (L3-4), "ሰሊሁ" (L5), "በሠርሩ" (L13), and the three-way variant ስቡብ / ስዑብ / ስኩብ (L2, L12, L23). The bracketed transliterations in LITERAL and PROSE mark exactly where the translation stops.
