## UNCERTAIN
- L1 ጸድቅ (last glyph): written with a flat top bar and a curled, 3-shaped stem rather than the usual stem-and-foot of ቅ; read as ቅ from the context ጸድቅ/ስ (= ጻድቅሰ), but the form could equally be taken as ቕ or ቓ. Also note ጸ where standard orthography has ጻ (ጻድቅ); manuscript form kept. Same ጸ/ጻ in L15 ጸድቃን.
- L2 ስ (first glyph): read as the enclitic -ሰ written with the sixth-order form ስ; the glyph is ስ, not ሰ. Kept as written.
- L4 ጸዊ: the glyph after ጸ has a rounded body with a central stroke and a right-hand descending leg with a foot; read as ዊ, but ዕ (which would give ጸዕ, cf. ጻዕር "pain") or ዒ are possible. No word ጸዊ identified; a ፡ follows immediately, so the word is complete as the scribe left it. Left untranslated.
- L6–7 ተድ/ለ: L6 ends ተድ, L7 begins ለ (right-leg foot only slightly longer than the left, so not clearly ላ). Expected ተድላ "delight"; translated as that word but the manuscript shows ለ. Stray ink dot before the ለ, not a letter.
- L7 ወፍሥሐ followed by L8 ሥሙ: the ሥ at the start of L8 is drawn like the ሥ of ፍሥ (three prongs, right leg descending). ሥሙ is not an identifiable word; possibly a dittography of ሥ before an intended ሆሙ (giving ወፍሥሓሆሙ "and their gladness"), but that is a guess and not transcribed. Left untranslated.
- L8 ለዝሉፍ: the ዝ is an interlinear insertion in a smaller form of the same hand above the gap between ለ and ሉ; transcribed in line as ለዝሉፍ. Base text without the insertion reads ለሉፍ.
- L8 ጹ (last glyph, partly against the margin): read as ጸ with a right leg and hook, i.e. ጹ; could be ጸ. It stands alone between ፡ and the line end, and L9 begins a new word (እስከ). Not identified; left untranslated.
- L10 ነፍስቲሆሙ: third glyph read as ስ; standard form would be ነፍሳቲሆሙ (ሳ). Manuscript form kept. Note also singular verb ኢይመውት with this plural subject, as written.
- L13 ርግምት: read as ርግምት; the ግ could be ጉ, giving ርጉምት. No punctuation visible after ዕለት at the line end.
- L16 ኃጥእን / L16 end ኃጥአ / L19 ኃጥእን: the አ/እ distinction in this hand rests on a small hook at the top-left of the glyph. L16 first occurrence and L19 show the hook (read እ); L16 line-end glyph is flatter (read አ). Standard orthography ኃጥአን throughout; readings given as seen.
- L17 በእሳት: read ት, not ተ; a construct ሳተ would be expected before ኀጢአቶሙ. Kept as written.
- L18 ወመላእክት፡ ሰማይ: read ክት with ፡ following; a construct ክተ would be expected before ሰማይ. Kept as written.
- L21 ኵሉ: first glyph read as the labialized ኵ; could be ኩ.
- L22 ሠናያ: final glyph has a stem curving to the right at the foot, read ያ; standard ሠናየ. Kept as written.
- L22 ማ (last glyph): line ends mid-word; the following folio was not consulted.

## CONFIDENCE
- Opening (L1–7): 80%. The frame is clear (righteous at his right hand, eternal life, no death, garden of delight, rejoicing, gladness), but ጸዊ in L4 and the L1 ቅ form are not settled, and ተድ/ለ crosses a line break with a non-standard final.
- Middle (L8–14): 75%. L8 is the weak line: ሥሙ and the isolated ጹ are unidentified, and ለዝሉፍ carries an interlinear correction. L9–14 are clean.
- End (L15–22): 90%. Formulaic and legible; only the አ/እ alternation in ኃጥ(አ/እ)ን and the construct-state endings (ሳት, ክት) are in question.
- Would not put in print: "ጸዊ" (L4), "ሥሙ" (L8), "ጹ" (L8), and the L6–7 reading "ተድለ" for ተድላ, each until a second reading agrees.
