## CONFIDENCE
Opening (L1–8): 55%. The Joel/Acts formula in L4–7 (one God, one name, whoever calls on the name of God will be saved) is secure; L1–3 and the two verbs in L7–8 are not.
Middle (L9–16): 65%. The four-points-of-the-compass sentence and "went out to the desert, renounced the world" are secure; መዓዘሁ (three times), ንሕነሰኬ, ብነ, ቅዲስን and እጥብዑ are not.
End (L17–24): 50%. "gave their souls to death", "children", "desires and pleasure of this world", "heads were cut off", "right and left" are secure; ቁስተ, ተዴኅሙ, ዘመ?, ወመኒዮሙ, ወተመልሔ, ተሩእ are not.
Would not put in print: ደውሳዝ, ይኅሪተ, ወእተ፡አጣሪ, every rendering of መዓዘሁ as "time", ተምዕዙ, ወእምዓዙ, ነዓረብ, ንሕነሰኬ, ብነ, ቁስተ, ተዴኅሙ፡ዘመ?, ወመኒዮሙ, ወተመልሔ, ተሩእ.
