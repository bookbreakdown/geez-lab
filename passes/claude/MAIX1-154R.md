## UNCERTAIN
- L1 ስብአ — first glyph read as ስ; could be ሰ (giving standard ሰብአ) or possibly ሕ. The word is the tail of a sentence begun on the previous folio, so its sense is not recoverable from this page alone.
- L1 ኢየኀድጋ — third glyph read as ኀ; ኃ not excluded.
- L3–4 በእ|ጣኑ — first glyph of L4 read as ጣ; ማ is possible. Neither reading gives me a word I can translate confidently after ተጠመቅነ ("we were baptised in his ..."). Left untranslated rather than guessed.
- L4 ኑ፡፡ — two word-dividers in sequence read as a full stop (። written as ፡፡). Same treatment at L5 (red, after ቅዱስ), L6 (red, after ክርስቲያን), L10 (after ዓለም) and L18 (after ቀሪበት).
- L6 ሰማዕ፡እወልዶ፡የ፡ — transcribed as seen. The dividers fall oddly: ሰማዕ is likely ስማዕ ("hear"), the glyph after the divider looks like እ but ኦ (vocative) would fit, and ወልዶ፡የ looks like ወልድየ ("my son") split by a stray divider. Translated as "Hear, O my son" but the manuscript form is what is transcribed. ዶ vs ድ also uncertain.
- L7 ዘነገሬኔ — fourth and fifth glyphs read as ሬ and ኔ (with rings); the ring-vs-stroke distinction between ረ/ሬ and ኒ/ኔ is hard at this resolution. Standard form would be ዘነገረኒ ("which he told me"); translated so.
- L7 ቀሌምንጦስ — rubric in red; read as ቀ ሌ ም ን ጦ ስ.
- L11 የዓልዉ — first glyph read as የ (standard would be ይዓልዉ); ብዙኃን — ኃ vs ኀ.
- L12 ወይሴኵሱ — read as ሴ then ኵ (ኰ possible) then ሱ. I cannot identify the verb; left unresolved in LITERAL and PROSE.
- L12–13 እምአ|ኃው — read as ኃ; ኀ possible (እምአኀው, "from brothers"). Translated as "brothers" on that basis.
- L14 ሐሰውያን then ወሐሣውያነ — the manuscript spells the two occurrences with ሰ and ሣ respectively; transcribed as seen, not harmonised.
- L15 ስብአ — as L1, ስ vs ሰ.
- L16 ወበሃይማ — the ወ carries a small mark or stroke above it, possibly a correction or insertion; nothing legible resolved from it.
- L17–18 ቀሪ|በት — read as ቀሪበት (ሪ with stroke; final ት, ቱ possible). Standard would be ቀርበት ("it has drawn near"); translated so. Subject of the verb is not on the page.
- L19 ጽኡ — first glyph read as ጽ (completing ያመጽኡ).
- L22 ኵሳት — first glyph read as ኵ; ኩ (matching ርኩስት at L17) is possible.
- L23 ዴወኑ — read as ዴ ወ ኑ (ዲ possible). Verb not identified; left unresolved.
- L23 በነገሠት — fourth glyph read as ሠ; ሥ (ነገሥት, "kings") is possible and the translation assumes it.
- L24 ወፈድ — word cut off at the folio end; not completed.

## CONFIDENCE
- Opening (L1–8): 70%. L1 first word, L3–4 በእጣኑ and the L6 ሰማዕ፡እወልዶ፡የ cluster are the weak points; I would not put "ስብአ፡ኢየኀድጋ፡መዓዛሁ" or "በእጣኑ" in print as translated.
- Middle (L9–16): 80%. Clear text except ወይሴኵሱ (L12), which I would not print with any translation, and the ኃ/ኀ choices.
- End (L17–24): 75%. ቀሪበት (L17–18) and ዴወኑ (L23) are the phrases I would not put in print; the rest reads consistently.
