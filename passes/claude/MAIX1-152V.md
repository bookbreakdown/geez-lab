## UNCERTAIN

- l.2 ወሬደ — second glyph has a loop at the foot of the leg, read as ሬ; standard is ወረደ; may be a ረ with a curled tail.
- l.2 ኀበ — first glyph read as ኀ; ኀ/ኃ hard to separate in this hand.
- l.3 ወግብረ — second glyph has a foot, read ግ; expected form is ገብረ (ገ).
- l.3 ባሕረ — read ባ ሕ ረ; middle glyph could be ሔ; the word does not make obvious sense here ("sea"?) and I cannot confirm it.
- l.3 ግሩመ — middle glyph read ሩ (could be ሪ); final glyph could be መ or ም.
- l.4 እምኔሃ — third glyph read ኔ; could be ኒ.
- l.5 ወለዲተ — second glyph is plainly ለ, where standard orthography has ላ (ወላዲተ). Kept as written.

## CONFIDENCE
- Opening (ll.1–8): 80%. The Incarnation and Bethlehem passage reads clearly except l.3 (ባሕረ፡ግሩመ) and l.7 (ወተስምዓት), which I would not put in print without a second reading.
- Middle (ll.9–16): 70%. Place names (Qusqam, Alexandria, Misr, Egypt) are secure; the verbs ወአግዓዘ (l.11), ገብሩ (l.14) and the phrase ጽሑር፡በነቢይ፡እስተርአየ፡ወባዕደኒሂ (ll.14–15) are not.
- End (ll.17–24): 60%. The Hosea quotation (ll.16–17), Nazareth, Galilee, Tiberias, Jericho and the Zechariah attribution are secure; ወአምዓዘ (l.19), ወአባዬ፡ባሕር፡እግዘ (l.21) and ማዬ፡ዓ (l.24) I cannot translate with confidence and would not print.
