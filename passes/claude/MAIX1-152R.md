## UNCERTAIN
- Line 2 `፨ ፨ ፨`: three ornamental dot-clusters (black and red) filling the end of the line; not text, rendered as ፨ by convention.
- Line 4 `፩` (first character, red, with bar above and below): read as the numeral 1 (፩አምላክ, "one God"); the bars make it a numeral, but the sign is small and could be checked.
- Line 4 `ንጽሕፍ`: second character read as ጽ; the sixth-order marker is faint.
- Line 6 `ቀሌምንጠስ`: the seventh character reads ጠ with no clear seventh-order stroke; standard spelling of the name is ቀሌምንጦስ (ጦ). Kept as ጠ; may be ጦ with the stroke lost in the ink.
- Line 6 `ተልሚዱ`: second character read as ል; the sixth-order stroke on ለ is small and it could be plain ለ (ተለሚዱ).
- Line 7 `ሬድእ`: first character read as ሬ (hook at foot); standard spelling is ረድእ, and ራ is also possible in this hand.
- Line 7 end + line 8 start `ዘበቀሮ | ቢጣሁ`: every character is legible (ዘ በ ቀ ሮ / ቢ ጣ ሁ) but the resulting word or phrase makes no sense to me and I could not resolve it. ቀ could be ቁ; ጣ could be ጻ (ቢጻሁ?). Transcribed as seen; not translated.
- Line 8 `ወአለበው`: final character read as ው; ዎ (ወአለበዎ, "he taught him") is very possible and better fits the sense. Transcribed as seen.
- Line 8 `ኵሎ`: first character read as ኵ (small bulge on the leg of ከ); could be plain ከ (ከሎ) in this red ink.
- Line 11 `ታተ`: second character read as ተ; ት (ምሕረታት, "mercies") is possible — the seventh-order stroke is not clear.
- Line 12 `ለመድኃኒነ`: the character read as ድ resembles ዩ in this hand (ደ and የ with the loop look alike); read as ድ on the shape of the arch, but flagged.
- Line 12 end + line 13 start `ለ | ዕለ`: characters legible (ለ / ዕ ለ) but the word ለዕለ is not resolved; ላዕለ ("upon") would need ላ, and the final glyph of line 12 looks like plain ለ. Transcribed as seen.
- Line 13 `አቡሁ`: second character read as ቡ; ሁ is close in this hand (compare the clearer ቡ in line 16).
- Line 14 `ወመዓዘ` and line 17 `ወመዓዘሁ`, `አምዓዘ`: the fourth-order vowel character read as ዓ throughout; ዋ is a possible alternative reading of the same shape. Standard forms would be መዓዛ / አምዐዘ; transcribed as seen (ዘ, not ዛ).
- Line 15 `ፍቅሬ`: third character read as ሬ (same hook form as ሬ in lines 14 and 16); standard construct would be ፍቅረ.
- Line 16 end + line 17 start `ስ | ምሐተ`: first character of line 17 read as ም (two loops); ብ (giving ስብሐተ, "praise/glory") would fit the sense but the shape looks like ም. Transcribed as seen.
- Line 17 `አምዓዘ`: first character read as አ, could be እ.
