## WORK

NOT the Rest of the Words of Baruch (4 Baruch). Nothing on this side belongs
to the Jeremiah/Baruch/Abimelech narrative. It is a "Two Ways" homily in a
first-person apostolic frame:

- l.1 ይቤለኒ "he said to me"; l.11-12 እዘዘኒ፡...እግዚእኒ "my Lord commanded me";
  ንምሐሮሙ፡ለአሕዛብ "let us teach the nations" - a narrating apostle reporting
  the Lord's command.
- ll.1-10: the way of the nations is broad (ስፍሕት፡ፍኖት) and leads to
  perdition (ውስተ፡ኀጕል), itemised as greed, fornication, adultery,
  drunkenness, false oaths, song and theatre (ዘፈን፡ወተውኔት).
- ll.12-24: the narrow gate (ጸባብ፡አንቀጽ) that leads to life, itemised as
  humility, meekness, mercy, love, peace, fasting, prayer, pity for the
  poor, almsgiving, loving one's enemy, not repaying evil; these bring one
  into eternal life where there is no sickness or pain.
- Matt 7:13-14 is the base text (broad way / narrow gate), expanded into
  the Didache/Doctrina "Two Ways" vice-and-virtue lists.

The first-person Peter-to-Clement frame and this exact material are
characteristic of the Ethiopic Qalementos (Peter's discourse to Clement),
which is what the catalogue label says. So either 4 Baruch ended on f.166 and
the catalogue's Qalementos label is coincidentally right for this leaf, or
the leaf order is disturbed. The Ethiopic Sinodos "Two Ways" chapter is the
other candidate for the same material, but the narrating "me" whom the Lord
commanded favours Qalementos. I cannot name book and chapter from the ink
alone.

## GIST

"...he said to me, for the way of the nations is broad, the way that leads
into perdition: greed and fornication, and going to another man's wife,
drunkenness, and swearing falsely, song and theatre - this and whatever
resembles it is the way of the nations that leads into perdition. This [he
wrote/said], saying: my Lord commanded me: let us teach the nations to enter
by the narrow gate that leads into life - humility and meekness, mercy,
love and peace, fasting and prayer and pitying the poor and giving alms, and
that a man love his enemy and not repay evil. This and whatever resembles it
brings one into the narrow gate that brings one into eternal life, where
there is no sickness or pain."

## NOTES

Least certain readings, in order:

1. l.6 opening ትዲ. Two glyphs before the ፡. First: ተ-family cross with a
   lower-left foot (ት) and a dot above it. Second: ደ/ይ body with a short
   right-side mark (ዲ or ዪ). Neither ትዲ nor ታዪ nor እትዲ (joining l.5's
   final እ) is a word I can place; the phrase is ሐዊረ፡ብእሲት፡ብእሲ፡እ|ትዲ
   "going to another man's wife ...". Transcribed letter by letter, NOT
   resolved. Collect for the expert.
2. l.9 ዘወስ?ዶ. The hole sits between ስ and ዶ and has room for one letter.
   Sense wants "which leads (them) into perdition" (ዘይወስድ / ዘወሰደ). The
   ዶ has a clear top-right ring. Do not smooth.
3. l.10 ዘንተከ??. ከ is clear, then the hole eats one or two letters, then a
   fragment before ፡. ዘንተ፡ከ[ተበ]/ከ[መዝ] are guesses only.
4. l.11 the deleted word: five glyphs ወ ይ/ደ ቤ ለ ኒ under three strokes =
   ወይቤለኒ "and he said to me", cancelled by the scribe (it repeats l.1's
   verb after እዘዘኒ).
5. l.11-12 እግዚእ|ኒ. Expected እግዚእየ "my Lord"; the ink at l.12 head is the
   same ኒ shape as in እዘዘኒ (right dash, not the ኑ up-curl). Transcribed as
   ink. Possibly the scribe's ኒ for -የ, or a ነ ("our Lord") drawn with the
   dash; flag.
6. l.15 ትሕትና. At 2x I read ተሐቱኖ; at 2.5x the first ተ has the foot (ት),
   the ሐ has the tick (ሕ), and the last glyph reads ና rather than ኖ.
   Standard word, but recorded because the 2x reading was different.
7. l.17 ወምሕሬ and l.6 ወስክሬት: both ረ carry a small black hook at top
   right. Standard is ምሒረ/ምሕረ (infinitive, parallel to ውሂበ l.18) and
   ስክር. Not a red dot this time (black ink), so transcribed as ሬ; could be
   this hand's ሪ tick. Editor to decide.
8. l.20 ዘንተኪ. The final glyph is ክ-family with a mid-right dash (ኪ), not
   ሂ. Parallel at l.7-8 is ዘን|ት with no suffix. Unplaced enclitic; flag.
9. l.24 ትኀበ. Two cross-shaped glyphs before በ: the first has ት's foot and
   tick, the second has the top-right hook this hand uses for ኀ (compare
   l.4 and l.9 ኀ). Sense is ኀበ፡አልቦ "where there is no ...". The stray ት
   has no deletion strokes. Transcribed as ink.
10. l.7-8 ዘን|ት for ዘንተ: the l.8 initial is ት with foot, not ተ.

New letterform facts for the key:
- Interlinear insertion confirmed on l.3: a single omitted letter (ኖ) is
  written small above the gap, no caret.
- Three-stroke deletion confirmed on l.11 over a whole five-letter word;
  the strokes run through the letters' middles and the text stays legible.
- The section mark on l.10 is a black ፡ with four red dots in a cross
  around it, not two ፡ pairs.
- ኔ (l.7 ተውኔት) carries a top-right ring; ኒ carries a mid-right dash; ና
  (l.15) a right up-hook - three distinguishable ነ-family marks.
- ጾ, ሎ, ዶ, ሮ, ቦ rings are all genuine on this page (ll.10, 17, 9, 12,
  24); the "ring" on ረ at ll.6 and 17 is a smaller hook and is the doubtful
  one.
- ሣ (l.15) is ሠ with a right descender, separable from the plain ሠ pair on
  l.4.
- ዓ in ዘለዓለም (l.23) is the 9-bowl with descender; transcribed ዓ on sense,
  ዐ/ዓ remain undistinguished.
