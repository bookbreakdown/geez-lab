## WORK

**Cannot be fixed to a named work from one side. What the ink does fix:**

1. **It continues the yearly-attendance canon of f.184v, not the 181v–183v
   homily.** 184v ll.18-23 (drafted): the lower grades "obey in everything and
   go to them yearly (ወይሔሩ፡ኀቤሆሙ፡በበዓመት), taking the books of the church,
   until the archbishop examines …". This side has the same matter in the same
   register: ወለለዓመት (l.3, "every year"), ይበጽሐ፡ኀቤሁ፡ርሑቀ፡እስከ፡አጽናፍ
   (ll.3-4, "reaches him from far, to the ends"), በዘዓመት፡እስከ፡ተራከቡ፡በ-
   (ll.5-6, "in that year, until they meet at …"), then the dismissal: "as he
   commanded them let them do, and let them go home into their houses and
   their lands in peace, rejoicing and glad and blessing God" (ll.19-24). That
   is the close of a rule about travelling to the superior and returning.
2. **Voice: third-person jussive with a second-person-plural insert.** Finite
   verbs are jussive/3pl: ይግበሩ, ይእትዉ, ይትፌሣሕ, ይትሐሠዩ, ይባርክዎ, and the
   modal ይከልኡ (ll.15-16, "while they withhold"). But ll.7-9 carry three 2pl
   suffixes: ለነፍስከሙ, ለእበዊከሙ, -ከሙ on l.9 — "for your souls … your fathers
   the archbishops … your alms". So this side is not purely the canon voice of
   184v (which had no second person at all) and not the homily voice of 183v
   (no first-person, no vocative here). Either the compiler's hand shows, or
   the canon itself turns to address its audience for three lines. Recorded,
   not decided.
3. **Agenda list.** ll.12-15 ወበእንተ፡ሕንፃ፡ዘቅድስት፡ቤተ፡ክርስቲያን፡ወበእንተ፡ዓሥራት፡ለቀዳስት፡
   ቤተ፡ክርስቲያን — "and concerning the building of the holy church, and concerning
   tithes for the sanctuary of the church" — is a list of the matters the
   superior hears yearly: complaints of withheld dues (ll.15-16 ይከልኡ …
   ወመዓር "withhold … and honey"), of women (l.17), and "all the evil they saw
   and heard" which "they cry out to the archbishop" (ll.17-19). Same shape as
   184v's "until the archbishop examines".
4. **Charity list shared with the almsgiving paraenesis of ff.174r–175v:**
   ለነዳያን፡ወለሙቁሐን፡ወፈላስያን፡ለዕቢራት፡ወለእጓለ፡ማውታ (ll.9-12). 175V l.4-5 has
   ለዕቢራት / ወለእጓለ፡ማውታ in the same order, and ዕቢራት occurs at 174R l.19.
   That is one scribe's fixed list, and it argues that the 174-175 material and
   this canon come through the same translator/compiler, whatever their sources.
5. **Sees / grades named:** only ሊቃነ፡ጳጳሳት (l.8, plural) and ሊቀ፡ጳጳሳት
   (l.19, singular). No other grade, no see. Not used as a stratum marker.

**Citations: NONE, marked or unmarked.** ወበከመ (l.19) is "and as [he
commanded them]", a conjunction inside the rule, not a citation formula; no
ይቤ on the side; no scriptural phrase identified. The absence matches 184v
(zero citations) and is against the homily folios (five woven phrases in 24
lines on 183v).

**Arabic loans: none claimed.** Nothing on the side requires a root outside
Ge'ez; the unplaced strings (እንግልጋ, ኢታስተቱ, በቍዒት, ኀሠሣ, ይዲዙዙ) are not
offered as loans because no root can be shown for any of them.

## GIST

The subordinate clergy are to come to their archbishop every year, however far,
and meet him at a set place; there he hears the matters of church-building,
tithes withheld from the sanctuary, offerings (honey and something else)
withheld, women, and every evil they have seen and heard, and your alms are to
go to the poor, prisoners, exiles, ዕቢራት and orphans. Then, as he commanded
them, they do it, and go home to their houses and lands in peace, rejoicing,
glad and blessing God.
