## GIST
[...un]til they repent. And when he does penance as the elders of the church told him, let him do it; and when he has completed his penance, let him enter and be re-admitted (ይትከነስ) and draw near. The ርኩብ and the ሰዑብ, and he who does the work of the pagans and joins with them in the keeping of feasts, let him stand away from the church, and until he completes all his penance, let him do — let him do — what the elders of the church commanded him; and after he has completed his penance let him enter and be re-admitted and draw near. All this he has abandoned, his former doing, and he shall not return to it, until great penance: wisdom, the fear of God and good counsel, and all that they do; and his glory also abides forever. Keep this canon and give it to the ሰዑማን of the church, that they may keep it.

Uncertain readings, in order of doubt:
- l.23 ለሰዑማኒ: ink reads ለ-ሰ-ዑ-ማ-ኒ (final glyph could be ን). Unplaced. Looks like the same root as the key's ሰዑብ (l.6 here, always paired with ርኩስ). Plausible sense from context "the ? of the church, that they may keep it" = the church's officers/guardians. Collect for the expert; do not smooth. Second choice for glyph 3: ኡ.
- l.6 ርኩብ: the last glyph is this hand's box ብ, not the ticked ስ. The key records ርኩስ every other time ሰዑብ occurs. Ink says ርኩብ; l.15 ወይቀርብ on the same page shows the identical ብ. The ኩ has a top-right hook and may be ኵ (compare ኵሎ ll.11, 16).

- l.12 ይግበር፡እዘዝዎ: no ዘ before እዘዝዎ; either omitted or ወይ/ግበር፡ይግበር is dittography. Alef is the hooked form (standard አዘዝዎ).
