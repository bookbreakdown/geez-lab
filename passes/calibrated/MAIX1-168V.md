## WORK
Unidentified. It is NOT Qalementos Book IV as catalogued, and it is not 4 Baruch.
What the ink shows:
- A judgment homily, not narrative. Scripture is cited twice with በከመ፡ይቤ "as he
  said": l.7 (no one brings the wicked to the ቀስት — cf. Ps 1:5, the wicked do not
  stand in the judgment) and ll.14-16 ይጕይይ፡ርእስ፡እንዘ፡እልቦ፡ዘይሰድዶ, which is
  Prov 28:1 "the wicked flee when no one pursues" (standard Ge'ez ይጐይይ፡ረሲዕ፡
  ወአልቦ፡ዘይሰድዶ; the subject word here reads ርእስ, see NOTES).
- Stock eschatology: sinners separated from the righteous (ll.9-10, Mt 25),
  repaid according to their works whether evil or good (ll.10-13, Rom 2:6 /
  2 Cor 5:10), the river of Sheol (l.14, Dan 7:10 / Apoc. Peter's river of
  fire), Gehenna of fire (l.6), the land of torment where there is fire
  (ll.17-18).
- Voice: first person singular መጻእኩ "I came" (l.2), then first person plural
  ንሕነ፡ንቀውም፡ሎሙ፡በሰማያት "we stand for them in heaven" (l.21) with ሠርዓተነ,
  ፈቃደነ, ትእዘዘነ "our ordinances, our will, our commandment" (ll.19, 22-24).
  The speakers are the law-givers who intercede for those who kept the law.
  That is the same first-person apostolic voice found on f.167, so this page
  continues that homily rather than starting a new work. Candidates for the
  parent text: the apostolic/Petrine homiletic material that circulates with
  the Qalementos and the Didascalia (Apocalypse of Peter-type Gehenna language
  in a "we the apostles" frame). I cannot name the passage from the ink.

## GIST
"And if it was not [ጊጎያ?] for them, now I have come and I [ወእትጋሪክሙ?] you;
now there is no excuse for the judgment. And without ቀስት the wicked enter
Gehenna of fire, as he said: there is no one who brings the wicked into ቀስት;
the ቀስት is the ስለት of judgment, when the sinners are separated from the
righteous. According to their works they are repaid for what they did, whether
evil or good; and the sinners are driven into the river of Sheol, as he said:
the ርእስ flees when there is no one pursuing him, and likewise the sinner
enters the land of torment where there is fire. But those who kept our
ordinances according to this, and [ወተሠኡ?] their kinsmen the law, we stand
for them in heaven, because they kept for us our ordinances and the doing of
our will and our commandment, because [ኢክነ፡ስፉቀ ...]."

## NOTES
Unresolved vocabulary (transcribed letter by letter, not smoothed):
- l.1 ጊጎያ — ጊ (ገ with left foot, per key) then a ገ whose right leg descends
  and hooks left at the foot (ጎ-shape; not ዜ, not ዝ), then ያ. Not a word I
  can place. Context wants "time/season" (ኢኮኖሙ፡ጊዜ?), but the ink is not ዜ.
- l.2-3 ወእትጋሪክሙ — ት has the top-left tick, ጋ is 7-shaped, ሪ has the tick.
  First-person imperfect with 2mp object; root not placed.
- ll.4-5, 8 ቀስት (three times), and ቀስትስ = ቀስት + -ሰ written with ስ. Not
  ኵነኔ (which is what l.4 uses for "judgment"). The page seems to gloss it:
  ቀስትስ፡ስለት፡ደይን "the ቀስት is the ስለት of judgment". Sense demands a word for
  trial/reckoning. Possibly an Arabic loan (qisṭ "justice"?) — for the expert.
- l.8 ስለት — ስ is the Λ-with-foot form, not the 9-bowl ዕ, so not ዕለተ "day of"
  by shape, though "day of judgment" is what the sentence wants. Flagged.
- l.15 ርእስ — the glyph after ር is this hand's hooked alef with a bottom-right
  foot, identical to the እ of እንዘ on the same line; not ኩ (which has its own
  shape at l.4 ለኩነኔ). Prov 28:1 wants ረሲዕ/ኃጥእ. Ink says ርእስ (or ርኡስ).
- l.20 ወተሠኡ — ሠ three-pronged, then alef with a right foot at the baseline
  (ኡ or እ), then ፡. Only four letters; no ሉ/ሀ. ተሠሀሉ "were merciful" or ተስእሉ
  "asked" would fit the sense but neither is the ink.
- l.23 ወግብራ — ግ (this hand's tailed ገ, not the ት-like form), ብ box, ራ. Reads
  as ወግብረ "and the doing of" or ወገብሩ "and they did"; ink is ራ.
- l.24 ኢክነ፡ስፉቀ — ኢ with the left-curling right leg, ክ, I-beam ነ; then ስ, a
  ፉ-loop (inside a word, no bars: a letter, not ፪), ቀ. ኢክነ may be this
  scribe's ኢኮነ. Continues onto f.169r.
