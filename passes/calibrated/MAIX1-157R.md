## GIST
The page continues a resurrection sequence keyed to three trumpet blasts. "...divinity. And at the first blast of the trumpet the bodies (flesh) which had become like dust are gathered/return; and at the second blast of the trumpet the houses (tombs) are opened, [and] those within them; the souls which were gathered are hidden/placed (or: return) into the bodies by the dew of mercy which comes down to them from God, as Isaiah said: 'for the dew that is from you is their life.' And after this, at the third blast of the trumpet the dead rise in the twinkling of an eye, and they make them stand before his face, when he sets the sheep on his right and the goats on his left and says to them, 'Go, cursed ones, who did not keep the commandment of God,' and they take them away to weeping and gnashing of teeth."

## NOTES
Least certain readings, in order:
- L7 first glyph, read ኀ (ኀብዑ "they hid/placed"). At 450% it matches the ኀ opening L9 (ኀቤሆሙ): top bar with a left hook, centre stem, foot. Second choice ግ (giving ግብዑ, "they returned", the verb the scribe used in L2), but this hand's ግ (L4 ደግም, L17 አባግዐ) is a hooked ገ with a bottom-left leg and looks nothing like it. Sense-wise ወይግብኡ would be expected; the ink does not show ይ.
- L5 ትረኀው: second glyph is plain ረ (the "ring" seen at lower zoom is a stray dot on the page); third glyph read ኀ against the linguistic reading ይትረኀው "will be opened" (ተርኅወ). This hand's ነ (L14 ይትነሥኡ, L22 ስነን) has only a left hook on the top bar; ኀ has a bar with a knob at the right end, which this glyph has. Second choice ነ.
- L5 እብያት: first glyph carries the top-left tick this hand gives እ, not plain አ; standard is አብያት "houses/tombs". Transcribed as ink.
- L6 ዘተግቡ: ግ-ቡ as ink; likely scribal for ዘተገብኡ "which were gathered". Second choice for the ግ is ገ (the leg is at the crop edge).
- L2 ይትግብዑ: nonstandard for ይትገብኡ; the last glyph is a rounded ዑ, not ኡ.
- L2, L7 ሠጋት: plain ሠ, no hook, for standard ሥጋት. L14 ይትነሥኡ: rightmost stroke of the ś-glyph curls, read ሥ; second choice ሣ. L13 ሣልስ: straight descending leg, ሣ.
- L1 ቀዳሚ: ዳ has a clear right foot (contrast L4 ደግም, plain ደ). The ሚ has a downward curl rather than a clean ring; ሜ is possible.
