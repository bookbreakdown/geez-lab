## WORK

Apostolic church-order discourse, continuing the same stratum as ff.168-169
(NOT Qalementos Book IV as the catalogue label says; nothing here matches
Clement). Evidence from the ink:

- l.10-11 ንሕነ፡ሐዋርያት "we the apostles" — first-person apostolic voice, and
  l.9 ቀኖና፡ዘእዘዝናክሙ "the canon which we commanded you", l.8 ጽንዑ "be
  steadfast" — the canon-law frame of an apostolic order (Sinodos / Didascalia
  type), not a narrative.
- l.1-3 renunciation of kindred, wife, children and "all the sweetness of this
  world, which quickly passes away" — an echo of Luke 14:26 / Matt 19:29, used
  as exhortation, not quoted.
- l.7-8 "hand over your souls to death upon the earth" — martyrdom
  exhortation (reading ወጠው as መጠው, see NOTES).
- l.12-13 ደብተራ "tabernacle" — the tabernacle theme of f.169v recurs here.
- l.14-15 ንጉሠ፡ነገሠት፡ወእግዚእ፡እግዕዝት "King of kings and Lord of lords"
  (Deut 10:17; 1 Tim 6:15; Rev 19:16), followed by an unmistakable paraphrase
  of **1 Samuel 2:6-7** (Hannah's song; cf. Deut 32:39, Tobit 13:2):
  l.17 ይክል፡ቀቲለ፡ወአሕይዎ "he can kill and make alive",
  l.18-19 አውርዶ፡ውስተ፡ሲኦል፡ወአዕረጎ፡ውስተ፡ሕይወት "bring down to Sheol and
  bring up to life",
  l.20-21 ያነዲ፡ወያብዕል፡ያኃሥር፡ወያከብር "make poor and make rich, humble and
  honour" — this is verse-for-verse 1 Sam 2:6-7 in Ge'ez wording (ይቀትል፡
  ወያሐዩ፤ ያወርድ፡ውስተ፡ሲኦል፡ወያዐርግ፤ ያነዲ፡ወያብዕል፤ ያኀሥር፡ወያከብር), recast
  as a doxology of divine power.
- l.22-23 extend the doxology with cosmic pairs: plants/uproots, makes rise/
  makes set, makes morning — Ps 104 / Amos 5:8-type language, not a fixed
  citation.

Specific work: unidentified; a Ge'ez apostolic canon collection with a
doxological interlude. The 1 Sam 2:6-7 identification is firm.

## GIST

...[whoever leaves] kindred and wife and children and all the sweetness of
this world, which passes quickly and does not stand firm, [is bound] by the law
of the canon of the holy church. If it is possible for you, hand over your souls
to death upon the earth; be steadfast in the law of the canon which we
commanded you and the law of the church — we the apostles and the [?] of the
ordinance of our Lord, we kept the tabernacle of his righteousness for him who
created the world. King of kings and Lord of lords, who has authority over all
creation, who is able to kill and to make alive, and is able to bring down to
Sheol and bring up to life, and is able to make all poor and make rich, to
humble and to honour, who plants and uproots, makes rise and makes set, and
makes morning, and sends men out into...

## NOTES

Uncertain readings, in order of doubt:

- **l.2 end / l.3 start ረ|ኅለ?ፊ.** Sense demands ዘኀላፊ "which passes away"
  (parallel to l.3 ዘፍጡን፡የሐልፍ). The ink at the end of l.2 is a plain ረ, not
  ዘ (4x confirms: curved body with top serif, no H). l.3 opens with a ኀ-body
  carrying a left tick (the hand's third-order mark; cf. key on ኢ / እኂሁ), then
  ለ, then an ink blot the size of one letter, then ፊ (ፈ with low right dash,
  not the ring of ፌ). Whether a letter sits under the blot cannot be seen.
  Transcribed as ink; flagged for the editor as probably ዘኀላፊ miswritten.
- **l.7 ወጠው.** First glyph is the round ወ, not the double-lobed መ this hand
  uses in ዘመደ (l.1). Sense wants መጠው "hand over (pl. impv.)". Transcribed
  as ink ወጠው; editor's call.
- **l.11 ወጋስያነ** — unresolved vocabulary. ጋ is the 7-shape (= scribe's ግ,
  see below), ስ plain, ያ, ነ I-beam. "ወግስያነ፡ሠርዓተ፡እግዚእነ" — something
  coordinated with ሐዋርያት. Not ወራስያነ "heirs": ራ in this hand (l.13 ደብተራ)
  is ረ + right tick, not a 7. Add to the key's unplaced-vocabulary list.
- **l.15 end ሠ?** — the glyph after ሠ is under a black smudge at the margin.
  Continuation l.16 ጠነ makes ሥልጣነ "authority" the target; the smudged glyph
  is consistent with ል. Not readable as ink.
- **l.22 ወይምሔ** — ሐ with a right mid-height dash attached to the leg; could
  be ሔ, ሒ or ሑ. Target is ወይምሕው "and uproots" (pair to ዘይተክል). Flag.
- **l.13 ጽድቁ** — ቀ with right tick (ቁ) rather than foot (ቅ); ጽድቅ expected.
- **l.8 በደበ** — ይ/ደ collision; the second glyph has no visible tick. Sense
  (በዲበ፡ምድር "upon the earth") decides; transcribed as ink.
- **l.21 ያኃሥር** — the ኀ carries a descending right leg, so ኃ is what the ink
  shows; the ሥ foot is faint. Order flagged per key rule.
- **l.18 ሲኦል** — the ኦ is a rounded-top alef, distinguishable here from the
  hooked እ of l.17-19; read as ኦ on sense.

New letterform facts for the key:

- **ግ is written as the 7-shaped ጋ in this hand**, at least in the divine
  titles: እጋዚእ (l.12, l.15), እጋዕዝት (l.15), and ነጋሃ = ነግሀ "morning"
  (l.23). But በሕግ (l.4, l.8-9) shows a distinct ግ with foot. So ጋ-shape does
  NOT reliably mean 3rd order; read ጋ/ግ on sense and say so. This joins the
  key's "ጉ/ግ read as ት" entry: ግ has at least two shapes on one page.
- **ኖ vs ና at a line break (l.4|5 ቀኖ|ናሃ):** ኖ has an up-right hook on the
  stem head; ና has a left flag at the head. Distinct at 4x, mergeable at 2x.
- **ነ (I-beam) and ነ (up-curling foot) both on this page:** I-beam in ንሕነ
  (l.11), ዓቀብነ (l.12), ነገሠት (l.14), ነጋሃ (l.23); foot form in ነፍስክሙ (l.7).
  Confirms the widened key rule; decided on morphology.
- **A ghost offset from the facing page** sits at the right margin of ll.2-3
  (grey outline glyphs, not ink). Do not read the margin ghosts as letters.
- ። is written as two spaced ፡ pairs at l.4 and l.14; kept as ።.
- Line count 24, matching the 22-25 range in the key.
