## WORK

Rest of the Words of Baruch (Paralipomena Jeremiae, 4 Baruch), chapter 8 — the
foreign-wives episode, continuing directly from what f.165v was found to hold.
Not Qalementos. Evidence from the ink itself, not from the catalogue label:

- ኤርምያስ (Jeremiah) named five times (ll. 7, 11, 14, 16, 21), twice with the
  double title ነቢይ ... ወከህን "prophet and priest" (ll. 7, 11-12, 14, 17) — the
  title 4 Baruch gives him.
- ኢየሩሳሌም (l. 3-4), ከለውድዮን "Chaldeans" twice (ll. 1, 10), ብሔረ ከለውድዮን
  "the land of the Chaldeans" = Babylon.
- The plot is exactly 4 Bar 8:1-7: the people are brought out of the Chaldeans'
  land to return to Jerusalem (ll. 1-4); they report to Jeremiah that the
  children of Israel will not leave the wives they took there (ll. 6-10);
  Jeremiah rebukes them (ll. 11-13), orders a separation (ll. 14-16), and the
  people split into those who say "we will not leave" and those who say "we
  will leave" and go with Jeremiah (ll. 18-21); the refusers begin a speech
  about loving their wives and children (ll. 21-24).
- The verb ኀደገ "leave, abandon" recurs four times (ll. 8, 15-16, 19, 20) — the
  keyword of the episode.

The Ethiopic here is fuller than the Greek (three days' journey, the two
parties' speeches), which is normal for the Ethiopic recension.

## NUMERALS

above or below. Not ፲, not ፩. No other candidate on the page.

## NOTES

Unresolved — transcribed letter by letter, NOT smoothed. Collect for the expert:

- l.2 እሠ (between ወይቢሎ፡ and ፡ወተንሠኡ). Hooked alef + plain three-pronged ሠ,
  no ring, no foot. Nothing in 4 Bar 8:1 ("Rise, you and the people")
  corresponds. Could be a botched ተንሥእ that the scribe restarted as ወተንሠኡ.
- l.4 ሀገሩ፡ለንጉሠ፡በቢይ። Two boxes, the second with a low right tick (ቢ), then
  the ይ/ደ form, then ። Context wants ባቢሎን "Babylon" after ንጉሠ "king of",
  and ሀገሩ "his city" is odd after ኢየሩሳሌም; but the ink has no ሎ or ን.
  Second reading of the last letter: በቢደ.
- l.13 ወተግዓዙ፡ወተለኮዩ። Neither resolves. Glyph 3 of the first is the
  7-with-descender that reads ግ elsewhere on the page (l.8, l.16); ጋ possible.
  In the second, the letter after ለ has the ከ body with a loop at the LOWER
  right — ኮ as read, but ኵ/ኰ possible; the following letter is the ይ/ደ form
  with a foot, so ዩ or ዱ. Something like "why did you bring [them] and ...
  and ...?" — Jeremiah's rebuke.
- l.15 ተፈልጡ፡እነ፡ወእነ፡ Two identical tokens. Stem is asymmetric (ነ), see
  NUMERALS. Amharic እነ "those of ..." (a plural marker) would give "separate,
  these-and-those", which fits an Ankobar scribe; register the guess only.
- l.21-22 ገብ/ሩ. Line-initial letter on l.22 is a closed bowl with a right
  foot; ሩ as read (ገብሩ "those who did [so]"), but it is rounder than the ሩ of
  ll. 4 and 5. ኡ (ገብኡ "those who went back") is ruled out — no hooked alef.
