## GIST

[...unclean and abominable is he who steals] the boundary of his neighbour, and he who beats (or plunders) from the house of God, which being (?) one whom the abbot of that place does not approve; and let him do as much as the elders of the church command him. As for the law that was spoken in the canon, he adds upon it [one unread word]; and after he has repented, and his return (?) as the elders of the church command him, let him enter, be ...ed (ይትከነስ, root not identified), and approach. Unclean and abominable is he who ...s (ያመውጽ, unidentified) the elders, and those who are commanded, servants to their masters, and maidservants who do not obey their mistresses: let them do as the elders of the church command them, and afterwards let them enter, be ...ed, and approach. Unclean and abominable is he who perverts their faith, the Christians', and worships either to pagans or to Jews; as for penance, it is spoken in the canon [sentence continues on 161v].

## NOTES

Join with 160v: 160v ends on ዘይሠርቅ (he who steals); 161r l.1 opens ደወለ ፡ ቢጹ, "the boundary of his neighbour" (Deut 19:14 / 27:17 formula). Clean join; the prediction registered on 160v holds.

Uncertain readings, in order of doubt:
- l.8 "[mark] ት ፡ እደ ።": the two words after the mark do not parse. ት is a clear ticked sadis (not ፲: no bounding bars). እደ could be "hand of". The whole may be the residue of a correction; the sentence ይዌስክ ፡ ደቢሁ (l.7) "he adds upon it" wants an object here. Lowest-confidence spot on the page.
- l.12-13 ዘያመ|ውጽ: verb not identified. l.13 first glyph is a rounded bowl with a right leg; ው chosen, ዑ possible. Object is ሊቀናት (the elders), so an act against superiors is meant.
- l.9 ወግብዓ: fourth glyph read ዓ (open arm, stem at lower left); ፃ is the second choice. Sense wants ግብአ "return" (cf. l.19 ይባዑ); the ይ of an expected ወይግባዕ is not in the ink.
- l.4 ወይግለር: fourth glyph is drawn as ለ (arch, right foot), not the box of በ. Sense demands ወይግበር "let him do" (cf. l.16 ይግበሩ ፡ በከመ ፡ እዘዝዎሙ, same formula). Transcribed as ink, ለ; probable scribal slip.
- l.11 ባስ: second glyph is angular, ስ-like, not the hooked alef. Sense and the l.19 parallel ይባዑ want ወይባእ "let him enter". Transcribed as ink.
- l.3 ዘኢያሰመሪ: negation ኢ chosen on sense (the ኢ/እ curl is faint); word not securely identified. Best guess ዘኢያሰምር "whom [the abbot] does not approve / who does not please". Final glyph is ሪ (hook), not ር.
- l.2-3 ከዊ|ኖ: ink is clear; word unclear. ከዊኖ (gerund "being") is the only Ge'ez form that fits the letters.
- l.19 ሬ ፡ ሁ ፡: a ፡ separates ሬ and ሁ; l.8 has ወእምድኅሬ ፡ ነሰሐ without ሁ. Either a stray divider inside ድኅሬሁ or ሁ added after the fact. Transcribed as ink.
- l.21 struck-out cluster after ዘያዓሉ ፡: two glyph-spaces crossed through with an X. Probably a false start of ሃይማኖቶሙ; the surviving word lacks its ይ (ሃማኖቶሙ).
- l.21 ዘያዓሉ: third glyph read ዓ; ዐ possible. Sense ዘየዐሉ "who transgresses/perverts".
- l.23 ለእሬሚ: the ረ has a closed loop at the foot, as in ድኅሬ (l.8) and unlike the plain ረ of ቀረቡ (l.20); ሬ as ink, standard አረሚ.
- l.24 ተናገረ: last glyph plain ረ at 2x; ሪ at page scale. በቀኖና read at page scale only, anchored on the identical l.7 word.
- l.7 ደቢሁ: first glyph is the ይ/ደ form with no extended leg; sense ዲቤሁ "upon it".
- l.12 ዘያመ: ያ read; ዘደመ rejected on sense.
