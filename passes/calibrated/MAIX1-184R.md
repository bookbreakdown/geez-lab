(184R-strip0..7) and 3x half-line crops (184R-L1a/b, L9a/b, L13a/b, L15a/b,

## WORK

Continuation of the single second-person exhortation on church rank that runs
ff.181v–183v. This side cannot be assigned to a named work from its own ink, but
it can be placed inside the codex:

Evidence for the placement:
- The page opens mid-sentence (ማዕደረ፡ሕይወት is the complement of a verb that must sit on 183v) and closes mid-word (ወኢያሠ-). It is not a unit boundary on either side.
- Addressee: singular imperatives ገሠጽ፡ወጸናዕ፡ወመሐር (l.8–9, "rebuke, be firm, teach") and በቀድሜክ (l.15, "before you"), then plural ኢትበሉ / ኢት…ከሙ (ll.16–17). The same shift between a singular addressee and a plural audience is what the 181v–183v exhortation does.
- Vocabulary shared with the canon folios: the anathema pair ርጉማን፡ወቀውማን፡እሙንቱ (ll.17–18), i.e. the ወቀውም of the ff.160v–177v formula, here in the -ān plural. ሥርዓታ፡ለቤት፡ክርስቲያን "the order of the church" three times (ll.7, 11 with ሕጋ, 23–24).
- The three-grade list bishop / priests / deacons at ll.20–22 is NOT used as a stratum marker (withdrawn claim). It is recorded only as content: whoever of any of them "does not keep the order of the holy church" is under the anathema.

Citations on this page, in order, all WOVEN, none carrying ይቤ / በከመ፡ይቤ:
1. ll.3–5 ብካይ፡ወሐቆየ፡ስነን … የዓሠሩ፡እደዊሆሙ፡ወእገሪሆሙ፡ወይትወደዩ — Mt 22:13 (bind hand and foot, cast out, weeping and gnashing). Order of clauses is reversed against Matthew (weeping first, binding second).
2. l.8–9 ገሠጽ … ወመሐር — 2 Tim 4:2 register (rebuke, exhort, teach); a resemblance, not a diagnostic.
3. ll.12–15 ወእመሰ፡ዐበይክ … ይክውን፡ከመ፡እረማዊ፡ወመጸብሐዊ — Mt 18:17 (if he refuses, let him be to you as a pagan and tax-collector). This is the one diagnostic citation: the pairing አረማዊ + መጸብሓዊ is Mt 18:17 and nothing else.
4. ll.15–17 ጥቀ፡ኢትበሉ … ኢት…ከሙ — the register of 1 Cor 5:11 / 2 John 10 (do not even eat with / greet), but the ink is unresolved; do not count it as an identified citation.

No citation formula on the page. That is the homily habit, not the canon habit — but this page is inside a passage already established as continuous with the canon block on other grounds, so the absence of formulae is consistent with the 181v–183v exhortation being paraenesis appended to the canons rather than a separate homily. What this side alone establishes: paraenetic conclusion to a church-order text, with a Mt 18:17 excommunication rule applied to clergy of all three grades.

No Arabic loan claimed. ወቀውም/ቀውማን remains unplaced; the plural is new evidence that it is an adjective or participle (like ሰዑባን on f.179r), not a verb or abstract noun.

## GIST

Those who do not keep the order of the church are not in the dwelling of life but below, bound hand and foot and cast into weeping and gnashing of teeth and into judgment. You (sg.) are to rebuke, be firm, teach, and put to shame the apostates from the faith who [must] be governed by the law of the holy church; if one refuses you he becomes to you as a pagan and a tax-collector; do not even [eat/greet — unresolved] with them and do not [spare?] them, for they are accursed and ቀውማን from the ranks of the church — whether from the bishop, or from the priests, or from the deacons, whoever does not keep the order of the holy church, and does not [continues on 184v].

## NOTES

- ORDER OF CITATIONS: Mt 22:13 (ll.3–5) → [2 Tim 4:2 register, ll.8–9] → Mt 18:17 (ll.12–15) → [1 Cor 5:11 / 2 John 10 register, ll.15–17, unresolved]. All woven, no formula.
- Scribal habits confirmed on this side: sadis for other orders (ክ for ከ in ይክውን; ቀስውስት; ቀድስት; ስነን), ኢ- for ኤ- (ኢጳስቆጶስ, ትኢዘዙ), ይ/ደ collision at ll.13 and 16, single-loop ው with tail in ውእቱ.
- Spelling of the ranks here: ኢጳስቆጶስ, ቀስውስት, ዲያቆናት. The key attests ደያቆናት on earlier folios; this side has ዲ with a clear tick. Worth a tgrep (see falsifiers).
- ወቀውማን (l.18) is the -ān plural of the unplaced ወቀውም. Paired with ርጉማን and predicated with እሙንቱ. If ወቀውም elsewhere always sits in ርኩስ/ርጉም + ወቀውም + ውእቱ, then the ወ- is the conjunction and the lexeme is ቀውም; the plural here supports that parse.
- JOIN PREDICTIONS (registered before reading either neighbour):
  - 183v must END mid-sentence with a verb (probably negated or with አኮ on this side closing it) whose complement is "in the dwelling of life": something like "…ኢይነብሩ / ኢይክሉ፡ነቢረ" and its final word must not end a clause.
  - 184v must OPEN with the completion of ወኢያሠ- : expected -ምር (ወኢያሠምር "and does not approve") or -ንይ / -ነዩ (ወኢያሠንይ "and does not do well"), then the apodosis of the l.22–24 "whoever does not keep…" clause, i.e. an anathema (ርጉም / ውጉዝ / ወቀውም + ውእቱ).

  2. `tgrep ሥርዓታ` / `tgrep ሥርዓተ`: continuity with 181v–183v predicts the phrase ሥርዓት + ቤተ፡ክርስቲያን there; absence weakens the placement (does not kill it).
