## WORK
Unidentified as a named work, but the genre is settled by the ink: a homiletic
church-order discourse on the teacher/pastor (መምሕር, l.13) as shepherd of God's
flock (ዘራዊ፡መርዔቱ፡ለእግዚአብሔር, ll.13-14 and 17-18). This is the same discourse
already running on ff.168-171, which quoted John 10:30; here it quotes the
neighbouring verses of the same chapter. Scriptural material, with the ink:

- ll.1-5: a healing catalogue, "heals the leper, guides the blind, makes the
  deaf hear, gladdens the sick, [and] the grieving and the sorrowful" —
  Matthew 11:5 / Isaiah 35:5-6 language, applied to the good teacher.
- ll.5-7: ውእቱኪ፡ዓቢየ፡ይከውን፡በመንግሠተ፡ሰማያት — Matthew 5:19 ("shall be [called]
  great in the kingdom of heaven"), with ይከውን for the Gospel's ይሰመይ.
- ll.18-20: እስብ፡ዘኢኮነ፡ዚእሁ ... ይመጽእ፡ተኩላ፡ወይዘሩ፡አባግዓ፡ወይጐይይ — John 10:12
  (the hireling, whose own the sheep are not, sees the wolf come, scatters the
  sheep and flees). The Ge'ez Gospel has ዐሳብ, ዚአሁ, ተኵላ, ወይዘርዎን, ወይጐይይ.
- ll.20-22: ኖላዊ፡ይሜጡ፡ነፍሶ፡ቤዛ፡[አባ]ግዒሁ — John 10:11 (the shepherd lays down his
  life for his sheep), quoted AFTER v.12, i.e. the homilist reverses the order.
- l.24 ዘኢያሤንዩሙ፡ለኃጥእን "who does not amend the sinners" — the pastor's duty to
  the sinner, the theme of the whole page.

Not 4 Baruch, not the Two Ways homily. Whether this is Qalementos Book IV
cannot be decided from this leaf; nothing on it contradicts a church-order
setting and nothing names Clement or Peter.

## GIST
"...[he raises?] the fallen, heals the leper, guides the blind, makes the deaf
hear, gladdens the sick, and the grieving and the sorrowful. He then shall be
great in the kingdom of heaven. But whoever says to himself, 'If I do good
works, what does it matter to me about the way of sinners? Are not all repaid
according to their works?' — [such a one] ... the teacher, is he not the
shepherd of God's flock, so that a man should not be like him who made peace
with [the sinner]? He is the shepherd of God's flock. A hireling, whose own they
are not: the wolf comes and scatters the sheep, and he flees; but the shepherd
gives his life for his sheep so that the wolf may not take [them]. And likewise
he too who does not amend the sinners..." [continues on 174r].

## NOTES
Least certain readings, in order:
- l.1 ለውዱቱ: 3rd glyph is ደ with a foot hook (ዱ), 4th is a cross with a foot
  hook and no loop, i.e. ቱ, not ቅ. Sense wants ውዱቅ "the fallen" (ያነሥኦ ለውዱቅ).
  The key notes ጉ is drawn as ተ with a foot hook, so a ቅ/ጉ/ቱ confusion is
  possible, but the ink is ቱ. Flagged, not smoothed.
- ll.9-10 ተኃልፍ|የ: transcribed letter by letter. The word after ምንተ is
  ተ + this hand's ኃ + ል + ፍ, then የ opens l.10. Standard Ge'ez does not give a
  clean word; possibly ትኀልፍ "passes/befalls" with an object suffix. Unresolved.
- l.11 ሥሉ: the 2nd word is unambiguously the ሠ-family (three prongs, right
  descender), yet sense requires ኵሉ "all". Transcribed as ink; flagged as a
  scribal slip or an unrecorded ኵ form.
- ll.12-13 ዘኃተሰ፡ዘይሠ|ል: both unplaced. The glyph after ዘ matches the ኃ of
  ኃጥእን (ll.10, 24) at 4x. ዘይሠል is not a Ge'ez word I can place; an Arabic
  root is possible (s-l-l? or the ል is a mis-cut ይ: ዘይሠይም "who appoints"
  would suit "teacher" but the ink is ል). Hypothesis only.
- l.13 and l.17 ዘራዊ: the final glyph has TWO loops plus a tick — ዊ, not ዒ —
  at 4x on both lines. Sense wants ራዒ "shepherd" (cf. Arabic rāʿī), and the
  word pairs with መርዔት both times. Transcribed ዊ; treat ራዊ for ራዒ as a
  candidate scribal form (ው/ዐ substitution) for the key.
- l.18 እስብ: hooked alef, not ዐ. Context is John 10:12, whose word is ዐሳብ
  "hireling"; the scribe's አ/ዐ merger plus sadis gives እስብ. Hypothesis.
- l.19 ወይዘሩ: final glyph is ረ with a bottom-right hook (ሩ); ራ second choice.
  Gospel has ወይዘርዎን.
- l.20 ወይጐይይ: the 3rd glyph is a ገ-shape with a small loop at lower left,
  read as ጐ on sense (John 10:12 ወይጐይይ "flees"). Second choice ኍ.
- l.20 ቲርስ: cross with a right dash below mid = ቲ per the key, then ር ስ.
  Unplaced. It stands where John 10:11 has ኄር "good" (ኖላዊ ኄር); ኄርሰ "but the
  good [shepherd]" is what sense wants, and the ink is not ኄ. Recorded as
  ink; a corrupt ኄርሰ is the hypothesis.
- l.22 እግዒሁ: four glyphs, the 3rd a 9-bowl with tick (ዒ, not ዚ). John 10:11
  has አባግዒሁ; the scribe appears to have dropped ባ (haplography). Not "his
  lord".
- l.5 ወለሓዝን: 3rd glyph ambiguous ሓ/ሳ; ሓ chosen on sense (ሐዙን "sorrowful").
- l.5 ውእቱኪ and l.17 ኪ: a ከ-shaped glyph with a small tick at the right foot,
  i.e. the same third-order marking this hand uses on ኢ (ll.15, 18, 22, 24 —
  a dot at the right foot, not the key's left curl). Sense wants the enclitic
  -ኬ "then". Contrast l.23 ውእቱኒ, which is a narrow-stem ነ with tick, clearly
  different. No colon between ቱ and ኪ on l.5.

Nonstandard spellings for the key: ቀጥቁጥ (for ቈጥቍጥ), መንግሠተ (ሠ for ሥ), ሠናየ,
ወያስተትፌሥሖ (a doubled ተ/ት before ፌሥሖ — dittography, both crosses are on the
page, the second with the ት top-left tick), ምሰለ (plain ሰ for ስ), ተኵላ as
ተኩላ twice (ኩ with a foot hook, no top-right ኵ hook), መምሕረ (ሕ for ህ, cf. the
key's ሀ/ሐ swaps), እግዚእብሔር (እ for አ, as the key predicts), ኃጥእን twice,
ዓቢየ, ዘተዓርቀ, ስብእ (attested in the key), ዚእሁ.

Letterform facts new to the key:
- ተ frequently carries a short LEFT dash just below the crossbar (ll.3, 7,
  9, 12, 19). It is still ተ; do not read it as ቲ (right dash) or ት (top-left
  tick plus foot).
- The third order (ኢ, ኪ) is marked here by a small detached dot or tick at
  the right foot, not only by the left curl the key records.
- ር at l.2 (ወይመርሆ) looked ringed (ሮ) at 2x and is plain ር at 4x — the key's
  false-ring warning holds for black ink too, not only for red dots.
- ሤ (l.24) is the ሠ-shape with a curled hook at the right foot; ሥ (ll.4, 11,
  22) has a straight slanted descender. They are distinguishable at 4x.
- ዓ (ll.6, 16, 20) is a 9-bowl with a right stem and looks like ባ at 1x;
  ባ (l.20 አባግዓ) is a box with a leg. Zoom before reading either.
- Ghost offset: faint grey ፡-like marks in the right margin at ll.5 and 24
  are transfer, not punctuation; l.24 ends at ን.
