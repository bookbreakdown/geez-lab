## WORK

**Rest of the Words of Baruch (4 Baruch / Paraleipomena Jeremiou), end of
chapter 8 running into chapter 9.** Not Qalementos. Identified from what was
read, and the identification did not shape any reading:

- l.1 "with our wives and our children" (ምስለ፡አንስቲያነ፡ወውሉድነ) — the
  foreign-wives party of 4 Bar 8, the same episode found on f.165v.
- ll.3-6 "let them return to the land of the Chaldeans (ብሔረ፡ከለውድዮን),
  whence they had gone out at first; and when the Chaldeans saw them they
  said: the children of Israel who went out from us..." — 4 Bar 8:7, the
  Babylonians refusing the returnees.
- ll.8-9, 12-15 "we will not bring them back into our city ... you went out
  from our city first, without our driving you out; now too we will not bring
  you back into our city" — 4 Bar 8:8.
- ll.15-21 "they drove them out, and they came to that city ... Jeremiah ...
  said: Shomron/Samaria (ስምርን), this shall be our city; and for that reason
  the city was named Samaria (ስምር፡ተሰምየት)" — 4 Bar 8:9, the founding and
  naming of Samaria.
- ll.21-23 "and the Lord commanded that we preach the news of the gospel
  (ዜና፡ወንጌል) ... the way of the nations" — the Christian prophecy material
  that opens 4 Bar 9 in the Ethiopic recension.

The Ethiopic here is visibly not a word-for-word match to the Greek (Jeremiah
speaks the name of the city; the gospel commission is in the first person
plural), which is what one expects of the Ethiopic recension, not of a
different work.

## GIST

returned to the land of the Chaldeans, from where they had first gone out; and
when the Chaldeans saw them, they said: "The children of Israel, who went out
from us — we will not bring them back into our city." And they took their

Least certain readings, ink letter by letter, NOT smoothed:

- l.1 **ምሕለ** — second glyph is a rounded arch with a left leg, this hand's

  l.5 ስበ). Sense demands ምስለ "with". Either a slip or an unrecorded ስ
  allograph; flagged.
- l.1 **ወውሉድኑ**, l.8 **ኑ**, l.9 **ወኑሥኡ** — a stem with a long up-curling
  foot, which the key calls ኑ. Sense demands ነ in all three (ወውሉድነ "our
  children"; ወነሥኡ "they took"). The l.8 line-initial ኑ stands alone before

  l.7. See letterform note below.
- l.2 **ለሂሎሙ** — fifth glyph is a clear ለ (two legs, left bar), not this
  hand's boxed ብ (compare ግብዑ l.2-3, ንስብክ l.22). Sense demands ብሂሎሙ
  "saying to them". Transcribed as ink.
- ll.2-3 **ድ|ረ፡ኀሙ፡** — unresolved. Ink is ድ (line end) / ረ ፡ ኀ ሙ ፡. The
  ረ has what may be a small loop at the end of its upper arm (ሬ?), but the
  key's false-ring warning applies; ኀ order unmarked. Context wants "back"
  (ድኅረ) or similar; no smoothing applied. Collect for the expert.
- ll.3-4, 5-6 **ከ|ለውድዮን**, **ለከ|ለውድዮን** — the ethnonym for the

- l.7 **እምኒነ፡** — ኒ (right mid tick) then a ነ-form with a mark; expected

- l.9 **ሥያኒሆ|ሙ** — unresolved vocabulary. Glyph 1 is ሥ or ሠ (three humps

- l.10 **ወእሔዓሁሙ** — unresolved verb. ሔ (ሐ with bottom-right ring), then

as the key says.

New letterform facts for the key:

- **ነ has two shapes on this page.** (a) A serifed I-beam — top bar, stem,
  bottom bar, no bowl — used for the possessive suffix -ነ in እንስቲያነ,
  ሀገርነ (five times). It is indistinguishable in isolation from the key's ፲
  and must be ruled out as a numeral by the absence of bounding bars and by
  its position inside a word. (b) A stem with a long up-curling foot, the
  form the key currently calls ኑ, in ወውሉድ- (l.1), l.8 initial, and ወ-ሥኡ
  (l.9), where sense demands ነ every time. The key's ነ/ኑ rule needs
  widening: an up-curling foot alone does not make ኑ in this hand.
- **ን has a foot** (ዘንተ l.2, ስምርን l.18): stem, top-right bend, right foot.
  It differs from the I-beam ነ by the bend and from ኒ by lacking the mid
  tick.
- **ጋ is a 7-shape with a descending left leg** (l.17), consistent with the
  key; ግ (ግብዑ ll.2-3, እግዚእ l.21) is the plain 7-shape with a foot.
- **ሔ = ሐ with a small ring at the bottom right** (ብሔረ l.3, l.10, l.14).
- **ሎ** is ለ with a bottom-right ring (ሂሎሙ l.2, ይቤሎ ll.6, 18).
- **ሆ** is ሀ with a top-right loop (l.9).
- The **red-dotted cross ።** appears four times; each time the black ink is
  two spaced ፡ pairs and the red is added around it.

## JOIN PREDICTIONS (registered before reading 166r or 167r)

  either Jeremiah's refusal ("you shall not enter Jerusalem ...") or the
  returnees' plea, with the last word governing "with our wives and our

  verb about the nations and the gospel way), then continue the Christian
  prophecy of 4 Bar 9 — Jeremiah's vision of the coming of the Son of God,
