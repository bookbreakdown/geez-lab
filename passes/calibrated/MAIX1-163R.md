## GIST
(The page opens mid-word: "...let him enter, be received and approach.") Unclean and [ስዑብ, sense unresolved] is he who gazes at a virgin and casts his eye so as to defile; and if he himself defiles his virginity, let him stand outside the church until he repents, and let him do as the elders of the church command him; and after he has completed his penance let him enter and be received and approach. [ስዑብ] and unclean is he who adorns himself in a man's finery so as to seduce another man's wife, or otherwise who makes himself out so as to fornicate with women who have no husband, harlots and widows: let him stand outside the church until he repents, and when he has completed what the elders of the church tell him, let him do it and enter and be received and approach. [ስኩብ] and unclean is everyone who eats... (the object is on the next page).

## NOTES
- Page is a run of three parallel disciplinary formulae: "X ወርኩስ ውእቱ ዘ... ይቁም እምቤተ ክርስቲያን እስከ ይኒስሕ ... ይባዕ ወይተከነስ ወይቀረብ". The three-fold repetition anchors most readings; the seams between lines were checked against the repeats (ll.1/11/22-23, ll.6-7/18-19, ll.8-9/20-21).
- **X = ስዑብ (ll.2, 12) vs ስኩብ (l.23).** Zoomed side by side: ll.2 and 12 have a bar-less U with right hook (ዑ); l.23 has the ከ top-bar plus right ring (ኩ), identical to ኩ in ርኩስ on the same line. Ink differs; the formula is the same word. Sense of ስዑብ unresolved (not ስኩብ "lying down"); editor to decide. Least certain lexical item on the page.
- l.4 **ባይኖ**: ink is a boxed በ-shape with a right leg, i.e. ባ. Sense wants ዓይኖ "his eye" (ወይውግር ዓይኖ, "casts his eye"). Same shape recurs l.18.
- l.18 **ወመማስበተ**: third glyph is a two-bowl መ-body with a right leg (ማ). Sense wants ወመዓስበተ "and widows" after ዘማተ "harlots". Two places on one page where an expected ዓ shows as a box/bowl + right leg: **possible new letterform fact — this scribe's ዓ may be angular, colliding with ባ/ማ.** Needs a third anchor before entering the key.
- l.4 superscript: a small hooked glyph with a dot, inserted above the line between ከመ፡ and ርኩስ. Written as [^እ]. Best reading is an inserted hooked alef making እርኩስ (= አርኰሰ "defile"), which is the form written in-line at l.5. Second choice ዖ/ዕ.
- l.3 **ያስተሓይፅ**: last glyph is a boxed body with an internal stroke and a right tick, unlike the rounded ጸ of ፈጸመ (ll.10, 20). Read ፅ; second choice ጽ. Scribe's ፅ-for-ጽ is already attested (ግብፅ). Standard would be ያስተሐይጽ.
- l.5 **ሰሊሁ**: ink ሰ (no foot seen). Sense wants ለሊሁ "he himself". ለ in ወለእመ on the same line is clearly a different shape, so this is not a ለ/ሰ collision; it is the scribe's spelling or a slip.
- l.3-4 **ወይ|ዉግር**: ዉ (ወ with lower-right ring) where standard is ው (ወይውግር).
- l.7 **እስከ፡ወይኒስ|ሕ** carries a ወ that the parallel at l.19 (እስከ፡ይኒስሕ) lacks. Ink is ወ, not ዘ.
- l.9 **ኒቤት**: glyph is the same ኒ as in ይኒስሕ (ll.7, 19). Expected ለቤተ. ቤት carries the tick (ት), not ተ.
- l.10 **ባር** (ይግ|ባር): ba with right leg; parallel at l.22 is ይግበር. Ink kept.
- l.10 **ድኀረ**: ኀ-order not clearly marked; written ኀ per key rule, editor supplies ኅ.
- l.13 **በሠርጐ**: labialised ገ with ring; second choice ጕ. Sense ሠርጐ "adornment" (ተባዕት follows: "a man's finery").
- l.14 ያስሓቱ, l.11 ንስሓሁ: ሐ carries a right-leg extension, read ሓ.
- l.22 **ወዩባዕ**: ይ with a right foot (ዩ); parallels ll.1, 11 have plain ይባዕ. Could be ይ with a flourish, or ዱ under the ይ/ደ collision; ዩ is the ink.
- l.22-23 **ወይ|ከነስ** lacks the ተ of ይተከነስ (ll.1, 11). Ink kept.
- Alef: all hooked, transcribed እ per key: እዘዝዎ l.8, እኮ l.15, እልቦን l.17, እምቤተ ll.6, 18. Editor to normalise.
- ። at ll.8 and 12 is written as two spaced ፡ pairs; kept as ።.
- Sadis for standard vowels throughout: ይተከነስ, ይቀረብ, ስዑብ, ይኒስሕ.
