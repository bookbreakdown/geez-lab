## WORK

Unidentified as to title. What the ink establishes:

- ደብተራ "tabernacle" occurs seven times (ll.1, 3-4, 5, 8-9, 11, 23-24), once as
  ደብተራ፡ ጽድቅ፡ ዘበሰማያት "the tabernacle of righteousness which is in the
  heavens" (ll.8-9; cf. Heb 8:2, 9:24) and once as ቅድስት፡ ደብተራ "the holy
  tabernacle" (ll.5, 23-24).
- ሙሴ "Moses" three times (ll.10, 12, 17); ደብረ፡ ሲና "Mount Sinai" (l.13);
  አምደ፡ ደመና "pillar of cloud" (l.14); "as He showed Moses the making of the
  tabernacle" (ll.10-12) = Ex 25:9, 25:40.
- ፵ መዓልተ ወ፵ ሌሊተ ... እክለ ኢበላዕኩ ወማየ ኢሰተይኩ "forty days and forty nights ...
  bread I did not eat and water I did not drink" (ll.15, 18-20) = Deut 9:9 /
  Ex 34:28, put into Moses' own mouth (ወይቤ ለሊሁ ሙሴ, l.17), with an added
  motive: "while I thirst for the judgement of God, how I should do as He
  commanded me in the holy tabernacle which He founded first" (ll.20-24).
- The frame is prescriptive (ll.1-9): a class of people (priests/Levites?)
  "shall honour all the [vessels] of the work of the holy tabernacle"
  (ll.4-5), "shall not remove and shall not depart from what is commanded in
  the law which God ordained for the tabernacle of righteousness which is in
  the heavens" (ll.6-9).

So: a canon-style prescription about the tabernacle and its vessels, backed
by an expanded first-person Moses narrative. Not 4 Baruch, not a Two Ways
text. The nearest genres are the church-order literature (Sinodos /
Testament of Our Lord / Qalementos, where the church is patterned on the
tabernacle Moses saw) and the Ethiopic tabernacle/Zion homilies. I cannot
name the work from this side alone; nothing here contradicts the "Qalementos"
label but nothing confirms it either. The Moses-speech expansion is not
canonical Exodus/Deuteronomy.

## NOTES

Unresolved ink, transcribed letter by letter, not smoothed:

- l.1, l.4 ንቀየ: read twice, identical both times. Second glyph is a vertical
  stem with two stacked bowls (ቀ), NOT the wide horizontal oval this hand uses
  for ወ/ዋ. Sense at l.4 (ኵሎ፡ ንቀየ፡ ግብራሃ፡ ለቅድስት፡ ደብተራ "all the ___ of the work
  of the holy tabernacle") demands ንዋየ "vessels", the Exodus formula. Either
  the scribe writes ዋ in a ቀ-like shape (new letterform fact if confirmed
  elsewhere) or the exemplar is corrupt. Flagged; ink says ንቀየ.
- l.1 ትእቢት: hooked alef; if the word is ትዕቢት "pride" it is a ዕ>እ spelling.
  "ንዋየ፡ ትዕቢት" makes no sense; the word may be something else. Unresolved.
- l.2 ደቀቡ: ደ/ይ collision; ይቀቡ "they anoint" is the only verb I can see. Low.
- l.2 ወደግለሠኢ: letters are ወ ደ(ይ) ግ ለ ሠ ኢ then ፡. The ሠ is three-pronged with
  no ring (not ሜ). The final glyph has the ኢ foot. No word found. Collect for
  the expert.
