## GIST

The page opens with the fig-tree saying (cf. Matt 24:32): when its branches
grow soft, know that its time for harvest has come. The fig tree has sprouted:
those who are called are the [good] ones who become martyrs by his hand, and
his angels receive them into joy, and not a hair of their head is lost. After
him Enoch and Elijah come down, preach, and put him to shame, the enemy of
righteousness and of his lie; then their heads are cut off, Michael and Gabriel
raise them and carry them into the garden of joy, and not a drop of his blood
falls to the earth. At the last the enemy, death, is abolished, and our Lord
comes from heaven to set up the covenant of the kingdom, and (a word I cannot
resolve, ወስለፋሪያኒሆሙ) the trumpet is sounded three times; Michael (continues
on the next folio).

## NOTES

Uncertain readings, in page order:

- L2 ለስ፡አእምሩ — the first two glyphs of line 2 are ለ ስ, completing ለበለስ
  ("of the fig tree") from line 1. Confident.
- L4 end: after እሙንቱ there is a black ፡, then a second ፡ group with faded red
  over it, then the final glyph. Two separate punctuation groups are on the
  page; this is NOT a decomposed ። and has been written as ፡፡ to preserve
  the count. The final glyph reads ኒ (vertical stem, top-left bar, bottom-right
  foot), giving ኒራን with line 5, which is not a word I know. Second choice:
  ኄ, giving ኄራን "the good ones", which fits the sense; but the ink does not
  show a ኀ bowl. Least certain reading on the page.
- L8 ወኢይትኃጐሉ — the ኢ has the short right-hand mark of this hand, not the
  long hook of ኤ (compare ኤ in L10 ወኤልያስ). Read ኢ.
- L8-9 ሥዕ|ርተ — line 8 ends ሥ ዕ, line 9 opens ር ተ: ሥዕርተ "a hair of".
- L9 ርእሰሙ — the third glyph has no ring; ink says ሰ, not the standard ሶ.
  Transcribed as written.
- L10 ጌኖክ — the first glyph has the flat top bar of ገ, extending left past the
  legs, not the rounded arch of ሀ (compare ሁ in L6, ሆ in L15/L23). Nonstandard
  for ሄኖክ; transcribed as written. Second choice ሄ.
- L12 መምዕለይ — five clear glyphs after the ፨, but the word is opaque to me.
  The third glyph is ዕ (ዐ with stem), not ስ (which in this hand is ሰ with a
  right hook). Ink transcribed as seen. Possibly a corrupt or unusual form of
  a word from ዐለወ "rebel". Uncertain.
- L13 ወጼይ — the second glyph is the ጸ tent (compare ጸ in L19) with a small
  hook at the lower right, so ጼ; the third is the ይ/ደ shape (see below).
  Uncertain whole word.

  a bowl, a foot to the right). L8 ወኢይትኃጐሉ fixes the shape as ይ; L18 and
  L19 have the same shape where the sense demands ደ (ደሙኒ "his blood also";
  ወለደኃሬኒ "and at the last also"). I have read ደ in L18-19 on sense and ይ
  elsewhere. Flag: L13 ወጼይ, L18 ደሙኒ, L19 ወለደኃሬኒ carry this ambiguity.
