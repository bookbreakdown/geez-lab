## GIST

The page opens mid-sentence in a question: "... did you not tell me, [our/my] Lord, that he would abolish the Gospel, and Paul, and Acts (ግብር), and all the books of the Law; did he not tell me that they would seek them out (ይኅሠሦሙ), their inheritance (ርስቶሙ?), for his books which he ordained first?" The reply: "But we (ንሕነስ) will not obey this word." Then a description of a persecution: those who [abandon?] their kin, their wives and their children, and flee into the mountains and into [caves?] and the pits of the earth, and hide themselves, "as Isaiah the prophet said: Go, my people, enter your house and hide a little while, until the wrath of the Lord passes" (Isaiah 26:20). But those who do not fear the terror of that king go to him and revile him (ወይጼርፉ፡ሎቱ), and their heads are cut off, and they become martyrs. "And at that time, so that what was said in the Gospel may be [fulfilled], and from..." (page ends mid-sentence).

## NUMERALS

None. There are no Ethiopic numerals on this folio. The only figure is the modern foliation "156" in pencil (Arabic numerals, top right), which is not part of the text. Every glyph on the page was checked against the numeral key; nothing matches ፲ ፳ ፴ ፵ ፯ ፰ ፻ ፩ or any other numeral form. No tens glyph to state a second choice for.

## CONFIDENCE

- Top third (lines 1-8): 0.70. The first two lines carry the hardest readings on the page (ሰዓቱ፡አዶ, the smudged start of line 2, ኢነግርከኒ), and lines 3-6 have two non-standard forms (ሦ for ኵ, ሦ for ሥዎ) that I read by shape.
- Middle third (lines 9-16): 0.80. Lines 10-11 and 14-16 are clear and the Isaiah quotation anchors them; line 9 opening ወሮ and line 12 ወበእኀቱ and line 13 ወይትኀግዑ are the weak spots.
- Bottom third (lines 17-24): 0.82. Clean ink throughout; doubts are confined to single letters (ተ/ት in line 21, ጼ in line 20, ስ in line 23, ዝ/ዘ and ሕ/ህ in line 24).

## NOTES

Letter forms established on this page (used to settle doubtful glyphs):
- ኢ in this hand: አ whose right leg ends in a left-turning curl. Confirmed by ኢሳይያስ (line 14). The same glyph is read as ኢ in lines 1, 5, 8, 18.
- ብ is a box (በ with a foot): confirmed by ብየ (line 15) and አብሔር (line 17). ዝ is an H with a curled top: confirmed by ሕዝ (line 14). These do not overlap, so ግብር (lines 3-4) is ብ, not ዝ.
- ጌ = ገ with a ring (line 24 ወንጌል); ጊ = ገ with a left foot (line 3). So line 3 reads ንጊል, a non-standard spelling of ወንጌል within four lines of the standard one.
- The ኀ-family glyph (top-left hook, stem, foot) is written identically in lines 9, 12, 13, 15, 16, 16-end and 20. The scribe does not visibly mark the order. I have written the base form ኀ everywhere except ኅዳጠ (line 16) and የኃል (line 16 end), where a small extra top mark seemed present; treat the ኀ/ኅ/ኃ choice in all seven places as editorial.
- ጳ in ጳውሉስ (line 3) is shaped exactly like the ኀ-family glyph above. I have written ጳ because the name is unmistakable; ink alone would give ኀውሉስ.

Uncertain readings, in order:
- L1 ሰዓቱ፡አዶ፡ — first glyph read ሰ (ለ with a top-left extension; a faint pencil/bleed mark sits above it). አዶ is read from the shape (rounded cup with a ring below) and makes no word I know; second choice ዖ/ዑ. The sentence is the tail of something begun on 155v.
- L1 ኢነግርከኒ — second choice ኢነግረከኑ ("did he not tell you?"), which would read better with L2 እግዚእነ as subject. The ር/ረ and ኒ/ኑ feet are both marginal.
- L2 እነ — the first glyph is smeared (ink blot); read as እ from the surviving outline. Word = እግዚእነ "our Lord" or, if the second glyph is ኒ, something else. Low confidence on the blot.
- L2 end ለወል + L3 ንጊል — the ink shows a cup-and-stem glyph (ል) at the end of line 2 and then ንጊል at the start of line 3, giving ለወልንጊል. Probably a false start on ወል(ድ) not cancelled, or the scribe treated ል as a line-filler. Transcribed as ink.
- L4 ሦሉሙ — the glyph has a three-pronged ሠ top with a ring at the base, i.e. ሦ. Standard would be ኵሉሙ/ኵሎሙ. Second choice ኵ. The same glyph opens L6.
- L5 ሕግ — the ሕ has a longer tail than the ሕ of መጻሕፍተ; second choice ሔ.
- L5 ኢነገሪኒ — ሪ read from a left foot on the ረ; second choice ኢነገረኒ.
- L5 ይኅሠ / L6 ሦሙ — ይኅሠሦሙ, apparently ይኅሥሥዎሙ with ሥዎ written as ሦ. The glyph between ይ and ሠ has a ን body with a top tick; second choice ን (ይንሠሦሙ, "take them away").
- L6 ርስቶሙ — ስ looks like ሰ (the hand does not mark the sixth order clearly on ሰ); ቶ is ተ with a ring.
- L7 ንሕኑ / L8 ስ — foot on the last glyph goes right (ኑ); standard would be ንሕነስ.
- L9 ወሮ — second glyph is ረ with a ring, ሮ. Not a known word after ነገር; possibly the tail of ነገር/ወሮ is a corruption of ነገሮሙ. Transcribed as ink.
- L9 ኀየኑ — read from shape; meaning unclear.
- L12 ወበእኀቱ — the ኀ glyph could alternatively be ጻ (ወበእጻቱ); the crossbar on እ is clear. Followed by ወግበበ (ግ+በ+በ, no በ before ግ).
- L13 ወይትኀግዑ — expected ወይትኀብኡ "they hide". The fifth glyph is not the box ብ of this hand; it is a ገ-body with a descender, read ግ. The last glyph is a cup with a right leg, ዑ. Transcribed as ink.
- L13 ። — the ink is two ፡ side by side (four dots in a row, not a 2x2 square). Written as ። per the rule; note the shape.
- L15 ቢተከ — በ with a straight descender (ቢ), not the ringed ቤ seen in ኀቤሁ (L20).
- L16 ዕ — line opens with a cup-with-tick glyph, ዕ, giving ወተኀባዕ for standard ወተኀባእ.
- L17 — line ends with a single ፡, not ።.
- L18 ተግርም / L19 ቱ — ተግርምቱ; ግ read by shape.
- L20 ወይጼርፉ — the fourth glyph is ጸ with a bottom-right ring, ጼ; second choice ፄ.
- L21 ወይተመተር — the second ተ is a plain cross with no visible sixth-order mark; standard ወይትመተር.
- L23 እሚረ — crossbar visible on the first glyph (እ not አ); ሚ read from a right-side descender on መ.
- L23 ይስጸሕ — ስ has a top tick; ጸ has a top-left mark that may make it ጻ.
