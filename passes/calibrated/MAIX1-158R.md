## NOTES

Uncertain readings, in order of doubt:

- l.1 first word "?ዩሪ": glyph 1 is an ink blot with a whitish flaked patch; nothing recoverable beyond a bowl with a hooked tail (ዲ/ዱ-like). Glyph 2 is the loop-with-foot form (ዩ, but the ይ/ደ collision extends to this order: ደ/ዱ possible). Glyph 3 is ረ or ሪ (foot goes right with a small up-tick). The join with 157v should settle this word; I have not read 157v.
- l.1 end / l.2 start "እኵ|ዩ ፡ ግብሩ": read as this scribe's ኵ (same top-right-hooked ከ form as ኵነኔ on l.2) plus ዩ; sense = እኩየ ፡ ግብሩ "did evil". Glyph could be ኩ rather than ኵ. l.2 glyph 3 is ረ with a detached right curl = ሩ, not ፋ as it looks at page scale.
- l.1 "እለስ": the ስ carries the sadis stroke; standard would be እለሰ. Transcribed as ink.
- l.3 "ሀልዩ": open-top ሀ (not ሐ), ል with inward foot; sense = ሐልዩ "consider". Another ሀ-for-ሐ spelling for the key.
- l.7-8 "እንቲ|እነ": = እንቲአነ "ours (fem.)", with the hooked alef. Stem-with-foot glyph is ነ, not ኑ (see letterform note).
- l.8 "መሐሪነ": expected መሐረነ "taught us". The glyph's foot runs right with an up-tick, i.e. this hand's ሪ; the same shape closes l.1's third glyph. Possibly the scribe's ረ. Transcribed as ink.
- l.10 "ዑቁ": single-bowl ዐ with right hook (ዑ), ቀ with small right appendage (ቁ). Not ውቅ.
- l.14 "ለእቡየ": alef is the hooked form, as everywhere in this hand; = ለአቡየ.
- l.16 "ቦሙ": the ink is a single stem with a top bar running right and a faded blob at the bar's end - it looks more like ሮ than a boxed ቦ. Read ቦ on sense (እለ ፡ ቦሙ ፡ ልቡና "who have understanding"). Lowest-confidence single glyph on the page. See letterform note.
- l.17-18 "ይነግ|ርሙ": C-form ር then ሙ, no ዎ. Either scribal omission for ይነግርዎሙ or a genuine ይነግርሙ. Transcribed as ink.
- l.18 "ነገሠት": three-pronged ሠ with no sadis marking, for ነገሥት. Consistent with the key's "sadis unmarked" rule.
- l.18-19 "ወመኳንን|ተ": ተ for ት at the line break.

New letterform facts about this hand:

- ነ vs ኑ: ነ is a stem with a short right foot; ኑ (l.12 የእምኑ) has a longer foot that curls up at the end. At page scale every ነ looks like ኑ. Anchors: ነ in l.5 ነገርናክሙ, l.10 ይቤለነ; ኑ in l.12.
- ኵ form: ከ-body with a hook at the top right of the stem (l.2 ኵነኔ, l.1 end). It is not the standard mid-stem loop.
- ዑ vs ው: ዑ is a single closed bowl with a right hook (l.4 እርቱዑ, l.10 ዑቁ, l.20 ይስምዑ); ው is the double-loop ወ with a hook (l.2 ውስተ, l.23 ይከውኖሙ). They do not collide once the loop count is checked.
- ቦ (l.16) may be drawn as an open form - stem, top bar, ring at the bar's end - with no right wall to the box. One instance only; treat as a hypothesis until a second ቦ turns up.
- ሪ/ረ: the ረ-family glyph has its foot running right and ending in a small up-tick in every instance on this page (l.1, l.8). Either the scribe's ረ carries this tick or he writes ሪ where ረ is expected; two instances both in ambiguous positions, so unresolved.
- Numeral bar: ፩ on l.11 carries a clear supralinear bar, so numerals are bar-marked in this hand, which helps separate them from letters.
- Page has 23 lines, one more than the 22 this key's sides typically show; count before cutting strips.
