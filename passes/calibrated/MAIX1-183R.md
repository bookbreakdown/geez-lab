## WORK

**A hortatory church-order addressed in the second person to the clergy, carrying
the FIVE-grade ladder in the ff.168-174 order.** Evidence from the ink, in
order of weight:

1. **Rank list, ll.1-3, five grades, fixed order:** ኤጲስ፡ቆጶስት (= ኤጲስቆጶሳት),
   ቀሰውስተ (= ቀሳውስት), ወዲያቆናት, ወንፍቀ፡ዲያቆናት, ወእናጉንስጢስ — bishops, priests,
   deacons, subdeacons, readers. That is the same set and the same descending
   order as f.174r ll.10-13 (ጳጳስ ... ንፍቀ፡ዲያቆናት ... ወአናጉንስጢስ ... ዲያቆ-). It is NOT
   the three-grade list of ff.179v and 181v, which stop at deacons. A sixth item
   follows at l.3-4 (ወሥሉ|ፍጸጣ), unresolved; if it is a rank it would be the
   rung below reader (singers or doorkeepers are what the Sinodos ladders put
   there), but the ink does not yield either word and I do not claim one.
2. **Person and mood:** second-person plural imperative and subjunctive
   throughout the first movement — ዕቀቡ (l.5), ትዕርጉ፡ወትንበሩ (l.7) — then a
   switch at l.21 to second-person SINGULAR: እንተስ፡ዕቀብ ... ከመ፡ትኩን፡ዐቢየ፡እንዘ፡ንዑስ፡
   እንተ ... ቀሲስ (ll.21-24), "but you, keep God's flock, so that you become great
   while you are small ... priest". No first-person "we command" anywhere on the
   page; no third-person jussive canon; no tariff; no numbered canon; no
   source-citation formula (no ይቤ, no በከመ፡ይቤ, no book named). So this is not the
   canon stratum of ff.176r-180v, and it is not a ruling with a penalty.
3. **Theme:** promotion and demotion through the ማዕርግ ("rank", 6 occurrences:
   ll.8, 11, 15, 20-21, plus ትዕርጉ/የዓርጉ/ይወርዱ "ascend/descend") governed by
   teaching (ይምህሩ, ምህሮ, ምህር: ll.12, 17, 24). The small who teach well become
   great and sit in the seat of rank (ll.9-12); those who do not teach the
   Christian people under their authority well (ll.16-19) go down into the lower
   rank (ll.19-21). This is paraenesis on clerical merit, not law.
4. **Vocabulary link to f.181v, which matters for the strata question:** 181v
   l.15 has ዕቀቡ፡መርዔት፡ክሙ addressed to ጳጳሳት፡ቀሳውስት፡ወዲያቆናት; this page has ዕቀቡ፡
   ሠርዓታ (l.5) and ዕቀብ፡መርዒተ፡ለእግዚአብሔር (ll.21-22). Same verb, same object
   (the flock), same 2pl address to a rank list, two leaves apart — but 181v's
   list has three grades and 183r's has five. Either the three-/five-grade
   split is a variation INSIDE one hortatory work (which would dissolve the
   stratum boundary drawn at 179v/181v vs 174r), or two works share a stock
   formula. The ink on this page cannot decide it; ff.182r-v can (see
   falsifiers). I record the link and do not pick.

What it is not: not Rest of the Words of Baruch, not the Two Ways homily (no
two-ways vocabulary), not the paradise catalogue, not a divination or
impurity canon, not the Didascalia (already excluded; and the subdeacon/reader
rungs are post-Didascalia in any case, as 174r's note says). Nothing on the
page names an apostle, Peter, Clement, or Christ speaking, so "Qalementos"
is neither confirmed nor refuted here.

Bishop-word: ኤጲስ፡ቆጶስ- here, ጳጳስ on 174r and 181v — per the brief this does not
track the strata and is not used as a marker.

## GIST

(Text begins mid-word.) "...(-g) to the bishops, priests, deacons, subdeacons,
readers and [unresolved] ... of our church. Keep the order of the holy church
so that you may ascend and sit in the high rank, you who teach (?) in
uprightness; and even while they are small they become great in the seat of
rank, the high ones who teach the good, straight way (and law?). Those who do
not act rightly for the church, even while [its high ones?] go up into the
rank which is lower (?), because they do not do well the teaching of the
Christian people who are under their authority — they for their part shall go
down into the lower rank. But you, keep the flock of God, so that you may
become great while you are small, by good teaching, O priest, and by..."

The two questioned clauses (ll.13-16) are where the unresolved word sits; the
overall sense — rise by teaching, fall by neglecting it — is not in doubt.

**Falsifiers.**
1. **(Testable now, against drafted 174r.)** The five-grade order here is
   bishop, priest, deacon, subdeacon, reader. 174r gives the same order
   (checked: 174r ll.10-13). If a later re-read of 174r moves the reader
   above the subdeacon, "same ladder" weakens to "same set".
2. **(Testable when 182r-v are drafted.)** If 182r-v run continuously from
   181v's 2pl "keep your flock" exhortation into this page with no rubric,
   no ። ። block-end, and no change of person, then 181v and 183r are ONE work
   and the three-grade/five-grade distinction is not a stratum boundary. If
   182r-v carry a canon with a tariff, or a rubric, or a first-person "we
   command" between the two, the boundary stands and the shared formula is
   stock phrasing.
3. **(Testable against 183v.)** If 183v opens with a first-person "we
   command", a numbered canon, or a penalty tariff, the "hortatory, not
   canon" reading of this page fails and this is a canon's preamble.
4. **(Testable against any drafted folio.)** Any occurrence of ንፍቀ፡ዲያቆን or
   አናጉንስጢስ on 179v-181v would already have broken the three-grade claim
   there; conversely, a THREE-grade list on 183v or 184r inside this same
   2sg/2pl exhortation would show the list length varies within a work and
   is not diagnostic.
5. **(Reading-level.)** l.9 ጣ: if a certain ማ elsewhere on this folio shows
   the identical flat-topped single-bowl form, my ጣ is a ማ and the loan
   hypothesis dies. l.13 ኢያረትዑ: a ring on the ረ at 4x would make it ሬ; there
