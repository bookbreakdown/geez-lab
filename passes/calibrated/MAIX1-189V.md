## WORK

Not fixable to either of the two established registers from this side alone,
and the ink says why: the page is LEGISLATIVE in content but HOMILETIC in
citation habit.

Evidence from the ink:

- Voice is first-person plural decree addressed to a plural "you":
  ወኢትባኡ (l.1), ወኢትኩኑ (ll.6-7), ዕበይዎሙ (l.10), እዘዝነ "we command" (l.17),
  ገሠጽዎሙ (l.21), እዝዝዎሙ (ll.23-24). The church is ቤተ፡ክርስቲያን፡እንቲአነ
  "our church", three times (ll.9-10, 14, 19).
- Penalty formula: ይኩን፡ምቱረ፡እማዕርጊሃ፡ለቀድስት፡ቤተ፡ክርስቲያን "let him be cut
  off from the ranks of the holy church" (ll.17-19). This is the same
  ማዕርጊሃ፡ለቤተ፡ክርስቲያን / እማዕርጊሃ register that tgrep finds on 182v ll.7, 10,
  23 - so this page sits in the same run as the rank canons of ff.182v-183r,
  not in the 160v-164r anathema run (no ርኩስ፡ወሰዑብ, no ርጉም፡ወውጉዝ here).
- Citation habit: ZERO formula markers. No በከመ፡ይቤ, no ወካዕበ፡ይቤ, no ይቤ at
  all on the page. Every scriptural echo is woven:
  1. ll.3-4 ወፍሬሆሙ፡ተአምርዎሙ - Matt 7:16/20 "by their fruits you shall know
     them" - woven, unmarked, in a causal clause (እስመ).
  2. ll.4-5 ነኪረ፡ትምህርተ "strange doctrine" - Heb 13:9 phrase - woven.
  3. ll.10-12 ለእለ፡ያመጽኡ፡ኀቤክሙ፡ካልአ፡ትምህርተ "those who bring you another
     doctrine" - 2 John 10 / Gal 1:9 - woven, unmarked.
  4. ll.23-24 ያክብርዎሙ፡ለመኳንንት "honour the rulers" - 1 Pet 2:17 / Rom 13:7
     - woven, unmarked.
  Order on the page: Matt 7 -> Heb 13 -> 2 John/Gal 1 -> 1 Pet 2. All four
  woven; none formula-marked.

Reading: if the VOICE/CITATION marker is the boundary criterion, this page
falls on the HOMILY side by citation and on the CANON side by content
(decree + penalty + "we command"). That is a third register - pseudo-apostolic
decree in the first person, the Sinodos/Didascalia "we the apostles command"
mode - and this page alone cannot say whether the codex treats it as canon or
as homily. ጴጥሮስ does not occur here either.

## GIST

Do not entertain strange teaching, for by their fruits you will know those who
teach a foreign doctrine within our own [church], over the whole church; do not
be half-hearted, lest you stray from the practice and ordinance of the holy
church. Reject those who bring you another doctrine not written in the law and
canon of the holy church; whoever does not stand by the canon ordained for the
holy church, we command that he be cut off from the ranks of our holy church.
And especially if they be kings and princes, admonish them away from every evil
that God your God does not approve; and command the people also to honour the
princes [and let them learn to love their neighbours - continues on 190r].
