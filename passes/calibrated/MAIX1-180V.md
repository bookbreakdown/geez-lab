## WORK

Canonical-homiletic stratum continuing ff.176r-179v, not Qalementos narrative
and not a rank list. Evidence from the ink:

- **2mpl prohibition addressed to clergy about penitents, ll.4-6:**
  ኢታብዕዎሙ፡ወኢታቀርብዎሙ፡ከመ፡ይፈጽሙ፡ንስሐሆሙ "do not admit them and do not
  bring them near, so that they complete their penance". Same register and
  same subject (admission/re-admission after penance) as the ይትከነስ tariffs
  on ff.176-179. Third-person jussive for the sinner, second-person plural
  negative imperative for the addressees. Not "we command".
- **Sin catalogue with sorcery, ll.7-13:** ዘይጼልዕ፡ቢጾ (hates his fellow),
  ወይብል፡...፡ከመዝ፡ግበሩ (says "do thus"), ይቀትል፡ነፍስ፡ካልኡ (kills another's
  life), ወይገብር፡ሠራየ (does sorcery, ሥራይ), ቀተሌ (=ቀታሊ, murderer), ምውተነ
  (=ምውታነ, the dead, l.12-13, possibly necromancy). This is the tail of the
  divination/sorcery canon that f.179v carries.
- **Verdict formula with a shepherd image, ll.13-18:** ዘይብል፡ወዘየእምን፡ከመዝ
  ኢኮነ፡መርዔተ፡ለእግዚአብሔር፡አላ፡ጸላዒሁ፡ውእቱ፡... ዘይዘራ፡አባግዓ፡መርዔቱ "he who
  says and believes thus is not of God's flock but His enemy, one who scatters
  the sheep of His flock, not knowing nor understanding (ኢያእሚሮ፡ወለቢዎ)
  what the Book itself says".
- **Two source-citation formulae, in this order:**
  1. l.18-19 ዘይቤ፡ለሊሁ፡መጽሐፍኒ "which the Book itself also says" ->
     quotation ending ...ለኵነኔ "to judgment" (l.20, not identified).
  2. l.21 ወካዕበ፡ይቤ "and again he said" -> a string of 2mpl perfects:
     ሰደድክሙ (you drove out / persecuted), ወ?ል?ክሙ, ወስቀ?ክሙ, ቀስፎክሙ (you
     scourged), ወተስለ?ኩ (ተሠለጠ, ruled/dominated). The cluster
     persecute + crucify(?) + scourge points at the Matt 23:34 family; the
     shepherd context and ተሠለጠ point at Ezek 34:4 (LXX "the strong you
     worked down"). Neither is confirmed from the ink; recorded as the two
     candidates, not as a reading. Note the citation is introduced as "he
     said", not "the prophet/the Gospel said".

What it is NOT: no rank list of any length on this page; no first-person
"we command"; no Levitical-impurity vocabulary (ርኩስ/ሰዑብ absent). Shared
vocabulary with the anathema formula (ርጉም/ውጉዝ) is absent too. The page is a
homily-shaped canon: sin list -> exclusion until penance -> proof texts.

## GIST

Whoever does these things has the scourge of God coming on him; the clergy
are not to admit such people or bring them near until they have completed
their penance. One who hates his fellow, teaches others to do the same,
kills, practises sorcery or deals with the dead is not of God's flock but
God's enemy, a scatterer of His sheep who does not know what Scripture says
about him; two proof texts follow, the second an indictment in the
second-person plural ("you drove out ... you scourged ... ruled").
