## GIST

The page opens mid-sentence: "...for the sake of the name, for the one who makes his heart firm and stands for him, so that he may make him inherit the kingdom and [union?] with those who receive the crown of the kingdom of heaven." Then: "And likewise let the deacons be after the pattern of Stephen, the first martyr of Christ; and moreover he saw the Holy Spirit descending upon the altar, within the inside of the veil." A damaged-sense passage follows: "And when he saw it descending upon [?]. And likewise the deacons [?] the [?] of the Holy Spirit [?] bread and cup in the church; the Holy Spirit descends." The page closes: "And likewise the deacons who have purified themselves will see, while the Holy Spirit descends upon the altar of the church, and [at that time] they find the wonder of the Most High, as Stephen the first [martyr]..." The last word, ቀዳሚ, runs onto 159v.

## NOTES

Uncertain readings, in order of doubt:

- l.1 opening: the first glyph is a smudged blot; the second is ሎ or ሦ (ring at top right, base possibly three-pronged). Transcribed ?ሎሑ. The 158v join will settle it.
- l.3 ኅመ: the glyph has the ኀ crossbar plus a top-left hook, i.e. ኅ, not this hand's ከ (see letterform note below). Sense wants ከመ ("so that he may make inherit"). Transcribed the ink; editor's call.
- l.4 ወድርገተ: second glyph is the ደ/ይ shape with a small top-left hook, read ድ; second choice ይ. ድርገት (root ደረገ, "join, unite") fits "union with those who receive the crown". Not a confident word.
- l.4 ይነሥኡ: the glyph after ይ is ነ-shaped with a right serif; could be ኔ. Sense (imperfect of ነሥአ) wants ነ. The ሥ may be plain ሠ; the left stroke is faint.
- l.13 ዓተፋጸየ: after ዲበ፡ comes the "9"-shaped bowl-with-descender glyph (this hand's ዐ/ዓ, see below), then ተ ፋ ጸ የ clearly. No Ge'ez word recognised. Second choice for the first glyph: ጌ (see collision note). Least certain word on the page.
- l.14 ይትፋጸዩ: letters clear (ይ ት ፋ ጸ ዩ, the ዩ with right foot), word unrecognised; same root as l.13.
- l.15 ጠርዩተ: ጠ ር then a ደ/ይ shape (read ዩ, second choice ደ) then ተ. Unrecognised; a purity word (ጽርየት, from ጸረየ) would fit "of the Holy Spirit" but the first glyph is ጠ, not ጸ.
- l.15-16 seam ይ|ዓ፡: l.15 ends with a bare ይ/ደ glyph; l.16 opens with the bowl-descender glyph, half-hidden at the gutter, then ፡ with red dots. Transcribed ዓ. Not a known word split.
- l.22-23 seam ወዓ|ዜ: ink is ወ + bowl-descender glyph, then ዜ (H with ring) ፡ ይረክቡ. Sense strongly wants ወጊዜ "and at that time"; if this hand's ጌ (ገ + ring) coincides with its ዓ, the reading is ወጌዜ for ጊዜ (cf. the key's ጌኖክ for ሄኖክ). Transcribed the ink as ዓ.
- l.6 ደያቆ|ናት, l.14, l.18-19: ዲያቆናት is written with the plain ደ/ይ glyph (no foot), so ደያቆናት. ት in ናት carries this hand's sadis foot (see below), not ቲ.
- l.11 ውስጢ: ጠ with right foot; standard would be ውስጠ.
- l.16, l.22 ቢተ for ቤተ: the glyph has a foot, not a ring, both times.
- l.19 እንጸሑ for አንጽሑ: hooked alef (key rule) and ጸ where ጽ is expected.
- l.20: single ፡ after ይርእዩ, not ።, though a sentence break reads better.
- l.7 margin: a small dark mark at the left margin beside ናት (not a letter, possibly offset ink).
- l.8 ዳ in ቀዳሚ has a heavy ink blot; ዳ is the only reading that fits ቀዳሚ.

Nonstandard spellings attested here (add to key): እክሊለ (አክሊለ), በእርእያ (በአርአያ), ደያቆናት (ዲያቆናት), እንጸሑ (አንጽሑ), ቢተ (ቤተ) x2, ውስጢ (ውስጠ), ብዓ for ብዐ (አጥብዐ), ኅመ apparently for ከመ.

NEW LETTERFORM FACTS for the key:

1. ዐ and ዓ are drawn the same: a closed bowl with a stem descending on the right, like a 9 or q. Anchors: l.2 ብዓ (where አጥብዐ demands ዐ) and l.9 ወዓዲ (demands ዓ). Treat ዐ/ዓ as undistinguished, like አ/እ. ዕ is distinct (bowl with a top-left tick: ስማዕቱ, ላዕተ, ዋዕ). ዑ is a bowl with a right downstroke (ልዑል l.23) and is hard to separate from ዐ/ዓ except by sense.
2. Likely collision ዐ/ዓ vs ጌ: ገ + ring would produce the same 9-shape. Two words on this page (l.13, l.22) read better with ጌ/ጊ than with ዓ. Not yet anchored; needs a page with a certain ጌ.
3. ት (sadis) carries a horizontal foot at bottom right; ቱ has a downward tick; ተ is bare. Do not read the footed form as ቲ. Anchors: ደያቆናት l.14, ይትፋጸዩ l.14, ክርስቲያን l.17 (ቲ there is footed too, so ት/ቲ collide on foot alone; disambiguate on sense).
4. ከ has a top hook curling right on the vertical (ኸ-like), no crossbar: ወከማሁ l.6, l.18; በከመ l.24. ኅ has the ኀ crossbar and a top-left hook: ኅብስተ l.16. They are distinguishable; l.3 ኅመ was read on that basis.
5. ዲ has a bottom-right foot (ዲበ l.10, l.13; ወዓዲ l.9); the bare ደ/ይ shape is used for both ደ and ይ and, in ደያቆናት, for ዲ.
6. ብ is the box (key confirmed); ኅብስተ and ብዓ.
7. ። is written as two spaced pairs (ll. 6, 12, 13, 18, 22), and once with red dots beside a single ፡ (l.16 opening). The red-dotted one is kept as ፡ since only one pair is inked.
