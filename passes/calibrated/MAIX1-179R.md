## WORK

Church-order canons in the first-person apostolic voice — the same work as
ff.176r–177v, not a new one. Evidence from the ink:

- **l.10 ዘእዘዝነ "which we have commanded"**: first-person plural. The
  legislators speak as a body. That is the Sinodos/Apostolic-canon register
  (the codex has already quoted "the Canons of the Apostles" by name on
  176r–177v). It is not Qalementos' frame, where Peter speaks to Clement in
  the singular, and not a homily.
- **Direct continuity with 177v.** 177v paired ስዕበት with ኢኮነ፡ንጹሐ "is not
  clean" and barred such a person from the church until cleansed. 179r
  ll.5–8 legislate the same case with the same root: ሰዑባን፡ዘእንበለ፡ይትሐፀቡ
  "[while] ሰዑባን, without having washed — if he enters the altar of God's
  house he defiles his holy things". Same rule, same vocabulary, same
  sanctuary-access concern as the 176r–177v canon on who may not enter the
  sanctuary.
- **ll.21–24 a catalogue of the excluded**: the demon-possessed (ጋኔን),
  diviners (ያስግሉ, root ስግል), two unplaced practitioner terms, and "by a
  star" (በኮከብ) — the magic/astrology exclusion list of the apostolic canons
  (the sorcerer/astrologer/enchanter clause found in the Canons of Hippolytus
  and the Apostolic Tradition family). The list, not the vocabulary, is the
  diagnostic.
- **What is absent**: no narrative, no dialogue frame, no "Clement" or
  "Peter", no rank list, no source-citation formula. Nothing on this page
  supports the catalogue's "Qalementos Book IV" label; nothing on it
  contradicts the apostolic-canon identification made for 176r–177v.

Citation order: one scriptural echo only, uncited — l.12–14 ወኵሉ፡ዘፀውዓ፡ስመ፡
እግዚአብሔር፡በንጹሕ፡ይደሓን "and everyone who calls on the name of God in purity
shall be saved" = Joel 2:32 / Acts 2:21 / Rom 10:13, with በንጹሕ "in purity"
inserted into the formula. No citation formula, no book named. No second
citation, so no order to record.

## GIST

A man who fornicates with a woman not his wife, or with beasts, and comes
while still ሰዑብ without washing: if he enters the altar of God's house he
defiles the holy things — this is what "we have commanded", because the
Spirit of God stands at every altar and whoever calls on God's name in purity
is saved. That Spirit sanctifies the altar and those who offer and pray in
purity; and it confronts those who defile the altar — the demon-possessed,
the [unplaced], those who [unplaced], diviners, those who [unplaced], and
whoever [works] by a star and ... (continues on 179v).

## NUMERALS

None. Every loop-like glyph checked for bounding bars; none has them. The
pencil "179" top right is the modern foliation, not ink of the hand.

## CONFIDENCE

- **First third (ll.1–8)**: high on ll.2–8 apart from three words. l.1 first
  word ንስበት is letters-only (ን/ኀ on the first glyph; word unplaced). l.3
  በበዕደን letters secure, word unplaced. l.4 በእንስስ (for እንስሳ) — final glyph
  has no right dash, kept as ink. l.5 ጌ vs ጹ decided on shape, see NOTES.
- **Middle third (ll.9–16)**: high. Weak spots: l.9 እምነ (word unplaced,
  ነ/ኑ undecidable); l.10 ነ chosen on sense ("we commanded"); l.15 ተወኪፈ
  letters-only. ኵ at ll.10, 12 secure by the top-right hook.
- **Last third (ll.17–24)**: high on ll.17–20; ll.21–24 carry the three
  unplaced words (ነገርጋር, ይትሜረዩ, ያጠዕው) — letters transcribed as drawn and
  not smoothed. ረ at ll.21, 23 chosen over ሬ/ሪ at 5x per the key's rule.

## NOTES

- **ሰዑብ — a third independent pairing.** l.5–6 ሰዑባን (plural) is followed
  directly by ዘእንበለ፡ይትሐፀቡ "without having washed", inside a canon on
  sexual defilement (adultery, bestiality) and sanctuary entry. With 177v's
  ስዕበት / ኢኮነ፡ንጹሐ and the 44 ርኩስ፡ወሰዑብ pairings, the field is now fixed at
  ritual defilement that ablution removes. The plural in -ān shows it is an
  adjective/participle, not an abstract. Etymology still for the expert; no
  Arabic root offered.
- l.1 ንስበት፡ንስሓሁ: ንስሓሁ "his penance" is the closing formula of the
  penitential canons (ff.160v–164r). The word before it, ንስበት, is unplaced;
  first glyph has the top-left tick and foot of ን, not the bowl of ኀ. If
  178v ends in a numeral this is the duration slot, but the scribe's letter
  order does not give ሰንበት, and it is not normalised.
- l.1–2 ሠራዕዩ: third glyph is the Y-with-foot that is ዩ/ዱ in this hand.
  Possibly a nonstandard ሥርዓቱ "its rule" (ወከመዝ፡ሥርዓቱ "and this is its
  ordinance"), but that requires two order changes; left as ink.
- l.3 በበዕደን: letters ዕ (ticked) and ደ/ይ secure. Sense wants ብዕድ "another
  (woman)", i.e. በብዕድ... with the ን unexplained. Flagged.
- l.5 በንጌሕ vs l.14, l.19 በንጹሕ. Three near-identical slots. At 4x the l.14
  and l.19 glyphs have the squared lower bowl with the leftward foot of this
  hand's ጸ (cf. ጼ in ይጼልዩ l.19) and a bottom-right hook: ጹ. The l.5 glyph
  is a plain 7-stroke (ገ) with a small bottom-right appendage: read ጌ,
  በንጌሕ "in the morning" (i.e. still unwashed by morning). Sense allows
  either; shape decided it. Flagged for the editor: if the l.5 glyph is
  ጹ too, the phrase is በንጹሕ and the sentence loses its time reference.
- l.8 ቢት for ቤት (key: ቢተ attested). ምሥዋዕ፡ቢት፡እግዚአብሔር = altar of the
  house of God, construct unmarked.
- l.8 እርኮሱ for አርኰሱ (key: ኮ for ኰ attested). l.9 ቀድሳቲሁ for ቅድሳቲሁ.
- l.9 እምነ: hooked alef, ም, then an I-beam. Word unplaced. Candidates:
  አመነ "confessed/acknowledged" (ወለእመ፡አመነ፡ኀጢአቱ "and if he confessed his
  sin") or እምነ "from". Neither smoothed into the text.
- l.10 ዘእዘዝነ: final glyph is the I-beam; no bounding bars, so ነ not ፲.
  Read ነ on sense (1pl "we commanded"); ኑ would be 3pl-object and is not
  excluded by the shape.
- l.14 ይደሓን for ይድኅን; ሓ per the key's right-leg-extension rule; second
  glyph ደ/ይ undistinguished, ደ chosen on sense.
- l.15 ሀየ for ህየ. ተወኪፈ: ተ (cross, no clear tick), ወ (two loops, no tail),
  ke-family glyph with a mid-right dash (ኪ), ፈ. Word unplaced; ተወከፈ
  "received" is the nearest, but the dash is in the ink.
- l.16 ዘይቄድስ, ll.17–18 ይቄድሶሙ, l.20 ይትቄበሎሙ: ring on ቄ visible at 3x on
  all four; D-stem imperfects, standard.
- l.21 ረኵሱ: the ኵ has the top-right hook (same form as ኵሉ ll.10, 12). The
  initial ረ has the bottom-left curl this hand gives every ረ; not promoted
  to ሬ. Sense: ይረኵሱ / (D) ይሬኵሱ "who defile" — the scribe's orders are loose.
- l.21–22 ዘእኀዘ፡ጋኔን "whom a demon has seized": ኀ order unmarked as usual.
- l.22 ወነገርጋር: letters ወ-ነ(I-beam)-ገ-ር-ጋ-ር with no separator inside.
  Unplaced. In a list of practitioners between "demon-possessed" and
  "diviners" it should be a class of magician; collect for the expert.
- l.22–23 ይትሜ|ረዩ: ሜ carries a ring (not the foot of ሚ); ረ plain; ዩ/ዱ
  undistinguished. Unplaced practitioner verb, tD-stem. Collect.
- l.23 ያስግሉ: ሰገለ "divine, practise astrology" — secure.
- l.23–24 ያ|ጠዕው: ጠ, ticked ዕ, two-loop ው. Unplaced. Collect.
- l.24 በኮከብ "by a star": astrology; the sentence runs onto 179v.
- No margin text; the left-margin grey column is the facing page's ghost
  offset, not ink of this side.
- **Arabic loans**: none claimed. ጋኔን, ሰገለ, ኮከብ are native Ge'ez. The four
  unplaced words are listed above without etymologies.

## JOIN PREDICTIONS (registered before reading 178v or 179v)

- **178v must end** inside a penitential canon, with the words immediately
  before ንስበት፡ንስሓሁ — most likely a duration (a numeral with bars, or
  ዓመት/ሱባዔ) or a conditional clause, and NOT with a sentence-final ።.
  Falsified if 178v ends with ። and a complete sentence, or if 178v is
  narrative rather than canons.
- **179v must open** by completing ወእመሂ፡በኮከብ፡ወ — a second astral or
  natural object (በፀሐይ, በወርኅ, or similar) — and then deliver the verdict
  on the whole list (a ban from the altar/church, or the ርኩስ፡ወሰዑብ anathema).
  Falsified if 179v opens with a new sentence, a rubric, or anything that
  does not take ወ- as its first letter's conjunction.
