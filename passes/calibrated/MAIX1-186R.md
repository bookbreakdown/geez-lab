## WORK

**Church-order canon, continuing ff.184v–185v.** Evidence from the ink, in
order of weight:

1. **Seam.** f.185v ends ወይ⟨ተ⟩?- in the clergy-return-from-synod passage. 186r
   opens ሠዩ ፡ ሎቱ. I cropped 185v's last line (crops/186R-seam-185v-tail.png):
   the disputed last glyph joins as ኃ, giving **ወይትኃሠዩ ፡ ሎቱ** — plural
   jussive of ተሐሠየ "rejoice" with the scribe's ኃ for ሐ — "and let them rejoice
   in him, because he saved them from the hardship of the journey and brought
   them to safety, praising God and thanking him." That is the end of the
   synod-return canon, and it is one sentence across the seam.
2. **Register.** ll.5–12 are third-person plural jussive ordinance (ወእሙንቱሂ ፡
   ይሠርዑ ፡ በሀገሮሙ "and they too shall ordain in their towns") over the whole
   category-list of persons (small/great, women, elders, children, servants,
   maidservants), then second-person plural address to clergy: ትምሕርተክሙ "your
   teaching", ኢትሤወሩ, ኢታሕፅፁ. That is the voice of ff.168–181 and 184v–185v,
   not of the homily block 181v–184r.
3. **Citation habit.** The one scripture on the page is introduced with the
   canon formula **በከመ ፡ ይቤ ፡ በወንጊል** (l.17). Corpus check: በከመ፡ይቤ has six
   prior hits, all in canon folios (156R, 157R, 164R, 168V ×2, 178R). The
   homily folios weave unmarked.
4. **Cross-witness inside the codex.** The same Matthew 18:10 text is on
   f.158r ll.10–14, near-verbatim (ዑቁ ፡ ኢታስተሐቀርዎሙ ፡ ለ፩እምእለ ፡ ንዑሳን … ዘልፈ ፡
   ይሬእዩ ፡ ገጾ ፡ ለእቡየ ፡ ዘበሰማያት), but there it is introduced ወይቤለነ "he said
   TO US" — first-person apostolic narration. Here it is በከመ ፡ ይቤ ፡ በወንጊል.
   Same verse, two citation habits, two works. That is the boundary marker
   holding a third time.

No Peter, no Clement on this page either. Nothing ties it to Qalementos.

## GIST

The clergy, back from the yearly synod, are to rejoice and thank God who
brought them safely home; and they in turn are to make provision in their own
towns for everyone — small and great, women, elders, children, servants and
maidservants, and for the sanctuaries of our church — so that their teaching
is spread like a gold-adorned curtain, hiding God's word from no one and
stinting neither small nor great. As proof the compiler cites the Gospel with
the canon formula: do not despise one of these little ones who believe in me,
for their angels always see my Father's face; not one sheep of these little
ones shall be lost — of those baptized in the holy church.

## NOTES

  f.185v pass left that glyph as ዳ/ኃ/ጓ; **this side settles it as ኃ**. The
  185v pass should be amended. Prediction registered there ("186r must open
  with the completion of a plural jussive") is met.
- **በሪዓይ (l.1)** — በ-ሪ-ዓ-ይ letter by letter, tick on ሪ real at 2.5x, ዓ the
  9-shaped bowl (not 7-shaped ጋ), final ይ/ደ undecidable by shape. Sense slot:
  "let them rejoice in him, in/by ___". A shepherd word from ርዕየ "to
  pasture" would fit the sheep imagery of l.23, but that is a guess and is
  not the ink. tgrep ሪዓይ and ራእይ: 0 hits each. Collect for the expert.
- **⟨ማ⟩ዓበራ (l.8)** — interlinear insertion of ማ (or ም) before ዓበራ, so the
  corrected word is ማዓበራ/ምዓበራ, or ዓበራ if the superscript belongs to ፍጻሜ
  (it sits after the ፡, so it does not). Slot: something the clergy "ordain
  … for all", followed by "for the sanctuaries of our church". Unplaced.
  tgrep ዓበራ: 0.
- **ዘእኑም ፡ በወርቅ (l.13)** — third glyph is the up-curling-foot form, so ኑ or
  ነ by the widened key rule; ም and ወርቅ certain. Slot: "a curtain that is ___
  with gold" (adorned/embroidered). No Arabic root claimed. tgrep እኑም: 0.
- **ኢታስተኃቅርዎሙ (l.18)** — proleptic object suffix before ለ፩, exactly as
  f.158r l.11 ኢታስተሐቀርዎሙ ፡ ለ፩. The ዎ is the two-loop mid-line form. Note

- **ንዑሳት (l.23)** — top-left tick and foot at 2.5x, so ት, not ን. Same word

  ፃ in ሕፃናት, ጸ in ተጸልዑ and ገጸ, ፅፁ in ኢታሕፅፁ. Treat every one as
