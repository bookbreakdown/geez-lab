## WORK

**Voice: first-person plural of a collective that "received the gift of God
after Moses" and "commands" a plural audience. Citation habit: everything is
woven, nothing is formula-marked. Verdict: apostolic church-order discourse of
the same stratum as ff.169r–170r (the tabernacle material), NOT the homily type
of ff.181v–188r, and NOT the penitential-canon type of ff.160–164 either. The
title cannot be fixed from one side; the stratum can.**

Evidence from the ink:

- **The speakers are "we" who stand after Moses.** ll.12–14 ወእምድኅሬሁ፡ንሕነ፡
  ነሣእነ፡ሀብቱ፡ለእግዚእብሔር፡እምላክነ፡ከመ፡ንዕቀባ፡ለቤተ፡ክርስቲያን፡ቀድስት "and after
  him WE received the gift of God our God, that we should keep the holy
  church". The antecedent of -ሁ is Moses (ll.3, 6). That is the apostles
  speaking as a body about their own commission — the same "we" that 169r
  l.17 uses in the same lexical cluster (በስማያት።እስመ፡ንሕነ፡, tgrep) and that
  171v l.9 uses in ቀኖና፡ዘእዘዝናክሙ "the canon which we commanded you".
- **The audience is "you (pl.)" and is commanded, not preached at.** l.19
  ግበሩ፡ሠርዐተ "do the ordinance" (imperative pl.), and the seam: 189r ll.1–4
  open ሙ፡እእኃዊነ፡ፍቁራን፡እለ፡እፍቀርክሙ፡ሠርዐተ፡ለቀዱስት፡ቤተ፡ክርስቲያን፡ከመ፡ታጽንዑ፡
  ዘእዘዝናክሙ፡ቀደመ "…you, O our beloved brethren who loved the ordinance of
  the holy church, that you hold fast what WE commanded you before"
  (crop `188V-seam-189R-head.png`). ዘእዘዝናክሙ is the canon-folio phrase
  (171v l.9), and ሠርዐተ recurs across the seam.
- **Zero citation formulae.** No ይቤ, no በከመ፡ይቤ, no ወካዕበ፡ይቤ, no ወይቤለነ
  anywhere on the side. Yet the side is dense with scripture, all of it
  absorbed into the speakers' own syntax:
    1. ll.5–11 Ex 25:9, 25:40 (the tabernacle shown to Moses, "the pattern"
       እርእያ, "according to what is in the heavens"), read through Heb 8:5
       ("the pattern of the heavenly things") — woven.
    2. l.16 Acts 20:28 ("the church … which he acquired with his own blood")
       + Eph 5:25–27 (the church chosen and sealed by Christ) — woven; ink has
       እኀሪያ፡በደሙ፡ወእተባ፡በእፀ፡መስቀሉ "chose her by his blood and sealed her with
       the wood of his cross".
    3. ll.20–23 Heb 8:5 / 10:1, Col 2:17 ("a shadow of the good things to
       come"): ትሂይስ፡እምደብተራ፡እስመ፡ይእቲ፡እርእያ፡ወፅለሉታ፡ለእንተ፡ተመፀእ፡ሐዲስ፡ሕግ
       "she is better than the tabernacle, for that [tabernacle] is the
       pattern and shadow of the new law that was to come" — woven.
  Order of citations on the page: Exodus → Acts/Ephesians → Hebrews/Colossians.
- **This is the 169v–170r argument resumed.** 170r l.15 has ደብተራ፡እርእያሃ፡
  ለቅድስት፡ "the tabernacle, her pattern, of the holy [church]"; 169v has ደብተራ
  seven times with Ex 25:9/40 and Heb 8:2. 188v takes the same equation
  (tabernacle = pattern/shadow, church = the reality) and draws the conclusion
  "keep the church, do the ordinance". Nothing between 171r and 188r uses ደብተራ
  at all (tgrep: 169v, 170r, 177v only). So the compilation returns here to
  a work it left at 170r, or the same work was split by the compiler.
- **What it is not.** Not the exhortation type of 181v–188r: 187v/188r have
  ዑቁ, -ክሙ warnings, renunciation lists, no ranks. 188r's LAST lines, however,
  are a ranks list (…ደብ]ተራ፡… ወደያቆናት፡ወንፍቀ፡ደያቆናት፡ወአናጉንስጢስ፡… ወእንስቲ፡ —
  crop `188V-seam-188R-tail.png`; the 188r pass reads ll.21–24 ተራ፡ቀሰውስት፡
  ወዲያቆናት / ወንፍቀ፡ዲያቆናት፡ወእናጉን / ስጢስ፡እምስለ፡ሊቀውያኒ። / ወእሙንቱኒ፡እድ፡ወእንስት፡),
  i.e. the transition into this church-order
  material happens on 188r itself, and 188v l.1 ወሐፃናት "and the children"
  closes that list. Not the penitential-canon type of 160–164 either: no
  anathema formula, no ርኩስ፡ወሰዑብ, no ይትከነስ.
- **Consequence for the boundary marker.** This side has canon-type VOICE
  (apostolic "we", "what we commanded you", imperative ordinance) with
  homily-type CITATION HABIT (woven, zero formulae). The two markers split
  here. That is not fatal to the marker — it says the formula habit belongs to
  the penitential-canon folios specifically, not to everything spoken in the
  apostolic voice — but it means "formula = canon, woven = homily" is too
  coarse by one stratum: there are at least three habits in the codex, and
  the tabernacle/church-order stratum (169r–170r, 188r–189r) is the third.
- Neither Peter nor Clement is named. Consistent with the zero count.

## GIST

"…and the children, with the children of Israel. And for this reason Moses
ordained that the chiefs of the priests be commanded, for God ordained in that
night that they give Moses the tabernacle of the law, and he showed him the
making of the tabernacle, that whatever he commands they should do in it,
everything that is required for it, according to what is in the heavens, the
pattern of what is in heaven, which the angels do rejoicing. And after him we
received the gift of God our God, that we should keep the holy church, which he
chose by his blood and sealed with the wood of his cross, so that she may be
the [expiation] of sins for those who believe in her. Do the ordinance, for she
is better than the tabernacle; for that one is the pattern and the shadow of
the new law that was to come. She is it — the holy church. And those who …"
