## WORK
Unidentified as a titled work, but the genre is clear and continuous with
ff.168-169v: a church-order / catechetical discourse on the sanctity of the
church building, arguing by a catena of scripture that the church is the holy
ground, the mourning woman, Zion, and the golden lampstand. Three citations are
in the ink:

- **Exodus 3:5** (= Joshua 5:15), ll.2-4: ክለዕ፡እሠዕኒክ፡እምእግሪክ፡እስመ፡ምድር፡ኀበ፡
  ትቀውም፡ቅድስት፡ይእቲ "take off your sandals from your feet, for the ground where
  you stand is holy" - and the homilist glosses it: ቤት፡ክርስቲያን "[it is] the
  church". The verb ክለዕ is NOT the Ge'ez Bible's ፍታሕ; it is Arabic خلع (iḫlaʿ,
  "take off [shoes]"), the very verb of the Arabic Ex 3:5. Evidence of an Arabic
  Vorlage, matching the key's ይትከነስ.
- **4 Ezra 9:38-10:27** (the mourning woman who becomes a city), ll.6-11:
  ወዕዝራኒ፡ርእያ፡በእምሳለ፡ብእሲት፡ዓዝንት፡ወተክዝት፡እንዘ፡ተበኪ፡በእንት፡ደቂቃ፡ወሱበ፡ርእያ፡
  ኢኮነት፡ብእሲት፡እለ(=አላ)፡ሀገር፡መንፈሳዊ፡ጽዮንሃ "and Ezra too saw her in the likeness
  of a woman grieving and sad, weeping for her children, and when he saw her
  she was not a woman but a spiritual city, Zion" (cf. 4 Ezra 10:44 "the woman
  you saw is Zion").
- **Zechariah 4:2-3, 11-12**, ll.12-16: ማኅትዊሃ "her lamps", እዕፁቀ፡ዘይት፡እምየማና፡
  ወእምፀጋማ "olive branches on her right and on her left", ወዘከርያስሂ፡ርእየ፡ተቀዋመ፡
  ማኅቶት፡ኵለንታሃ፡ዘወርቅ "and Zechariah too saw the lampstand, all of it gold".
- ll.19-24 then allegorise: her pillars of gold are the apostles (cf. Gal 2:9
  "pillars"), firm ከመ፡ዕምድ፡ጽኑዕ "like a firm pillar" ... በኀበ፡ነገሠት፡ወመኳንንት
  "before kings and governors" (Matt 10:18 language).

So: the same apostolic church-order discourse as ff.168-169v, here in a section
on the church as holy place. Not 4 Baruch, not Qalementos Book IV as catalogued.

## GIST
"...is holy. And he said to you(?): take off your sandals from your feet, for
the ground on which you stand is holy - it is the church. When they say 'take
off your sandals from your feet': Ezra too saw her in the likeness of a woman
grieving and sad, weeping for her children, and when he saw her she was not a
woman but a spiritual city, holy Zion, [with] her lamps and her ?, [and] olive
branches on her right and on her left. Zechariah too saw the lampstand of the
lamp, all of it gold; and it too, all of her, her ?(bridegroom?), is the
lampstand of the lamp that was spoken of, and her pillars of gold, ?, which
the apostles are called(?), they whose right hand (or: faith) is like a firm
pillar in all that ?pleased them before kings and governors and in all ..."

## NOTES
Least certain readings, letter by letter:
- l.1 ስት፡ውእቱ፡እስት፡ወደቢለኪ - ስት completes a word from 170r (ቅድ/ስት likely, since
  ቅድስት ውእቱ "it is holy" fits the argument; ውእቱ is masc. so the antecedent is
  not ቤተ ክርስቲያን directly). እስት will not resolve: hooked alef, ሰ sadis, ት with
  tick - not እስመ, not እንተ. ወደቢለኪ: ደ/ይ collision; ቢ has a foot-tick not a ring
  (ቤ in l.4 ቤት has the ring, so ink is ቢ); sense wants ወይቤለኪ/ወይቤሎ "and he said
  to her/him" but the ink does not give ሎ. Same glyph pattern recurs l.20 ዘደቢሉ
  (= ዘይቤሉ/ዘይብሉ "which they say/call"). Treat ደቢ- = ይቤ- as this scribe's spelling,
  pending the editor.
- Line-final glyph of ll.1, 9, 16: box-ke with a right descending leg ending in
  a tick. In l.9 it is certainly ኪ (ትበኪ, 3fs of በከየ). Read ኪ in ll.1 and 16
  too; ኬ (enclitic "then, indeed") would fit l.16 ወውእቱኬ well and the tick/ring
  distinction is below the resolution of the scan. ኒ is excluded: this hand's
  ኒ (l.2, l.6, l.14) is a narrow ነ with a right dash, a different silhouette.
- l.2 ክለዕ: box ከ with foot, ለ, ዕ. Not a Ge'ez verb; Arabic khalaʿa "take off
  (footwear)". Recurs l.5. Add to the unplaced-vocabulary list with the Arabic
  proposal; do not smooth to ፍታሕ.
- l.6 ዕኒ?፡እምግሪክ - a small glyph between ኒ and ፡, roughly the height of a
  punctuation stroke; probably a cramped ከ (the parallel in l.2 is ዕኒክ) but
  transcribed as ?. Note also እምግሪክ here vs እምእግሪክ in l.2: the scribe dropped
  the alef on the second citation.
- l.8 ዓዝንት፡ወተክዝት - sense ሕዝንት፡ወትኩዝት "sorrowful and sad" (fem. participles);
  ink has ዓ (9-bowl) for ሐ and ክ sadis for ኩ. Nonstandard, attested pattern.
- l.9 ወሱበ vs l.5 ስበ (both = ሶበ "when"): l.9 has a right-foot on the ሰ, l.5 does
  not. Recorded as ink.
- l.10 ኢኮነት: ኢ with the key's left-curling leg (confirmed); the ኮ has its ring
  at the TOP right of the stem - i.e. the same position the key gives for ኵ.
  So ኮ and ኵ may collide in this hand; decided on sense (ኢኮነት "she was not").
- l.10 እለ = አላ "but" (alef undistinguished, ላ written ለ).
- l.11 ጽዮንሃ: the ሃ attaches to ጽዮን with no ፡; accusative -ሃ. ቅድስቲ with a ቲ
  (mid dash) rather than ት.
- l.12 ማኅትዊሃ = መኃትዊሃ "her lamps"; the ኀ carries the top-left curl (ኅ). ወመስውሪሃ
  unresolved: መ ስ ው(two loops) ሪ ሃ - possibly the Zech 4:2/12 "pipes/funnels"
  word; collect for the expert.
- l.12-13 ዘእ/ቢ: hooked alef then ቢ across the line break. Unresolved; ዘ + ?
  before እዕፁቀ (=አዕፁቀ) ዘይት.
- l.15, 17, 22, 24 ኵ: the glyph looks like ሥ (three-pronged with a stem) at 2x
  and 3x. Read ኵ on sense (ኵለንታሃ twice, በኵሉ twice). NEW KEY FACT: ኵ/ሥ
  silhouette collision; disambiguate on sense.
- l.17 መርእዊሃ: መ ር hooked-alef ዊ ሃ. Not resolvable from the ink. If the alef
  stands for ዓ, መርዓዊሃ "her bridegroom" (Christ as bridegroom of the Church)
  fits the allegory; flagged, not adopted.
- l.17-18 ተቀ/ዋመ: line-18-initial glyph is a ring on a stem with a small left
  foot - ቃ in shape, ዋ by sense (ተቅዋመ "lampstand", spelled ተቀዋመ in l.15 with a
  clear ዋ). Recorded as ዋ; the shape should be checked against other ዋ/ቃ.
- l.19 ጸዕልት: ጸ ዕ ል ት - no Ge'ez word found; between ዘወርቅ and ዘደቢሉ፡ሐዋርያት.
  Left as ink. Also እዕማዲሃ = አዕማዲሃ "her pillars".
- l.21 የማኖሙ: literally "their right hand" (የማን + ኦሙ), which parses; or a
  clipped ሃይማኖቶሙ "their faith". Ink supports the first; sense favours either.
- l.22-23 ዘእሠ/መርዎሙ: ዘ hooked-alef ሠ / መ ር ዎ ሙ. ሠመረ "be pleased" plausible
  (ዘአሠመርዎሙ), or ዘኢሠመርዎሙ if the alef is ኢ (no curl seen). Unresolved.
- l.24 እደሁ: hooked alef, ደ/ይ, ሁ (ሀ with right leg). Word continues on 171r.
- Punctuation: ። at ll.5, 16, 23 is written as two ፡ with red dots around it
  (l.5 and l.16 in the red-cross form; l.23 plain). Kept as ።. Red ፡ dots also
  after l.16 ዘወርቅ. A superscript mark sits over the line-final ዓ of l.7 and the
  line-final በ of l.8 (margin, possibly bleed-through, not a correction).
- Orthography, all consistent with the key: ክ/ከ sadis for -ከ suffix (እሠዕኒክ,
  እምእግሪክ), ስበ/ሱበ for ሶበ, እምሳለ for አምሳለ, ዘከርያስ for ዘካርያስ, እመንቱ for እሙንቱ,
  ነገሠት for ነገሥት (already in key), ተቀዋመ for ተቅዋመ. ቅድስት in l.4 DOES carry a
  left foot on the ቀ (ቅ), unlike the key's ቀድስት - so this scribe marks ቅ at
  least sometimes.
