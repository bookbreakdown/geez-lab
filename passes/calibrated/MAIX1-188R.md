five lines' worth), as on 187v. Folio number "188" in a modern hand, top right.
A hole in the leaf sits between ll.8 and 9 near the centre (under ትሙ of l.8);
no ink is lost to it.

Notation: [⊗] = a glyph cancelled by crossed diagonal strokes (l.2). ⸢ ⸣ =
interlinear superscript insertion by the scribe (l.12). The hooked alef is
transcribed እ throughout per the key, including where sense wants አ/ኦ
(እላ, ለእረሚ, ለእቡክሙ, እብሔር, እሮን, እናጉንስጢስ, እንስት, እድ).

## WORK

**Voice: first-person-plural legislators ("we ordained you", three times)
addressing "you" (pl.) who are themselves ordainers of a clergy below them.
Citation habit: three scriptural echoes, all WOVEN, none formula-marked. Zero
ይቤ on the page. Verdict: it belongs with the woven/exhortation type of 187v
(with which it joins directly) and cannot be assigned to a named work from
this side.**

Evidence from the ink:

- **Seam with 187v confirmed from my own reading.** 187v ends ወፈድፋደሰ። "and
  above all"; 188r l.1 opens ትዓቀቡ፡እምዝሙት "keep yourselves from fornication".
  The ት of ትዓቀቡ carries the top-left tick, ዓ is the 9-bowl, ቡ has the right
  foot. The cropping agent's reading stands. So the blank foot of 187v is a
  scribal gap, not a work boundary.

- **The speakers are the ordainers.** ሠራዕናክሙ "we ordained you" (l.15, and
  l.11–12 with the ና restored superscript), ወካዕበ፡ሠራዕነ፡ሊቃነ፡ቢተ፡ክርስቲያን "and
  again we ordained the heads of the church" (ll.13–15), then ወእንትሙኒ፡ሠርዑ፡
  ለእለ፡እምታሕቲክሙ "and you too, ordain those who are under you" (ll.16–18),
  "as God ordained Moses and Aaron …" (ll.18–19). That is a chain
  apostles → bishops → lower clergy, in the apostles' own "we". tgrep shows the
  same "we ordained" at 164V l.13 (በከመ፡ሠራዕነ፡በቀዳስት፡ቢተ), inside the canon
  range, so the apostolic first person is shared across both types and is NOT
  itself a boundary marker. The person of the speaker does not separate the
  types; the citation habit still does.

- **ወካዕበ occurs but with ሠራዕነ, not ይቤ (ll.13–14).** Every other ወካዕበ in the
  64 folios introduces ይቤ (170R l.20, 180V l.21, 185R l.10). Here the same
  connective introduces the legislators' own act. That is a direct instance
  of the two habits: the canon folios say "and again HE said", this page says
  "and again WE ordained".

- **Citations, in order, all woven and unmarked:**
  1. ll.9–10 ኵሉ፡ብእሲ፡ይንበር፡በብእሲት፡ወብእሲትኒ፡ትንበር፡በምታ "let every man dwell
     with a wife, and the woman too dwell with her husband" — 1 Cor 7:2 shape,
     no marker.
  2. ll.12–13 ከመ፡ትኩኑ፡ውሉደ፡ለእቡክሙ፡ዘበሰማያት "that you may be children of your
     Father who is in heaven" — Matt 5:45 verbatim shape, no marker, and it is
     made the purpose clause of "we ordained you".
  3. ll.18–19 በከመ፡ሠርዖሙ፡እግዚእብሔር፡ለሙሴ፡ወለእሮን "as God ordained Moses and
     Aaron" — Exodus/Numbers allusion, no marker.

- **Rank list (ll.20–23): elders, [ሸደቅተራ], priests, deacons, subdeacons,
  readers, with the [ሊቀውያኒ]; then "they too, men and women" (l.24).** tgrep
  finds the same list on 174R ll.12–16 (ንፍቀ፡ዲያቆናት, ወአናጉንስጢስ, and the
  እድ … አን[ስት] pair) and 183R l.2. So rank vocabulary sits in both types, as
  already established; it does not place the page.

- **Neither Peter nor Clement is named.** No name of any kind on the side.

What cannot be fixed from one side: whether the "we" here is the apostles of a
Didascalia/Sinodos-type church order (the ordination chain and the Moses–Aaron
model both fit that) or the same preacher-"we" of 187v/158r. The join with
187v is continuous, so whatever 187v is, this is.

## GIST

Keep yourselves from fornication and from [approaching] another man's wife; all
that belongs to the pagans who have no law but are a law to themselves, a law
that is not true, and all their ways are error; but you — let every man live
with a wife and every woman with her husband, and be firm as we ordained you,
so that you may be children of your Father in heaven. And again we ordained the
heads of the church, as we ordained you yourselves [in our presence]; and you
too ordain those below you, as God ordained Moses and Aaron and [ጸቂቁ] and the
elders, [ሸደቅተራ], priests, deacons, subdeacons and readers, together with the
[ሊቀውያኒ]; and they too, men and women, …

## CONFIDENCE

Upper third (ll.1–8): HIGH. Every word resolves on sense except two: the word
straddling ll.1–2 (ዢ|ሬ, see notes) and the cancelled glyph + ኁ at the end of
l.2. ኢኮነ ×2, ለክርስቲያን, ለእረሚ, እለ፡እልቦሙ፡ሕግ, ለሊሆሙ, ፍናዊሆሙ, ስሕትት, ወእንትሙሰ all
read cleanly.

Middle third (ll.9–16): HIGH on ll.9–15 (the 1 Cor 7:2 pair, the ሠራዕናክሙ
sentences, Matt 5:45, ወካዕበ፡ሠራዕነ, ሊቃነ፡ቢተ፡ክርስቲያን). MEDIUM on l.16 በኀቢነ.

Lower third (ll.17–24): MEDIUM. The frame (ሠርዑ፡ለእለ፡እምታሕቲክሙ, ሠርዖሙ፡እግዚእብሔር፡
ለሙሴ፡ወለእሮን, ወዲያቆናት, ወንፍቀ፡ዲያቆናት, ወእናጉንስጢስ, ወእሙንቱኒ፡እድ፡ወእንስት) is secure;
three words are NOT resolved and are transcribed letter by letter: ወለጸቂቁ
(l.20), ሸደቅ|ተራ (ll.20–21), ሊቀውያኒ (l.23).

## NOTES

Unresolved, transcribed letter by letter — do not smooth:

- **l.1–2 ወእምዢ|ሬ፡ብእሲት፡ብእሲ.** Last glyph of l.1 is the H-with-curled-top ዝ
  body carrying a small lower-right foot (ዢ or ዥ; 4x crop `188R-L1-lastword`).
  First glyph of l.2 is ረ with an attached dark-ink appendage at lower right
  (ሬ; not a red dot, 4x crop `188R-L2-firstword`). The slot is a verbal noun
  governing "another man's wife": tgrep gives the exact collocation at 167R l.5
  ወሐዊረ፡ብእሲት፡ብእሲ "going in to a man's wife". So the sense is fixed
  (adultery), the word is not. Collect for the expert.
- **l.2 እስመ፡[⊗]ኁ.** One glyph cancelled with crossed DIAGONAL strokes, not the
  three horizontal strokes the key records for deletion — a second deletion
  form for this hand, or a different corrector. The following glyph is an
  ኀ-body with right foot: ኁ, or ን by the key's ን-reads-as-ኀ rule. Nothing in
  ዝንቱ፡ኵሉ (l.3) needs it; it may be a false start on ኵሉ. Transcribed as inked.
- **l.9 ይንበር, l.10 ትንበር.** The second glyph in both is the footed ኀ-shape;
  read ን on the key's rule and on the parallel (each verb governs በ- "with").
  ይ/ደ decided on sense (jussive). l.9 ends በብእሲት፡ with ticked ት, where the
  parallel በምታ wants በብእሲቱ; transcribed as inked.
- **l.12 ዕ⸢ና⸣ክሙ.** Superscript ና between ዕ and ክ; l.15 has ሠራዕናክሙ inline,
  which settles the insertion.
- **l.12 ትኩኑ, l.24 እሙንቱ.** ኑ/ቱ by morphology (2mp jussive; independent
  pronoun), not by the foot.
- **l.14 ቢተ for ቤተ** — the 17th attestation (tgrep ቢተ፡ክር = 16 prior hits).
- **l.16 በኀቢነ.** በ + footed ኀ/ን-shape + ቢ (right dash, not ring) + I-beam ነ.
  Best sense-reading በኀቤነ "with us / in our presence" with ቢ for ቤ as in ቢተ;
  tgrep ኀቤ finds six ኀቤ- forms in the codex, none spelled ኀቢ. Flagged.
- **l.18 ሠርዖሙ.** Fourth glyph is the 9-bowl with descender; ዖ/ዕ order not
  confidently marked. "he ordained them" fits; ሠራዕ- forms elsewhere on the
  page use ራ.
- **l.20 ወለጸቂቁ.** After ጸ, two ቀ-family glyphs (ring on stem, 4x crop
  `188R-L20-w1`); the first has a lower-right dash. Zadok (ጻዶቅ) and Eleazar
  were tested as the third name after Moses and Aaron; neither matches the
  ink, and tgrep ጻዶቅ = 0. Left as inked.
- **ll.20–21 ሸደቅ|ተራ.** First glyph read ሸ at 2x and 4x but not securely; ደ
  and ቅ then ተ (no tick) ራ. A rank term between "elders" and "priests". No
  match to ደብተራ (would need a box ብ where a ቅ-like glyph stands). Collect
  for the expert.
- **l.21 ቀሰውስት** for ቀሳውስት; the second glyph is three-legged ሰ here, not the
  two-legged ላ the key records for 184v.
- **l.23 እምስለ** as inked (እም + ስለ, or ወምስለ with the hand's alef for ወ?).
  **ሊቀውያኒ**: ሊ, ring-on-stem ቀ, two-loop ው, ያ, footed ኒ/ን. Not ሌዋውያን
  (Levites): the second glyph is ቀ, not ዋ. Possibly a scribal plural ሊቃውያን
  on the ወለዶያን pattern. Collect for the expert.
- **l.24 እድ.** Footed ድ (the promoted rule; reads ዮ at 2x, `188R-L24-left`).
  Sense ዕድ "men" paired with ወእንስት "women", the same pair tgrep finds at
  174R l.16 beside the same rank list.

Key rules used: footed ድ (l.24), ን-reads-as-ኀ (ll.9, 10, 16, 22 ንፍቀ), ጉ/ግ as
ተ-with-foot (l.6 ሕግ; l.5 ሕግ has the 7-shaped form instead — two ግ forms on one
page), ኢ as alef with left-curling leg (ll.3, 6), numeral bars (none present),
ቢተ orthography, hooked alef = እ.
