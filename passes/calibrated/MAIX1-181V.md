## WORK

A pastoral exhortation on penance addressed in the second person plural to a THREE-grade clergy, with an embedded first-person divine speech. Not a tariff canon and not a rank-ladder canon. Evidence from the ink, in order of weight:

1. **Rank list, l.15-16: እጳጳስት፡ቀሳውስት፡ወዲያቆናት** = (ኦ)ጳጳሳት፡ቀሳውስት፡ወዲያቆናት, "O bishops, priests and deacons". Three grades, vocative (the key's rule: vocative ኦ is written as the plain hooked alef). No subdeacon, no reader. This is the SAME three-grade stratum as f.179v and NOT the five-grade ladder of ff.168-174. Note also the word chosen for the top grade: ጳጳስ (Gk. πάπας by way of the Arabic-period translation layer, where it is the ordinary word for bishop/metropolitan), not the Aksumite-layer ኤጲስ ቆጶስ. That places the translation in the Sinodos/Qalementos layer, not the old Aksumite corpus. (Not claimed as an Arabic loan: the root is Greek.)

2. **Voice.** ll.10-14 are first-person divine speech: እስመ፡እነ፡እግዚእብሔር፡ዘእሠሪ፡ኀጢእት፡ለኀጥእ፡ወእተቢቀል፡ኀጢእቱ፡ለዘኢይኒስሕ "for I am the Lord who forgives sin for the sinner and avenges his sin on him who does not repent" - with ተመይጡ፡ኀቢየ "returned to ME" (l.7) and እምኒየ "from ME" (l.9-10). But ll.15-19 speak ABOUT God in the third person (ወኢይ?ኀሠሠክሙ፡እግዚእብሔር ... ደሞሙ፡እምኒክሙ "and the Lord will not require ... their blood from you"). So the divine speech is a quotation embedded inside a compiler's/apostle's address to the clergy. That is the Qalementos/Sinodos shape (an apostolic narrator instructing, citing the Lord), not the Testamentum Domini shape (Christ speaking throughout to the apostles).

3. **Genre markers.** Confession to an ordained priest of the church (l.2-3 ካህን፡ስዩም፡ዘቤተ፡ክርስቲያን), obedience to what he commands (l.4 ወበከመ፡አዘዘ), and the priest is not to lay on the penitent a heavy [burden] he cannot carry (l.4-6, 8-9). Then the penitents are to be pure in thought and deed and give alms from the work of their hands (l.20-23). This is the penitential discipline of ff.176-179 seen from its hortatory side, not its tariff side: no "if he does X, N years" here, and nothing in the third-person jussive tariff register except ወይግበሩ፡ሠናየ፡ወየሀቡ፡ምጽዋተ at the end.

4. **Scriptural echoes, in order** (no citation formula anywhere on the page; these are allusions, not citations): (a) ll.5-6, 8-9 the heavy burden that cannot be borne (Matt 23:4 ጾረ፡ክቡደ ... ዘኢይክሉ፡ጸዊሮ), inverted into an instruction to the confessor; (b) ll.17-19 "require their blood from you" (Ezek 3:18/33:8, the watchman). Matthew first, Ezekiel second. Neither is named.

Didascalia is excluded a third time by this page: the Didascalia's pastoral chapter on the bishop and Ezekiel 34 addresses the bishop alone, with the five-grade ladder elsewhere; here the Ezekiel-blood warning is addressed to bishops-priests-deacons as one body.

Best label: the same three-grade church-order stratum as f.179v, at its penitential-hortatory close (or the opening of a homily on penance within it). The catalogue's "Qalementos" is not contradicted by this page - ጳጳስ, embedded divine quotation, apostolic exhortation to three grades all fit the Ethiopic Clement - but nothing on the page names Peter or Clement, so it is not proven either.

## GIST

If a man turns from his sin he is to come to an ordained priest of the church and do as he commands; the priest is not to lay a heavy burden on him that he cannot carry, so that the penitents are not [discouraged], do not say "it is heavy and he cannot bear it," and do not flee from Me, "for I am the Lord who forgives the sinner's sin and avenges the sin of him who does not repent with a pure heart and straighten his way after penance." Then the address turns: guard your flock, O bishops, priests and deacons, so that God be not angry with you and not require their blood from you; let them be pure in thought and deed, do good, and give alms from the work of their hands.

- **l.2 ⟦ክ?⟧ክህን.** Three horizontal strokes run through a ክ and a following ኀ/ን-shaped glyph; then ክህን stands unstruck. The scribe restarted the word. Read ካህን (scribe writes ክ for ካ). ስዩም "appointed/ordained" follows, so ካህን፡ስዩም፡ዘቤተ፡ክርስቲያን "an ordained priest of the church".

## PREDICTIONS (registered before reading 181r/182r)

- **181r must end** mid-sentence with the subject that governs እምኀጢእቱ: a verb of turning/repenting (ዘይትመየጥ / ዘይኔስሕ / እመ፡ተመይጠ) and probably a rubric shift from tariff canons to this hortatory close. - **Falsifiers.** (1) 182r not opening with a ዊ-/ው- syllable completing እደ- breaks l.23. (2) A rank list on 181r or 182r containing ንፍቀ፡ዲያቆን or አናጕንስጢስ breaks the three-grade stratum claim. (4) A numbered canon with a tariff on 182r continuing without break makes this a canon's preamble rather than a peroration.
