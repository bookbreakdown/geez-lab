## WORK

Apostolic church-order, canon on who may not enter the sanctuary (ምሥዋዕ) or
the church. NOT Apocalypse of Paul. Evidence from the ink, strongest first:

1. **First-person apostolic legislation.** l.15-16 ወበእንተዝ፡ንሕነ፡አዘዝነ፡በቀኖና
   "and therefore WE have commanded in the canon". Only a church-order speaks
   this way; an apocalypse never does. This is the same frame as ff.168-174.
2. **The argument runs Law -> Kings -> "our canon".** l.6 ሕግ፡ዘሙሴ፡አዘዘ (the
   Law of Moses commanded), l.9-10 ወመጻሕፍት፡ነግሠት፡ኒ፡አዘዘ (and the Books of
   Kings ALSO commanded), l.15-16 ንሕነ፡አዘዝነ (we commanded). A typological
   chain justifying a canon from two OT precedents is the method of the
   Clementine/Sinodos canonical material, not of AC VIII, which legislates
   without OT proof-texts of this shape.
3. **The Kings citation is 2 Sam 5:6-8 by content, not by name.** ll.10-14:
   "concerning those who ተቀመዎ [opposed? him] ... David commanded that the
   blind and the lame should not enter the ደብተራ". The blind-and-lame ban on
   entering "the house" is 2 Sam 5:8 and nothing else in Kings. The scribe
   calls it መጻሕፍት፡ነግሠት (Books of Kings), which is where Samuel sits in the
   Ethiopic canon (1-4 Kings).
4. **The Moses citation is the camp-exclusion, Num 5:2-3 / Lev 13:46.** l.8-9
   ርቱቅ፡እምትዕይንት "far from the CAMP" (ትዕይንት), and l.3 ወእለ፡ለምጽ "those with
   leprosy". Num 5:2 orders lepers, dischargers and corpse-defiled out of the
   camp; Lev 13:46 puts the leper "outside the camp". The word ትዕይንት is the
   diagnostic: it is a wilderness-narrative word, not a temple word.
5. **Subject is access to the altar, with women first.** l.1-2 ኢይባዕ፡አንስት፡
   ውስተ፡ምሥዋዕ "let not a woman enter the sanctuary", then the blind, the
   ዕቁባን, the lepers, the ዝልፉስን - they are to stand OUTSIDE (በአፍአ, ll.4, 7)
   and pray to God from there (ll.4-5, 17-19). Then a purity rule: he who is in
   ስዕበት or not clean shall not enter the church until cleansed and repentant
   (ll.19-22). Canon 44 Laodicea (women not to the altar) and the Lev 21
   blemish-list are the raw material; the assembly of them under "we the
   apostles" is Sinodos/Qalementos-type.

**Fit with the preceding frame.** This page is squarely back in (or never
left) the church-order that ff.168-174 established. If f.175v really is
Apocalypse of Paul 23, then either an excerpt was embedded inside the
church-order, or the shift back is as unannounced as the shift in. One
alternative worth registering for the f.175v reader: the Ethiopic Qalementos
(Kitab al-Majall) itself carries paradise cosmography from the Cave of
Treasures tradition, so a non-Genesis river order does not by itself force
Apoc. Paul. The catalogue label may be less wrong than it looked.

**Citations, in the order they appear:** (1) Law of Moses, Num 5:2-3 / Lev
13:46, ll.6-9; (2) Books of Kings = 2 Sam 5:6-8, ll.9-14; (3) "our canon",
ll.15-16. Forward canonical order (Torah, then Former Prophets). No NT
citation on this page. No name of Paul, no Michael, no Paradise vocabulary.

**Arabic loans:** none claimed. Nothing here has a root I can show. ስዕበት
(l.19) is unplaced - see NOTES.

## GIST

Women, the blind, the [ዕቁባን], lepers and the [ዝልፉስን] are not to enter the
sanctuary; they stand outside and pray to God, as the Law of Moses first
ordered the unclean to stand far from the camp, and as the Books of Kings also
record that David ordered the blind and lame not to enter the [tabernacle].
Therefore we have commanded in the canon that they not enter the sanctuary but
stand [outside/in the ...], approach, and pray to God; and whoever is in ስዕበት
or is not clean shall not enter the church until he is cleansed and repents.

## NOTES

- **ስዕበት (l.19)** paired with ኢኮነ፡ንጹሐ "is not clean": this looks like the
  abstract noun of the key's unplaced ሰዑብ, "always paired with ርኩስ". Same
  semantic slot, same root letters ስ-ዕ-ብ. Collect for the expert. Arabic
  ṣ-ʿ-b "difficult" does not fit the sense; no loan claimed.
- **ዕቁባን (ll.2-3)** in a list between the blind and the lepers. Not Lev 21
  vocabulary as I know it. Ink: ወ-ዕ-ቁ / ባ-ን, all four letters clear.
- **ዝልፉስን (ll.3-4)** closes the list. Ink: ወ-ዝ-ል-ፉ / ስ-ን. The ዝ is the
  curled-top H, not ዘ. ፉ has no bounding bars.
- **ቀሠፉ፡እቱት (l.8)** between ይቁሙ and ርቱቅ. ቅሡፋን "the stricken" is a
  tempting emendation (ቀሠፈ = smite with plague) but the ink is ቀ-ሠ-ፉ.
- **ወሐንከላን (ll.13-14)** a second lame-like word after ወሐ..ሳን. Unplaced.
- **ደብተራ፡መርዕል (ll.14-15)** "tabernacle of ..."; 2 Sam 5:8 has "the
  house". The scribe substitutes ደብተራ; the qualifier is unread.
- **በገ{ይ|ደ}ም (l.17)** where the blemished are to stand. A corrupt ገዳም
  "wilderness/outside" would parallel በአፍአ of l.7 but is not what is written.
- Punctuation: three ። on the page, each written as ፡ pairs with red dots
  (l.11, l.15, l.19) - kept as ። per the key.
- Orthography seen: ባዕ/ባዑ for ባእ/ባኡ, እፍእ for አፍአ, ነግሠት for ነገሥት, ቢተ for
  ቤተ, ዕውራን for ዕዉራን. Consistent with the key's attested habits.
- Margins: the right margin carries the ghost-offset column from the facing
  recto; not text, not read.
- Crops: scratchpad 177V-A..D (strips) and 177V-z1..z11 (3x zooms).

## SEAM PREDICTIONS (registered before reading either neighbour)

- **177r must end** mid-word with the syllable before መ, most likely ከ (ከመ
  "that"), following a verb of commanding or a subject such as "the apostles
  said" - i.e. its last line is the head of the canon whose body is l.1 here.
  Its content must already be church-order, not Paradise.
- **178r must open** with more purity/exclusion legislation in the same
  apostolic "we" voice - the next class of the unclean (menstruants, seminal
  emission, or the corpse-defiled of Num 5:2), or the penalty for entering
  uncleansed. It should not open with a narrative or an angel.
