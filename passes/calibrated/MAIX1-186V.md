## GIST

A first-person speaker warns "you" (plural) to guard yourselves against
deceivers who come to him pretending to be of "us", teach crooked speech,
twist scripture to their own sense, and lead the simple astray with their
own books; their way does not join to the kingdom of heaven, and you must
keep clear of them lest they drag you with them into Gehenna, which has no
exit for ever. Their audience is the wicked, the fools and the negligent who
were never sealed by the Holy Spirit in baptism; Satan has taken from their
heart the word of God they were taught, and they trust in the deceivers —
the sentence runs on to 187r.
