## WORK

**Homily / exhortation, not canon.** Evidence from the ink:

- **Voice.** Opens in second-person plural imperative (ዑቁ፡ወተባቀቡ "beware and
  guard yourselves", ll.1-2), and turns to first-person plural confession at
  l.19 (ንሕነሰ "but we", -ነ suffixes six times in ll.19-24: በእንቲእነ, ቢዘወነ,
  እድኀነነ, ዘይተቃረነ-, ወእቦታነ). Canon folios do not say "we ... whom he
  redeemed".
- **Citation habit.** Five scriptural passages and a creed clause, all
  **woven**, none formula-marked. Neither በከመ፡ይቤ nor ወካዕበ፡ይቤ nor any ይቤ
  occurs on the page. Order:
  1. l.1 — Mt 24:24 (false messiahs), woven. The slot for ሐሳውያን is filled
     by ወመጠባውያን (see NOTES).
  2. ll.2-4 — Mt 7:15-16 (false prophets; "by their fruits you shall know
     them": እምፍሬሆሙ፡ተእምርዎሙ), woven.
  3. ll.10-12 — Ps 103:12 (as far as the east is from the west: በከመ፡ይርኃቅ፡
     ሠርቀ፡እምዓረብ), woven, then given an allegorical gloss of the compiler's
     own (ll.12-14: the east is the souls of the righteous, the west the
     souls of sinners) using the -ሰ ... -ኒ contrast pair.
  4. ll.15-16 — Ps 35(34):6 (their way dark and slippery: ጽልመት፡ወደኃፅ፡
     ፍኖቶሙ), woven.
  5. ll.16-17 — Isa 57:21 / 48:22 (no peace for them: ወእልቦሙ፡ሰላም), woven.
  6. ll.19-22 — creed clause: crucified, suffered, died for us, redeemed us,
     saved us (ዘተሰቀለ፡ወሐመ፡ወሞተ፡በእንቲእነ፡ወቢዘወነ፡ወእድኀነነ), woven.
- **No ranks, no anathema formula, no ርኩስ፡ወሰዑብ, no ርጉም፡ወውጉዝ, no
  numerals, no በከመ፡ይቤ.** Every canon marker the corpus has established is
  absent.
- The polemical targets are the ሐሰውያን፡ነቢያት (false prophets) and
  መስሐታን (seducers) who are "of Satan" (l.18), set against "we, the
  disciples of Jesus Christ". This is anti-heretical paraenesis.

Whether it is the same work as the ff.181v-184r exhortation cannot be fixed
from this side: f.185v was canons, ff.186r-186v are undrafted, so there is a
gap of at least two sides between the last known exhortation and this page.
What can be said is that 187r is in the exhortation register, not the canon
register, and its vocabulary (ሰይጣን, ሐሳውያን, ሠርቀ/ዓረብ, ንሕነሰ, ተሰቀለ, ቤዘወ) is
absent from all 60 drafted folios (tgrep, see FALSIFIERS), so it is not a
repeat of anything already read.

## GIST

Beware of false messiahs and guard yourselves against false prophets; you
will know them by their fruits, so do not approach them, do not [go about /
eat] with them, for they are enemies and there is no repentance with them
... the church. Our way is as far from them as the east is from the west,
the east being the souls of the righteous and the west the souls of sinners,
the seducers, the false prophets, whose way is darkness and slipperiness and
who have no peace, for they are of Satan; but we are the disciples of Jesus
Christ who was crucified and suffered and died for us and redeemed us and
saved us from [?] and from everything that opposes us, [?] the enemy, and
... us [continues on 187v].

## NOTES

Letterforms and readings, in line order.

- **l.1 ወመጠባውያን.** The second glyph is smudged, possibly overwritten, but
  reads መ. The fourth glyph is a box with a right descender: this hand's ባ,
  NOT ዐ (ዓ on ll.12, 13, 22 is the 9-shaped bowl the key describes). The slot
  is the ሐሳውያን of Mt 24:24 (ሐሳውያን፡መሲሓን፡ወሐሳውያን፡ነቢያት) and the page then
  delivers ነቢያት at l.3, so the structure is certain and the word is not.
  Not smoothed. Candidate for the expert: an Amharic-influenced formation
  (Amh. ጠበቀ "guard, watch for", cf. l.2 below) is possible but I cannot
  give a root that yields -ውያን.
- **l.1 ዑቁ.** ዑ single bowl with hook (key rule); ቁ with foot. "Beware."
- **l.2 ወተባቀቡ.** Slot is ተዐቀቡ "guard yourselves" (Mt 7:15). The ink has
  the box-with-leg glyph = ባ, the same glyph as in l.1, not the 9-shaped ዓ.
  So the scribe wrote ba where ʿa is expected, twice on one page, OR ባ is
  his ዐ here. Test: ll.12-13 ዓረብ has the 9-shape in the same ink on the same
  side, so the two glyphs are distinct in his hand. Left as ባ, flagged.
- **l.2 ስሐስ / l.3 ውያን.** Sense wants ለሐሳውያን፡ነቢያት. Both ስ glyphs are the
  flat-topped two-legged form that this hand also uses for ለ (compare ለቢተ
  l.9 and ሠርቀሰ l.12). Third glyph shows no right extension, so not ሳ. Ink
  kept; ስ/ለ flagged.
- **l.4 ተእምርዎሙ.** Hooked alef = እ (key). ዎ two loops.
- **l.4-5 ወኢቀሪብዎሙ.** Standard would be ወኢትቅረብዎሙ. Transcribed as written.
- **l.5 ወኢተሐወሉ.** Final glyph ለ-body with foot = ሉ, not ሩ. Word not placed
  (ተሐወረ "walk with" would want ሩ). Ink kept.
- **l.6 ወኢተበልዎሙ፡ባሕ፡.** Not placed. ባሕ is followed by ፡ and then እስመ, so
  it is not ባሕቱ split across lines. ባ is again the box-with-leg glyph.
- **l.7 ጸላዕያን.** ጸ + ላ (arch with right extension) + ዕ + ያ + ን. Plural in
  -yān of ጸላዒ "enemy"; nonstandard for ጸላእት. Cf. l.24 ጸላዒ.
- **l.8, l.16 እልቦሙ.** Third glyph is a box with a top-right tail. Read ቦ on
  sense (አልቦሙ "they have no ..."), and Isa 57:21 at l.16-17 confirms the
