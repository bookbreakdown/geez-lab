## GIST
The page continues a run of anathemas in a fixed three-word formula, "cursed and qäwəm(?) and excommunicated is he who...", closely tracking the Deuteronomy 27 curse list. The offences named are: sacrificing and eating (the offering); taking bribes and killing innocent [blood]; [dishonouring] one's father and mother and [ill-treating] them in everything, "for they are his begetters... to those who do thus, says the Lord"; leading the blind astray on the road and speaking evil to the deaf; fornicating with wild beasts and cattle, and with a male as with a woman. The last four lines turn from the curse to the remedy: let him act according to the [command?] of the elders of the church, and they shall give him penance, and they shall return grieving and sorrowing in... (continues on 160v). The lower quarter of the page is blank below line 23.

## NOTES
Uncertain readings, by line:
- l.1: after ወ, two letters ኤስ are struck through with three horizontal pen lines; the lines run on into the following ዊ, so the deletion may cover ኤስዊ. Marked ⟦ ⟧. ዊእውግር is read as ink; the intended word is unclear (ወግር "stoning"? ይአውግር?). Last word: ደ/ይ collision resolved by sense to ወይሰውዕ "and he sacrifices" (ll. 1-2); the ሰ may carry a foot (ሱ).
- l.2, 5, 13, 16, 19: ወቀውም. Read as ink five times; consistent. Not a word I can place (ውጉዝ/ርጉም/ሕሩም is the usual triad). Second choice ወቍውም. Editor should check.
- l.3: first glyph is the odd line-initial ው described below, followed by a normal ው: ውውጉዝ, a dittograph for ወውጉዝ/ውጉዝ.
- l.4: ጸመ ፡ ንዴሐ. Deut 27:25 (Ge'ez) wants ደመ ንጹሐ "innocent blood". The first glyph is a ጸ/ፀ shape, not this hand's Y-shaped ደ; the second is ደ+ring (ዴ/ዲ), not ጹ. Read as ink, flagged.
- l.5: ሥከመ. First glyph matches the ሥ of ይነሥእ (l.3): three prongs with right stroke. No sense found; possibly ሥ is a miswritten/alternate letter and the word is ከመ preceded by something. Low confidence.
- l.6: ዘያስተባኪ. Sense (Deut 27:16) wants a verb "dishonours" (ያስተኃስር / የሐምይ). The fourth glyph is a box with right stroke (ባ); the key warns this hand's ኀ/ጳ family can look similar, so ዘያስተኀኪ is a second choice. ኪ has the left foot.
- l.7: ወይነጥዩሙ, unparsed. ጥ could be ጡ.
- l.8: ወያስተኤክዩሙ, unparsed (root ኤከየ/አእከየ "do evil"?).
- l.9: ተቢየ — second choice ዐቢየ "great" (ተ vs ዐ). ወለዶያን — sense wants ወላድያን "begetters/parents"; ink has ለ (not ላ) and ደ/ይ+ring (ዶ or ዮ).
- l.10: እኒ (maybe for እመኒ); እኩያንናሙ — no separator between ን and ና; ና could be ኖ. Unparsed.
- l.12: ። written as two spaced ፡ pairs (kept as ።). Same at l.15.
- l.13-14: ዘያስሐ|ቲ for ዘያስሕት "leads astray"; ቲ has the right dash at mid-low height (same form as ቲ in ክርስቲያን l.21).
- l.15: ሕሱመ for ሕሡመ "evil"; ሱ is ሰ with a right-down stroke.
- l.17: ዙሙ for ዜሙ (ዘይዜሙ "fornicates"); the glyph carries a right stroke, not a ring. Reads ዙ as ink.
- l.20: በካመ for በከመ — the ከ carries an extra top-left stroke; could be this hand's ከ variant. እዘዝ + l.21 ዲ: unparsed; expected something like አዘዝዎ or ትእዛዘ. l.21 initial is ደ/ይ with a bottom-right foot (ዲ/ዪ), not the ring form of ዎ seen in ወየሀብዎ l.22.
- l.22: ወይገብዑ for ወይገብኡ "they return"; the glyph is ዐ-shaped with right stroke, distinct from the hooked እ.
- l.23: ወይቲክዙ for ወይቴክዙ "they are sorrowful"; ቲ not ቴ.
- All alefs transcribed እ per the key (እቡሁ, ግዚእብሔር, ዊእውግር, እራዊት, እንስት).

NEW LETTERFORM FACTS for the key:
1. Line-initial ው (ll. 3 and 16, both in ውጉዝ) is drawn as a single loop at lower left with a tall stem and a top-right hook, i.e. it looks like a printed ቍ, and quite unlike the mid-line ው (two loops + right dash, ውእቱ passim, ወውጉ l.12). Sense at l.16 (ርጉም ፡ ወ|ውጉዝ) fixes the value as ው. Do not read ቍ at line-start.
2. ቲ in this hand: ተ with a short right dash set slightly below mid-height (ክርስቲያን l.21, ዘያስሐቲ l.14, ወይቲክዙ l.23). It sits close to ቱ (dash at mid); distinguish by height, and expect the scribe to use ቲ where standard orthography has ት or ቴ.
3. ዎ carries a ring at top right (ወየሀብዎ l.22); ዑ is the ዐ bowl with a right stroke (ወይገብዑ l.22) — both cleanly distinct from the ው family.
4. ዜ/ዙ: this scribe used the right-stroke form (ዙ) where ዜ is expected (l.17). Treat sixth-order ዘ-family ring vs stroke as unreliable.
5. Deletion: the scribe cancels letters with three horizontal pen strokes through the glyphs (l.1), not with dots.
6. ሥ = three prongs with a right stroke (ይነሥእ l.3), same form line-initial at l.5.
