## GIST
The page opens mid-sentence with the end of a clause about "the edge(s) of darkness" (ወጽናፌ፡ጽልመት።), then turns to the righteous: those who go on his right hand go into everlasting life, where there is no toil(?) and no sickness, where they do not die; and then again to the garden of delight, and their joy and gladness do not end, for ever, unto the age of ages. Their souls do not die again, unto the age of ages. And this will come to pass on that cursed day, a day of weeping and fear and trembling: if the righteous weep on account of sinners, and if sinners weep on account of their sins, the angels of heaven weep on account of sinners. And on that day all return to their inheritance, those who did good into ... (cut off; next folio).

- Line 1, last glyph: ink is abraded/smudged at the outer margin. The surviving ink most resembles ን (top bar with a curved descender), with a faded blot beneath that could be the remains of a ring. Sense demands ጻድቃን (→ ጻድቃንሰ with the ስ of line 2: "but the righteous, who go on his right ..."), but only ONE glyph slot exists between ድ and the line end, so either ቃ or ን is missing from the ink. Marked ? ; second choice ቃ, third choice ን.
- Line 1 ወጽናፌ: ና and ፌ (ring on the right leg) are clear. Standard would be ወጽንፈ ("and the edge of"); transcribed as written.
- Line 2 first glyph: read ስ (Λ-with-bar, same as ውስተ line 3); ሰ not excluded. Topicaliser -ሰ expected.
- Line 4 እልቦ: this scribe's alef is consistently the hooked form (bottom-left tail); it is written the same in እልቦ (std. አልቦ), እመ, መላእክት, ኃጢእቶሙ (std. ኃጢአቶሙ). አ vs እ cannot be distinguished in this hand; transcribed as እ throughout. Normalise on the editorial side, not here.
- Line 4 ጸዊ: both glyphs clear at 5x — ጸ (T-bar, bowl, plain right stem, no ring) and ዊ (ወ with right leg ending in a hook at the baseline, unlike ማ in ወሐማ on the same line, whose ring is on the LEFT leg).
