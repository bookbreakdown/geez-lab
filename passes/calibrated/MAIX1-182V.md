## WORK

A hortatory homily on the church's ordinance and its "steps" (ማዕርግ), continuous in voice and matter with the peroration on f.181v. Not a tariff canon, not a rank-ladder canon, not a narrative. Evidence from the ink, weightiest first:

1. **Voice.** ll.1-14 are second-person plural imperatives and prohibitions addressed to the hearers: ዕቀቡ (l.1 "keep"), ኢትተገኅሡ (l.3 "do not turn aside"), ፅንዑ (l.5 "be firm"), ይቁም፡ሰኰናክሙ (l.6 "let your heel stand"), ኢታውርዱ፡ሕሊናክሙ (ll.8-9 "do not bring your mind down"), ኢትረዱ (l.10 "do not descend"), አጽንዑ (l.13). No first-person "we command" (ንኤዝዝ) anywhere; no third-person jussive tariff (no "if he does X, N years"); no source-citation formula (no ከመ፡ይቤ, no በከመ፡ጽሑፍ). That is the register of ff.181v ll.15-23 (ዕቀቡ፡መርዔተክሙ ... "guard your flock") carried on, not the register of the canons on ff.176r-180v.

2. **Leitmotif.** ማዕርግ "step/rank/degree" four times: ማዕርጊሃ፡ለቤተ፡ክርስቲያን (l.7), እም-ማዕርጊሃ፡ልዑላን "from its high steps" (l.10), ወማዕርጊሃ (l.23), ማዕር- (l.24, line-final). The image is a staircase the hearer can descend from (ll.10-12 "into the lowest depth") or ascend (ll.13-15 "into a bright and high land"). A homily on ecclesiastical rank as a ladder of ascent, which is a Sinodos-layer theme.

3. **Rank words, ll.16-17: ሊቃነ፡ጳጳሳት፡ወስዩማነ፡ቤተ፡ክርስቲያን** "chiefs of the bishops (metropolitans/archbishops) and the appointed [ministers] of the church". This is NOT a rank list: it is two collective terms, head and body, describing who dwells in the bright land. Neither the five-grade ladder of f.174r nor the three-grade list of ff.179v/181v appears. (Per the brief the bishop-word itself is not used as a stratum marker.) ስዩም "appointed/ordained" is the same word f.181v l.2 used for the confessor-priest (ካህን፡ስዩም), which ties the two sides lexically.

4. **Scriptural echoes, in order** (allusions, no citation formula):
   (a) ll.3-4 ወኢትተገኅሡ፡እምኔሃ፡ኢለየማን፡ወኢለፀጋም = Deut 5:32 / 17:11 / Josh 1:7, 23:6 in Ge'ez wording (ተገሐሠ + ኢለየማን፡ወኢለፀጋም). Verbatim formula.
   (b) ll.14-15 "that you may enter into a bright and high land" - the land-of-the-living / promised-land idiom, not a fixed verse.
   (c) ll.22-23 መናብርት፡ልዑላን "high thrones" - Matt 19:28 / Rev 4:4 thrones for those who followed; allusive only.
   Deuteronomy first, then the land, then thrones. One citation-grade echo (a), two weaker.

5. **Numbered catalogue head at l.24.** After a struck word, ፲ወ፫ with bars, then እማር?ት፡ማዕር-. If the reading holds, the homily is about to enumerate 13 (or 12) "steps". That is the f.174r/f.175v habit (a fixed-order catalogue quoted inside a homily) rather than the canon strata. The catalogue itself is on 183r.

Best label: the same hortatory homily on penance and clerical order that ff.181v (and presumably 182r) carry, now turned to the ladder of ranks, and opening a numbered catalogue at its foot. Nothing names Peter, Clement or the apostles; "Qalementos" is neither confirmed nor excluded. Didascalia is excluded a fourth time: the Didascalia has no ladder-of-steps homily and no 12/13-item rank catalogue.

Arabic loans: one candidate only, with a root. l.19 ወይጢሀሩ, if the root is ṭ-h-r (Arabic طهر ṭahhara "purify"), would be "and they purify the perverse of the people". Ge'ez has no native ṭ-h-r. Recorded as candidate, not claim; see NOTES.

## GIST

Keep the ordinance of the church as a good ordinance and do not turn aside from it to the right or the left; be firm in it, let your heel stand in the firmness of the church's steps, and do not bring your mind down from the right faith, lest you descend from its high steps into the lowest depth. Strengthen your heart and lift up your mind, that you may enter the bright and high land where the archbishops and the appointed of the church dwell, the good ones who set straight the church's law and cleanse(?) the perverse of the people and unite them to God; and they sit upon the high thrones and its steps - [struck word] thirteen (?) ... steps.

## NUMERALS

Bars: a bar is visible ABOVE ፲ and ABOVE ፫ in the 4x crop (182V-z-l24.png); a bar BELOW is present under ፲ but the three strike-through strokes of ⟦ልዕልን፡⟧ run into the same zone, so the lower bar under ፫ is not independently certain. This is the only glyph on the page with bounding bars; it is not a letter.

Tested per the key's standing bias: ፲ vs ፳ - the sign is a plain serifed I-beam with no bowl and no leg, so ፲, not ፳. ፫ vs ፪ - the sign is a closed bowl on a stem, top-right, which the key gives for ፫; ፪ (a loop) is the alternative and would make 12. Medium confidence on 13; 12 is the live alternative. 12 would suit an apostolic catalogue, 13 would not, so the value matters for what follows.

No other numeral. The I-beam ነ in ነብሩ (l.16), ሰኰናክሙ (l.6), ሕሊናክሙ (l.8-9) carries no bars.

**Bottom third (ll.17-24): medium.** ll.17-18 and 23 secure. Soft: l.17 ኂራን (ኒ alternative); l.19 ወይጢሀሩ unresolved; l.20-21 እእም|ር unresolved; l.21-22 ወእመ|ን፡ተኪ፡ይነብቱ unresolved; l.24 struck word, the numeral value, and እማር?ት with a stain over the fourth glyph.

## PREDICTIONS (registered before reading 182r/183r)

- **182r must end** with ክር- (completing ክርስቲያን at l.1 here) and must itself begin ዊሆሙ፡ per the f.181v pass; between them it should carry the transition from f.181v's "give alms from the work of their hands" to an address about the church's ordinance (ሠርዓት). 182r should still be in the second person plural.
- **183r must begin** with the completion of ማዕር- (ግ or ጊሃ), and, if the numeral is a catalogue head, continue with a fixed-order list of 13 (or 12) steps/ranks, probably descending from ሊቀ፡ጳጳሳት, using ማዕርግ as the counting noun. It should also complete or explain በቀ- from l.23.
