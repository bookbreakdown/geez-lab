## WORK

**A homily, not a canon. The stratum of ff.167 and 175v, not of ff.176r-181v.**

The join with 181v is clean: 181v ends ...እምግብሪ፡እደ and 182r opens ዊሆሙ, so
"alms from the work of their hands" completes across the seam, and the list
continues in the same third-person plural: ወይትወከፉ፡ነገደ "and they receive
the stranger", ወይልብሱ ... ዕራቀነ "and they clothe the naked" (ll.1-2).

Evidence from the ink for "homily":

1. **The instruction is narrated, not issued.** l.3 ዘንተ፡ከመ፡ይግበሩ፡እዘዎሙ
   "this he commanded them to do" - a third-person report of someone else's
   command, with a singular subject. The canons of ff.176r-181v speak in
   jussives and tariffs; f.174r's list speaks in ranks. Neither reports "he
   commanded them". There is no ንኤዝዝ "we command", no ይደሉ/ኢይደሉ, no
   penance figure, no rank list anywhere on the page.
2. **Eschatological argument.** ll.10-14: "these on the last day (በደኃሪት፡ዕለት)
   will be [afflicted], because they spoke folly and falsehood against their
   Creator (ፈጣሪሆሙ), and according to the evil of their deeds they will find
   their recompense (ፍዳሆሙ)". A judgment-preaching sequence.
3. **A two-destinies formula.** ll.15-16 ለእኩያን፡እኩየ፡ወለኄራን፡ሠናየ "for the
   evil, evil; and for the good, good" - the same binary the Two Ways homily
   on f.167 trades in.
4. **A source citation with ይቤ.** ll.21-22 ወበእንተዝ፡ይቤ፡ውስተ፡ቤትየ፡ብዙኃን፡
   ማኅደር "and for this reason he said: in my house are many dwellings" -
   John 14:2, paraphrased (standard Ge'ez has ቤተ፡አቡየ "my Father's house";
   here the corrected ink reads ቤትየ "my house", subject unnamed). An unmarked
   Psalter echo precedes it: l.8-9 እለ፡ያመልኩ፡ጣዖት፡ወእለ፡ይት[ሜ]ክሑ፡በጣዖቲሆሙ
   matches Ps 96(97):7 "who worship graven images and boast in their idols".
5. **Direct address to the congregation.** l.23 ወአንትሙኒ፡ደቂቀ፡ቤተ፡ክርስቲያን
   "And you also, children of the church" - second-person plural vocative,
   the preacher turning to his hearers. Canons do not address "children of the
   church".
6. **Dwellings for the righteous.** ll.18-20 "and there is [a place] which is
   beautiful, dwellings (ማኅደር) within it for the righteous who do good" - the
   same paradise-as-dwellings theme as the catalogue on f.175v.

So the codex has left the canon block (ff.176r-181v) somewhere on 181v and is
back in a homily by 182r, exactly as it slid from homily into canons earlier.
Whether this is the *same* homily as f.167/175v (resumed) or a third one I
cannot say from one page; the John 14:2 dwellings and the two-destinies
formula are the hooks to test against those folios.

Citation ORDER on this page: (1) Ps 96:7 echo, unmarked, ll.8-9; (2) John
14:2, marked with ይቤ, ll.21-22. No named authority, no "as it is written".

No Arabic loan claimed. ሪሲአን (l.6) is unplaced and could be one (see NOTES),
but I have no root and will not claim one.

## GIST

The list of works of mercy continues from 181v (alms, receiving strangers,
clothing the naked), then the preacher says this is what "he commanded them",
that they must not withdraw from the church, and warns against those without
law or order who worship idols and boast in them: on the last day they will be
punished for the folly and lies they spoke against their Creator, each finding
the recompense of his deeds. There the evil get evil and the good get good;
there is a place that keeps judgment for evildoers and a beautiful place with
dwellings for the righteous, "for this reason he said, in my house are many
dwellings", and the page ends turning to the hearers: "and you also, children
of the church..."

## NOTES

- Join with 181v: clean (እደ|ዊሆሙ). Predicted from this side before reading 182v:
  **182v must open with ስቲያን** completing ቤተ፡ክር|ስቲያን, and must continue a
  second-person plural exhortation (imperatives or ኢት- prohibitions addressed
  to the "children of the church"), since l.23 has just opened a vocative.
  If 182v opens in the third person or with a canon, this reading is wrong.
- Falsifiers testable against drafted folios:
  1. If f.167 (Two Ways) or f.175v (paradise catalogue) contains the pair
     ለእኩያን፡እኩየ፡ወለኄራን፡ሠናየ or the phrase ብዙኃን፡ማኅደር, this page is the same
     homily resumed. If neither has them AND f.175v's paradise is described in
     different vocabulary (no ማኅደር), then this is a third homiletic piece and
     "same stratum as 175v" is only a genre match, not an identity.
  2. If 181v's lower half already carries second-person address (ወአንትሙኒ,
     ወአንትሙ) rather than jussives, the canon block ends earlier than I have
     placed it; if 181v is all jussive/third-person, the list on ll.1-2 is a
     narrated summary of duties, and the switch to homily happens exactly at
     l.3 አዘዎሙ.
  3. If any drafted folio in ff.176r-181v uses ይቤ + a Gospel quotation as a
     proof-text, the "citation formula = homily" diagnostic weakens and should
     be dropped.
  4. If ሪሲአን (l.6) recurs on a drafted folio in an anathema formula alongside
     ርኩስ/ሰዑብ, it belongs to the canon vocabulary and this page is a canon's
     hortatory tail rather than a separate homily.
