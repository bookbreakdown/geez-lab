## WORK

**A canon on the order of the patriarchal sees and the obedience of the lower clergy — canon-collection voice, NOT the second-person homily of ff.181v–183v.** Evidence from the ink, not from resemblance:

1. **Voice.** Every finite verb on the page is third person: ይተመዓዕ (l.4-5, "he is angry"), ዘኢያክብሮሙ (l.9, "who does not honour them"), ያክብርዎሙ (l.16, "they shall honour them"), ይትእዘዙ (l.19, "they shall obey"), ወይሔሩ (l.19-20, "and they shall go"), የኅሠሡ (l.22), ይርክብ (l.23). There is not one second-person form on the side. f.183v (drafted) is second person throughout (ንግሮሙ, ወዕቀብ ለሊከ, ተኩን, ስምዑ, ተግበሩ፡ወትምሀሩ, ኢተኩኑ). The voice has changed between 183v and 184v. f.184r is not drafted, so the seam cannot be placed closer than "on 184r or at the 184r/184v turn".
2. **Case-law frame.** l.7-9 እመቦ፡እምኤጲስቆጶስ፡ወእመሂ፡እምዲያቆናት፡ዘኢያክብሮሙ "if there be any, whether from the bishops or from the deacons, who does not honour them…" — the እመቦ … ዘ- protasis is the canon formula (cf. the penitential canons ff.160v–164r, which open cases the same way), and it is preceded by an anger clause (l.3-5: the "angel of the church" is angry at those who do not keep the order of the holy church). Homilies exhort; this side legislates.
3. **The see list, in ink order:** ርሜ (Rome, l.11) → ዘእለክድርያ፡ለመንበረ፡ማርቆስ (Alexandria, "the seat of Mark", l.12-13) → ለመንበረ፡ሉቃስ፡ዘእንጾኪያ (Antioch, "the seat of LUKE", l.14). Each step is joined by ወእምድኅሬሁ "and after it" (ll.11-12, 13). The attribution of Antioch to Luke rather than Peter is a real variant, not a slip of mine: ሉቃስ is unambiguous at 3x (ሉ with the right foot, ቃ, ስ), and Luke-of-Antioch is the Eusebian tradition (HE 3.4.6). This is the single most identifying feature on the page — any published Ge'ez canon list ranking Rome, Alexandria, Antioch with Antioch as "seat of Luke" is this text.
4. **Grades named (for the record, NOT as a stratum marker):** ሊቃነ፡ጳጳሳት (l.10), ጳጳሳት (l.15), ኤጲስቆጶሳት (l.17), ቀ[ሳ]ውስት (l.18), ዲያቆናት (ll.9, 18); also ሊቀ፡ካህናት (l.2, "high priest", of Christ or God, not a church grade). Per the withdrawn-claim warning, length of this list says nothing about the source; the voice (point 1) is the diagnostic.
5. **Second clause (ll.18-24):** the lower grades "shall obey in everything and go to them (?) yearly, having taken the books of the church, until the patriarch searches them (?) so that he may find …" — a yearly-attendance-on-the-superior rule with a book-inspection clause. The exact syntax of ll.20-24 is not secure (see NOTES), but jussive + በበዓመት "yearly" is legible.

**Citations: NONE.** No scripture on the side, marked or unmarked. tgrep shows 27 ይቤ and 35 በከመ hits across the drafted canon folios; this side has zero of either. The absence is itself consistent with a ranking canon (they rarely quote) and inconsistent with the 183v homily, which wove five scriptural phrases in 24 lines.

**Arabic loans: none claimed.** ጳጳስ/ኤጲስቆጶስ are Greek via Coptic/Arabic and standard Ge'ez; ሲኖዶስ does not occur; ርሜ, ዘእለክድርያ, እንጾኪያ are the usual toponyms. Nothing on the page requires an Arabic root.

**Candidate identification, held loosely:** the shape (Rome, Alexandria/Mark, Antioch, then subordination of bishops/priests/deacons and yearly attendance) is the shape of the ranking-of-sees canon in the Arabic Nicene corpus as carried in the Ethiopic Sinodos. That corpus usually ranks Rome, Alexandria, Ephesus, Antioch (four), with Antioch as Peter's. This page has THREE sees and Antioch as Luke's. Either a different recension, or the compiler's abridgement. Cannot be fixed from one side; a published Sinodos text of that canon is the check.

## GIST

The angel of the church is angry at whoever does not keep the order of our holy church, whether bishop or deacon, who does not honour the patriarchs — above all the seat of Rome, then Alexandria (the seat of Mark), then Antioch (the seat of Luke); and the bishops below them shall honour them, and those below them — bishops, priests, deacons — shall obey in everything. Yearly they go to them, having taken the books of the church, until the patriarch has searched them and found (something — the object is on 185r).
