## WORK

Not identified with certainty. What the ink establishes:

- First-person apostolic plural, continuous with f.167: "he commanded US
  that we teach" (ll.7-8), "OUR teaching" (ll.10, 12), "OUR word is the word
  of the Son" (l.17), "OUR Saviour said" (l.22). Christ's own words are
  embedded in first person singular at ll.1-6 ("by my will I came to taste
  death; after I died and rose, the teaching of the apostles was to abound
  to the ends of the world").
- ll.22-24 quote John 15:22 by name of speaker and addressee ("as our
  Saviour said to the Jews: if I had not come and spoken to them ..."). The
  quotation runs off the page, so the apodosis (ኃጢአት / "no sin") must open
  f.168v.
- Theme is the reception and rejection of the apostles' preaching and the
  judgment that follows, i.e. the continuation of the Two Ways / narrow-gate
  material on f.167, now in its "consequence" section.

Candidates consistent with this voice, none proven: the Ethiopic Sinodos /
Apostolic Church Order (the apostles speak in turn), the Didascalia, and the
Qalementos itself, where Peter narrates to Clement in the first person. The
catalogue label therefore cannot be ruled out on this leaf as it could on
ff.165v-166v. Do not use the label as evidence either way.

## GIST

"And this my saying, 'by my will' — by my will I came so that I would taste
death; and once I had died and risen, the teaching of the apostles was to
abound to the ends of the world." And he commanded us also that we teach to
the ends of the world. Those who receive our teaching enter into life; but
those who do not receive our teaching, severely [it condemns?] them upon their
own heads, and it reproves them greatly because of the word he spoke to them,
and they will not escape from judgment [? one is saved], for our word is the
word of the Son, which he received from his Father and taught to us. And our
word is not empty (?): even if it is for judgment, it becomes for them [?] —
as our Saviour said to the Jews, "If I had not come and spoken to them, from
judgment ..."

## NOTES

Least certain readings, in order:

1. l.13 ይድየሙ — four glyphs: [ይ/ደ][ደ/ይ with a base bar, read ድ as in l.5
   ፈድፍድ][a የ-shaped loop, possibly with a top ring = ዮ][ሙ]. Sense wants a
   verb "condemns/brings [judgment] upon them" (ይዴይኖሙ), but the third
   glyph is a የ loop, not an I-beam ነ, so that word is NOT what the ink
   says. Transcribed letter by letter; unresolved. Collect for the expert.
2. l.16 ይደኃን — [ይ/ደ][ደ/ይ][ኀ-family, order unmarked][ን, foot form]. If ይ ድ
   ኅ ን, "he is saved", which sits oddly after plural ወኢያምስጡ. Second choice
   ደኃሪ "last" (ኵነኔ ደኃሪ, "the last judgment") would need the final glyph to
   be ሪ; at 4x it is a single stem with a foot, i.e. ን, not a ረ shape. So
   ይደኃን stands and the syntax is left to the editor.
3. l.19 ወኤከነ — second glyph is a hooked alef with a right-side ring (ኤ),
   third is ከ with the top-left serif. Possibly a garbled ወኢኮነ ("and it is
   not") governing ዕራቃነ "empty" — "our word is not empty" — but ኢ in this
   hand is the LEFT-curl form and this glyph rings to the right. Flagged.
4. l.21 ለምዓ — ለ, ም (with top tick), and a 9-shaped bowl with a right
   descender (ዐ/ዓ, undistinguished). Not a word I can place. Could be ለመዓት
   "for wrath" with the ት dropped, or ለምንት "for what". Flagged.
5. l.24 እምኵነኔ — final word, ink runs to the edge. Glyph 3 is a ከ with a
   right-side hook that sits lower than the top-right ኵ hook seen in ll.16
   and 20; it could be read ኮ. Glyphs 4-5 are the foot-form ነ and ኔ, which
   fix ኵነኔ. Second choice, if glyph 3 is ኮ: እምኮነ..., the Gospel's
   እምኢኮነቶሙ (John 15:22) with ኢ dropped. The join with f.168v decides it.
6. l.14 ወይወቅሶሙ — the fifth glyph is three-pronged ሶ, not four-pronged ሦ
   (standard ይወቅሦሙ). Transcribed as ሶ.
7. ll.1-2: ብሂልየ፡በፈቃድየ፡ is written twice across the line break. Could be
   dittography, could be deliberate ("this my saying 'by my will' — by my
   will I came"). Transcribed as it stands.

Letterform facts for the key:
- ድ in this hand is ደ with a short horizontal bar under the left leg
  (ll.1, 2, 5 twice, 14, 17, 22, 23). It is separable from ደ/ይ, which have
  no bar; this is the only reliable way out of the ደ/ይ collision when the
  sixth order is in play.
- ኖ (l.21 ትከውኖሙ) is the I-beam ነ with a ring at bottom right; ኔ (ll.16,
  20, 24) is the same stem with a bottom-right tick and no ring.
- ኤ (l.19) is the hooked alef with a right-side mid ring, distinct from ኢ
  (left-curling leg, ll.11, 15, 23, 24).
- ሣ (l.4 ተንሣእኩ) is ሠ with a left-leg extension.
- The ። at ll.9, 11 and 22 is the four-dot cluster with red dots around
  it, as the key describes. Transcribed as ። in all three.
- Deletion at l.7: three horizontal strokes through six letters, the
  standard practice in the key.
- ኵ hook position varies: top-right at ll.16 and 20, lower at l.24. Do not
  use hook height alone to separate ኵ from ኮ; use the following letters.

## JOIN PREDICTIONS (registered before reading either neighbour)

- f.167v must END with a sentence that "ወዘንተ፡ብሂልየ" refers back to: a
  first-person saying of Christ, very likely containing ፈቃድ ("will") or a
  statement about his dying, so that "this my saying, 'by my will'" picks
  it up.
- f.168v must OPEN with the apodosis of John 15:22: a word completing
  "if I had not come and spoken to them, [they would have had no] sin" —
  expect ኃጢአት / ኃጢአቶሙ or a form of ኮነ within the first line, followed
  within two lines by ወይእዜሰ ("but now") and አልቦሙ ምክንያተ ("they have no
  excuse"). If 168v opens on anything else, the l.24 reading እምኵነኔ is
  suspect.
