## WORK

Continuation of the f.169v tabernacle canon with a first-person Moses
narrator. Not a verbatim copy of any canonical text; the work itself stays
**unidentified**. Evidence from the ink:

- ll.1-2 ደብሪ፡ሲና "Mount Sinai" and በደግምስ "and the second time":
  a second ascent, the frame of Ex 34 / Deut 10.
- ll.2-4 ከመ፡ይርእየኒ ... ወግብሪ፡ሕጉ፡ለቅዱስት፡ደብተራ "that he might show me
  ... and the making of its law for the holy tabernacle", and l.15
  እርእያሃ "her pattern": Ex 25:9, 25:40 (ἀρχέτυπος / አርአያ).
- ll.5-12 a list of six offerings: ዘበፈቃድ (freewill), ዘመድኀኒት (peace,
  lit. "of salvation", the standard Ethiopic name for the שלמים), ዘበእንተ፡
  ኃጢእት (sin), ዘልፈ (continual), ስብሐት (praise/thanksgiving), ዘበሕጉ፡
  ለዓመት (yearly by law). Leviticus 1-7 / Num 28-29, summarised, not quoted.
- ll.11-12 ውስተ፡ቅድስት፡ቤት፡ክርስቲያን "in the holy house of the Church":
  the offerings are read typologically; l.16-19 make the tabernacle the
  pattern of what is built in the last days, the bride (ሜርዓት) redeemed by
  the blood of the Messiah. Christian composition, not a Jewish source.
- ll.20-23 ወካዕበ፡ይቤ፡ሙሴ፡ርእኩ፡...፡እሳት፡ይነድድ፡ወቀረብኩ፡ከመ፡እጠይቅ፡
  ግርማሁ፡ወወጽእ፡ቃል፡እምው[ስተ] "And again Moses said: I saw a ??? of fire
  burning, and I drew near to inquire into its terror, and a voice came
  out from within..." — Ex 3:2-4 in the shape of Acts 7:30-31 ("as he drew
  near to look, the voice of the Lord came"). The burning-bush noun is the
  unresolved word in l.21.

The catalogue's "Qalementos Book IV" label is not supported by anything on
this page: no Peter, no Clement, no apostolic frame; the speaker is Moses.

## GIST

And the second time [I went] round(?) Mount Sinai for him, so that he might
show me the ??? and the making of its law for the holy tabernacle. And
further, that I should sacrifice to him a sacrifice of righteousness to God:
the freewill offering, and the peace offering, and the sacrifice for sin, and
the continual sacrifice, the sacrifice of salvation and the sacrifice of
praise, and the sacrifice that is by law for the year, in the holy house of
the Church. This my Lord commanded me, that I should make well her work for
the holy tabernacle, her pattern for the holy one, for that which will be
made in the last days, which is the bride of their souls, for the Christians,
she whom he redeemed by the blood of the Messiah. And again Moses said: I
saw a ??? of fire burning, and I drew near to inquire into its terror, and a
voice came out from within...

## NOTES

Least certain readings, in order:

1. l.21 ጣጠስ — transcribed letter by letter, not smoothed. At 5x: glyph 1
   is a three-pronged arch with a right descender (ጣ; ጡ possible), glyph 2
   a plain three-pronged arch (ጠ), glyph 3 a two-pronged ሰ with a top-left
   tick (ስ). Position demands the burning-bush noun ("I saw a ___ of fire
   burning"). Not ዕፅ, not ኰሓት. Possibly a loan (Greek βάτος?) but that is
   a guess and is not in the transcription. **For the expert.**
2. ll.2-3 ግብ|ፉ — the l.3 initial is a two-humped ፈ-family glyph with a leg
   (ፉ), checked at 3x against the single-bump ረ of l.22 and ሪ of l.3. Sense
   wants ግብረ/ግብሩ "the work". Ink says ፉ. Flagged, not corrected.
3. l.1 ዓውደ፡ሎቱ — ዓ (9-bowl, right descender) or ባ; ደ or ይ. Sense unclear
   ("round about it"? ዖደ "went round"?). Ink transcribed.
4. l.4 ወዓደ — final glyph is the ደ/ይ shape with a top-left flag; ወዓዲ "and
   further" is what the sentence wants. Ink transcribed as ደ, flagged.
5. l.4 እሡዕ — ሠ with a right descending stem read as ሡ (parallel to the
   ፴/፵ stem rule); gives the standard subjunctive of ሦዐ "that I sacrifice".
   ሠ possible.
6. l.20 መሢሕ — three-pronged ሠ-family glyph with a bottom-right curl, not the
   two-pronged ሰ used in ሲና (l.2), ስብሐት (l.10), ሶ (l.18). Nonstandard for
   መሲሕ. ሡ possible for ሢ.
7. l.20 ይቤ — the ቤ foot is a curled hook; line 19 ቤዘዋ has a flat dash in the
   same position. In this hand both are ቤ; there is no ring. Sense (ይቤ፡ሙሴ
   "Moses said") decides ይ over ደ.
8. l.5 ጽድቅ — final ቀ with a top tick; ቁ not excluded.
9. l.3 ለቅዱስት vs ll.14-15 ለቅድስት — checked at 3x: l.3 has a bottom-right leg
   (ዱ); ll.14-15 both have the top-left tick (ድ). Same word, two spellings
   on one page.

ደ/ይ decisions by sense: l.2 ይርእየኒ, l.16 ለይእቲ, l.17 ይእቲ, l.20 ይቤ, l.21
ይነድድ, l.22 እጠይቅ. All others read ደ.

Hooked alef transcribed እ throughout per key: ይርእየኒ, እሡዕ, እግዚእብሔር,
ኃጢእት, እዘዘኒ, እግዚእየ, እርእያሃ, ወወጽእ. Editor to normalise.

(l.3), ሜርዓት for መርዓት (l.18), መሢሕ for መሲሕ (l.20), ተትግበር for ትትገበር

Letterform facts:
- ሙ (l.18, l.20) is መ with a right trailing leg; at 2x it read as መ and the
  following ሴ (l.20) fused with it into a false ሓ. Count the leg at 3x+.
- ሴ (l.20) is the two-pronged ሰ with a small bottom-right loop; ሶ (l.18) has
  its ring on the right side. Distinct.
- The three-pronged arch ጠ and the two-pronged ሰ sit side by side in l.21;
  ጠ is the wider glyph.
- No line-end ፡ after ሙሴ (l.20) or ይእቲ (l.17), ክርስ (l.18); the scribe drops
  the word divider at the right margin when the line is full.
- Two red-dotted ። ornaments (l.4, l.20); l.12 ends with a plain ።. The
  l.4 and l.20 ones are ፡፡ pairs bracketed by red dots — kept as ።.
- Folio number "170" in a later European hand, top right.

Predictions registered before reading the neighbours:
- 169v must end on a completed clause (፡ or ።), since 170r l.1 opens a new
  sentence with በደግምስ "and the second time"; its last lines should be the
  first Sinai ascent or the first giving of the tabernacle instruction
  (Ex 24-25), first person, Moses.
- 170v l.1 must open with ስተ፡ completing እምውስተ "from within", followed by
  the source of the voice (እሳት or the l.21 noun), then the words spoken to
  Moses (Ex 3:5-6 shape: sandals, "I am the God of your fathers"), still
  first person.
