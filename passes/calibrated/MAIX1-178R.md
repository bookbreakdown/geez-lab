## WORK

Same work and same section as ff.176r-177v: the church-order canons on who may not
enter the sanctuary. This page is the ritual-impurity clause of that section, and the
evidence is in the ink rather than in resemblance:

1. **Continuity of subject and of the two-zone vocabulary.** ቤተ፡ክርስቲያን (church, ll.8,
   20) and ምሥዋዕ (altar/sanctuary, ll.1, 11, 23) are legislated as concentric zones: the
   impure person is barred from the church until clean (እስከ፡ይነጽሕ / እስከ፡ትነጽሕ), and a
   heavier penalty falls on one who enters the *sanctuary* concealing the condition from
   the priest. 177v was already legislating sanctuary entry (Num/Lev then 2 Sam 5:8).
2. **The diagnostic that excludes the Didascalia Apostolorum.** Didascalia ch. 26
   argues at length that the baptized keep no Levitical observance for menstruation or
   seminal emission. This page enforces exactly those exclusions: ዘይትከዓው፡ዘርኡ "whose
   seed is poured out" (ll.6-7), ብእሲት፡እንተ፡ደም፡ይውኅዝ "a woman whose blood flows"
   (ll.19-20). This agrees with f.174r's finding (ranks that exclude the Didascalia)
   from an independent direction.
3. **Divine first person, no apostolic first person.** The only "I" on the page is
   God's: ቀዱሳነ፡ኩኑ፡እስመ፡እነሂ፡ቀዱስ፡እነ (ll.5-6) and እኤመዓዖ … ወእቀምዎ፡ገጾየ፡ለዕሌሁ (ll.15-17),
   each framed by a citation formula (በከመ፡ይቤ፡ለሌሁ "as he himself said", l.4; ይቤ፡
   እግዚአብሔር "says God", ll.15-16). There is no "we command". The only legislating
   plural is the impersonal እዘዝዎ "as they commanded him" (ll.13-14).
4. **Two priest-mediated confession clauses**, ወኢነገሪ፡ለካህን (ll.12-13) and, by
   prediction, its feminine twin on 178v. Levitical purity administered by a single
   ካህን with an oracle-curse for concealment is the Arabic-derived strand of Ethiopic
   church order (the Clementine Kitāb al-Majāll material, which the Ethiopic Qalementos
   carries, and the purity canons of the Sinodos). On this page alone I cannot separate
   Qalementos from Sinodos; what the page does is *not contradict* the catalogue label,
   whereas ff.165v-175v did. Shared vocabulary has not been used as evidence.

Citation order on the page: (i) Lev 19:2 / 11:44 holiness formula (ll.5-6), then (ii)
the "I will set my face against him" formula (Lev 17:10 / 20:3-6) fused with (iii) Ps
33(34):17 "to root out their memory from the earth" (ወእቀምዎ፡ገጾየ፡ለዕሌሁ፡ከመ፡እስሩ፡እምድር፡
ዘክሩ, ll.16-18; the Ge'ez Psalter has ከመ፡ይሠርዎ፡ዝክሮሙ፡እምድር). (ii)+(iii) are delivered as
one oracle with one formula (ይቤ፡እግዚአብሔር), so the author may have known them as a
single text. Order: Lev holiness, Lev face, Ps memory.

Arabic loans: none claimed. No root identified for ተባወረ / ተዓውሮ (see NOTES).

## GIST

The sanctuary is pure and holy and the Holy Spirit dwells in it among the holy and the
pure, as he himself said, "Be holy, for I also am holy." A man with a seminal emission
may not enter the church until his seed is clean; if he [ተባወረ, unresolved: dares?]
enters the sanctuary hiding himself, not telling the priest what befell him as they
commanded, then "I will [be angry with him?], says God, and set my face against him to
root his memory out of the earth." Likewise a woman whose blood flows may not enter the
church until she is clean, and if she [ተባወረት] in [ተዓውሮ, unresolved] enters the
sanctuary hiding herself, what befell her, the sick[ness ... continues on 178v].
