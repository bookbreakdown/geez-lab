## WORK

**Not a church order, not Baruch, not the canons.** This is a parenetic homily
built on the Sermon on the Mount, spoken in **Christ's own first person** and
then turning to pastoral instruction. The catalogue label (Qalementos Book IV)
is neither confirmed nor excluded by the ink; what is excluded is everything
else established for ff.165–179.

Diagnostics from the ink, strongest first:

1. **First-person singular ስምየ "my name", twice (ll.2–3), inside a saying
   addressed to a 2pl "you" (ለዕሌክሙ, ዕሤትክሙ, ቢጽክሙ).** The speaker is Christ.
   Church orders speak as "we [the apostles] command"; canons use the
   third-person jussive with a penalty; neither says "for my name's sake".
   No "we command" anywhere on the page; no rank word; no penitential
   tariff; no numeral.
2. **The gospel material is quoted without a source-citation formula.** No
   "as it says in the Gospel", no "the Lord said". It is spoken, not cited.
   ll.1–5 run Matt 5:11–12 / Luke 6:22–23 (ወ…ውጽኡ፡ስመ… እኩየ = Luke 6:22
   "cast out your name as evil"; ወአንፈርዕፁ "leap for joy" = Luke 6:23;
   ብዙኅ፡ዕሤትክሙ፡በሰማያት = Matt 5:12). ll.6–9 run Matt 5:22 (ወይቤ፡…፡ለእኁሁ …
   ይረስሕ፡ለኵነኔ "says to his brother … is liable to the judgment"), with the
   homilist's expansion **ግሃነም፡እንተ፡እልባቲ፡ሙፃእ፡እስከ፡ለዓለም "Gehenna which has no
   exit for ever"** — a formula, repeated verbatim at ll.14–16, and not
   in Matthew.
3. **A shift from 2pl to 2sg at l.16** (ርኢክ, ርእሰክ, እዴክ, ይትከሀለክ), with
   imperatives (ረሲ, ንግሮ, ወናዝዞ) and negative jussives (ኢታስተኃቅሮ, ኢይቀብጽ):
   pastoral counsel on how to treat a brother seen sinning — do not
   despise him, lest he despair; reach him a hand out of the depth of sin;
   tell him and comfort him so that he turns. This is the Matt 18:15 /
   Gal 6:1 topos, in homily form.
4. **The forbidding clause ኢታውጽኡ፡ለቢጽክሙ፡እስመ፡እኩየ (ll.10–11)** turns the
   saying of l.1 back on the hearers ("do not bring out [an evil name]
   against your neighbour, for it is evil") and ranks lying against the
   neighbour above it with ወፈድፋደሰ "and even more". A homilist's move,
   not a canon's.

What it is consistent with: the f.167 "Two Ways" homily stratum, or the
dominical-discourse stratum read at ff.156–157 (gospel sayings, fig tree,
trumpet, weeping and gnashing), both of which put gospel material in a
preaching voice. The same expansion formula ("no exit for ever") would be
the test: if it recurs on ff.156–157 or f.167, this page belongs with them.

What it is not: (a) Didascalia / apostolic church order — no "we", no
ranks; (b) the penitential/divination canons of ff.176–179 — no tariff,
no numeral, no ርኩስ፡ወሰዑብ formula; (c) Rest of the Words of Baruch — no
narrative, no names; (d) the paradise catalogue — no list.

Citations, in order of appearance: Matt 5:11 (with Luke 6:22 wording) →
Matt 5:12 (with Luke 6:23 ወአንፈርዕፁ) → Matt 5:22 → [homilist's Gehenna
formula] → Matt 5:22 again (ll.13–16) → [Gehenna formula again] → Matt
18:15 / Gal 6:1 topos (ll.16–23). All unmarked; none introduced by a
formula. No Arabic loans claimed: nothing on this page needs one.

## GIST

Christ speaking: "…against you, and cast out your name as evil for my
name's sake, [for my name's sake,] while they lie: rejoice and leap for
joy, for great is your reward in heaven. But if one [steals?] and says
'[fool?]' to his brother, he will be condemned and is liable to the
judgment of Gehenna, which has no exit for ever. Do not bring [an evil
name] out against your neighbour, for it is evil; and even more he who
lies against his neighbour and says '[…]' and […] while he […]: he will
be condemned in Gehenna, which has no exit for ever. And if you see one
who sins among your brothers, do not despise him, lest he lose his hope;
rather make yourself as one who [dives?] and give him your hand out of
the depth of the [?] of sin; as you are able, tell him and comfort him,
so that he turns away [from…]."

## CONFIDENCE

- **ll.1–8 (first third): high.** Matt 5:11–12 and 5:22 anchor nearly
  every word. Two words are not resolved: ሠሪቀ (l.6) and እብጾ (l.7); see
  NOTES. ወኤውጽኡ (l.1) is read letter by letter.
- **ll.9–16 (second third): mid.** The Gehenna formula (twice) and
  ወፈድፋደሰ are secure; l.12 end + l.13 (ይጼ|?ል፡ወይሠሪ፡እንዘ፡ይሠሉ፡እው) is the
  worst stretch on the page and is transcribed letter by letter without
  a reading. l.14 እስት is ink, flagged.
- **ll.17–23 (last third): mid-high.** The pastoral counsel reads
  continuously; ትጥምቁ (l.20) and ሠርመ (l.21) are unplaced, and the final
  word's third and fourth letters depend on this hand's ኀ/ኂ marking.

## NOTES

Letter-level flags, in line order. "Ink" is what is written; "expected"
is the standard form the sense wants. Nothing has been normalised.

- **l.1 ወኤውጽኡ.** Second glyph is አ with a closed ring at the lower right of
  the leg — this hand's ኤ (compare ዘይኤብስ l.17, same ring). Sense wants
  ያውጽኡ / ያወጽኡ "they bring out" (Luke 6:22). Neither ኤ nor the hand's
  ኢ (open curl) gives that; transcribed as the ink shows, ኤ, and flagged.
  Third glyph is ወ with a right tick = ው. Last glyph ኡ (hook down-right).
- **ll.2–3 እኩየ፡በእንተ፡ስምየ / እኩየ፡በእንተ፡ስምየ.** Dittography: the scribe wrote
  the phrase twice. Both instances are on the page; neither is struck.
- **l.3 ይሌስ|ው (l.4).** Second glyph is ለ with a ring at lower right = ሌ,
  not ሐ. Sense wants ይሔስዉ "they lie" (Matt 5:11 እንዘ፡ይሔስዉ); the root is
  confirmed on the same page by ዘሐሰወ (l.12). Ink ሌ, expected ሔ. Final
  ው for ዉ, as this scribe writes elsewhere.
- **l.4 ወእንፈርዕፁ.** All alefs on this page are the hooked form; transcribed
  እ per the key (expected ወአንፈርዕፁ). Same rule applied at l.10 (እታውጽኡ
  for ኢታውጽኡ), l.19 (እለ for አላ "but"), l.22 (ጢእት for ጢአት).
- **l.5 ብዙኅ.** ኀ-family glyph; order not clearly marked. ኅ is the sense.
- **l.6 ሠሪቀ.** ሠ + ረ with a dark (not red) tick = ሪ + ቀ. Unplaced. If the
  tick is stray, ሠረቀ "he stole" — "but if he steals and says…" — which
  sits oddly before Matt 5:22 but is grammatical. Left as ink.
- **l.7 እብጾ.** Third glyph is a ጸ-shape with a ring at top right; ጾ or ፆ.
  Slot is Matt 5:22's insult to the brother, where the standard text has
  ኣብድ "fool" (or ረቃ). ብ is a clean box. Transcribed as ink with the ring
  read as ጾ; the ድ this hand writes (ደ with a foot hook) does not look
  like this. Flagged for the expert; a variant insult is possible.
- **l.7 ይትኳነን** (also l.14). ከ-family glyph with a ring; standard
  ይትኴነን. Ink kept.
- **l.8 ወይሬስሕ.** Ring on the ረ is in the dark ink, not a red dot; ሬ as
  written. Expected ይረስሕ "is liable" (Matt 5:22 ይረስሖ፡ለኵነኔ).
- **l.9, l.15 ሙፃዓ.** Last glyph is the 9-shaped bowl with right descender
  = ዐ/ዓ in this hand. Expected ሙፃእ "exit". Written identically both
  times, so it is the scribe's form, not a slip.
- **l.10 እታውጽኡ.** ታ (ተ with a right foot hook), ው (ወ + tick), ኡ. Read
  as ኢታውጽኡ "do not bring out" (2pl negative), answering ወ…ውጽኡ of l.1.
- **l.11 ወፈድፋደሰ.** Third glyph has the foot hook = ድ; fifth is the ደ/ይ
  shape, read ደ on sense per the key. ፈድፋደ "even more".
- **l.12 ለዕለ.** Middle glyph is the open-topped bowl (ዕ), not ሰ. = ላዕለ
  "against", with the scribe's usual sadis for ላ.
- **l.12–13 ይጼ|?ል፡ወይሠሪ፡እንዘ፡ይሠሉ፡እው.** The crux. l.12 ends ይ + ጸ with ring
  (ጼ). l.13 opens with a two-legged ል-like glyph carrying a curl at top
  left — not a plain ል, not ሐ; marked ?. Then ል, ፡, ወ, ይ, ሠ, ረ with tick
  (ሪ), ፡, እንዘ, ፡, ይ, ሠ, ለ with right tick (ሉ), ፡, hooked alef, ወ with
  tick (ው). No reading is offered. ወይሠሪ could be ሠረየ (absolve / charm)
  and ይሠሉ is unplaced; the pair is not smoothed into anything.
- **l.14 እስት.** Glyph after ስ is cruciform with a top-left tick and a
  foot: this hand's ት, not the serifed ከ of l.15 እስከ. Expected እስከ (the
  clause is the Gehenna formula, whose second እስከ follows at l.15). Ink
  kept; likely an anticipatory slip.
- **l.16 ዘርኢክ.** ዘ is the plain H (not the curl-topped ዝ). ኢ has the
  left curl. -ክ for -ከ throughout this page (ርኢክ, ርእሰክ, እዴክ, ይትከሀለክ,
  እምእኀዊክ): scribal, consistent, not normalised.
- **l.17 እምእኀዊክ.** ኀ-family glyph; order not confidently marked. = እምአኀዊከ.
- **l.17–18 ኢተ|ስተኃቀሮ.** = ኢታስተኃቅሮ "do not despise him", with the scribe's
  sadis for ታ and ቅ. First glyph has the left curl = ኢ.
- **l.19 እለ፡ረሲ.** = አላ፡ረሲ "but make". ረሲ: ረ + ሲ (tick).
- **l.20 ትጥምቁ.** ጥ has the T top; final ቀ with right hook = ቁ. Unplaced.
  If ጠመቀ "dip/plunge" it would be "as one who dives", which fits the
  next clause (a hand out of the depth). Not smoothed.
- **l.21 ሠርመ.** ሠ, ር, መ — clear letters, no word. Governs ኃጢአት. Collect
  for the expert. Not an Arabic loan claim: no root offered.
- **l.23 ይትገኂሠ.** After ገ, an ኀ-glyph with the left curl the key assigns
  to third order (ኂ); final glyph ሠ where standard ይትገኀሥ wants ሥ. The
  order marks are the least reliable thing in this hand; sense is
  "turn away". Ink kept.
- **Punctuation.** The scribe's ፡ is often drawn with the dots offset like
  a semicolon (ll.1, 3, 12); all are ፡. ። at ll.1 and 16 is the four-dot
  cross with red dots and is kept as ።.
- **Margin.** Left margin carries the grey ghost-offset of the facing
  page (180v) down the full height. Not text; not transcribed.
- **Join predictions (registered before reading the neighbours).**
  180v must end with the Matt 5:11 chain ending right before ላዕሌክሙ —
  i.e. "…and speak all evil" (ወይነብቡ፡ኵሎ፡እኩየ or the like), possibly with
  ይሰድዱክሙ before it. 181v must open with the complement of ይትገኀሥ —
  "from his sin" (እምኃጢአቱ) or "from evil" — and continue the 2sg pastoral
  counsel, plausibly with a "but if he does not listen" (ወእመሰ፡ኢሰምዐክ)
  step in the Matt 18:15–17 pattern.
  **Falsifiers:** 180v ending in a rank list, a penalty tariff, a numeral
  or a Baruch narrative; 181v opening with anything other than a
  complement of "turn away"; the Gehenna formula "no exit for ever"
  turning out to be a canon's penalty clause elsewhere in the codex
  rather than a homilist's flourish. Any of these and the "Christ-voice
  homily" identification is wrong.
