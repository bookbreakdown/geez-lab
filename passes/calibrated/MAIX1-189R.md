## WORK

**Voice: first-person-plural author addressing a second-person-plural
congregation, with the author claiming prior legislative authority. Citation
habit: zero formula markers; three scriptural echoes, all woven unmarked. On
the codex's one surviving boundary marker this side is HOMILY/EXHORTATION
type, not canon type — but it quotes the canon folios' own formulae back at the
reader, so it is the exhortation that belongs to those canons, not a foreign
work. A named work cannot be fixed from this side.**

Evidence from the ink:

- **Addressee "you (pl.)"**: ፍቀርክሙ (l.2), እዘዝናክሙ (l.4), ወኢትት…ው (l.5),
  ወሀበክሙ (l.6), ያደንግፅክሙ (l.7). Speaker "we": ዘእዘዝናክሙ "which WE commanded
  you" (l.4). Vocative opening ሙ፡እእኃዊነ፡ፍቁራን "…O our beloved brothers" (l.1;
  the vocative ኦ is the plain hooked alef, as the key records). This is the
  same persona as 187v (ፀርነ "our enemy", ዑቁ) and 188r.
- **Zero citation formulae.** No ይቤ, no በከመ፡ይቤ, no ወካዕበ፡ይቤ, no ጽሑፍ on the
  side. Nothing is introduced.
- **Three echoes, all woven into the preacher's own syntax with no marker**,
  in this order:
  1. ll.6–9 "let them not frighten you, neither by vision nor by writing nor
     by [ተኀቢት] nor by falsehood nor by divination" — the false-prophet /
     Deut 13:1–3 dreamer topos. The phrase ኢበራዕይ is the SAME phrase 187v
     l.6 uses (ኤያስሕቱክሙ፡ኢበራዕ/ይ), tgrep ኢበራዕ = 1 hit, 187V l.6. Same
     preacher, same stock sentence, two folios apart.
  2. ll.12–13 እንዘ፡ይፈቀዱ፡ይጥበቡ፡እብድ "while they wish to be wise, [being]
     fool(s)" — Rom 1:22, unmarked.
  3. ll.13–15 "for in the way of ቀያል (Cain) they walked; but the righteous
     walked in the way of እቢል (Abel)" — Jude 11 (ፍኖተ፡ቃየል), unmarked, and
     extended with an Abel antithesis Jude does not have. Both names are in
     this scribe's nonstandard spelling (ቀያል for ቃየል, እቢል for አቤል); tgrep
     ቃየል = 0, እቢል = 0, so the pair is new to the drafted range.
- **But the side quotes the canon folios' own wording**:
  - l.4 ዘእዘዝናክሙ = 171V l.9 ግ፡ቀኖና፡ዘእዘዝናክሙ (tgrep, 1 hit, verbatim).
  - ll.22–23 በሕግ፡ቀኖኖ፡ዘቀደሠት፡ቤተ፡ክርስቲያን = 174R l.6 ሕግ፡ቀኖና፡ዘቀደስተ፡ቤተ፡
    (tgrep ቀኖና, 7 hits; 174R l.6 is the same clause). "The law of the canon
    which the holy church sanctified" is the compiler's fixed name for his
    own rule-book.
  - So the "we" who "commanded you before" (ቀደሙ, l.4) is the legislator of
    ff.168–181, now in peroration mode. This side reads as the closing
    exhortation OF the canon collection — not as a second work — while
    exhibiting the homily citation habit. That is consistent with a single
    compiler who legislates with formula-marked citations and exhorts with
    woven ones; it does not by itself prove two works.
- **Neither ጴጥሮስ nor ቀሌምንጦስ is named.** Consistent with the zero count.
- **No rank vocabulary** (no ኤጲስ ቆጶስ, ቀሳውስት, ደያቆናት). Vocabulary of

## GIST

The preacher addresses "our beloved brothers who love the ordinances of the
holy church", telling them to hold firm to what "we commanded you before" and
not to turn away from the gift God gave them: let no one frighten them by
vision, writing, [ተኀቢት], falsehood or divination, for such people are
[መሐድምት] of sorcery who teach so as to lead their companions astray, wishing
to be wise while being fools; they walked in the way of Cain, whereas the
righteous walked in the way of Abel, in gentleness and humility, and learned
from God and from the word of the scriptures [and the ?4 …]. The sinners hear
it but do not keep the word of the scriptures, for they walked in Satan's way;
and those who do not walk in the law of the canon which the holy church
sanctified have become outcasts from the law and from righteousness.

## NUMERALS

**One candidate figure, l.18, between ወ and ዓቀቢ.** It carries a bar ABOVE and
a bar BELOW (both present at 4x and 6x — crops `189R-z-l18.png`,
`189R-z-l18-6x.png`), so by the key's load-bearing rule it is a numeral, not a
letter. The body is a vertical stem with a high horizontal crossbar and two
lobes below the bar, i.e. a cross standing over a double bowl. It is NOT the
plain I-beam ፲, NOT the key's ፳ (bar + bowl + leg), NOT the three-pronged
፴/፵, and NOT the closed bowl ፻. By shape family it is closest to the
δ-derived ፬, and I record it as [፬?] with the value UNVERIFIED — no ፬ has been
read anywhere in the 64 drafted folios (tgrep ፬ = 0), so there is no anchor in
this hand to measure against. Sense does not help: ወ[፬?]ዓቀቢ "and the
four(?) keepers(?)" after "they learned the word of the scriptures" is
possible but not compelling. The editor should treat value as open.

No other glyph on the side carries both bars. The I-beam ነ at l.1 (ኃዊነ), l.4
(ጽንዑ… ና), l.16 (እግ), l.24 (ውጹአነ) are all bar-less letters. The ፉ/፪-type
loops at l.19 (ዎ), l.20 (ቡዎ) are bar-less letters.
