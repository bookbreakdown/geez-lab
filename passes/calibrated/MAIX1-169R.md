## WORK

Not the Rest of the Words of Baruch, and not a narrative. It is apostolic
church-order discourse in the first person plural, with a single narrator
("my Lord said to US", l.4-5 ወእግዚእየኒ፡ይቤለነ) speaking for the apostles as a
body. The evidence from the ink:

- ll.5-8 quote Mt 10:40 / Lk 10:16 in the Ethiopic Gospel wording
  (ዘኪያክሙ፡ሰምዓ ... ዘኪያክሙ፡ተወክፈ ... ለዘፈነወኒ), then Jn 10:30
  (ወእነ፡፩ንሕነ "and I, we are one"), both put in the mouth of the Lord
  addressing "you" (the apostles).
- ll.11-12 and 16-17 ንቀውም፡ሎሙ፡በስማያት "we stand for them in the heavens",
  twice, is the binding-in-heaven idea of Mt 18:18 applied by the apostles
  to those who keep "the canon of the holy Church" (l.15 ቀናና፡ዘቀዱስተ፡ቤተ፡
  ክርስቲያን).
- ll.17-18 እስመ፡ንሕነ፡መጋብያነ፡ሠርዓቱ፡ለእግዚእብሔር "for we are the stewards of
  God's ordinance", ll.19-24 a Moses/Levi proof-text for handing on the law.

This is the same voice as f.167 (Two Ways / narrow gate, first person
apostolic). The frame "my Lord said to us" is Peter's in the Clementine
literature, so the catalogue's Qalementos label may be right for this stretch
even though it was wrong for ff.165v-166v; but the Ethiopic Didascalia and the
Sinodos apostolic canons use the same "we the apostles" frame with the same
proof-texts, and nothing on this page pins one against the others. Work:
apostolic church-order discourse, exact work unidentified.

## GIST

"...our word. And concerning this we ask for them from him who sent me, that
we may [understand?] and teach them the tidings of the holy gospel. And my
Lord said to us: he who hears you, he who receives you, receives him who sent
me, my Father; for my Father and I are one; and those who hear you hear me.
Hear the congregation of the holy Church." We stand for them in the heavens,
because of this our word which they heard, and they kept it and made [?],
while not abandoning the canon of the holy Church; we stand for them in the
heavens. For we are the stewards of God's ordinance; let them become children
of God; we ordained by our word that we entrust them with our God, as God said
to Moses: "I entrusted them to Jacob (and) Levi..."

## NOTES

Unresolved words, transcribed letter by letter and NOT smoothed:

- l.3 ንለብክ — ን ለ ብ(box with top-left tick) ክ. Sense wants ንለብው "that we
  may understand"; fourth glyph is ከ-family with mid tick, not two-loop ው.
- l.7 ተወክሮ — fourth glyph is a single-bowl ረ base with a ring = ሮ. Gospel
  wording wants ተወክፎ. Ink says ሮ.
- l.7 and l.8 እሉየ (twice) — second glyph is ለ with a right descender = ሉ,
  not a box. The Mt 10:40 + Jn 10:30 sense wants አቡየ "my Father" both
  times. Scribe writes ሉ consistently; either this hand's ቡ is open-bottomed
  like ሉ (a new letterform fact if so) or the exemplar had ሉ.
- l.10 ዓኀበራ — first glyph is the single 9-shaped bowl with right descender,
  identical to the ዓ of ወዓቀቡ in l.13, not two-bowl ማ; second glyph is the
  stem-with-top-left-hook ኀ of l.2 and l.22. Sense wants ማኅበራ "the
  congregation of (the holy Church)". Ink says ዓኀበራ.
- l.12-13 ይእ|ቲ — the ይ carries a dark ring at top right (not red), so the
  ink could be ዬ; read ይ (ይእቲ "that (fem.)", with ቀለነ) on sense. ይ/ደ
  collision also applies.
- l.14 ጥቁሙ — first glyph two bowls with a short vertical stroke on top (ጥ
  rather than ሞ: it is a stroke, not a ring, at 5x); second glyph a bowl
  left of a stem with a lower-right foot, read ቁ, second choice ዋ/ቍ. Word
  unplaced. Context: "and they made ? while not abandoning the canon".
- l.24 ለያቁቆ፡ሌዊ — after ለያ two ቀ-base glyphs: the first with a lower-right
  foot (ቁ), the second with a top-right ring (ቆ), then ፡ then ሌዊ. Sense
  wants ለያዕቆብ "to Jacob"; no ዕ and no ብ box in the ink. Flag hard.
- l.22-23 ይቤ|ሉ — ሉ (ለ + descender, no ring). Sense wants ይቤሎ "he said to
  him"; note this is the third place on the page where ink ሉ stands where
  sense wants another letter (ቡ ×2, ሎ ×1).
