## WORK

**Third-person church-order canon with formula-cited Psalms — the canon
stratum (ff.168–181, 184v), NOT the second-person homily of ff.181v–183v.**
It continues f.184v across a split word (see NOTES, join). Evidence from the
ink:

1. **Voice.** Every finite verb outside the quotations is third person:
   ይትመዓዲ(?) l.1-2, ይጠናቀቁ l.2, ይትፈሥሑ l.4, ይሴባሕ l.5, ይደሉ፡ይግበሩ l.16-17,
   ርኅቀ l.18, ኢይበጽሕዎ l.19, በጽሐ l.24. The only second-person forms on the
   page (ባርክዎ l.8, ለከ l.11, እሴብሐከ l.13, ተዘከር l.14) are inside the three
   Psalm quotations. No vocative, no -ክሙ, no 2pl imperative. f.184v predicted
   exactly this ("third-person jussives with no second-person address") —
   confirmed.
2. **Case-law frame.** l.18 እመሰ፡ርኅቀ፡… "and if … is far …" — the እመ(ሰ)
   protasis of the canon formula, same frame as 184v l.7-9 እመቦ፡….
3. **Citations, in ink order, ALL carrying a formula:**
   - l.6-7 በከመ፡ይቤ፡ዳዊት፡ነቢይ፡ → Ps 67:27 (LXX; 68:26 Heb.) "In the
     congregations bless God…" (ll.7-9).
   - l.10-11 ወካዕበ፡ይቤ፡በውስተ፡ካልዕ፡መዝሙር፡ → Ps 34:18 (LXX; 35:18) "I will
     give thanks to thee, O Lord, in the great congregation; I will praise
     thee among a mighty (ክቡድ = βαρεῖ) people" (ll.11-14).
   - l.14 ወዓዲ፡ይቤ፡ → Ps 73:2 (LXX; 74:2) "Remember thy congregation which
     thou didst acquire of old" (ll.14-15).
   Formula-marked, named source, "in another psalm" — the canon-folio habit
   (በከመ፡ይቤ at 156R, 157R, 164R, 168V, 178R; ወካዕበ፡ይቤ at 170R, 180V), not
   the unmarked weaving of the homily folios. ወዓዲ፡ይቤ is a new formula
   variant, 0 hits in drafted folios.
4. **Author's self-reference shared with 180R.** l.16 ወበእንተ፡ኵሉ፡ዝዘከርነ
   "and concerning all that we have mentioned" = 180R l.24 በኵሉ፡ዘዘከርነ. Same
   phrase, same first-plural editorial voice, in the canon block. That is a
   compositional link, not a resemblance.
5. **Subject matter.** ll.16-24: a synod/assembly (ገባኤ = ጉባኤ) is to be
   held; if the bishop's congregation is far so that they cannot reach him
   except in half a year or a year, or because of the sea, or because of
   [ውኢዝ ነኪረ ምት — unresolved], or because of the distance of the road … and
   when he arrives to him, he … — the "twice-yearly synod / distance excuse"
   material of the conciliar canons (Apost. can. 37, Nicaea 5, Antioch 20,
   and the Arabic pseudo-Nicene canons), which in Ethiopic circulates in the
   Senodos. f.184v's see-list (Rome, Alexandria seat of Mark, Antioch seat of
   Luke) is likewise Arabic-Nicene material. So the WORK is most plausibly a
   Senodos-type canon collection of Arabic derivation; which exact canon I
   cannot fix from one side. What I can fix: same stratum as 184v and 180r.
6. **A diagnostic worth testing (Arabic Vorlage vs. Ge'ez Psalter):** the Ps
   67:27 quotation reads ወለእምላከነ፡በእንቅዕተ፡እስራኤል "and to our God, in the
   fountains of Israel". If the Ge'ez Psalter has ወለእግዚእ፡እምአንቅዕተ (κύριον ἐκ
   πηγῶν), then this compiler translated the quotation fresh from his
   (Arabic) exemplar rather than copying the Psalter — the mark of a
   translated canon collection. If the Psalter also reads ወለአምላክነ, the
   diagnostic is void.

Arabic loans: none claimed. ገባኤ/ጉባኤ is native (ገብአ). Nothing on this page
needs an Arabic root.

## GIST

Assemble and be diligent about the work of the holy church, so that they may
rejoice in good works and there the name of God is praised — as David the
prophet said, "In the congregations bless God, and our God from the fountains
of Israel", and again in another psalm, "I will give thanks to thee, O Lord,
in the great congregation, and praise thee among a mighty people", and further,
"Remember thy congregation which thou didst acquire of old." Concerning all we
have mentioned, it is fitting that they hold a synod [of three days?]; and if
the congregation of our father the bishop is so far that they cannot reach him
except in half a year or a year, or because of the sea, or because of [a
river? — unresolved], or because of the distance of the road, then when he
arrives at him, he … (continues 185v).
