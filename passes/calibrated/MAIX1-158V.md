## GIST

"Hear from me: let them not [ኢያስቱቱ, unresolved]; keep [them] from their negligence(?)." And if you do what I commanded you, you have no sin, for on your account all keep vigil(?) every day and night and ask God for the forgiveness of their sins. And you, deacons, run and hasten in your ministry, as Stephen the first martyr, head of the deacons who were of the house(?), who had no hand [laid?] where he joined(?) and nowhere offered sacrifice(?); and likewise let the deacons run in the church, [in] its ministry. And that Stephen, mighty(?) and full of the Holy Spirit, when they stoned him with stones, saw the [gates?] of heaven opened and the Son of God at the right hand of the Father, and he [continues on 159r].
