## GIST

The page opens mid-sentence, closing a clause about "what they did at first", then: "For God said, I [verb uncertain] the penitents who are received for repentance; the sinner, if he repents, is like a virgin (?)". Then a run of the formula "unclean and abominable is he who...": he who swears by the name of God falsely, and especially kings and rulers who eat [ma'kota, uncertain] and, further, the Christian who speaks an evil word and is [cast out?] from the church; let them give them repentance and let them enter and be [re-admitted?] and draw near. Unclean and abominable is he who goes to his brother's wife while his brother is alive. Unclean and abominable is he who perverts justice for the orphan and does not deliver the oppressed from the hand of him who wrongs him; sinners and [oppressors?] love plunder, and David too said, "Deliver them from the hand of sinners". Unclean and abominable is he who steals [continues on next page].

## NOTES

Uncertain readings, in descending order of doubt:
- l.2 end / l.3 start: "እግ | ኦሙ". The line-3 opener is a bowl with a stem, the shape of ኦ. "እግኦሙ" is not a word; possibilities are a skipped syllable (እግዚኦሙ?) or a misread of the l.2 final glyph (ግ vs ኅ). Left as ink.
- l.3 "ዘይትዌከፉ": 4th glyph is a bowl with a ring (ዌ), not a stemmed ቀ; alternative ቄ. Last glyph read ፉ; alternative ፎ. Sense "who are received" supports ዌ...ፉ.
- l.5 "ዮንግል": first glyph is the ደ/ይ shape with a ring at top-left. Sense wants ድንግል (virgin); the scribe's ድ elsewhere on this page (l.7 ወፈድፋደሰ) has a foot hook, not a ring, so the ring form is transcribed as ዮ and flagged.
- l.9 "ማዕኮተ": third glyph is the ከ-family with a curled top-left; ኮ/ከ undecidable. Word not identified.
- l.10 end "ወይቁዑ" + l.11 "ም": fourth glyph is a rounded bowl with ring; ዑ or ፁ. Word not identified; context wants something like "cast out (ውጹእ) from the church".
- l.13 "ትከነሱሂ": read as ink; root not identified.
- l.20 "ወመጻብሐን": ጸ/ጻ order unclear on the third glyph; word not identified (a class of wrongdoers paired with sinners).
- l.22 "ፍዎሙ": the ዎ is drawn as a ring-topped bowl closer to ዖ; ዎ chosen on sense (Ps 82:4 አንግፍዎሙ). Compare l.12 ሀብዎሙ where ዎ is a ወ with a ring above.
- l.17: a large ink blot after "ዘይግምጽ ፡" occupies one letter-space before ፍትሐ. Could cover a deleted letter or a ።. Marked [blot].
- l.1 first glyph read ን (top-left flag on a stem); the preceding word is on 160r.

New letterform facts for the key:
- ት (sadis) is distinguished from ተ by a short tick at the top-left of the cross, consistently (ትከነሱሂ, ብእሲት, ፍትሐ, ዘይትዔገሎ, ወዳዊትኒ). Plain cross = ተ.
- ኂ vs ኁ: in እኂሁ (ll. 15, 16, sense "his brother", standard እኁሁ) the right leg of ኀ curls to the left at the foot, the same left-curl the key records for ኢ. This hand marks third order on ኀ the same way as on አ; expect ኂ where standard has ኁ, or treat the curl as this scribe's u-marker. Undecided; two instances on this page.
- The ከ family carries a short horizontal serif at the top-left of the stem (ከመ l.5, ትከነሱሂ l.13, ክርስቲያን l.11). That serif is not a ring and does not make it ኮ.
- ግ is a stem with a closed loop at top-left and a foot; distinct from ዓ (open arm) on the same page (ወዓዲ l.9).
- ዎ has two shapes on one page: ወ with a ring above (l.12) and a ring-topped bowl like ዖ (l.22).
- ፉ (l.3, l.19 ግፉዕ) is a loop with a bottom-right tail and can be mistaken for the ፪ loop; numerals on this page would need bounding bars, and there are none.
- Red-dotted ። occurs at ll. 1, 19, 22; plain ። at l.7.
