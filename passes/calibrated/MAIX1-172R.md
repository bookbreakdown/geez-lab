## WORK

Unidentified as a named work. The page is a discourse on God's providence
in creation turning into paraenesis for "the children of the church"
(ደቂቀ ቤት ክርስቲያን, l.19-20). It continues the apostolic church-order
material reported for ff.168-171 rather than Qalementos Book IV.

Evidence from the ink:

- ll.6-9 ታውጽእ ... ሐመልማለ ሣዕር ወፍሬያት ዘበበዘመዱ: the wording of Genesis
  1:11-12 (ታውጽእ ምድር ሐመልማለ ሣዕር ... ዘበበዘመዱ), used as a catalogue of
  creation, not quoted as scripture. ዘበበዘመዱ "each after its kind" recurs
  three times (ll.8-9, 10, 12).
- ll.1-2 ወይሁብ ሲሳዮ ለዘስዓሎ "he gives his food to whoever asks him" is the
  Psalm 104(103):27-28 / 147(146):9 motif; ll.2-6 rain, clouds and seasons
  is the Acts 14:17 cluster (rains, fruitful seasons, food and gladness:
  ሲሳይ, ጊዜያት, ፍሬያት, ይትፈሥሑ all occur here). Echoes, not citations.
- The creation-catalogue-then-exhortation shape is that of the
  Apostolic Constitutions VII.34-35 / VIII.12 prayers and of 1 Clement 20,
  which fits a Sinodos-type church-order text. Hypothesis only.
- No named speaker, no scriptural formula (ከመ ይቤ / ዘይቤ) on this side.

## NOTES

Unresolved words (ink transcribed letter by letter, not smoothed):

- l.1 ትግባሩ: first glyph has the top-left tick this hand uses for ት. Sense
  wants ተግባሩ "his work". Either the tick is not diagnostic on this page
  (see the ት remark below) or the word is a continuation from 171v.
- l.2 ስዓሎ for ሰአሎ: sadis ስ and ዓ for the glottal አ. Same ዓ-for-አ in l.3
  ወየዓዝዘሙ, presumably ወይእዝዞሙ "and he commands them". New for the key:
  ዐ/ዓ is written for etymological አ in verbs (ስዓሎ, ዓዝዘ) on this page.
- l.3 ክረምት: first glyph is the same "ኻ"-like form as the ክ of ክርስቲያን
  (ll.20, 24) and of l.15, so ክ not ኅ. The ረ ends in a downward base
  serif that could be ሪ's tick; the same serif on ገብረ l.16. Neither has a
  clean ረ on this page to compare against; transcribed ረ, flagged.
- l.4 ይዕቁሩ: ዕ (bowl with hook) + ቁ (ቀ with ring right of the stem) + ሩ.
  No standard word. Context "commands the clouds to __ and carry": possibly
  ዐቈረ "dam up, hold (water)" (Leslau). Hypothesis. Not an Arabic loan I can
  name.
- l.5 ወይስዲ: ስ is the λ-with-arm sadis form, then ዲ (ደ/ይ silhouette with
  tick). Standard ሰደደ "drive" has imperfect ይሰድድ; ይስዲ looks like the
  Amharic-shaped ይስድ "sends". Flagged; not smoothed.
- l.6 ወእኤዝዘ: two alefs, the second with a bottom-right tick (ኤ), then ዝ
  (curled H) + ዘ (plain H). Sense wants ወይእዝዝ "and he commands". Ink kept.
- l.9, l.11 ኍልቁ: the ኍ is a T-shaped stem with a bottom-right hook, not
  the ኀ-with-ring of print. Read as ኍ on the sense ዘእልቦ ኍልቁ "without
  number", which the key's attested እልቦ supports.
- l.11 ቈኍልቁ: an extra ቀ-with-ring glyph stands before ኍልቁ where l.9 has
  none. Probably a false start on ቁ that was not deleted (no deletion
  strokes visible). Transcribed as written.
- l.14-15 ወባር|ክ: ባ (በ with leg), ር, then line-initial ክ, then ፡. Not a
  season name I know; ወባርኮ "and blessed it" would need a ring the ink does
  not have. Unresolved.
- l.16 ጸቃውዒ: last glyph is a hooked bowl with a bottom-right tick (ዒ; ዕ
  possible). "Honeycomb(s)"? is a guess from context (made from the leaf,
  with sweet flowers). Collect for the expert.
- l.16 እምቈጽል: the ቈ is drawn as ቀ with a ring at right, i.e. the same
  shape as ቁ in ኍልቁ. Read ቈጽል "leaf" on sense.
- l.17 ጸጊያት: first glyph lacks the tick that ጽ carries in ll.9, 11
  (ወጽጊያት). Ink ጸ; expected ጽጊያት. Also ጊ (left foot) for ጌ throughout:
  ጽጊያት for ጽጌያት.
- l.19 ለደቂቀ: the second glyph is the ይ/ደ silhouette (key rule), and only
  ደ yields a word: ደቂቀ ቤት ክርስቲያን "the children of the church". The third
  glyph is ቀ with a bottom-right hook (ቂ). A first pass read this as
  ለሂቄቀ; the key's ይ/ደ rule is what resolved it.
- l.21 በመቃዕሊሆሙ: the third glyph is a single bowl on a stem with a right
  leg (ቃ), not this hand's two-loop ወ with a leg. Sense wants
  በመዋዕሊሆሙ "in their days". Ink kept.
- l.22-23 ዘበኀ|ለሓ: the last glyph of l.22 is the ን/ኀ collision the key
  warns about (top-left tick, stem, right foot); ን cannot be excluded.
  ለሓ is ለ + ሐ-with-leg. No word found for ኀለሓ / ንለሓ. Unresolved.
- l.23 ወይስቀቡ: third glyph is the sadis ስ form, not the hooked bowl ዕ.
  Sense wants ወይዕቀቡ "and let them keep". Ink kept.
- l.23 ሕጋ for ሕገ: ጋ is the 7-shape with leg.
- l.24 ቀድስት for ቅድስት (key already has ቀድስት); the ድ has a bottom-right
  stroke that could be read ዱ.

Letterform facts for the key:

- On this page every ተ-family glyph in ት position carries the top-left
  tick, and so do the two where ተ is expected (ትግባሩ l.1, ለቤት l.19/24).
  Either the tick is not diagnostic for ት vs ተ in this stint, or the scribe
  writes ቤት ክርስቲያን with sadis. Do not rely on the tick alone.
- ቢ and ቤ are not separable here: ll.19 and 23 both show a box with a
  single bottom-right tick. Transcribed ቤ; the key's ቢተ shows the other
  choice has been made before.
- ው mid-line is the two-loop form with a right tail (ll.13, 17, 19
  ውእቱ); ወ has no tail. Reliable on this page.
- Punctuation: l.15 has two three-dot marks (፡ with a third dot below);
  transcribed ፤. l.17 and l.23 have ። written as two spaced pairs.
- Margins: the left margin carries ghost-offset glyphs from 171v along the
  full height; nothing there is text. Folio number 172 top right.
- No red ink anywhere on this side; no numerals, no bounding bars.

Nonstandard spellings attested here: ስዓሎ, ዓዝዘሙ, እልቦ, እዘዘ (for አዘዘ),
ጽጊያት/ጸጊያት for ጽጌያት, ሕጋ, ቀድስት, ፀደያ, መጸወ, ንጹሓ, ቤት for ቤተ.
