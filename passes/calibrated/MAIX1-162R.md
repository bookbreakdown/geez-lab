## NOTES

Uncertainties, in order of weight:

- **ሰዑብ (ll.1, 4, 14, 21)** — the page's refrain, always paired with ርኩስ ("unclean and X is he who..."). Four clear instances: ወ + three-pronged ሰ on a base (not ጸ, no bowl) + ዑ + box ብ. Not a Ge'ez word I can place. Candidates for the editor: (a) ስዑር "deposed" with ብ for ር — but the box is unambiguous all four times; (b) ጽዑብ "hard/grievous" with ሰ for ጸ; (c) an Arabic loan from the Kitāb al-Majāll source. Read the ink as ሰዑብ.
- **l.3 ወክልብ** — ወ + ክ (ከ-family with the top-left serif) + ል + box. At strip resolution the box looked like ኤ; at 2.5x it is a box. ወከልብ "and a dog" is the only lexical reading; flagged.
- **l.8 first glyph** — a boxy compound (looks like ጅ/ጃ or a decorated ቀ-form similar to l.9's ቁ) followed by ሚህር. Left as ?. Context "ይሢም በዘ?ሚህር ላዕለ ሕዝብ" wants something like "one who is learned/teaches" (ምሁር / ይሜህር); the ink does not give either cleanly.
- **l.9 ይሥብለ + l.10 ኑ** — ደ/ይ then ሥ (ሠ with a descender) then box then a glyph I read as ለ at 2.5x (ሐ at 1x). ይሥብለኑ is not a word I know; sense unresolved.
- **l.10 ንግሣዕ** and **ll.11-12 ንግሠ/ጸሙ** — both look like forms of ግሠጸ "rebuke" (ንግሥጽ, ንግሥጾሙ) written with the scribe's usual vowel-order looseness; ንግሣዕ's last glyph could be ጽ rather than ዕ. Also possible for l.10: ንንሣእ "let us take" (with ኢ lost), which would give "we commanded that [no one] take a bribe from anyone" — the Arabic-shaped sense. Ink transcribed.
- **ኑ vs ነ** — the scribe distinguishes them (ኑ has a right-side stroke; ነ in l.4 ዘይነሥእ and l.13 ሕልያነ is plain). So ሕልያኑ (ll.2, 5, 10-11) and እምኑ (l.11) are genuinely ኑ, not ነ, even though እምነ መኑሂ "from anyone" is what the sense wants.
- **ll.2, 6 ምኒታቱ** — ኒ vs ኔ (foot vs ring) uncertain; ምኔታቱ "his monasteries" would be lexical, ምኒት is not. Left as ኒ (foot seen).
- **l.18 ወግብፃ** — fourth glyph is a rounded box with a descending leg, ፃ/ፀ family; "ወግብረ ... ይግበር" (do the work as commanded) is the expected sense, but the ink is not ረ.
- **l.20 ይትክነስ** — second glyph carries the ት tick; third has the bent left leg of ክ. No lexical match (ይትወከፍ "let him be received" is what the sentence wants).
- **l.22 እዲገ** — clear glyphs, unknown word; object of ወየሀይድ "robs". ዲ could be ዴ.
- **l.23 ወዕቢሬ** — ዕ/ሕ ambiguous at both magnifications; ቢ/ቤ ambiguous; final ሬ has a ring. Position (after እጓለ ማውታ "orphans") wants "widows"; the standard word is መበለት, so this is either a rare lexeme or a corruption.
- **l.24** — one glyph after ዘይፈቅ is struck through with an X; the following glyph is a ተ-family cross (ት), not ዱ. ወየ፡ is an isolated two-letter word (ወ + የ) — possibly the start of a word the scribe broke off before the point. በዕባዱ unknown.
- **l.17 እምቢት** — ቢ (foot) not ቤ (ring); scribe's ቢት for ቤተ.
- **l.13 ቢጾሙ** — ጾ/ጸ ambiguous; standard ቢጾሙ.
- **l.16 ወውእቱሰ** — final glyph ሰ (on a base); ስ possible.
