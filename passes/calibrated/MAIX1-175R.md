## WORK
Not 4 Baruch and not narrative. The page is a paraenetic homily on
almsgiving (ምጽዋት, seven times: ll.5, 8, 9, 13, 22-23, plus ጽድቀ፡ወምጽዋተ twice)
built on one contrast: the gift given with a hurtful word (ll.6-8) does not
count as righteousness-and-alms, and the gift given with a shining face and a
glad heart (ll.9-13, 16-17) is approved by God and opens the kingdom of heaven
(ll.19-24). Evidence from the ink for the sources it is paraphrasing; none is
introduced by a citation formula (no ይቤ, no በከመ፡ይብል on the page):

- ll.6-8 እንዘ፡የሐምም፡ቃሎ፡ወሀበ ... ኢይትኌለቁ፡ጽድቀ፡ወምጽዋተ: the grudging giver
  whose gift is void = Sirach 18:15-18 ("do not add reproach to your good
  deeds, nor a grieving word to a gift ... the gift of the grudging giver")
  and Didache 4:7 / Barnabas 19:11 / Apostolic Constitutions VII.12 ("do not
  hesitate to give, nor grumble when you give"), i.e. Two Ways material — the
  same stratum already met on f.167.
- ll.9-11 ይበርህ፡ገጹ፡ወያስተፌሥሕ፡ልቡ, ll.22-23 በፍሡሕ፡ልብ: Sirach 35:8-9 LXX ("with
  every gift show a cheerful face") and 2 Corinthians 9:7 ("God loves a
  cheerful giver"). ll.12-13 ሎቱኬ፡ይሠምር፡እግዚአብሔር፡ምጽዋቶ is the Ge'ez of that
  clause's sense.
- ll.14-15 ወይትወከፎ፡ሎቱ፡ዘገብረ፡ምሕረተ፡በዲበ፡ምድር: God "accepts from him the mercy
  he did on earth" = Proverbs 19:17 / Tobit 12:8-9 language.
- ll.19-24 the kingdom of heaven opened to the almsgiver, and the heavenly
  ones rejoicing: cf. Luke 15:7, 10 (joy in heaven); Tobit 4:10.

So the work is a homily that stitches Sirach/Tobit/Two Ways material on
alms into a church-order sermon. That is consistent with the running
hypothesis for ff.168-173 (an apostolic church-order homily; Didascalia ch.2
= Apostolic Constitutions II.5-6 was proposed) but does not prove it: the
Didascalia's almsgiving chapters (ch.17-18 = AC IV.1-10) are about the bishop
receiving and distributing alms, and nothing on this page names the bishop,
the widow, or the giver's rank. Qalementos and the Two Ways homily of f.167
both remain possible. Citations here are paraphrases, so the reverse-order
test seen on ff.167v and 173v cannot be run on this leaf; the order of
material as it stands is Sirach 18 (negative) THEN Sirach 35 / 2 Cor 9
(positive), i.e. ascending, if it is Sirach at all.

## GIST
"...to him. [ll.1-2 unresolved] For the rich man gave to the poor [ll.3-4
unresolved] righteousness and alms that are not counted; for when his word
wounds, and he gives to such a one, righteousness and alms are not counted.
But he who does alms while his face shines and his heart rejoices, and gives
while [not] making his word bitter — with him then God is pleased in his alms,
and accepts from him the mercy he did on earth. For he gave while his heart
was open and his mind bright; there is nothing at all, no evil deed, that
[bars/can bring] entry into the kingdom of heaven. For the kingdom of heaven
is opened to him, to the man who does alms with a joyful heart; the heavenly
ones rejoice, and the soul of the righteous..." [continues on 175v].

## NUMERALS
none. No glyph on the page carries bounding bars above and below. Two ። marks
carry red dots (l.8, l.20); both are punctuation. The I-beam ነ appears at l.1
(twice, ነሂ- and ነሠአ), l.3 (ለነደይ) and l.24 (ወነፍስ), unbarred, all letters.
The ፉ/፪ collision does not arise (no ፉ on the page); the ቁ of l.1 and ll.5,
8 is a letter with a right-foot hook and no bars.

## NOTES
Least certain readings, in order:
- l.1 ጽቁ፡ነሂደስኤ: transcribed letter by letter. ጽ is the capped X; ቁ is ቀ with
  a right-foot hook and no bars (letter, not figure). The 3rd glyph of the
  next word is the V-with-top-bar-and-right-leg shape I read as ሂ; it is the
  same shape the key resolved to ደ on f.172r (ለሂቄቀ→ለደቂቀ), and if it is ደ
  here the word begins ነደይ- = ነዳይ "the poor man" as written in l.3 (ለነደይ).
  The following ስኤ (or ስእ: the alef may be the hooked እ) then remains
  unplaced. No Arabic root offered. Unresolved.
- l.1 ነሠአ: I-beam ነ, three-pronged ሠ, hooked alef — this hand's ነሥአ "he
  took/received" (cf. ወነሥኡ on f.166v). Fairly confident.
