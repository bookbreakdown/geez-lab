## WORK

Unidentified. It is not narrative and it is not a canon; it is a homiletic
promise of paradise addressed to the faithful of the church, and it reads as a
continuation of the church-order discourse of ff.168-171 rather than of
Qalementos Book IV (no Clement, no Peter, no apostle named on the page).

Scriptural echoes in the ink, none a verbatim citation:
- ll.2-3 ይባዑ፡ውስተ፡ሕይወት፡ዘለዓለም "enter into eternal life" — the wording of
  Matthew 18:8-9 / Mark 9:43-45 (ውስተ፡ሕይወት) fused with Matthew 25:46
  (ሕይወት፡ዘለዓለም).
- ll.18-19 ይእቲ፡መርዓት፡እንተ፡ክርስቶስ፡መርዓዊሃ "she is the bride of Christ her
  bridegroom" — the Ephesians 5:25-32 / Revelation 21:2, 9 image applied to
  ቤተ፡ክርስቲያን, which is named explicitly on l.17.
- ll.3-6 "garden of joy ... where rest is spread out ... the land of the garden
  adorned with flowers" and ll.9-14 "flowers whose sweetness surpasses all the
  spices on earth ... fruits sweeter than any on earth" is the paradise topos of
  the Apocalypse of Paul / 4 Ezra 2:12, 19 / 2 Enoch 8 tradition; the vocabulary
  (ዕረፍት, ተሠርገወት, አፈው, ፍሬያት፡ጥዑማት) is the Ge'ez stock for it. I cannot tie it
  to a single source from this page.
- l.22 በዕለተ፡ፍዳ "on the day of recompense" and l.24 እለ፡ተወከሉ፡በእግዚአብሔር
  "those who trusted in the Lord" (Psalm 2:12 / 124:1 phrasing).

The page therefore continues the "reward for those who keep the ordinance of
the church" theme of ff.168-171. No Arabic loanword recognised on this page; the
unplaceable words (ll.1, 14-15, 23) do not look Arabic.

## GIST

"...they shall not [be cut off?] from my good word, so that they may enter into
eternal life, into the garden of joy, where rest is spread out, where the land
of the garden is adorned with [trees of?] flowers. And not this which appears —
passing flowers that are on the earth — but flowers again(?) whose sweetness
surpasses all the spices that are on the earth; and sweet fruits. Not as on the
earth do fruits appear, whose sweetness surpasses all [?] and [?] that are on
the earth. It has been given to those who keep the ordinance of the holy church
which is ours; she is the bride of Christ her bridegroom. And everyone who does
the law and keeps the ordinance, he then shall rejoice on the day of
recompense, if [?] the rich(?); and they shall rejoice who trusted in the Lord
[God — continues on 173r]."

## NUMERALS

none. No glyph on the page carries bounding bars above and below. Two I-beams
that silhouette as ፲ (l.6 ገነት, l.18 እንቲእነ) are ነ; the loop-forms on ll.2, 10,
14, 24 are ዑ/ኵ/ው inside words.

## NOTES

Line count 24; single column; ghost offset of the recto shows in the right
gutter (grey mirrored glyphs on every line) and was ignored. Red ink: the ።
after l.6 ጽጊያት and after l.15 ተወህበ are written as the four-dot cluster with
red dots around it, kept as ።. Punctuation on ll.11, 12, 18 is a ፡ with a tail
under the lower dot; transcribed ፤.

Unresolved, letter by letter:
- l.1 ኢደጽርዑ — glyphs: ኢ (curled-leg alef, clear), ደ/ይ (the collision), ጽ, ር,
  ዑ (single bowl with hook, not ው). The verb is unplaced; ጸርዐ is not a root I
  can vouch for. Do not smooth to ኢየጽርዑ or ኢያጽርሑ. The next word: hooked alef,
  ም, serifed I-beam (ነ, no bars), ገ, ረ WITH a closed ring on top (ሬ, ring is
  black not red, checked at 3x). So እምነገሬ፡ሠናይ "from my good word" is what the
  ink says, but the whole line is a hypothesis.
- l.6 በዕ?፡ — after ዕ there is a short foot-bearing I-beam, the same shape as
  the ነ in ገነት two words earlier but shorter. On form it is ነ; on sense the
  slot wants ፀ (ዕፀ፡ጽጌያት "trees of flowers"). Left as ?, both readings noted.
- l.7 end ኀ + l.8 ለፊ — ኀ-order unmarked; almost certainly ኃላፊ "passing,
  transitory", spelled by this scribe with sadis-for-rabi as usual (ኀለፊ).
- l.9 ደጋመ — ደ/ይ collision, then a 7-shaped ጋ with a right hook. Sense wants
  ዳግመ "again / secondly". Ink kept.
- l.14 እያያት — hooked alef, then two ያ-shaped glyphs (the first slightly
  narrower, could be ዳ), then ት. Unplaced. Not smoothed.
- l.15 ዓር — a 9-shaped bowl with right descender (ዓ/ዐ undistinguished) then ር.
  With the ወ that ends l.14 this gives ወዓር, unplaced. Possibly ll.14-15 name
  two classes of earthly plants; possibly one word broken across the seam.
- l.21 ውእቲኪ — ተ with right dash below mid (ቲ, near-collides with ቱ), then ከ
  with a dash (ኪ, no ring). Sense wants ውእቱኬ "he then". Ink kept; supports the
  key's ቲ/ቱ warning.
- l.23 ይነዩነ — ደ/ይ, I-beam with foot, ይ with a bottom hook (ዩ), I-beam with
  foot. Unplaced. Neither I-beam has bars. Do not smooth.

Nonstandard spellings attested here (add to the key):
- ሠርዓት for ሥርዓት (ll.16, 21) — ሠ for ሥ.
- ጽጊያት for ጽጌያት three times (ll.6, 7, 8): the ጊ has the LEFT FOOT, not a ring,
  every time. Either this scribe writes ጌ with a foot in this word, or he
  spells it ጽጊያት. Either way the key's ጌ-ring / ጊ-foot rule does not carry the
  vowel here.
- ቤት፡ክርስቲያን for ቤተ፡ክርስቲያን (l.17), ት with tick.
- መርእዊሃ for መርዓዊሃ (l.19): plain hooked alef where the standard has ዓ.
- እንቲእነ for እንቲአነ (l.18); እላ for አላ (l.8); እፈው for አፈው (l.10); እኮ for አኮ
  (ll.6, 12); እብዕልት for አብዕልት (l.23); በእግዚእ- (l.24). All the hooked alef.
- ገነት with the serifed I-beam ነ (ll.3, 6) — a fourth clean anchor for the
  I-beam-is-ነ rule; ኵ on ll.10, 14, 20 with the top-right hook, as the key says.
- ዘይፈደፍድ (ll.9, 13): ደ and ድ are two of the collision glyph; the word is
  secured by its recurrence and by ጣዕሙ፡እምኵሉ following it both times.
