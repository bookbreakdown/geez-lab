## NOTES

Least certain readings, in order of doubt:

- l.1 ወኤያሪ + l.2 ለያሙ. Read letter by letter at 4x: ወ, ኤ (hooked alef with a flat right foot, not the ኢ left-curl), ያ, ሪ (ረ with tick, no ring), then ለ ያ ሙ. Not resolvable as a word; transcribed as ink. If the ያ is the ይ/ደ collision and the ሪ a ር, the string is still opaque.
- l.1 first glyph carries the top-left tick, so ት by this key (ትነበያ), but sense wants ተነበያ/ተነበየ "prophesied". Flagged ት/ተ.
- l.3 ዒዋዲሆሙ: ዐ-bowl with right tick, ዋ (ወ with descender and foot), ደ/ይ-with-tick, ሆ, ሙ. Not placeable. Second choice ዐዋዲሆሙ (ዐውድ "circuit/assembly"?). Collect for the expert.
- l.4 ስበ: ስ/ለ ambiguous at 4x; sense wants ሶበ "when" (sadis for sabi is this scribe's habit). ተመይጠሙ: ጠ not ጦ in the ink; expected ተመይጦሙ.
- l.7 ወእባ፡ባሮክ: the ink has a separated word before ባሮክ (hooked alef + ባ, then ፡). Transcribed as it stands; "Abba Baruch" or a dittography of ባ.
- l.8 ንዓ vs l.17 ንዑ: the two second glyphs differ (l.8 bowl with right descender = ዐ/ዓ; l.17 bowl with hook and foot = ዑ). Singular imperative to Jeremiah, plural to Israel; both fit.
- l.9 ተሣለቱ: 4th glyph is a cross with foot and NO top-left tick, so ቱ not ት (see letterform fact below). Word unplaced; context wants ተሣሀለ "was merciful". ወይትጋሠሃ: 7-shaped glyph (this hand's ግ/ጋ, cf. እግዚ), ሠ three-pronged, final ሃ. Unplaced; collect.
- l.10 ለዕሊነ: final glyph is ነ (short foot) at 4x, not ኑ. Read as ላዕሌነ "upon us" with ለ for ላ and ሊ for ሌ, the same substitution as ምስሊክ (ll.13-14) for ምስሌከ and እቢሚለክ (ll.6-7) for አቢሜሌክ.
- l.11 ቀዲሙ: ደ/ይ collision; ቀዪሙ possible; sense (ቀዳሚ "formerly") favours ደ. መሃክ|ርቱ across the seam: መ, ሃ (ሀ with right tick), ክ, ር, ቱ. Unplaced; collect.
- l.12-13 ከመ፡ት|ክት: ትክት for ትካት "of old" (sadis for rabi again). l.13 second glyph has tick + foot = ት.
- l.14 ከሀደያን: ደ/ይ collision at glyph 3; ከሀይያን the alternative. Neither is a known word; ከለደውያን "Chaldeans" is what the sense wants, and the same people are written ንለደውያን on l.22.
- l.15 ወሉዲዥ: ሉ (Λ with left arm and straight foot), ደ/ይ with lower-right tick, H-with-curled-top (ዝ family) with an extra top mark. Unplaced. Context (do not give X / do not take his daughter for your son) wants ወለተከ "your daughter". Transcribed as ink.
- l.18 ኢያተመዓዕ: ያ (with descender) where ይ is expected (ኢይትመዓዕ "let him not be angry"); ተ is tickless.
- l.19 ወኢያጥፍዕ: ጥ then ፍ then a bowl-with-hook (ዕ) where ኢያጥፍእ "not destroy" needs እ. Transcribed ዕ as drawn.
- l.20 ደግሙ: expected ዳግመ "again"; 7-shaped ግ.
- l.21 እንዘ, l.24 እንስቲያክሙ, l.22 opener: the same glyph (stem, top-left bar, right foot) occurs in all three; in two of them ን is forced by sense (እንዘ፡ሀለው "while they were", እንስቲያክሙ "your wives"), so it is ን everywhere. That leaves l.22 ንለደውያን unexplained after l.21's final ከ; the sense is ከለደውያን "Chaldeans" and the ን is intrusive or the scribe's error. Not smoothed.
- l.23 ለዱቂቀ: glyph 2 ደ/ይ with foot (ዱ/ዩ), glyph 3 ቀ with a lower-right curl read ቂ (ቁ possible). Sense ለደቂቀ "to the children of". ሳዱ?ጉ: first glyph an arch with left curl and right descending foot, read ሳ; ኅ is the second candidate (cf. key: ጳ is shaped like this hand's ኀ), which would give ኅድጉ "leave!" — exactly what l.24 "your wives whom you took" wants. No ፡ between ዱ and ጉ; the marks seen at 2x are the blot's fringe. Last glyph a 7-shape with a right mid dash: ጉ, or ጊ.
- ወይቤልዎሙ l.22: ዎ (ወ with foot) not ሎ; "they said to them", plural subject.

Nonstandard spellings attested on this page (add to the key): እግዚእብሔር (hooked alef throughout, per key rule), እቢሚለክ for አቢሜሌክ, ለዕሊነ for ላዕሌነ, ምስሊክ for ምስሌከ, ትክት for ትካት, ስብእ (already attested), ከሀናት for ካህናት, ደግሙ for ዳግመ, ለወልድክ / ባሕቲትክሙ / እንስቲያክሙ (ክ for ከ 2nd-person suffix), ኢያ- for ኢይ- in negative jussives (ኢያተመዓዕ, ኢያጥፍዕ), ሀለው for ሀለዉ.

New letterform facts for the key:

- ት vs ቱ: ት = cross with top-left tick AND a foot; ቱ = cross with a foot and no tick. Tested on ወባሕቱ (ll.13, 16) against ባሕቲትክሙ (l.17) and ትክት (l.13). ቲ = right dash at mid, as the key says.
- ን in this hand = stem with a short top-left bar and a right foot; at 2x it looks like ኀ. Confirmed by sense on እንዘ and እንስቲያክሙ. ነ = stem with short foot, no top bar (ለዕሊነ, ዘነሣእክሙ).
- ግ (in እግዚአብሔር) is the 7-shape, i.e. the same shape the key gives for ጋ; the ተ-with-foot-hook reading applies to ጉ, not to ግ on this page.
- ኤ = hooked alef with a flat foot to the right (ወኤያሪ, ኤርምያስ); ኢ = left-curling leg (ኢታምጽእ, ኢያተመዓዕ). They are distinct here.
- ኡ = hooked alef with a right foot (ወግብኡ, l.5), separable from ሉ by the hooked top.
- ዑ (l.17) = bowl with hook and a right foot; ዓ/ዐ (l.8, l.18) = bowl with a right descender.
- Deletion: l.4, a single box glyph struck with three horizontal strokes, as the key records.
- ። on l.6 and l.16 written as two spaced ፡ pairs; on l.20 as a tighter pair. Kept as ።.
- The margin at the right edge (x > 1400 at 1424px width) is show-through from the facing leaf, not text on this page.

Content note: the passage is Jeremiah, Abimelech and Baruch at the return from Babylon, with the prohibition on foreign wives — the matter of Paraleipomena Jeremiou / Rest of the Words of Baruch chs. 6-8 — even though the folio is labelled Qalementos Book IV. Recorded as an observation, not used for any reading.
