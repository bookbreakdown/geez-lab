## WORK

**Continuation of the church-order canons stratum of ff.176r–179v: the sanction and rationale clause of the divination canon.** Not a new work. Evidence from the ink, in order of weight:

1. **The seam.** l.1 opens ዎሙ፡ቁርባነ, which completes f.179v's broken ወኢይመጥ → ወኢይመጥዎሙ፡ቁርባነ "and they shall not give them the qurbān [eucharist]". That is the penalty clause of the litany 179v was running. The page does not start a work; it finishes a sentence.
2. **First-person plural authorial voice.** l.24 ዘዘከርነ "which we mentioned" — the compiler's "we", the same voice as the "we command" canons of 176r–179v. No third-person jussive frame, no "the apostles said", no source-citation formula anywhere on the page.
3. **Penitential-tariff shape.** Exclusion from the qurbān እስከ፡ይኔስሕ፡ወይነጽሕ፡እምኀጢእቱ "until he repents and is cleansed from his sin" (ll.1–3) is the same sanction machinery as the tariffs on 177v–178r (cf. the ይትከነስ readmission formula in the key). Readmission-on-penance, not anathema.
4. **The subject matter is the 179v litany continued.** ll.22–23 add augury to the list: በንቃወ፡እራዊት፡ወእስዋፍ "by the cries of beasts and birds", then l.24 closes "by all that we mentioned ?…". Two agent-nouns for the practitioners are named, ማርያን and ጠባውያን (ll.19–20), in that order, and their clients are defined three ways (trust them / hear their word / do as they command).
5. **No rank list, no fixed-order catalogue, no scripture citation** on this page, so it cannot be tied to either rank stratum (174r or 179v); it is tied to 179v by the seam alone.

The middle of the page (ll.3–18) is the theological rationale, not a canon: the diviner's father is the devil (ዲያብሎስ፡እቡሁ, l.6–7 — the John 8:44 idea, not a citation), who shows him what he has in mind and "accomplishes it for him" with ተእምረ፡ወመንክረ "signs and wonders" (l.9–10, the Deut 13 / Matt 24:24 / 2 Thess 2:9 pairing, again not cited), and not for his own sake but "for the sake of his tale, for those who believe and trust in his word, so as to lead them astray; for all his work is the devil's and his demons', so that they lead the sons of men astray into worshipping the demons" (ll.11–18). This is homiletic expansion inside a canon, which is exactly the texture 176r–179v has shown.

**What it is not.** Not Didascalia (consistent with the two exclusions already established; nothing here reverses them). Not Qalementos Book IV: no Clement/Peter frame, no narrative. Shared vocabulary with the Sinodos canons on sorcerers is not shared source; I claim only the stratum continuity above.

**Citations:** none. Order of named practitioner classes: ማርያን then ጠባውያን (l.19–20). Order of the augury pair: beasts then birds (l.23).

**Arabic loans:** none claimed. ቁርባን is standard Ge'ez (Semitic q-r-b, not diagnostic of Arabic). ጠባውያን can be Ge'ez ጠበብ "be skilled/wise" (ጠቢብ) with a nonstandard plural; an Arabic ṭ-b-b "healer" reading would need the expert and is not claimed. ማርያን is unplaced (see NOTES).

## GIST

The canon that 179v was running reaches its penalty: those who practise the listed divinations are not to be given the eucharist until they repent and are cleansed of their sin; the page then explains that the diviner's father is the devil, who shows him what he has in mind and works signs and wonders through him to lead astray those who believe his word, since all his work is the devil's and his demons', aimed at making men worship demons. It closes by extending the sanction to the clients — those who trust the ማርያን and the ጠባውያን, who hear their word and do as they command, and who trust the cries of beasts and birds — "by all that we mentioned" before.

## NUMERALS

None. Three I-beam glyphs (ቁርባነ l.1, ኮነ l.3, ዘከርነ l.24) were checked for bounding bars: none has any. All three are the serifed I-beam ነ. No figure on the page.

## NOTES

**Join to 179v.** The brief predicted 180r would complete ወኢይመጥ; it does, with ዎሙ, and the object ቁርባነ follows. Clean join.

**ኒ/ኔ (ll.1, 4).** One glyph shape — I-beam ነ with a small rounded blob at the lower right — serves both ይኔስሕ (l.1, where ኔ is expected) and ከልዓኒሁ (l.4, where ኒ is expected). At 3x the blob is neither a clear ring nor a clear tick. Resolved on sense in both places; recorded here so the editor can override. Add to the key: **ኒ and ኔ are not separable in this hand.**

**The 7-shaped glyph in ግብር slots (ll.6, 15).** ይጋበር (l.6) and ጋብሩ (l.15) both show the 7-shape the key calls ጋ, in positions where standard Ge'ez has ግ (ይግበር, ግብሩ). The same 7-shape appears in እጋንንት (ll.16, 18) where ጋ is correct. Meanwhile ይግብር (l.9), ዘይገብር (l.12), ወይገብሩ (l.21), ነግሩ (l.13) use the ተ-with-foot form the key gives for ግ. So the scribe has two ግ/ጋ glyphs and uses both in the ገብረ root on one page. Transcribed as ink (ጋ); flagged. Key candidate: **the 7-shape is not proof of ጋ.**

**l.3 ኮነ።** Letters secure (I-beam ነ, no bars; decorated ። with red dots). Sense is awkward — "until it be" closing the penalty clause. Possibly ellipsis ("until it is [done]"); left as read.

**l.3 መስሐተ፡በልቡ.** Letters secure. A noun from ስሕተ "err/lead astray" (ምስሓት? መስሐት?) or a participle "the deceiver, in his heart"; the sentence then continues "and they lead astray the hearts of his fellows". Lexeme for the expert.

**l.8–9 ያስተፍፅም.** Last glyph of l.8 read ፅ (bowl with top hook) over ዕ; ያስተፍጽም "he brings to pass" fits, and this scribe writes ፅ for ጽ (ግብፅ in the key). If it is ዕ the word is unresolved.

**l.19 ይትእመኑቃሙ.** Ink: ት እ መ [foot-glyph] ቃ ሙ, no ፡ inside. The foot-glyph is the ን/ኑ form (reads as ኀ or ዓ at 2x, per the key); on morphology ይትእመኑ "they trust". The following ቃሙ has no ፡ before it and no ሎ; l.21 has the full ቃሎሙ. Either the scribe dropped ሎ (ቃ[ሎ]ሙ "their word") or the word is something else. **Weakest reading on the page.** Do not smooth to ቃሎሙ without the editor.

**ll.22–23 ባ / ቤ where alef is expected.** ወይትባመኑ (expected ወይትአመኑ "they trust") and ወይትቤመሩ (expected ወይትአመሩ or similar). In both the glyph body is box-like (ብ family) with a right leg (ባ) or a bottom-right ring (ቤ) — exactly where printed ኣ and ኤ would carry a leg and a ring. Two hypotheses: (a) the scribe wrote ብ-family letters; (b) this hand's fourth- and fifth-order alef (ኣ, ኤ) are box-bodied and the key's "hooked alef everywhere" covers only orders 1/6. Transcribed as ink. **Test on other folios where ኣ/ኤ are expected (e.g. ኤልያስ, ኣ- suffixes).**

**l.22 በንቃወ.** ን by the foot form; ቃ over ቀ by a short right foot at the stem base, and ንቃወ "cry" fits the augury sense (ንቃወ፡እራዊት፡ወእስዋፍ "the cry of beasts and birds"). ቀ possible.

**l.23 ወእስዋፍ.** Expected አዕዋፍ "birds". The glyph after እ has legs (ስ-shaped), not the bowl-with-hook ዕ this scribe uses in ይሰምዑ (l.20). Ink ስ; flagged.

**l.24 ?ጣምተ፡.** First glyph under a brown stain; silhouette ቀ-like (ring on a stem). Then ጣ, ም, ተ, ፡. The page edge may hide a second ፡ (i.e. ።). Word unresolved: ቀጣምተ / ጣዖተ (with ም misread for ዖ) / ቀዳሚተ are all guesses and none is adopted. Letter-by-letter as given.

**l.14 ዓምኑ.** Line-initial 9-shaped bowl with descender = ዐ/ዓ, where ወይአምኑ would want አ. Ink kept.

**Decorated ።** at ll.3, 10, 20: black dot pairs with red dots in a rosette. Kept as ። per the key. The l.10 one splits ተእምረ፡ወመንክረ from በከመ፡ሀለየ, which reads as a clause break, not a section break.

**Orthography consistent with the key:** sadis for other orders (ነግሩ, ስብእ, ዘከርነ), ሀ for ሐ (ሀለየ ×2), hooked alef everywhere, ዘዘከርነ with the I-beam suffix -ነ.

**No ghost offset read.** The left-margin grey column is offset from the facing verso and was not transcribed.

## PREDICTIONS (registered before any adjacent page is read)

- **f.179v must end** with ወኢይመጥ as the brief states, and the divination litany immediately before it must be in the third-person plural (ዘ…/እለ… "those who…"), because 180r's ዎሙ is a 3pl object and ll.18–23 resume an እለ-list.
- **f.180v must open** by completing or following "?ጣምተ፡" — either a new item continuing the እለ-list ("ወእለ…"), or the penalty for the clients ("ኢይመጥዎሙ…" / "ይትከነስ…" / "ርኩስ፡ወሰዑብ"). It should stay in the compiler's first-person plural and should NOT open with a rank list, a scripture citation formula, or narrative.
- ማርያን and/or ጠባውያን should recur within a page if they are technical terms of this canon; if neither recurs and 180v moves to a new topic, they are one-off glosses.

**What would falsify this reading:** 179v's last word not being ወኢይመጥ (the join then fails and ዎሙ needs another antecedent); 180v opening in a different genre or voice (third-person jussive, "the apostles said", a rank ladder), which would make 180r a stratum boundary rather than a continuation; a clear ኤ/ኣ elsewhere in this hand drawn as a hooked alef, which would force ll.22–23 back to ብ-family letters and leave ወይትባመኑ/ወይትቤመሩ unexplained; a second occurrence of the l.19 word showing ቃሎሙ in full, which would confirm the dropped-ሎ hypothesis, or showing a different word, which would retire it.
