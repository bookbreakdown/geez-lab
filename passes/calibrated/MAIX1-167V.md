## WORK
Not the Rest of the Words of Baruch: no Baruch, Jeremiah, Abimelech or Babylon
anywhere on the side. It is a first-person divine discourse ("my commandments"
l.2, "for my name's sake" l.15, "said God" ll.20-21) that strings Gospel
sayings together with the citation formula "as Luke the apostle says in the
Gospel" (ll.4-5). What follows the formula is in fact the MATTHEW 10:34-36
wording (ኢያምጻእኩ፡ሰላመ፡እለ(=አላ)፡መጥባሕተ ... ወለተ ... ወመርዓተ፡እምሐማታ ...
ወፀሩ፡ለሰብእ፡ሰብአ፡ቤቱ), then LUKE 12:52-53 (five in one house, divided
three/two, ll.13-15), then Luke 12:51 (sword not peace, ll.16-18), Luke 12:50
(baptism, "how I am pressed until I accomplish it", ll.21-22) and Luke 12:49
(fire cast on the earth, ll.22-24), i.e. Luke 12:49-53 quoted in REVERSE order
around the Matthew parallel. That is a homily/catena or a pseudo-apostolic
discourse (Christ speaking), consistent with the Qalementos genre where Christ
addresses Peter, but nothing on the page names the work. Call it:
unidentified first-person Christ-discourse quoting Mt 10:34-36 and Lk 12:49-53.

## GIST
For this is [ማዓሪ - unresolved] for those who keep my commandments, and let
those who do evil be separated from their kin, as Luke the apostle says in the
Gospel: let it not seem that it is peace I brought to the land; I did not bring
peace but a sword, to separate daughter and her mother, bride from her
mother-in-law, son from his father; and a man's enemies are those of his
household, he who [ያስደሮ - unresolved] him and he who loves [ደዓሪሮ -
unresolved]. If there are five in one house they will be divided, three from
two and one[sic] from three, for my name's sake. And as to why, he said "I
brought the sword and not peace to the land", and those who are divided from
their kin, these I love exceedingly, said God. "I have a baptism, and how I am
pressed until I accomplish it; fire I brought to the land, and what shall I say
if it has already burned."

## NUMERALS
All five carry red bounding bars above and below (checked at 4x); none of the
loop-like letters on the page do.
- l.13 ፭ = 5, the people in the house (Lk 12:52 ኀምስቱ). Curly ξ-form. Second
  choice: none plausible. Confidence 0.9.
- l.14 ፩ = 1, "one house" (Lk 12:52 አሐዱ ቤት). Narrow-capped triangle, matches
  the key. Confidence 0.95.
- l.14 end ፫ = 3 (first "three"). Closed bowl top-LEFT with a stem and a
  bottom-right foot; same glyph as l.15 third figure. Read as 3 because the
  passage needs 3 twice and this glyph occurs twice. Second choice ፪ (the key
  calls this scribe's ፪ "a loop"). Confidence 0.6.
- l.15 first ፪ = 2 ("two"). Γ-shape with a bottom-left foot, i.e. the printed
  ፪ shape. Second choice ፯ (the key's "ገ-shape with a foot" is ፯), which the
  sense excludes. Confidence 0.6.
- l.15 second ፩ = 1 BY INK: a capped triangle identical at 4x to the ፩ of l.14.
  The sense (Lk 12:52 ወክልኤቱ) demands ፪. Transcribed as the ink; treat as a
  scribal slip or a variant reading. Second choice ፪. Confidence in the glyph
  0.85, in the value-as-intended 0.4.
- l.15 third ፫ = 3 (second "three"), same form as l.14 end. Confidence 0.6.

## NOTES
- l.1 እ{ስ}መ: the ስ is an interlinear superscript self-correction above the
  first letter (scribe wrote እመ, added ስ). Braces mark the superscript.
- l.1 ደእቲኪ፡ማዓሪ: ink is ደ/ይ-እ-ቲ-ኪ (ቲ has the right dash, ኪ the right curl),
  then ማ-ዓ-ሪ (ሪ with tick). Expected sense "for this is [reward/life] for
  those who keep my commandments"; neither word resolves. Possibly ይእቲ + a
  word; not smoothed. FOR THE EXPERT.
- l.1 last position: a faint ghost glyph in the outer margin is bleed-through,
  not ink on this side.
- ይ/ደ: written ደ throughout per the key's collision rule (ደትፈለጡ, ደገብሩ,
  ደቤ, ደዓሪሮ). All are ይ by sense except ደዓሪሮ, which is unresolved.
- l.5 ወንጊል: the ጊ has a left foot, no ring, so ጊ not ጌ (ንጊል attested in key).
- ll.5, 7 ኢያ-: the second letter has a right-bending stem like the ያ of l.12,
  unlike the straight-stem የ of l.2, so ያ. ኢያምጻእኩ = ኢያምጻእኩ "I did not
  bring", which is what the sense needs; likewise ዘእምጻእኩ (ll.6, 16, 23) =
  ዘአምጻእኩ "which I brought". Not a corruption of መጻእኩ.
- l.5 ends ል (small lower hook) rather than ለ: ኢያምስል "let it not seem".
- l.7 ሰለመ vs l.6 ሰላመ: the ink differs (plain ለ in l.7), kept as written.
- l.8 እለ for አላ "but" (Mt 10:34); the hooked alef everywhere, per key.
- ll.9-10 hole: about two glyph-widths lost at the centre of each line.
  l.9 reads continuously ወእማ፡ | ወመርዓተ and l.10 ወወ | ልደ (= ወወልደ), so
  probably nothing but a divider is lost; flagged rather than assumed.
- l.10 እምእቡሁ: alef written as እ (key rule); standard እምአቡሁ.
- l.11 ወጸቱ: expected ወፀሩ (Mt 10:36). Second letter is the ጸ-shape; third
  has a T-top and a foot, reading ቱ; ሩ is the alternative. Not smoothed.
- l.11 ሰብእ፡ቢቱ: ቢ for ቤ (ቢተ attested in key), ሰብእ for ሰብአ.
- l.12 ያስደሮ and ደዓሪሮ: unresolved. First: ያ-ስ(with foot)-ደ/ይ-ሮ (black ring,
  no red dots nearby, so a real ሮ). Second: ደ/ይ-ዓ(9-bowl)-ሪ(tick)-ሮ. Context
  "a man's enemies are those of his household, he who ...s him and he who
  loves ..." FOR THE EXPERT.
- l.13 ሀለው: two loops, so ው; the ሀ is plain (ሀልዩ-type spelling in key).
- l.14 ቢት for ቤት.
- l.16 በእንተስ: final letter is ስ (foot), not the H-form ዝ used in l.19
  ወእንተዝ. Kept as ink.
- l.17 ወእኮ = ወአኮ.
- l.19 ወእንተዝ፡እፍቀሮሙ፡ፈድፋደ: "and these I love exceedingly"; ፈድፋደ is the
  Mt 10:37 word, used here of the divided ones, not of parents.
- l.20 እግዚ: the ግ is the ተ-with-foot-hook form the key says reads as ት;
  resolved to ግ by እግዚ|እብሔር.
- l.21 ወእፎ: ፈ with a black ring on the right, ፎ (Lk 12:50 ወእፎ).
- l.22 ተኢገሠ: ink is ተ, hooked alef WITH the left curl (the ኢ/ኂ form), ገ, ሠ
  (possibly ሥ; the right leg is slightly extended). Sense wants
  እ|ትዕገሥ "I endure" (variant of Lk 12:50 እትሐዘብ), i.e. the scribe's alef
  standing for ዕ. Transcribed as ink. New key fact: the left-curl alef may
  also stand for ዕ.
- l.23 first alef after ተ፡ has NO curl, so እምጻእኩ (I brought fire), not ኢ.
- l.24 እለመ for ለእመ (metathesis, as written); ወደዓተ for ወድአት "already";
  ነደት with the short foot, ነ not ኑ.
- Punctuation: ። in ll.4, 18, 19 is the red-dotted form; kept as ።.
- Numeral shapes for the key: ፭ = curly ξ with bars; the 3-glyph is a closed
  bowl top-left + stem + bottom-right foot; the 2-glyph is the printed Γ-with-
  foot ፪, not a loop, so ፪ has two shapes in this hand (loop on 42r, Γ here).
