## WORK

Unidentified as a specific work. Genre is clear from the ink: an exhortation or
canon on confessors and martyrs under judicial torture, continuing the
church-order run of ff.168-169 rather than the Baruch/homily material earlier
in the gathering.

Evidence from the ink, not from a guess:
- l.2 ውስተ፡ዓውድ "in the tribunal/arena"; l.3 ይዝብጠሙ፡ወይኩኑንዎሙ "beat them and
  condemn them".
- ll.11-13 the torture catalogue: ዝብጠት (beating), [ምስርት, unresolved],
  መቅሠፍት (scourging), ሰይፍ (sword), ውግረተ፡እብን (stoning) — closed by ። with
  red dots.
- l.20 ስማዕት "the martyrs"; ll.20-21 እስከ፡መጠወ፡ነፍሶሙ "until they gave up their
  souls".
- ll.9-10, 17-18 ረሰዩ፡ርእሶሙ፡ከመ፡ዓምድ፡ጽኑዕ "they made themselves like a firm
  pillar" — the governing image of the page.
- The recurring motif of the raised right hand that is not lowered (ll.4-5, 7-8,
  14-16, 18: ኢያውርዱ፡የማኖሙ፡እምዘ፡አልዓሉ ... በእንተ፡እግዚአብሔር).

No verbatim scriptural citation was found. The raised-hands-not-lowered image
resembles Moses at Rephidim (Ex 17:11-12, hands held steady), which would sit
next to the Moses narrative of f.169v, but nothing on this page quotes it; treat
that as a resemblance, not an identification. A candidate for the source is the
Sinodos section on martyrs and confessors (the Apostolic Constitutions V.1-2
material, or the Apostolic Tradition canon on confessors), because that is what
a church-order collection carries at this point; this is unverified against any
witness and must not shape the readings.

## GIST

...and the rebellious nations, who did not let them stand in the tribunal so as
to beat them and condemn them, and who would lower their right hand from what
they had raised. He does not yield, and they do not say "yes" at the command of
those people, and they do not lower their right hand from what they raised —
those who made themselves like a firm pillar and did not say "yes", not under
beating, nor under [ምስርት], nor under scourging, nor under the sword, nor under
stoning with stones. And whatever happens, he does not lower their hands from
what they first raised, their right hand, for the sake of God. Those, however,
who made their right hand like a firm pillar raised it exceedingly; and
likewise, following his example, the martyrs act until they give up their souls;
they abandoned the world and all that is in it, the light of the world, the sun,
and gold...

## NOTES

Unresolved readings, in order of doubt:
- l.11 ኢሰዝብጠቱ — second glyph is drawn as a three-legged ሰ, not the two-legged
  በ that sense demands (ኢበዝብጠት "not under beating", parallel to ወኢበ- in the
  next four items). Transcribed as ink. The final glyph has a right mid hook, so
  ቱ rather than ት; ቱ/ቲ near-collide in this hand.
- l.11 ወኢበምስርት — every letter is clear (ም ስ ር ት) but the word is not placed.
  Sits between beating and scourging in the torture list, so an instrument of
  torture or restraint; ማእሰር "bonds" is the nearest lexical neighbour but the
  ink does not say that. Add to the unplaced-vocabulary list.
- l.2 ኢቀውምዎሙ — six clear glyphs, but the form does not parse; the sense wants
  causative ኢያቀውምዎሙ "they do not let them stand". No ያ in the ink.
- l.3 ይዝብጠሙ — ink is ጠ then ሙ, standard would be ይዝብጥዎሙ. Transcribed as ink.
- l.3 ወይኩኑንዎ(ሙ) — the ኩ has its hook at the bottom right (so ኩ, not this
  hand's top-right-hooked ኵ). The following glyph is the up-curling-foot form
  that the key now says can be ነ or ኑ; sense (ይኰንንዎሙ "they condemn them")
  wants ን. Transcribed ኑ on shape; flagged.
- l.13 ወዘኮ / l.14 ነ — the last glyph of l.13 has a small open curl at the top
  right of the right leg. That is the position the key gives for ኵ, but ወዘኵነ is
  nothing, and ወዘኮነ፡ከዊኖ "whatever happens" is an idiom. Read as ኮ on sense; the
  ኮ ring is drawn as an open hook in this hand. Say so before relying on it.
- l.19 እርእያሁ — last glyph has a right mid tick identical to the ሁ of ከማሁ on the
  same line, and unlike the ringed ሆ of እደዊሆ (l.14). So ሁ, "his example",
  and the next line's ይገብሩ፡ስማዕት is its predicate. Not ሆ(ሙ).
- l.21 ኀደጎሙ — ጎ has a ring; the standard would be ኀደጉ or ኀደግዎ. Transcribed
  as ink. ኀ has the ጳ-like shape the key warns of; no name here, so ኀ.
- l.22 ዘውስቲታ — the third glyph shows a right dash below mid (this hand's ቲ),
  no ring visible for ቴ. Transcribed ቲ; standard ውስቴታ.
- l.23 ፀሐየ — closed-oval ፀ, unmistakably not ዘ; then ሐ, then a የ-shaped glyph
  (loop top-left, stem down-right — not the ደ-like ይ of this hand). Read as
  ፀሐይ "sun" with a nonstandard final order, in the list "light of the world,
  sun, gold". The divider after it is ፡ with the lower dot smeared into a tail;
  kept as ፡.
- l.5 ኢይየውሕ — ኢ, ይ/ደ, የ, ው, ሕ all clear; parsed as ኢይየውህ (from የውሀ "be
  meek, yield"), with ሕ for ህ. Nonstandard but the letters are not in doubt.
- ll.6, 10 እሁ — hooked alef + ሁ, after ወኢይበሉ. On the key's rule that the
  vocative ኦ is written as the plain hooked alef, this is ኦሆ "yes"; ink kept.
- ll.6, 10 ወኢይበሉ — plain በ, not ቤ; ink kept (standard ኢይቤሉ/ኢይብሉ).
- l.7 ወኢያወርዱ — round ወ, not two-loop ው (ያውርዱ elsewhere on the page). Ink kept.
- ይ/ደ collision applied on sense at: l.1 (word-final, undecidable, written ይ),
  l.14 እደዊሆ (ደ), l.15 ቀዲሙ (ዲ), l.17 ረሰዩ (ዩ), l.20 ይገብሩ (ይ), l.21 ኀደጎሙ (ደ).

Orthography (all as ink, per the key): hooked alef transcribed እ throughout
(ወእሕዛብ, እልዓሉ ×3, እምእልዓሉ, እግዚእብሔር, ዚእሆሙ, እርእያሁ) — the editor should
read አ where standard has it. በትእዘዘ for በትእዛዘ. መቀሠፍት for መቅሠፍት. ዓለውያን
for ዐላውያን. ዕብን with the 9-shaped ዕ for እብን (so this scribe does distinguish
ዕ from the hooked alef when he wants to). ፀሐየ for ፀሐይ. ዘውስቲታ for ዘውስቴታ.

New letterform facts for the key:
- **ባ and ዓ collide.** On this page the same glyph — a በ-style arch with a
  top-right bar, not the 9-shaped bowl — serves for ባሕቱ (l.17, real ባ) and
  for ዓ in ዓለውያን (l.1), ዓውድ (l.2), እልዓሉ (ll.5, 8, 15, 18), ዓምድ (ll.9-10,
  17-18) and ዓለም (ll.21-22, 23). Every ዓ reading is forced by sense (ባምድ,
  ባውድ, ባለም are not words). The 9-shaped bowl is used for ዕ (ዕብን, ጽኑዕ,
  ስማዕት) but not for ዓ. Disambiguate ባ/ዓ on sense and say so.
- **ሁ vs ሆ are separable**: ሁ has a right mid tick (ከማሁ, እርእያሁ, l.19); ሆ has a
  ring at the top of the right leg (እደዊሆ, l.14; ለሊሆሙ, l.9).
- **ኩ (bottom-right hook) is distinct from ኵ (top-right hook)** on this page:
  ይኩኑንዎ l.3 vs ወኵሉ l.22.
- **ኮ** is drawn with an open top-right curl rather than a closed ring (l.13
  ወዘኮ), which puts it within reach of the ኵ description. Flag every ኮ/ኵ call.
- **ፀ** is a closed oval with a bar (l.23) and does not collide with ዘ.
- The ። at l.13 and l.19 is written as two spaced ፡ pairs with red dots around
  it, as the key describes; kept as ።.
- I-beam ነ without bars at ll.14, 21, 23 — three more instances of the ነ/፲
  silhouette collision, all resolved as letters by the absence of bars.
