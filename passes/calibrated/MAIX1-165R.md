## GIST

The page continues a catena of Old Testament figures, each introduced with "and from (ወእምኀበ) X": from Isaiah, joy, while [one] mounts upon a donkey in humility, and not in pride and in a chariot of horses; from ሣራ (Sarah?) something unresolved; from Miriam [sister?] of Moses, with the timbrel and with jubilation ...there; from Jacob the kid; from [unresolved]; and in [unresolved], salvation which became the gate of life. And we too, in [unresolved] our heart [unresolved], let us say always [unresolved] to the holy church, that they do in it as our Saviour himself ordained for us. And he commanded us saying: "The way of the Gentiles do not go(?), and the city of Samaria do not enter" — but as for the city of Samaria, if [unresolved] Israel [unresolved] with their [unresolved], and their priest Jeremiah, as he [continues on 165v].

## NOTES

Uncertain readings, in descending order of doubt:

- l.23 ንዑ?ሠ ... ንዑሠሙ: the second glyph is a single bowl with hook (ዑ by the key), the third a three-pronged ሠ. Neither ንዑሠ nor ንዑሠሙ is a word I can place. Sense would want ንጉሠ / ንጉሦሙ ("king / their king", pairing with ከሀኖሙ "their priest" in l.24), but this hand draws ጉ as ተ-with-foot, not as a bowl, so the ink does not support it. Flagged, not smoothed.
- l.22 እምናሁከደኑዖር: no word dividers between እም and ር፡; transcribed as one run. Glyphs letter by letter: እ ም ና ሁ ከ ደ(ይ) ኑ ዖ ር. ደ/ይ undecidable by shape; ኑ read on its long up-curling foot; ዖ on a top ring (could be ዓ with a stray mark).
- l.21 ተቂወው፡ጸቲቀ: read letter by letter; unplaced. ቂ has a right mid dash; the final ቀ may carry a right tick (ቂ).
- l.12-13 ንዴላ፡ንዴይ፡ልብነ፡ንዴጋና: the three ደ/ይ glyphs after ን each carry a small bottom-right tick, hence ዴ, but ይ is equally possible by shape (key collision). ላ in ንዴላ has a right foot; could be ለ. Unplaced; do not smooth.
- l.14 ንክለማ: the glyph after ን is ከ-with-foot (ክ) or possibly ቅ; unplaced.
- l.10 ጳጠስ: first glyph is this hand's ኀ/ጳ shape; no name demands ጳ here, so ኀጠስ is the alternative. Preceded by ዕፀ at the end of l.9 (ዕ/ዐ undistinguished). ወእምኀበ፡ዕፀ፡ጳጠስ unplaced.
- l.10 ወበናዓኒ: ና has the right hook (not the ring of l.24 ኖ); ዓ is 9-shaped, not the ኀ shape, so ኖኅ ("Noah", which the sense - salvation, gate of life - would invite) is NOT supported by the ink. Flagged.
- l.5-6 ሣራ፡ደፍንቻ: ሣ has a right tick on the third prong; ራ clear. ደ/ይ undecidable. The l.6-initial glyph is a cross with a large top-left hook and a bottom-right foot - not this hand's plain ት (small tick, no foot) nor ቱ. Transcribed ቻ as nearest shape; genuinely unresolved. Between Isaiah and Miriam this ought to be a person.
- l.6 ማርያ፡እላቲ: ማርያ followed by ፡ then እላቲ; sense wants ማርያም እኅቱ ለሙሴ (Miriam sister of Moses, l.7 ለሙሴ በከበሮ), but the ink has no ም and the second word's middle glyph is two-legged ላ, not this hand's ኀ shape. Transcribed as ink.
- l.8 ትጠፍስ፡በሀየ: ትጠፍስ unplaced (ጠፍሐ "clap"? would need ሕ; glyph is three-legged ስ). በሀየ = በህየ "there" by this scribe's unmarked sadis.
- l.3 ዘበትትሕትና: two distinct ት glyphs before ሕ, measured as separate ink at 3x. Likely dittography for ዘበትሕትና; transcribed as written.
- l.2 ስቀ|ለ (ll.2-3): ዲበ፡ስቀለ፡እድግ — ስቀለ unplaced; standard Zech 9:9 wording would be ዲበ፡አድግ. Transcribed as ink.
- l.19 ኢትሐቱ: sense (Mt 10:5) wants ኢትሑሩ; ink is ኢ ት ሐ ቱ (cross-shaped final, foot bottom-right). Transcribed as ink.
- l.22 እስራኤል: the fourth glyph's right leg ends in a small loop; ኢ (left-curling leg, cf. l.1 ኢሳይያስ) and ኤ are near-identical in this hand. ኤ chosen because the name demands it.
- l.7 ወበይባቢ: final glyph matches l.4's ቢ (right hook curving down), not a ring; standard ይባቤ.
- l.16-17 ሠርዑ|ነ: bowl-with-hook read ዑ; standard would be ሠርዐ ለነ.
- l.20 ኢትባዑ: final glyph is a round bowl with hook (ዑ), not the hooked alef; standard ኢትባኡ.
- ll.10, 17 መደኃኒት / መደኃኒነ: the ኀ carries a top-left stroke, read as ኃ; the editor may prefer unmarked ኀ. Sadis ደ for ድ (standard መድኃኒት), consistent with this scribe.
