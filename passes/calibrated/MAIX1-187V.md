## WORK

**Voice: second-person plural exhortation. Citation habit: none marked, none
clearly woven either. Verdict: this side belongs with the ff.181v–184r
exhortation type, not with the canon type — but the page cannot be assigned to
a named work from one side.**

Evidence from the ink:

- **Addressee is "you (pl.)"**, four times: ዑቁ (l.6, "beware!"), -ክሙ in
  ኤያስሕቱክሙ (l.6) and ኢይትማልዑክሙ (l.18), ወእንትሙሰ (ll.16–17, "but as for you"),
  ኢትሐሩ (l.17), ኢትፍቅዱ (l.21). Speaker uses "we/us" with the audience: ፀርነ
  "our enemy" (ll.2, 15), ያርኅቀነ "drive us away", ዘይትቃረነነ "who opposes us".
  That is preacher-to-congregation, not legislator-to-clergy.
- **Zero citation formulae.** No ይቤ, no በከመ፡ይቤ, no ወካዕበ፡ይቤ, no ከመ፡ጽሑፍ
  anywhere on the side. tgrep for ይቤ returns nothing from this folio.
- **Scripture is present only as unmarked echo, if at all.** The "they appear
  as though they prophesy" + "signs" + "neither by vision nor by dream"
  cluster (ll.6–10) is the false-prophet topos (Matt 7:15/24:24 with Deut 13:1–3
  dreamers). It is worked into the preacher's own syntax with no marker. That is
  the woven habit of the homily folios.
- **Baptismal-catechesis frame** (ll.19–22): "for [ስቲባን] … in holy baptism, so
  that you not love evil, and idols, and [ገብር], and sorcery, and all deeds God
  does not approve." Renunciation lists of this shape (idolatry, sorcery) are
  post-baptismal paraenesis, and this compiler's canon folios (168–181, 184v–185v)
  are about ranks, penance and church discipline, not this.
- **No rank vocabulary at all** on the side: no ኤጲስ ቆጶስ, ቀሳውስት, ደያቆናት,
  ሊቃነ ጳጳሳት. The 185v side (last drafted before this one) is dense with them.
- **Neither Peter nor Clement is named.** Consistent with the zero/one count
  over the drafted range; nothing here supports the Qalementos label.
- **Seam to 188r (cropped and checked, `187V-seam-188R-top.png`):** 188r l.1
  opens ትዓቀቡ፡እምዝሙት፡ወእምዢ… "keep yourselves from fornication and from …".
  That completes "and above all, [keep yourselves] …" directly. So despite the
  blank lower third of 187v, the SAME exhortation continues on 188r without a
  rubric or title. The blank is not an end-of-work; it is a scribal gap
  (quire boundary, damaged exemplar, or planned rubric never executed — cannot
  be fixed from this side). tgrep shows ወፈድፋደሰ። with a following ። at 160V l.7
  and ወፈድፋደሰ፡ዘ at 181R l.11, so this scribe punctuates after the particle as a
  habit; the ። at the end of l.24 is not a closing mark.

What I cannot say: whether ff.185v→186→187 is one continuous exhortation or
whether a boundary lies on 186r–187r (undrafted). 185v ends inside the
rank-list canons; 187v is exhortation. Somewhere between them the voice
changes, and only 186r/186v/187r can place it.

## GIST

The preacher asks that God keep "us" from every temptation of Satan our enemy,
who opposes us at all times to drive us from the law of God and from the hope
of the kingdom of heaven, then warns the congregation: beware lest they lead
you astray, neither by vision nor by dream, for they appear as if they prophesy
and boast, as one who speaks good, and bring signs, and sweeten it to them with
their word and make them believe. Our enemy himself works among them and they
will be judged as they chose; but you, do not walk in their way of life, lest
they carry you into judgment — for [at?] holy baptism you [undertook?] not to
love evil, idols, [ገብር], sorcery and every deed God does not approve; and above
all… (continues 188r: keep yourselves from fornication).
