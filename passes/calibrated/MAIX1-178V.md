## WORK

Penitential canons - the continuation of the church-order / penitential block
that runs from f.176r, and the same corpus as the ff.160v-164r anathema pages
(ርኩስ፡ወሰዑብ) and f.177v (ስዕበት, ኢኮነ፡ንጹሐ). Not the Qalementos label.

Evidence from the ink:

- **Penance measured in fasting weeks and standing in church** (ll.10-12):
  ይጹም፡፯፡ሱባዔ፡ወይቁም፡በቤተ፡ክርስቲያን፡እስከ፡ዓመቱ "let him fast seven weeks and
  stand in the church until his year". Fasting-plus-standing tariffs are the
  penitential genre, and "standing in the church" is the ordo of penitents.
- **ንስሐ vocabulary saturates the lower page**: ንስሐሆሙ (ll.13, 15, 20),
  ይትኒስሑ (l.14), ነስሐ (ll.16, 22), ንስሐሁ፡ዘወሀብዎ "his penance which they gave
  him" (l.21) - penance as a thing *assigned* by a confessor, then completed
  (ይፌጹሙ l.20) or denied (ክህደ l.21).
- **A three-way classification**: ወለሠለስቲሆሙ፡ከመዝ፡ንስሐሆሙ "and for the three
  of them, thus is their penance" (ll.12-13) - the page tariffs a triad of
  offender classes, which is canon-collection structure, not homily.
- **Third-person jussive, not "we command"**: ወበከመ፡እዘዝዋ፡ተጋበር "and as they
  commanded her, let her do" (ll.7-8). No first-person apostolic voice on the
  page. The only first person is divine (ll.1-4, see below), which is a
  quotation, not the legislator.
- **Ranks**: none named. Subject is "she" (ll.5-8: ኮነት, ኢተእምር, እዘዝዋ),
  then generic sinners (ዘስሕተ፡ወዘእበሰ l.9), then "they" plural.
- **Continuity with f.177v**: 177v legislated who may not enter the sanctuary
  from Lev/Num; 178v opens inside a divine first-person threat against a
  feminine referent (ገፅየ፡ለዕሌሃ "my face against her", ዝክራ "her memory",
  ኮነት/ኢተእምር "she was / she does not know") and then legislates for "her" who
  did something "while she did not know, inside the church" (ll.5-7). That is
  the Leviticus "I will set my face against that soul" cadence (ነፍስ is
  feminine) carried over the page turn and applied as a canon about
  unwitting defilement in church. The quotation itself must begin on 178r.

Citation order on this page: ONE scripture voice only, the divine
first-person of ll.1-4 (Leviticus-type: face-against, cut off memory/seed),
already in progress at l.1. No "as it is written" / "the Canons of the
Apostles" formula on this side. No name of a book.

Arabic loans: none claimed. Two words are unresolved (ጋብት l.5, ወከሰለ l.22)
and no root is established for either - see NOTES.

## GIST

The page opens mid-quotation in a divine threat: "[I will] not(?) set my face
against her, so as to blot out her memory and destroy all [her] seed upon the
earth"; then a canon: if she [unresolved verb] ... while not knowing, inside
the church, let her do as they command her. Whoever has erred and sinned in
the church is to fast seven weeks and stand in the church until his year; for
all three classes this is the penance until they repent, are cleansed and are
sanctified concerning their penance. If they repent, [those who labour?] and
do the work commanded them, they save themselves from sin, provided they do
not refuse their penance until they complete it; but he who refuses the
penance given him - [unresolved] - if he repents and confesses sin, they give
him [penance, continued on 179r].

## NUMERALS

- l.10 **፯ = 7**, in ይጹም፡፯፡ሱባዔ "let him fast seven weeks". ገ-shape with a
  foot, matching the key's anchored ፯ (42v). Bounded: red dots above AND
  below the glyph (two above, two below, on short bars), so a figure, not the
  letter ግ. Tens test applied per the standing low-on-tens bias: ፸ would need
  the added stem/stroke and has none here; the glyph is the plain ፯ silhouette.
  Compare l.17 ወግብራ, where the same ገ-with-foot silhouette carries no bars and
  is the letter ግ.
- No other numerals. The I-beams at l.1 (ወሌነግረት), l.5 (ከነት), l.14 (ወይንፅሑ),
  l.16 (ነስሐ) are all unbarred letters ነ/ን.

## CONFIDENCE

- **Top third (ll.1-8): LOW-MEDIUM.** Three words will not resolve
  (ወሌነግረት l.1, ይንቀት l.5, ጋብት l.5). Letters are individually clear; the
  words are not. ኢይቀውም፡ገፅየ፡ለዕሌሃ, ከመ፡እጥፍእ፡ዝክራ, ወእማስን, እስከ፡ኵሉ፡ዘርእ፡በዲበ፡ምድር,
  እንዘ፡ኢተእምር፡በውስተ፡ቤተ፡ክርስቲያን are secure.
- **Middle third (ll.9-16): HIGH.** ይጹም፡፯፡ሱባዔ, ወይቁም፡በቤተ፡ክርስቲያን,
  ወለሠለስቲሆሙ፡ከመዝ፡ንስሐሆሙ are secure. Two flags: ዓመቱ vs ፃመቱ (l.12), and
  ወይትቀደሑ where sense wants ወይትቀደሱ (l.15).
- **Bottom third (ll.17-23): HIGH** except ወከሰለ (l.22, unresolved) and
  ወግብራ (l.17, letters clear, form odd). ንስሐሁ፡ዘወሀብዎ, ለእመ፡ኢክህዱ, ይፌጹሙ,
  የሀብዎ are secure.

Scribal forms on this page, for the key:
