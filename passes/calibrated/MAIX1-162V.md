## NOTES

Uncertainties, in order of weight:

- l.1 በዛመዋ — the first word is the least certain thing on the page. Glyph 2 is an H with a top hook AND a bottom-right foot, i.e. ዛ rather than ዘ/ዝ; glyph 4 read as ዋ (ወ with a tail). No Ge'ez word ዛመዋ / መዋ is known to me; it may complete a word begun on 162r (e.g. ...መዋ|ዕል is ruled out, the ፡ follows ዋ). Sense of the clause (ይቁም፡እምቢተ፡ክርስቲያን) is secure; the opening word is not.
- l.5 and l.14 ይትከነስ — clear ink both times (ይ ት ከ ነ ስ), no such Ge'ez verb known to me. Hypothesis for the editor: a loan-verb from Arabic k-n-s (كنيسة, church), "be re-churched / re-admitted to the church", which is exactly what the context needs, always followed by ወይባዕ / ወይቀረብ. Not normalised.
- l.6 and l.14 ሰዑብ — clear ink both times, unknown word to me, paired with ርኩስ both times as a term of ritual state. Possibly Arabic ṣaʿb (hard / grave) in Ge'ez dress. Not normalised.
- l.6 end ወዳ — final glyph read as ዳ; ይ/ደ collide in this hand so ያ is a live second choice. ወዳ|ቲ (l.7) yields nothing I recognise; ? in effect.
- l.7 end ፈ — an isolated ፈ after the ። and before l.8 ውስተ. Read as written; it does not join with ውስተ to form any word I know. Possibly a scribal slip (a ፈ started and abandoned), possibly the first syllable of a word the scribe then lost at the line break.
- l.8 ወይተእመን — ተ has no top-left tick, so ተ not ት (standard ወይትአመን). ቦቱ: could be ቡቱ on shape; read ቦቱ on sense.
- l.9 እው — nonstandard for አው "or" (alef is the hooked form throughout, per key).
- l.13 ገብረ, l.16 ፈጠረ, l.7 ወነገረ — at 4x all three ረ are PLAIN, no ring. At 2x the red ። dots beside l.7 and a thickened terminal made them look like ሬ/ሮ. Do not read ring-ረ on this page from a 2x crop.
- l.15 ዘያሐዙ — ዙ read from a bottom-right hook; ዝ (H with curled top) is the second choice, giving ዘያሐዝ. Neither yields a verb I can place; the context (self-harm, "enemy of his own life, killer of himself", ll.21-23) suggests a verb of self-injury / self-mutilation. Compare the Apostolic canon on the self-mutilator, who is "a self-murderer and enemy of God's creation".
- l.16 ፈጠረ — after ወይገብር, "and works a ፈጠረ in his flesh"; expected ፍጥረተ or similar. Transcribed as inked.
- l.21 እለመ — clearly እ ለ መ (this hand's ስ is two-humped; this is a single-stem ለ). Sense wants እስመ (since) or ለእመ (if). Possibly metathesis of ለእመ. Not normalised.
- l.21 ጸለዒ — new nonstandard spelling for ፀላዒ (key has ጸለኤ for ፀላኢ on another folio; the scribe's ጸ-for-ፀ and ለ-for-ላ are stable, the final vowel is not).
- l.22 ሐይወተ for ሕይወተ; ዚእሁ for ዚአሁ. l.17 ሠጋሁ for ሥጋሁ (ሠ without tick).
- l.1, l.10, l.18 ቢተ after እም (foot form), but l.3, l.11, l.13, l.20 ቤተ (ring form) when standalone. Transcribed as seen; may be a real distributional habit of this scribe (ቢተ after እም-), worth checking on other folios.
- l.2, l.11, l.13, l.20 ሊቃናተ — construct written with final ተ every time (standard ሊቃናት/ሊቃነ). Consistent.
- ።: l.7, l.12, l.14, l.16, l.19, l.23. Those at ll.7, 12, 19, 23 have red dots around the black pairs; kept as ። per key.

New letterform facts for the key:
- ዛ exists in this hand: ዘ-form with an added bottom-right foot (l.1). Distinct from ዝ (curled top) and ዘ (top hook only).
- ዒ: ዐ with a foot at bottom (l.21 ጸለዒ).
- ጺ: ጸ with a foot (l.24 ፈጺሞ).
- Red ። dots sit close enough to a preceding ረ that at 2x they read as a ring; check at 4x before recording any ሬ/ሮ adjacent to a rubricated ።.
- ተ vs ት tick rule (key) held on every instance checked here (ት in ይትከነስ x2, ተ elsewhere).
