## WORK

Not the catalogue label. This is a church-order instruction addressed to a
class of WOMEN, almost certainly the widows' rule. Evidence from the ink:

- Every pronoun on the page is feminine plural: በበይናቲሆን (l.1), ጸሎቶን (l.4),
  ወይሁቦን (ll.4-5), ፈቃደን (l.5), ርእዮን (l.7), ልቦን (l.11), ምግባሮን (l.14),
  ቢጾን (l.15). Eight -ኦን/-ሆን suffixes and not one -ኦሙ. The verbs ይገብራ
  (l.5) and ይተወሀባ (l.1) are 3fp. Nobody but widows, virgins or deaconesses
  gets a run of feminine plurals in a church order.
- The topics are the classic widows' catalogue: prayer at fixed hours
  (evening, morning, noon, "the hours", ll.2-3), God accepting their prayer
  (ll.3-4), the promise of the kingdom with the righteous, martyrs and
  angels (ll.6-10), then the reverse: if their hearts are not upright in
  good works God judges by their deeds (ll.11-14), they despise their
  companion (ll.14-15) and do not do good to the poor with alms (ll.15-16).
  That is Didascalia ch.15 territory (= Apostolic Constitutions III.5-8:
  widows who pray vs. widows who gad about, envy their fellow widows and are
  greedy for gain). Hypothesis, same status as the ch.2 proposal for
  ff.168-173: the ff.168-175 stretch is an Ethiopic Didascalia-type
  church-order text moving from the bishop/teacher section to the widows
  section.
- ll.17-23 are a scriptural catena on giving: **Sirach 18:16-17-18, and in
  FORWARD order** (unlike the two reversed catenae logged on 167v and 173v).
  l.17-18 ቃል፡ሠናይ፡ይሄይስ፡እምውሂብ = 18:16 "a good word is better than a gift";
  l.18-20 ወኀበ፡?፡፪ሆን፡ይትረከቡ፡ጥዑመ፡ቃልሂ፡ወውሂብሂ = 18:17 "and with the [gracious
  one] both are found, the sweet word and the giving"; ll.21-23 God is
  pleased with the almsgiver, but the one who gives "while his heart
  grieves" = 18:18 (the grudging giver), the verdict on him running over to
  175r. Order as they appear: 16, 17, 18.

## GIST

Let them [the widows] greet one another with peace evening, morning, noon
and at the hours, so that God receives their prayer and grants them their
desire, so that they rejoice in his kingdom; God will see them and they
will rejoice with him together with the righteous, the martyrs and the
angels in the kingdom of God. But if they do not make their hearts upright
in good works, God is there to judge them by their deeds; for they despise
their companion and do not [accustom themselves?] to doing good to the poor
with alms. For a good word is better than giving, and with the [gracious?]
both are found, the sweet word and the giving; in this God is pleased with
the one who gives alms, but if he gives while his heart grieves, it is
reckoned [to him as...] (continues 175r).

## NUMERALS

- **l.19 ፪** = 2, in ፪ሆን "both of them" (for ክልኤሆን). Red bars ABOVE and
  BELOW, both present, checked at 4x (174V-crop14.png). The silhouette is
  ይ/ደ-like (loop at top-left, stem, foot), which is why I state the case:
  bars on both sides per the key rule, and the sense "both [word and gift]
  are found" is exactly Sirach 18:17 ἀμφότερα. Without the bars I would
  have read ይሆን, which is not Ge'ez. First instance in this stretch of the
  scribe writing a numeral with a pronominal suffix.
- No other figures. l.17 initial glyph (see NOTES) has NO bars.

## CONFIDENCE

- **Top third (ll.1-8): high.** Only open points: l.1 ወይተወሀባ has a plain
  ተ (no tick) where standard has ወይትዋሀባ; l.2 final ቀትረ may be ቀትሪ (small
  mark at the ረ hook, 174V-crop7.png, 60/40 for plain ረ); l.3 ወሰዓት carries
  the ት tick (standard ወሰዓተ). ይ/ደ decided on sense throughout (ይተወሀባ,
  ይትወከፍ, ይሁቦን, ይገብራ, ይትፌሣሕ).
- **Middle third (ll.9-16): high to medium.** l.10 በመንግሥት has the tick
  (174V-crop15.png) - the ink says ት, not ተ. l.12-13 ዘይኬ|ንኖ: ኬ vs ኪ not
  settled, key attests እኬንኖሙ for this scribe. l.14 እስተኀቀሩ: ኀ order
  unmarked (standard አስተሐቀሩ). **l.15 ወኢያለምዱ is the weakest word on the
  page**: letters ወ-ኢ-ያ-ለ-ም-ዱ, the last glyph could be ጹ (174V-crop18.png);
  no clean lexeme, "not accustomed to" (አልመደ) is the only fit I can offer
  and I do not trust it. l.15 ለዕለ read as scribe's ላዕለ. l.15-16 ነደ|ይ =
  ነዳይ with unmarked order.
- **Bottom third (ll.17-23): medium.** l.17 initial glyph unresolved
  (NOTES). l.17-18 ይሂይ|ስ = ይሄይስ with unmarked order, ሂ has its tick.
  **l.18 ቲርስ unresolved**: ቲ (ተ with right dash, checked), ር, ስ. Sense
  wants the "gracious man" of Sirach 18:17; ቲርስ is not a word I can
  place. Possibly a proper name or a corrupt ኄርሰ, but the ink is ቲ not ኄ.
  Left as written. l.19 ፪ see NUMERALS; ይትረከቡ ከ/ክ not settled. **l.20
  በዘዮቱ**: the ring is clear, so ዮ not ቦ; sense wants ቦቱ "in which". Left
  as written and flagged. l.23 ይትሐሰብ ሰ/ሠ not settled at 2x.

## NOTES

- **Alef**: hooked form everywhere, per key transcribed እ (እግዚእብሔር four
  times, ll.4, 7, 10, 13, 21; መላእክት l.9; ወለእመሰ l.11). Editor to normalise.
- **l.17 initial glyph "?"**: a squat, ring-bearing glyph wider than tall
  with horizontal spikes off both sides, greyish halo (174V-crop14.png,
  top-left). Not ቈ, not ቦ, not a numeral (no bars). The spikes look like
  strike-through and the halo like overwriting; best reading is a deleted
  or corrected letter, not text. Recorded as ? so the editor sees it.
- **Punctuation**: ። at l.1 and l.14 written as the two-spaced-pairs form
  with red dots around it, kept as ።. The l.14 ። closes the widows' promise
  and threat; እስመ opens the reason. l.23 ends with ፡ and then the page.
- **Reverse-order habit not seen here**: the Sirach 18 catena runs 16 → 17
  → 18. Worth logging against 167v and 173v.
- **Arabic loanwords**: none found. ቲርስ (l.18) is unplaced but I can give
  no root for it and do not claim one. ይትከነስ and ክለዕ do not occur.
- **Margins**: right margin carries the gutter-side fore-edge of the facing
  leaf and a column of Latin-script and Ethiopic scraps at x>1650 - that is
  the opposite page's edge in the scan, not text of 174v. Nothing read from
  it.
- **Orthography logged for the key**: ወይተወሀባ (plain ተ), ወሰዓት, በመንግሥት
