## WORK

**A second-person paraenetic homily that quotes, and then cites by name, the
Apostolic Canons. It is not the Apocalypse of Paul.** The 175v shift to Apoc. Paul
material was a quotation inside the homily, not a change of work; this page shows
the seam where the borrowed paradise-description is closed off and turned into
exhortation, and then into canon law.

Evidence from the ink, strongest first:

1. **Grammatical person.** ll.5-6 አንተኒ፡ኩን፡ኂረ "you too, be good"; ll.12-13
   ወአንተኒ፡ቀንዕ "and you too, be zealous"; l.14 ከመ፡ትባዕ "so that you may enter".
   Second-person singular imperative and subjunctive addressed to a hearer. The
   Apocalypse of Paul is a first-person seer narrative (ወርኢኩ "and I saw") with an
   angel interlocutor; neither ርኢኩ nor a መልአክ appears anywhere on this side. A
   homilist is speaking, not Paul.
2. **The text names its own source as an authority it is quoting.** ll.16-20
   ወዓቀቡ፡ሕጎ፡ለቅድስት፡ቤተ፡ክርስቲያን ... ወለቀኖና፡ዘሐዋርያትሂ፡እዘዙ፡እንዘ፡ይብሉ። "and they
   kept his law for the holy Church ... and the Canons of the Apostles too, [which]
   they commanded, saying:" and then a canon follows (l.20 እመቦ፡ዘ... "if there be
   one who..."). A text that says "the canons of the apostles, which say:" is not the
   canons; it is a work that cites them. This is the same diagnostic kind as the
   174r rank-ladder: structural, not resemblance.
3. **The rank order in the cited canon is ascending: deacon (l.22 ዲያቆን) then
   bishop (l.24 ኤጲ[ስቆጶስ]).** The Apostolic Canons and Ap. Const. VIII normally
   descend (bishop, presbyter, deacon). Ascending order is what a paraphrasing
   compiler produces, and it means 176v must contain the bishop clause and very
   probably a presbyter clause.
4. **The paradise-description is closed with a sensory catalogue, l.1-4:** ... ለርእይ
   "for seeing", ወጥዑም፡ለአፍ "and sweet for the mouth", ... ለከናፍር "for the lips",
   ... ለአዕይንት "for the eyes", ወኵሉ፡ዘግብረ፡ማኅደረ፡ለጻድቃን "and everything that he made
   as a dwelling for the righteous". That is a summarising close to the rivers of
   honey/milk/oil/wine of 175v, and it uses ማኅደር "dwelling" three times (ll.4-5,
   6-7, 10) as the homily's own keyword, not Apoc. Paul's ሀገር/city.
5. **l.15-16 ለእለ፡ወረሰዋ፡ለምድረ፡ሕይወት "those who inherited the land of life"** and
   l.7 ማኅደረ፡ሰማዕት "dwelling of the martyrs" echo Apoc. Paul 21-22 (the land of
   promise / the land of the meek) but are put in the third person as a completed
   fact about "them", which a hearer is urged to imitate (l.8 ከማከ፡ሰብእ፡እሙንቱ "they
   were men like you"). Borrowed imagery, homiletic use.
6. l.23 እስከ፡አፍአ፡ከመ፡ከልባን "out to the outside, like dogs" is an unmarked allusion
   to Rev 22:15 ("outside are the dogs"). It is not introduced as a citation.

So the page fits the frame of ff.167-175 (Two Ways + church-order paraenesis), and
the 175v rivers were an excerpt inside that frame. It does not fit a catalogue label
"Qalementos Book IV" any better or worse than the preceding pages did; nothing on
this side names Clement or Peter. If one wants a single label for 167-176, "church-
order paraenesis with apocalyptic borrowings" describes the ink; the Ethiopic
Sinodos/Qalementos family is the natural home for such a mixture, but that is
resemblance, not evidence.

**Arabic loans: none.** Every non-Ge'ez word is Greek: ቀኖና (κανών), ዲያቆን

## GIST

The homilist finishes a description of the dwelling God made for the righteous —
pleasant to the sight, sweet to the mouth, [fragrant?] to the lips, [fair?] to the
eyes — and turns to the hearer: be good, so that you may enter the dwelling of the
martyrs, for they were men like you who fixed their hearts on that dwelling and
entered what was prepared for them before the world was created; be zealous for
the greater grace, so that you may enter with those who inherited the land of life
and kept God's law for the holy Church and the Canons of the Apostles too, which
they commanded, saying: "If there be one who turns aside from the law of the holy
[Church], let the deacon [be excluded? / keep him out?] outside, like dogs ... let
them fear. And if a bishop ..." (continues on 176v).

## NUMERALS

**None.** No figure with bounding bars above and below occurs on this side.

One near-miss, recorded because the key says to: l.8 ስ[ብ]ብእ. The first ብ carries a
bar above and strokes below, so it passes the silhouette test for a numeral. It is
rejected because (a) it sits between ስ and a second identical ብ inside what reads as
ስብእ (this scribe's attested spelling of ሰብእ "men"), where no figure can stand;
(b) no Ethiopic figure has a ብ box silhouette with a left handle; (c) the marks are
three horizontal strokes — the key's deletion mark. Read as a cancelled dittography.

## NOTES

Letter-by-letter flags (never smoothed in the transcription above):

- **l.1 ምክ?ር.** Third glyph has the flat top and top-left serif of the ከ family
  (compare ከ in ከናፍር l.3), not the rounded top of ደ in አዳም on the same line. ምክር
  "counsel" makes no sense in a "quality for organ" list; ወምድር "and earth" would
  need ድ. Left as inked.
- **l.1 ርእይ.** Hooked alef, not distinguishable from ኢ per key. ለርእይ "for seeing"
  fits the list.
- **l.2 ሐሚ?ዕ.** ሐ + ማ with a right tick + 9-bowl. No Ge'ez word recognised; the
  slot (after ወአዳም "and pleasant", before ለ) wants an organ or sense noun. Left as
  inked; collect for the expert.
- **l.2-3 ወሣዕ|ዕሣዕ.** The ዕ is repeated across the line break. Either a
  reduplicated adjective (ሣዕሣዕ with a doubled ዕ) or a line-break dittography.
  Left as inked.
- **l.3 ሳ?ህየ.** Second glyph is a two-legged ሀ-form, not ና, so ሠናየ "beautiful"
  does not fit the ink even though it fits the slot ("... for the eyes"). First
  glyph could be ሰ or ሠ + right stroke. Left as inked; collect for the expert.
- **l.4 ዘግብረ.** Fourth-order ግ (drawn ተ-with-foot-hook per key), where ዘገብረ is
  expected. Scribal, not a different word.
- **l.6 ኂ?ረ.** ኀ with the left-curl third-order mark. Sense ኄረ/ኄር "good" (ኩን፡ኄረ).
- **l.6, l.14 ትባዕ.** ዕ for እ, twice, in ትባእ "you may enter". Consistent with the
  key's እ/ዕ/ኅ behaviour in this hand.
- **l.8 ክማክ.** Both outer glyphs have the ከ-family serif with a right-side
  descender; inked as ክ. Sense reading ከማከ "like you" (ከመ + -ከ) gives "while they
  were men like you", which the whole exhortation needs. Disambiguated on sense.
- **l.8 ስ[ብ]ብእ.** See NUMERALS. Cancelled dittography; ስብእ is the key's attested
  spelling of ሰብእ.
- **l.10 ቦኡ.** Alef with hooked foot after ቦ; "they entered".
- **l.13 ተዓቢ.** Plain ተ (no tick), so not ትዓቢ. ለእንተ፡ተዓቢ፡ጸጋ "for the grace that is
  greater" is the sense.
- **l.14 ጻጋ.** Right stroke on ጸ; the scribe writes ጸጋ with the same fourth-order
  form he uses in ጻድቃን. Scribal.
- **l.15 ወረሰዋ.** Proleptic object suffix on a feminine (ምድር), "they inherited
  it, the land". Grammatically right; the ዋ is clear.
- **l.15-16 ለምድር|ሕይወት.** Sixth-order ር where construct ለምድረ is expected. Scribal.
- **l.16-17, l.21-22 ለቀዱ|ስት, ለቀዱስ|ት.** The scribe's ቅድስት, twice, both times with
  ዱ. Extends the key's ቀድስት attestation.
- **l.17 ቢተ.** The key's ቢተ-for-ቤተ, here after ለቅድስት rather than after እም-.
- **l.17-18 ሕ?ር|በት.** Unresolved. Bowl-on-two-legs first glyph (ሐ family), ር, then
  a clear box በ and ት. Position wants a relative ("which God commanded") or a noun
  parallel to ሕጎ ("... and the ordinance"); ወሥርዓተ would need ሠ, which has three
  prongs and is not what is inked. Collect for the expert.
- **l.18 እዘዘ, l.20 እዘዙ.** Hooked alef for አዘዘ/አዘዙ, per key.
- **l.19 ቀናና.** Two ና where ቀኖና needs ኖ. Sense is certain (ቀኖና፡ዘሐዋርያት); ink is
  not. Inked as ና ና.
- **l.19 ዘሐዋርያትሂ.** Read as ሐዋርያት + enclitic -ሂ "too", parallel to ሕጎ ... ወለቀኖና.
  The alternative ሐዋርያቲሁ "his apostles" would need ቲ ሁ; the glyph before ሂ has
  the ት tick.
- **l.21 ተጋሓ?ሠ.** Fourth glyph is ሐ family + right stroke (not the box of በ). Sense
  ተግሕሠ "turn aside, deviate": ዘተግሕሠ፡እምሕጋ "whoever deviates from the law".
  Flagged for the vowel orders, not the root.
- **l.22 ይትከሎ?.** ይ ት ከ + ለ with a top-right ring. No form ይትከሎ exists;
  ይትከላእ "let him be excluded" (ተከልአ) or ይክልኦ "let [the deacon] keep him out"
  are the candidates. The verb decides whether ዲያቆን is subject or agent, so this
  matters for the canon; left as inked.
- **l.23 ከልባን.** ከ ል ባ ን clear. Not the standard plural of ከልብ (ከለባት/አክላብ), but
  the Rev 22:15 allusion ("outside are the dogs") makes "dogs" the only sense.
- **l.23-24 ርኩ?|ሙ.** ር, then a ከ-form with foot, then ሙ (right leg present, so
  not ም; rules out ርጉም "anathema", which is otherwise tempting in a canon). No
  word recovered. Left as inked; collect for the expert.
- **l.24 ይፍርሁ.** "let them fear". ሁ has the right leg.
- **l.24 ኤጲ.** ጲ written with the hand's ኀ-shape, read as ጲ because the name
  demands it (key rule for ጳ). Word cut at the page end.

Punctuation: ። at ll.5, 12, 14, 20, 24 written as spaced pairs; kept as ።. No red
ink visible on this side.
