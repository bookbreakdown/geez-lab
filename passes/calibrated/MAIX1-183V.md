## WORK

**A homiletic exhortation to the clergy on the ranks of the church, not a canon and not a rank list.** Evidence from the ink:

- **Address form.** Second person singular (l.12 ንግሮሙ "tell them", l.13 ወዕቀብ ለሊከ "and keep yourself", l.14 ተኩን "you [sg.] may be") switching to a plural vocative at l.21: ስምዑ፡እ-ስዩማነ፡ቢተ፡ክርስቲያን "Hear, O appointed ones (ሥዩማን) of the church", then plural ተግበሩ፡ወትምሀሩ (l.22-23) and ኢተኩኑ (l.24). No first-person "we command", no third-person jussive tariff. This is the voice of a homily/paraenesis, not of the canons on ff.176r-180v.
- **No rank list.** The page names no grade. It uses the collective ሥዩማን and the abstract ማዕረግ፡ታሕቲት "the lower rank" (l.16), which presupposes a graded ladder without stating it. That is consistent with either stratum and cannot decide five-grade vs three-grade.
- **Unmarked scripture weaving.** Five scriptural phrases, none introduced by a citation formula (no ከመ፡ይቤ, no በከመ፡ጽሑፍ). Canon-collections cite; homilies weave. The weaving here is dense enough to be the diagnostic.
- **Verdict.** Same family as the homily material established at f.167 (Two Ways) and f.175v (paradise catalogue inside a homily): a sermon to office-holders that the compiler set after the canons. Not the Didascalia (no first-person apostolic plural anywhere on the page). A specific title cannot be given from one side; see NOTES for what would fix it.

**Citations, in order on the page (all unmarked):**
1. ll.3-4 — Acts 20:28: the church "which he acquired (እጥራያ = አጥረያ) with [his] blood (በየመ, expected በደመ) and signed with the wood of his cross (ወዐተባ በዕፀ መስቀሉ)". The cross-clause is not in Acts; it is the homilist's extension.
2. ll.8-11 — 1 Cor 2:9 in full: ዘዓይን፡ኢርእየ፡ወእዝን፡ኢሰምዓ፡ውስተ፡ልበ፡ስብእ፡ዘኢተሀለየ፡ዘእስተደለወ፡እግዚእብሔር፡ለእለ፡ያፈቅርዎ። Note the verb ኢተሐለየ "did not think/conceive" for the third colon. Check against the Ge'ez NT (which I believe reads ኢዐርገ "did not go up" here): if so, this page's wording is an independent rendering and points to translation of a foreign homily rather than quotation from the Ethiopic NT.
3. ll.14-15 — Mt 5:19b: ከመ፡ተኩን፡ዓቢየ፡በመንግሠተ፡ሰማያት "that you may be great in the kingdom of heaven".
4. ll.17-18 — Ps 110:1 / Mt 22:44 / Heb 10:13: ወምክዓደ፡ታሕተ፡እግሪሁ "and a footstool beneath his feet" (ink ምክዓደ; the lexeme is ምክያድ).
5. ll.22-24 — Mt 5:19 again, both halves: ተግበሩ፡ወትምሀሩ "do and teach" (5:19b) and ኢተኩኑ፡መተሐተ፡ታሕቲተ "that you not be least/lowest" (5:19a, ንኡስ in the Ethiopic; here paraphrased with ታሕቲት, the same word used for the rank on l.16 — the homilist is punning rank on the Matthew verse).

**Arabic loans: none claimed.** ማዕረግ (rank) is native from ዐርገ; ሥዩማን is native from ሤመ. Nothing on the page requires a root outside Ge'ez.

## GIST

The page continues a sentence about the stubborn-hearted and the ordinances and law given to the church, which Christ acquired with his blood and sealed with the wood of his cross, for the faithful who believe in God's law; to them he gives honour and glory "which eye has not seen, nor ear heard, nor entered the heart of man, which God has prepared for those who love him" (1 Cor 2:9). The addressee (singular) is told to tell this to the children of the church and to keep himself so as to be great in the kingdom of heaven (Mt 5:19); those who approach the lower rank wrongly shall be [ስለቀ?] and a footstool under his feet, as against those who enter life; "for he commanded that this be done. Hear, O appointed ones of the church, and [be assured?] that you do and teach rightly, so that you do not become the lowest of the low."

## NUMERALS

None. Two letters were tested as figures and rejected for lack of bounding bars:
- l.18 the isolated መ between separators (three-humped, a ፵ silhouette candidate): no bar above or below → letter. It is an orphan; see NOTES.
- l.21 ማነ: the ነ is the I-beam/foot form that mimics ፲ → no bars → letter.

## CONFIDENCE

**Top third (secure):** ll.6-16 and 20-21, 23. The 1 Cor 2:9 quotation (ll.8-11), the Mt 5:19 "great in the kingdom of heaven" (ll.14-15), ማዕረግ፡ታሕቲት (l.16), ስምዑ፡[ኦ]፡ሥዩማነ፡ቤተ፡ክርስቲያን (l.21), ተግበሩ፡ወትምሀሩ፡ርቱዓ (l.23), and the ሥርዓት፡ወሕግ፡ለቤተ፡ክርስቲያን፡እንተ፡አጥረያ clause (ll.2-3).

**Middle third:** ll.4-5, 17, 19, 22, 24 word by word, with the letters given as ink but the words only partly resolved: ወእተባ (= ወዐተባ, secure on sense), ለመሔደምናን (= መሃይምናን; the ሔ has a tick I cannot place), ስለቀ, ወምክዓደ, ደበውዑ (= ይበውኡ), ውስቱ (ተ/ቱ), ወተባደቱ, መተሐተ.

**Bottom third (least certain):** l.1 ባርትዓ and ወበእርቱዓ (l.1-2); l.4-5 እምመ|ደእቲ; l.15 ወእልዕሮ; l.18 the orphan መ and the smudged final glyph; l.20 እኀዘዘ; l.21 ኪ (ኬ?); l.24 the faint final glyph.

## NOTES

**Letter-by-letter flags (do not smooth):**
- l.1 ባርትዓ — first word of the page, continues from 183r. Ink is certain (ባ = box with bottom-left leg, not ብ). Not resolved. Two readings: (a) -ባ is a 3fs object suffix ending a verb split across the page turn (the church is the feminine referent; cf. ወዐተባ, አጥረያ), followed by ርትዓ; (b) one word. The -ዓ ending recurs in ርቱዓ (ll.1-2 and l.23) where the scribe writes ዓ for standard ዐ/ዕ (ርቱዕ/ርቱዐ), so ርትዓ is plausibly ርትዕ "right".
- l.1-2 ወበእርቱ|ዓ — the alef may be ኢ (a lower-left hook is visible at 4x but not decisive). If ኢ: ወበኢርቱዕ "and in un-rightness", parallel to ጸዋግ፡ልብ "perverse of heart". Written እ per the key rule.
- l.3 በየመ — the third glyph has its stem from the bowl's centre (የ), unlike this hand's ይ/ደ (stem at left). Sense wants በደመ "with blood" (Acts 20:28). Either the scribe wrote የ for ደ, or the phrase is not the Acts one. Ink kept.
- l.3 እጥራያ — standard አጥረያ. ራ has the right stroke at 2x; not re-checked at 4x.
- l.4 ወእተባ — ወዐተባ "and he signed her (with the cross)", ዐተበ. Alef hooked, so እ by rule.
- l.4-5 እምመ|ደእቲ — five glyphs, no separator: hooked alef, ም (መ with top loop, same form as ም in ስምዑ l.21), መ, then line-initial ደ/ይ, እ, ቲ. ይእቲ "she/it is" is a natural reading of the last three; the first two (እምመ) are unplaced. Candidates: dittography of መ; or the run belongs to a word I do not know. Expert item.
- l.5 ለመሔደምናን — መሃይምናን "the faithful". ደ = ይ on sense. The ሐ carries a small top-right tick; I cannot say whether it is ሔ, ሓ or an accidental. Written ሔ.
- l.5 end / l.6 start ደ|እምኑ — ይእምኑ "they believe" (= ያአምኑ). ደ = ይ on sense.
- l.9 ውስተ, l.16 ውስተ, l.19 ው in ደበውዑ and ውስቱ — on this page ው is drawn as ወ with a short right tail at mid-height, not the two-loop form the key records for mid-line ው. Tail faint on l.9, clear on l.19. Transcribed ው on sense (ውስተ). Testable against 165r/166v.
- l.9 ኢሰ|ምዓ — ሰምዐ. ዐ/ዓ undistinguished in this hand; written ዓ throughout (ዘዓይን, ሠርዓት, ምዓ, ርቱዓ, ዓቢየ). Only ዑ (l.19, l.21) is distinct, by its hook.
- l.10 ሀለየ — ሐለየ with ሀ, attested habit of this scribe.
- l.10 ዘእስተደለወ — ዘአስተዳለወ; no ዳ marking visible.
- l.12 ለደቂቀ — ደቂቀ "children of"; ደ = ደ on sense (ይ/ደ collision), ቂ by its right dash.
- l.14 ተኩን — the ተ may carry a right-low tick (ቲ?) or a top-left tick (ት). Sense ትኩን. Written ተ.
- l.15 ወእልዕሮ — five glyphs: ወ, hooked alef, ል, ዕ (or a second ል-like glyph), ሮ (ር-shaped with a ring; ሎ also possible). Unresolved. If ወአልዕሎ: "and raise him who draws near"; if ዐልወ-root: "and he who transgresses". The following ዘይቀር|ቦ፡ውስተ፡ማዕረግ፡ታሕቲት "who approaches into the lower rank" is secure. Expert item.
- l.17 ስለቀ — three glyphs, secure as ink, unplaced as a word. ስላቅ "mockery/derision" (ይኩኑ ስላቀ "they shall become a mockery") would fit the threat and the scribe's sadis-for-other-orders habit. Not claimed.
- l.17-18 ወምክዓ|ደ — ink is ዓ (9-bowl), not ያ (compare ያ in ክርስቲያን l.3, l.13). Lexeme ምክያድ "footstool" is what the phrase ታሕተ፡እግሪሁ demands. Kept as ink; the ዓ/ያ substitution is a new orthographic habit if it holds.
- l.18 መ — an isolated መ between two separators after እግሪሁ. Not a numeral (no bars). Probably an abandoned start (መንግሥት?) or a dropped word. Kept.
- l.18 end ? — an oval smudge after ለእለ, the size of one glyph, ink wiped or erased. If it was ኢ, the clause reads ለእለ፡ኢይበውኡ "for those who do not enter into life", which is the better sense. Not readable.
- l.19 ደበውዑ — ይበውኡ "they enter"; ደ = ይ on sense; ዑ is the hooked bowl (this hand writes the ዐ-family with the hook where standard has ኡ). ውስቱ — the ተ has a right foot; ቱ or ት possible; sense ውስተ.
- l.20 እኀዘዘ — four glyphs, no separator: hooked alef, ኀ-shaped glyph (this hand's ኀ; ጳ excluded, no name), ዘ, ዘ. Sense wants አዘዘ "he commanded" (እስመ አዘዘ ገቢረ ዘንተ "for he commanded this to be done"). Either an intrusive ኀ, or አኀዘ "he began" with a dittographed ዘ. Expert item.
- l.20-21 ዘንተ|ኪ — the line-initial glyph has a left foot (ኪ per the ጊ/ጌ rule), not a ring. Sense wants the enclitic -ኬ (ዘንተኬ "this then"). Kept as ኪ.
- l.21 እስዩማነ — vocative ኦ written as the hooked alef (key rule): ኦ ሥዩማነ ቤተ ክርስቲያን. ስ for ሥ; ነ with foot read as ነ (construct), per the widened ነ/ኑ rule.
- l.22 ወተባደቱ — ወ, ተ, ባ (box with bottom-left leg; not ጠ, checked at 4x), ይ/ደ-form glyph (stem at left; not የ), ቱ (cross with right foot). Unresolved. ወተጠየቁ "and be assured" is what the clause wants but the ink does not give ጠ. Expert item.
- l.24 ኢተኩኑ — ኢ by its curled leg; ኢትኩኑ "that you not become". መተሐተ፡ታሕቲተ — መትሕተ ታሕቲት "below the lowest / the least of the low", the rank word again. Final faint glyph (ሞ/ዎ-like, browner than the text ink) at the line end: possibly the start of the next word, possibly offset; not counted as text.
- Orthography attested on this page, consistent with the key: ቢተ for ቤተ (x3), ስብእ for ሰብእ, ሀ for ሐ, ዓ for ዐ/ዕ, ስ for ሥ (ስዩማነ), hooked alef for አ/ኦ/እ.

**Predictions (registered before reading the neighbours):**
- 183r must end mid-sentence, with a clause whose grammatical subject continues into l.1 (a stubborn-hearted person, ጸዋግ ልብ, set against the ordinances of the church). If -ባ is a suffix, 183r's last line ends with a verb stem missing its 3fs object and the church is its topic. 183r should be the same homily: expect ሥዩማን or ማዕረግ vocabulary and unmarked scripture, no tariffs.
- 184r opens completing l.24: expect a phrase such as በመንግሥተ፡ሰማያት (Mt 5:19a, "least in the kingdom of heaven"), then continued plural exhortation to the ሥዩማን, likely ending with a doxology or a rubric within the first side. If 184r opens with a tariff (ይትከነስ, a number of years/days) or a third-person canon, the homily identification is wrong.

**Falsifiers testable against drafted folios (156r-181v):**
1. ሥዩማን as the collective for office-holders: if ff.168-174 (five-grade church order) use it and f.175v does not, this page sits with the church-order stratum; if only the homily material uses it, with 175v. Either result refines WORK; neither is assumed.
2. The ው-with-tail form: 165r l.11 and 166v (five ውስተ/ው cases) should show the two-loop mid-line ው the key records. If they show the same ወ+tail as here, the key's ው rule needs widening; if they show two loops and this page consistently does not, this is a second hand or a change of pen at f.183.
3. ጠ vs ባ at l.22: the penitential folios (176r-180v) write ኃጢአት repeatedly. If their ጠ is a box with a bottom-left leg, my ባ is ጠ and the word is ወተጠየቁ; if their ጠ is the rounded form, ባ stands and the word is still open.
4. Citation practice: if the canon folios (176r-180v) never quote scripture without a formula and the homily folios (167, 175v) weave it unmarked, the five unmarked quotations here are decisive for homily. If the canons also weave unmarked, the criterion collapses and WORK falls back to voice alone.
5. 1 Cor 2:9 wording: if any drafted folio quotes the verse with ኢዐርገ (NT wording), and this page has ኢተሐለየ, the two are from different Vorlagen — a stratum boundary marker independent of the bishop-word.
6. The -ዓ for -ዕ/-ዐ habit (ርቱዓ, ርትዓ, ምክዓደ): check ርቱዕ on any drafted folio. If it is written ርቱዕ there and ርቱዓ here, the orthography shifts at this point in the codex.
