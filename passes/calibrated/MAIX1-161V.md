## GIST

The page opens mid-rule: "...for him who kills the soul of his neighbour by [kwəḥlot: word not identified, possibly a poison or sorcery term], and like a murderer let them give him his penance, and let him stand [away] from the church until he completes his penance, for [this is] the Christian rule (?)". After he has completed his penance let him be [re-admitted], enter and draw near. Unclean and abominable is he who prays at a prayer-place of pagans and Jews and associates with them and eats of their sacrifices: he shares their sin; let him stand [away] from the church until, and if he repents, let him be re-admitted and enter and draw near. Unclean and abominable is he, [every one?] who angers his brother without cause and reviles the elders of the church and the bishop; and for such a one there is no penance unless he confesses to you (?) [ll. 20-21 unresolved]. To whoever wishes it he gives penance, and as they tell them let them do, and after that let him enter and be re-admitted and draw near. Un[clean, continues on 162r].

## NUMERALS

none. Two loop-topped glyphs at l.16 end and l.17 start (the ኁ pair) resemble the ፪/ፉ loop but carry no bounding bars and sit inside a word; the red-dotted ። at ll. 6, 16 and 21 are punctuation, not numeral bars.

## NOTES

Uncertain readings, in descending order of doubt:
- l.16 "ሤሎ": first word after the red ።. Three prongs on a base with a rising curl at the right; ሤ chosen, second choice ሢ. Not a word. Sense wants ኵሎ ("every one who angers his brother without cause", cf. Mt 5:22), but the scribe's ኵ at l.1 is a box with a hooked leg, not three prongs. Left as ink.
- ll. 19-21 "ወዝትሠ ... ኀበ ፡ ክሙ ፡ | እመንቱስ ።": l.19 second glyph is an H with a curled top (key: ዝ), second choice ን; third glyph carries the ት tick; fourth is three-pronged (ሠ/ሰ undecidable). Sense wants ወዝንቱሰ ("and for this one [there is no penance]"). l.20 ends "ኀበ ፡ ክሙ ፡" with a separator between በ and ክ; ክሙ has a visible right tail on the መ. Could be ኀቤክሙ "to you" split by the scribe (Qalementos addresses the clergy). l.21 "እመንቱስ" not identified; no separator visible inside it.
- l.1 "በኵሕሎ|ት": ኵ is the box form with a hooked leg (ኵ/ኩ), ሕ is the boxy ሕ, ሎ has a right ring. Word not identified; root ኰሐለ (kohl, paint) would give "by poison/paint". Possibly a sorcery term. Sense: the means by which the neighbour's soul is killed.
- l.1 "ቢጸ": ቢ with a foot (ቢ), not a ring; ጸ read with sadis unclear. Standard ቢጹ "his neighbour".
- l.7 "ወይባስ": the glyph after ባ is a three-pronged ስ with a foot, not the hooked alef this hand uses everywhere else (compare l.15 "በእ", l.24 "እ"). Sense wants ወይባእ ("let him enter"). Transcribed as ink; second choice ወይባእ.
- l.24 "ወይቀሪሉ": the glyph after ሪ is a ለ-shape with a right tail (ሉ), where ll. 8 and 15 have the box ብ for the same word ወይቀሪብ. Ink says ሉ; second choice ብ.
- l.5 "ሠሪጠ": three prongs, a ረ-form with foot, then a ጠ. Second choice ሢጠ. Sense wants ሥርዐተ ("the rule/order of the Christian"), which fits ll. 5-6 "እስመ ... ክርስቲያናዊ". Not resolved.
- l.10 "ወየሃብር": the third glyph is two verticals joined at the base with a right-rising tail, i.e. ሃ; but the root is ኀብረ "to associate", so the scribe's ኀ-family cannot be excluded (ኃ). Standard ወየኀብር.
- l.2 "የሀብዎ": the glyph after የ is H-shaped with a mid bar and reads ዘ on shape; sense (ንስሐሁ follows) requires ሀ. ሀ chosen and flagged.
- l.2 "ቀተሌ": the middle glyph carries a top-left tick (the key's ት marker) though ቀታሌ "murderer" wants ታ. Written ተ as the nearest plain reading; ሌ has a ring.
- l.9 "ይጼሌ": final glyph has a solid blob at the bottom right, read as a ring (ሌ); second choice ሊ (standard ይጼሊ).
- ll. 3-4 "ወይቁሙ", "ይፌጽሙ": both ሙ carry a right tail; the subject is singular (ንስሐሁ "his penance"). Ink kept.
- l.4 final glyph: smudged stem with a left-going flag, ን (needed by ን|ስሐሁ); it differs from ግ at l.23 "ይግበሩ", whose hook goes right.
- l.13 "እስከ" ends the line and l.14 opens "ወእመ": "until, and if he repents" reads as a dropped clause. Transcribed as ink.
- l.16-17 "እኁ | ኁሁ": both line-break glyphs are the ቀ-like stem with a top-right loop that this hand uses for the ኀ family (compare ጳ at ll. 18-19). Dittography across the line break; sense is እኁሁ "his brother".
- l.24: the ት of ወይትከነሱ is written above the line; the on-line glyph is a plain ተ (no tick). A scribal self-correction of order, not a later hand as far as ink colour shows.
- ll. 6, 16, 21: ። written as spaced dot pairs with red dots (the key's form). l.2 after ነፍስ has the two dots stacked more vertically than usual; still ፡.

Nonstandard spellings on this page (ink, not normalised):
- ንስሐሁ (ll. 3, 5, 7) with ሐ, where 160v had ንስሕ; ነስሐ l.14.
- ይስተፍ (l.12) for ይሳተፍ; ኀጢእቶሙ (l.12) for ኃጢአቶሙ (key attests ኃጢእቶሙ; order of ኀ unmarked here).
- እይሁድ (l.10) for አይሁድ; እረሚ (l.9) for አረሚ; እልቦ (l.19) as in the key; እመሠዋዕቶሙ (l.11) with ሠ for መሥዋዕት.
- ይትከነሱ / ይትከነስ (ll. 7, 14, 24): same unidentified root as 160v ትከነሱሂ; used in this text for readmission after penance.
- ወእምድኅሬ (l.6), ወእምድኅሬሁ (l.23): ኅ written with a top curl; order not securely marked.
- ሊቃናተ (ll. 17-18) for ሊቃናት.

New letterform facts for the key:
- **ኀ-family in this hand is a ቀ-like stem with a top-right loop**, the same shape as ጳ (ll. 16-19 give ኁ ኁ ጳ ጳ in sequence). Second order is the loop; do not read the loop as ፉ or ፪.
- **Interlinear self-correction attested**: a ት added above a plain ተ (l.24). The scribe corrects vowel order above the line rather than by erasure.
- **ሉ and ብ can collide** at line end (l.24 vs ll. 8, 15): ብ is the box, but a hastily closed ብ resembles ሉ.
- ተጋነየ (l.20) "confess/submit" is written with standard orders; ጋ carries the right foot clearly.
