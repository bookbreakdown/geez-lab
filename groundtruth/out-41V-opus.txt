4. HONEST ASSESSMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT I WOULD STAKE SOMETHING ON:
  - 23 lines.
  - Exactly three barred figures; the four other orange marks are rosettes.
  - Line 5 = ፯፻ = 700, and it is externally anchored by 1 Kings 11:3 in a slot
    that names Solomon and the verb "he married". This is the most secure figure
    on the page and the only one on any of the three ground-truth folios with an
    independent scriptural anchor.
  - Line 23's figure has a descending right stem. That is an observation, not an
    interpretation, and it was calibrated against a stemless three-pronged letter
    in the same hand on the same page.

WHAT I AM LESS SURE OF:
  - That the descending-stem form means 40 and cannot mean 50. The anchor table
    has no ፶ entry. I flagged this rather than assuming the table is complete,
    because assuming a table is complete is how the generative rule got promoted
    and then falsified.
  - The name ናሆም, and the whole of lines 10, 19 and 21.

WHAT I DEFERRED, PLAINLY:
  - For the TENS I deferred entirely to the anchor table and did not let the
    generative rule vote. That was the instruction and I followed it literally.
  - For the UNITS and ፻ I read on the rule first and then found the anchor table
    agreed. I did not need to break a tie.
  - I did NOT read out-41V.txt, out-42R*.txt or out-42V*.txt before or during
    this reading, so the only prior input I carried was what the brief told me:
    that a previous reader supplied ፴ = 30, and that Exodus says forty. Both are
    contamination and both are disclosed above. I would rather you discount my
    forty for that reason than believe it for the wrong one.
