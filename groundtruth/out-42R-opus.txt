METHOD NOTE — read this before the numeral table
======================================================================
I read the numerals from the ink BEFORE opening the numeral section of
SCRIBAL-KEY.md against them. The key was read first (as instructed) but the
letterform sections are what I used while transcribing; I deliberately did not
look up a value until I had written my own down.

The thing that actually let me read the figures is not in the key and I want it
on the record, because it is checkable and it is not deference: **these numerals
are Greek uncial letters with bounding bars.** 1=Α 2=Β 3=Γ 4=Δ 5=Ε 6=Ϛ 7=Ζ 8=Η
9=Θ 10=Ι 20=Κ 30=Λ 40=Μ 50=Ν 60=Ξ 70=Ο 80=Π 90=Ϙ 100=Ρ. Every shape the key
describes falls out of that mapping:

  key says ፰ is a "mid-hooked I-beam"      -> Η (eta), a crossbarred I
  key says ፲ is a "plain serifed I-beam"   -> Ι (iota), no crossbar
  key says ፵ is "three-pronged with a
     descending right stem"                -> Μ (mu)
  key says ፯ is a "ገ-shape with a foot"    -> Ζ (zeta)
  key says ፪ is "a loop, not a Z"          -> Β (beta), and the warning is
                                              precisely the beta/zeta collision
  key says ፻ is a "closed bowl"            -> Ρ (rho), a stem with a top-right lobe

That is a generative rule rather than a lookup table, and it is the reason I can
say which readings are mine. It also predicts the two dangerous confusions on
this page: Ι/Η (10 vs 8) and Ρ/Β (100 vs 2).

The other independent check available on this page is ARITHMETIC INTERNAL TO THE
PAGE: line 7 states a total and lines 7-9 state the three components that sum to
it. That fixes two glyph values without reference to any witness.

Eight-point rosettes of alternating orange and black dots (= ።) occur at least
eight times: l.6 init., l.9 mid-right, l.12 init., l.13 fin., l.18 init. and
fin., l.20 init., l.22 fin., l.23 fin. None was counted as a numeral. Every
figure below carries a bar ABOVE and a bar BELOW.

figure inside the first line or two, and should then continue the Judges
sequence: Barak, Gideon, Abimelech, Tola, Jair. If 42v opens with anything
other than the continuation of the Deborah entry, one of the two reads is
wrong.

an anno-mundi count (l.23, "4,000 from the creation", tied to Ehud's 27th
year). f. 42v should contain at least one more እምፍጥረት synchronism of a LARGER
value, and it should be built the same way — tens before ፻, multiplicative.

HONEST ASSESSMENT — did I read the hand, or infer from context?
======================================================================
Both, and I can separate them.

READ FROM THE INK, and I would defend these with no context at all:
  - the ፵ of ll.8, 9 and 23. The three prongs plus the below-baseline right stem
    are unmistakable at 4x and identical in all three places.
  - the ፰ of ll.13 and 18. The mid-height crossbar is inked, and it is what
    separates eta from iota.
  - the ፯ of l.22 and the ፪ of l.5. Different glyphs, and I can say how.
  - the rho/፻ of ll.7, 18 and 23 — I cropped l.7 and l.18 side by side at 4x
    precisely to test whether they were the same letter, and they are.
  - the whole letter-text. The words ወእምድኅሬሁ, ኮነኖሙ, ዓመት, ወልደ, ንጉሠ recur often
    enough that the hand becomes legible; I did not need the names to get them.

INFERRED FROM CONTEXT, and I am telling you so:
  - ፳ = 20 at l.7. The SHAPE alone I would have called a closed triangle, i.e.
    delta/4. What makes it 20 is the page's own arithmetic (40+40+40=120). That
    is a real derivation, but it is not letterform recognition, and if the
    arithmetic had not been sitting three lines below it I would have got it
    wrong.
  - ፹ = 80 at l.21. Pi is the best fit for a top bar with hanging legs, but
    there are three legs and pi wants two. Judges 3:30 says eighty, and I am
    aware that agreement made me comfortable faster than the ink warranted.
  - the names Cushan and Rishathaim at ll.12-13, and the name Israel at l.17.
    I read እስራኤል confidently; I read ኵሳ|ን on a partly-blotted ligature and the
    second element not at all. The narrative supplied the expectation.

WHERE I DEFERRED TO NOTHING, and the reading is therefore exposed:
  - l.15 = 30. Judges says 40, and the key's standing bias says this hand gets
