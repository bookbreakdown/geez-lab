SHAPE KEY FOR THIS SCRIBE (derived from cross-folio comparison)
======================================================================
The scribe's numerals are Greek-letter forms with red bars above and below.
The key change from the earlier read is the TEN / TWENTY assignment.

  ፲ 10  = a PLAIN vertical bar with short serifs top and bottom (Greek iota).
          Seen on 42v line 11 and 42v line 23. It does NOT occur on 42r.
  ፳ 20  = WIDE serifed top bar over a hollow triangle with splayed feet.
          This is the glyph the earlier read called ፲. Anchors: 42v line 8
          Tola "፳ወ፫" = 23 (Judges 10:2 = 23), and 42r line 7 Moses 120
          (= 40 + 40 + 40, the page's own arithmetic in lines 8-9 and 41v).
  ፴ 30  = ከ-like: flat top bar, left leg curving out to a foot, straight
          right stem with a foot, hollow between (a serifed lambda). 42r
          line 11 only. The earlier read called this ፳.
  ፵ 40  = three-prong W with a right descender (mu). Lines 8, 9, 23; 41v
          line 24; five times on 42v. Reliable.
  ፹ 80  = two legs under a wide bar, no hollow (pi). Line 21.
  ፻ 100 = P with a CLOSED bowl (rho). Lines 7, 18, 23; 41v line 5.
  ፩ 1   = hollow triangle with a NARROW cap, no wide serifs (41v line 7).
          Distinguished from ፳ only by cap width; not on this page.
  ፪ 2   = loop-topped, ደ-like (beta). Line 5.
  ፬ 4   = rounded box with a vertical hollow slit. Line 11.
  ፯ 7   = squared Z (zeta). Line 22.
  ፰ 8   = I-beam with pinched waist (eta). Lines 13, 18; 42v line 11.
