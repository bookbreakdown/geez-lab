NUMERALS:
All numerals are in black ink with red (orange) bars above and below. Values assigned by
glyph shape cross-checked against the internal sequence (the "ሠ"-shaped three-pronged glyph
recurs for Barak, Gideon and the Philistine oppression, all 40 in Judges, so it is ፵; the
"ገ"-with-foot glyph recurs for Midian and Ibzan, both 7, so it is ፯).
  line 1   ፵      = 40   Barak (ባርቅ) — length of his judgeship / years of rest (Judg 5:31)
  line 3   ፯      = 7    Midian (ስብእ ፡ ምድያም) — length of the Midianite oppression (Judg 6:1)
  line 5   ፵      = 40   Gideon (ጌዴዎን) — length of his judgeship
  line 7   ፫      = 3    Abimelech (አቢሜሌክ) — length of his rule
  line 8   ፳ወ፫    = 23   Tola son of Puah (ቱላ ፡ ወልደ ፡ ፉሐ) — length of his judgeship
  line 11  ፲ወ፪    = 12   Jair the Gileadite (ኢኤል ፡ ገላአዊ) — length of judgeship. Bible has 22;
                         the ink's first glyph is the plain bar ፲, not ፳. Second glyph is an
                         "I-beam" form read as ፪ (least certain identification on the page).
  line 13  ፯      = 7    Jephthah (ዮፍታሔ) — length of judgeship. Bible has 6; ink glyph is the
                         same ፯ form as lines 3 and 15.
  line 15  ፯      = 7    Hesebon = Ibzan (ሔሴቦን) — length of judgeship
  line 17  ፵      = 40   Philistines (ኤሎፍሊ) over the children of Israel — length of oppression
  line 18  ፵      = 40   Elon the Zebulunite (ኤሎሙ ፡ ዘቡለዊ) — length of judgeship. Bible has 10;
                         the ink glyph is unambiguously the same ፵ form as lines 1, 5, 17, 20.
  line 20  ፵      = 40   Philistines (ኤሎፍሊ) — length of oppression (second mention)
  line 23  ፲ወ፰ተ   = 18?  Samson (ሶምሶን) — length of judgeship. Bible has 20. Second glyph is
                         a stem with left pennant and right foot; read as ፰ (8), but ፫ (13)
                         is possible. The trailing ተ is unbarred and is probably a phonetic
                         complement (-tu) on the number, not a numeral.
  line 2   [struck through] the deleted word contains a bar-shaped glyph like ፲ with black
                         bars; it is part of a cancelled false start (probably a botched ኵኖሙ),
                         not a count.

NOTES:
- Folio identification: the caller expected the end of Book One / start of Book Two with
  the kings of Judah. The ink on this image runs from Barak to Samson (Judges 4-16). Either
  the image is a different folio than 42v, or the Judah regnal figures are on a later leaf.
  The Abijah/Jehoshaphat figures should be sought a few folios on.
- Line 2: a whole word is cancelled with a wavy strike-through in brown ink. Readable
  strokes under the cancellation: ኵ, a barred bar-glyph, a bowl-glyph, ሙ. Transcribed as
  [struck through: ኵ፲?ሙ].
- Line 2 ኅሬሁ (and each ወእምድኅሬሁ): the ኀ-glyph could be read ኃ; kept as ኅ.
- Line 3 ስብእ: first glyph has the bent lower leg of ስ; third glyph reads እ. Standard
  spelling would be ሰብአ. Ink kept.
- Line 6, 8, 10, 13, 14, 18, 22 ኵኖሙ: first glyph is ከ with a right-side tail (ኵ/ኰ form);
  standard ኮኖሙ "he was over them". Ink kept.
- Line 9-10 ኢ|ኤል: the name of Jair is split across the line break and reads ኢ-ኤል in the ink
  (a corruption of ኢያኤር/ኢያኢር).
- Line 13 ends ዓመተ with no ። before ወእምድኅሬሁ on line 14.
- Line 16 ለደቂቀ: second glyph is ደ, giving "the children of Israel".
- Line 18 ኤሎሙ: third glyph is ሙ not ን; ink kept (Elon).
- Line 18 ዘቡለዊ: ink kept (standard ዘብሎናዊ).
- Line 22 ኃያል: the ኀ-glyph read as ኃ.
- Line 23-24: line 23 ends ለ and line 24 begins ለሕዝብ — either dittography of ለ or the
  scribe restarted the word at the new line.
- Line 24 end: after ዘንተ ፡ there is a heavy sign in a darker ink (resembles ጀ/ያ) followed by
  ቀ ቁ ቀ in a paler, different hand with small strokes above the first ቀ. Probably a pen
  trial or an incomplete later addition, not part of the running text; transcribed as
  ? ቀቁቀ.
- Numeral identifications for ፵, ፯, ፫, ፳, ፲ are confident (shape and internal sequence
  agree). ፪ (line 11) and ፰ (line 23) are the two uncertain glyph identifications.
- Right margin carries the beginnings of the next page's lines showing through / a strip of
  the facing page; not transcribed.
