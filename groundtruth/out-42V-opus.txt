    bounding-bar pairs, not by going to the listed lines. I found one the key
    does not list at all (l.18) and I found the second member of the l.8 and
    l.23 compounds, which the key does not describe.
  * l.18 = 40.  The key has no value for this line.
  * l.11 second figure = 8, l.23 second figure = 5, l.8 second figure = 3.
    The key supplies no values for any of these.
  * l.8 first figure. I read it as LAMBDA/30 and I am reporting that AGAINST
    the key's 20. See the dissent below.

WHERE A PRIOR VALUE WAS IN MY HEAD:
  * ll. 1/5/17/20 (40), ll. 3/13/15 (7), ll. 11/23 (10), l.7 (3). In each of
    these the key named the line number first. What I can offer instead of a
    claim of independence is INTERNAL CONSISTENCY, which deference cannot fake:
    the four 40s are four mutually identical glyphs; the three 7s are three
    mutually identical glyphs; the two 10s are the same glyph. A model that was

VERDICT ON THE GREEK RULE — did it generalise off f.42r?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PARTLY. It earns its place, but it has one outright failure and it is the
failure that matters most.

HELPED (7 of 15 figures): [4] [6] [8] [15] strongly; [1][3][5 as a test][7][11]
[12][13][14] mildly.
  The strong wins share one property: the rule supplies a POSITIVE CRITERION
  where the anchor table supplies only a silhouette.
    - "rho's bowl must close back onto the stem" saved figures [4] and [6] from
      being promoted to 100. This is precisely the f.42r 108-for-18 error, and
      the rule is the thing that prevents it. The anchor table CANNOT prevent
      it: it describes ፫ as "closed bowl top-right" and ፻ as "closed bowl."
      Those are the same words for 3 and for 100.
    - "eta is an I-beam with a mid-stroke" gave me a filing slot for figure [8].
      Without it that glyph has no name and collapses into ፲.
    - "count the arms off the spine" gave me figure [15], for which the anchor
      table lists no shape at any value.

HINDERED (1 of 15, and it is the important one): figure [5], the tens of Tola.
  The rule says 20 = Κ. Kappa is a stem with two arms to the right. The glyph
  on l.8 is two diverging legs under a joined apex, between two black bars,
  with an arch-shaped counter. It is not a kappa by any reading. Following the
  rule sends me to Λ = 30 and away from the key's 20.
  THE KEY CONTRADICTS ITSELF HERE. Its anchor table describes ፳ as "bar + bowl
  + leg" and calls the ፳ identification "triple-anchored and the most reliable
  thing here." "Bar + bowl + leg" is not a description of kappa either. So the
  generative rule and the key's own most-trusted anchor disagree about the same
  sign, and the key has not noticed.
  ONE OF THREE THINGS IS TRUE, and the next reader should be sent to settle it:
    (a) the Ethiopic tens did NOT keep their Greek shapes (very plausible —
        the units are Greek but the tens drifted), in which case the rule must
        be cut back to "the UNITS are Greek uncials" and the tens go back to
        the observed table;
    (b) the key's ፳ is wrong and these are ፴; or
    (c) this hand's ፳ is a kappa so cursive it has closed into an arch.
  Test for (a): the same failure should appear on every ፳, ፴, ፶ in the corpus,
  and the Μ=40 form — which DOES look like a mu — argues against it. Test for
  (b): d'Abbadie 78 on Tola.

NO DIFFERENCE (3 of 15): [2] [9] [10], the three ፯ forms. The rule and the
  table pick out the same glyph by different words.

COLLISIONS THE RULE PREDICTED, AND WHETHER THEY FIRED ON THIS PAGE:

  Ι/Κ/Λ (10/20/30) — FIRED, at line 8, and the rule LOST. See above.

  **Ϛ / Ζ — 6 against 7.** Three of fifteen figures here are Ζ-forms. Judges

FLAGS — not smoothed
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* ሐሒብን (ll.14-15). The slot — a judge between Jephthah and Elon, seven years —
  is Ibzan of Bethlehem (Judges 12:8-9). The ink is NOT አብጻን or ኤስቦን. First
  glyph is unambiguously ሐ, not the hooked alef this hand uses for አ/እ/ኦ.
  Transcribed as written. For the expert.
* ለምሱን (l.22) against ሱምሶን (l.23) — the same man spelled two ways four glyphs
  apart, ለ- then ሱ-. Not normalised.
* ስብእ for ሰብአ (l.3). Already attested in the key's orthography list; this is an
  independent second attestation.
* ኢሎፊሊ for ኢሎፍሊ (ll.16, 20).
* THE DOUBLET. The Philistines oppress twice, at ll.16-17 and ll.19-20, forty
  years each, with Elon the Zebulonite between them. Both figures are the same
  Μ-form. This is what the page says; whether it is the compiler's doublet or a
  scribe's eyeskip is not decidable from this folio.
* ለደቂቀ (l.16) — exercises the ይ/ደ collision the key documents; read as ደ on
  sense ("the children of Israel"), not on shape.
* The bare ተ on l.23 between the units figure and ዓመተ. No bounding bars, so not
  a figure. Unexplained.
* ELON = 40. Judges 12:11 gives him ten years. The ink is the Μ-form, four
  times attested on this page. I am not adjusting it to fit Judges.
* JAIR = 18. Judges 10:3 gives him twenty-two. The first figure is the plain
  I-beam, not the l.8 arch. I am not adjusting it.
