MA IX 1, f.43r — NUMERAL READ
Tuebingen Ma IX 1, Qalementos Book One. Read 2026-09-17, Opus, no tooling,
crops only (crops/43R-*). 24 lines, single column.

=====================================================================
0. HEADLINE
=====================================================================
Six numeral positions on the page, seven signs. FOUR of them are the
three-pronged tens sign with a descending right stem. They count Eli,
Saul and David, and every one of them lands ON the received chronology
(40 / 40 / 40), not under it.

This page does NOT run low.

=====================================================================
1. THE NUMERALS
=====================================================================

core). I checked both bars on every one; none is promoted from a letter.

   wa [7] <noun> ." then a new clause "AND HE BUILT...".
   The two figures are joined by a written wa across the line break,
   exactly as L1's pair is, so the natural reading is the compound
   40 wa 7 = 47. I cannot rule out two independent figures.
   The noun they qualify I read as a-w-r-a-KH / a-w-r-a-T. If that is
   the plural of "month" it reads "47 months"; I could not settle the
   final letter and I am NOT smoothing it. FLAGGED, not resolved.
   Context is ships (l.22), gold, and a named city — the Ophir/Tarshish
   material — immediately before "and he built".

=====================================================================
3. THE 30 / 50 GAP — NOT CLOSED HERE
=====================================================================
NO securely-read 30 and NO securely-read 50 on f.43r. Nothing on this
page anchors either empty cell. The gap stands.

What this page DOES add to the key:

  * A FOURTH, FIFTH, SIXTH and SEVENTH attestation of the three-pronged
    descender sign, all on one folio, in one pen, at four places in a
    regnal list. Best exemplar: L18 (43R-L18-fig-6x.png) — bars clean,
    ink unbroken, descender unmistakable.

  * A measurement for the descender, which the key has never carried:
    the right prong's foot sits 15-23 px below the feet of the left and
    middle prongs at native scan resolution (3560x5000), i.e. roughly
    one fifth of the glyph's own height. It is not a wobble; it is a
    deliberate extra stroke length.

  * NEGATIVE evidence worth recording: on a page with seven numerals
    and four tens, every tens sign is this one sign. This scribe's
    regnal figures are not drawn from a wide tens repertoire.

HONEST LIMIT ON THE THREE 40s: 30 is unanchored, so I excluded it by
the descender alone — one criterion, not two. If the descender is not
in fact what separates 30 from 40 in this hand, all four tens on this
page move together and Eli/Saul/David become 30/30/30. I record that as
the live risk. I did NOT use the received chronology to read them; the
agreement noted in section 5 is a check made AFTER the read, not the
instrument of it.

=====================================================================
5. THE TEST
=====================================================================
Read first, compared afterwards. The figures this page gives:

    Eli the priest        40
    Samuel the prophet     7
    Saul, first king      40
    David son of Jesse    40
    (17 years of no ruler, subject on the previous folio)
    (40 wa 7 in the gold/ships clause)

Eli 40, Saul 40 and David 40 are the standard received figures. This
page does not run below the received chronology at any point I could
read. If anything the three 40s are the most conventional numbers in
the whole Judges-to-Kings chronology.

So: the Othniel 20 and Abimelech 3 divergences are NOT corroborated as
a systematic difference between this witness and d'Abbadie 78. Three
independent tens on this page sit exactly where the received text puts
them. On this evidence the two earlier lows look local, not systemic —
which is also what the retraction of the "low-tens bias" predicted.

Samuel's 7 is not a check either way: the biblical text gives Samuel no
regnal total, so 7 is this compiler's own figure and cannot confirm or
disconfirm anything.

6. READING OR INFERRING
=====================================================================
READING:
  - all seven bounding-bar pairs. Checked above AND below on each.
  - the 10 on L1: shape read off the ink, clean.
  - the three 7s: shape read off the ink; L1 and L5 are the same sign
    twice, which is a control I did not have to import.
  - all four tens: three prongs and a descending right prong, seen at
    6-7x on four separate crops.
  - the names: Eli, Samuel, Saul, David, Jesse, Solomon, Israel, the
    Philistines. These are read, not guessed; the Saul/David/Philistines
    cluster is mutually confirming and "David son of Jesse" is verbatim.

INFERRING — say so plainly:
  - 40 rather than 30 on the four tens. The shape is read; the VALUE is
    inferred from one unanchored contrast. Flagged above.
  - 47 for the L23+L24 pair. The wa is on the page; treating the pair
    as one compound is inference by analogy with L1.
  - the noun after the L24 seven. Not resolved. Left rough on purpose.
  - "17 years while they had no ruler" — the clause is read, but whose
    17 years is on the previous folio and I did not go there.

NOT DONE: I did not look at 42v or at our English for any figure before
writing this. No expected value entered the read.

REFUSALS: I refuse a value for the noun on l.24. I refuse to name the
subject of the 17 years. I refuse to call the four tens SETTLED at 40 —
narrowed to 40 by the only available discriminator, with 30 excluded by
absence of a shape rather than by presence of one.
