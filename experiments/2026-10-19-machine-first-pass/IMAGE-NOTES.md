## Practical consequence

Consecutive reading exposes joins and repeated imagery unavailable in isolated
samples. It does not resolve all ambiguous handwriting. Retain readable English
where supported, and a local gap where the source fails. All proposed departures
from the frozen translation should be listed in the revision log; do not replace
the original source files or call these observations character-error ground truth.
