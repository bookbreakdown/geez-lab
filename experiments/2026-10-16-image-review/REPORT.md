# Image review and first complete-side English packet

Ge’ez Lab / [EthiopianCanon.com](https://ethiopiancanon.com), October 16, 2026.

## Work completed

- Inspected upper, middle, and lower regions of 153r, 168r, and 41v, plus 168v
  opening context, using the source images.
- Aligned **71 main-text lines** across the three sides with the supplied
  recognition outputs and existing machine drafts. Preserved extra fragments
  outside main-line assignment. Both 153r variants remain visible.
- Recorded **20 targeted observations**, including agreement checks, as
  [review proposals](../../reviews/2026-10-16-image-comparison.md).
- Prepared [English covering all three sides](ENGLISH-REVIEW-PACKET.md), with
  gaps and alternatives explicitly marked and line ranges for every block.
  This is an AI draft for correction, not independent expert review.

## Findings

The specialist recogniser provides alternative letter sequences worth inspecting,
but loses numerals, word boundaries, and cancellation information in this sample.
On 168r, cancellation is clear in the image and must be preserved editorially.
On 153r, a repeated fragrance-family interpretation and an eyes-versus-hands
alternative deserve correction of the English, pending expert confirmation.
Other difficult words remain unresolved rather than reconstructed as facts.

The output is useful as an additional comparison layer; it is not ready for
unchecked replacement or automatic acceptance. No measured improvement over
existing drafting has been established. Architecture and agreement are not
independence, and input resolution cannot be ranked without corrected reference
text and reliable output-to-image mapping.

## Limits and next external step

No expert reference, blinded comparison, human correction timings, CER, or
significant-word error rate was produced. The 20 observations were selected
purposefully; they are not an error-count sample. No independent model call was
made in this pass. The assistant had access to earlier drafts and reference
comparisons. This is separate from the completed Grébaut excerpt pilot and does
not test a newly optimised translation procedure.

The three sides are nonconsecutive. They provide immediate review material but
are not the planned three-side Grébaut-aligned test or a production-ready batch.
Some source clauses remain unresolved. The next external requirement is a Ge’ez
reader who can correct a small sample and record time and residual English
errors. No account signup, paid processing, or further repeat uploads are needed
to review this packet. Contacting a reviewer has not been undertaken.
