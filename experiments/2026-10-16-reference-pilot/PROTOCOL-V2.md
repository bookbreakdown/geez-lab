# Evaluator revision after the first failed qualification

Ge’ez Lab / https://ethiopiancanon.com. 2026-10-16.

The first judge rejected all8 deliberately wrong controls but also rejected a
faithful translation for omitting “En effet”. The preregistered gate failed11/12.
No translation candidates were run or selected under that failed gate.

The first control set is now development/calibration material, not a test. This
revision clarifies that stylistic/connective differences are acceptable unless
they alter substantive logical relations. It retains checks for omissions,
negations, roles, quantities and unsupported additions. It also tests narrowly
scoped review-only variant exclusions.

The revised rubric gets one fresh qualification on14 newly constructed controls:
five acceptable(including a masked source variant) and nine defective(including
a wrong person not excused by the numeric mask). All14 must pass. Neither the
old controls nor French expected facts are sent to translators. If this gate
fails, record it and stop search rather than tune again on these controls.

The original dataset, splits, and three procedures are unchanged. The overall
budget remains14 calls including the initial failure. If needed, reserved
passage scoring for both candidates will be batched to respect that ceiling.
At most one general-instruction revision is allowed, with no reference-specific
names, numbers or source phrasing. Selection and freeze precede reserved tests.

French snippets come from Grébaut ROC17(1912), printed141–142, except source
context is reassembled as complete sentences and typographical variants are
normalised for the evaluator-only controls. This is a small constructed check,
not a validated translation benchmark. Other limits in PROTOCOL.md still apply.
